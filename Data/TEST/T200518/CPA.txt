Title: Device and method for operating a Coriolis mass flowmeter

Abstract: The invention relates to a device and a method for operating a Coriolis mass flowmeter with at least one measuring pipe, and is characterized in that at least two operative assemblies, which can respectively be operated as an exciter (actuator) and/or as a measuring signal pickup (sensor), are attached to the measuring pipe. The operative assemblies are formed as plunger-type armature sensors with a magnetic core. Each operative,assembly is operated either as a sensor or as an actuator or simultaneously as an actuator and as a sensor. Each operative assembly is switched over between the operating mode as a sensor and as an actuator by a switch assigned to it.

Type: US Patent

Claims: 

1. Device for operating a Coriolis mass flowmeter with at least one measuring pipe, wherein at least two operative assemblies, which can respectively be operated as an exciter (actuator) and/or as a measuring signal pickup (sensor), are attached to the measuring pipe.
2. Device according to claim 1, wherein the operative assemblies are formed as plunger-type armature sensors with a magnetic core.
3. Device according to claim 2, wherein each operative assembly is operated either as a sensor or as an actuator.
4. Device according to claim 2, wherein each operative assembly is operated simultaneously as an actuator and as a sensor.
5. Device according to claim 1, wherein each operative assembly can be switched over between the operating mode as a sensor and as an actuator by a switch assigned to it.
6. Device according to claim 1. wherein, for the operating mode as a sensor, each operative assembly is assigned at least one preamplifier, a lowpass filter and an analog-digital converter.
7. Device according to claim 1, wherein, for the operating mode as an actuator, for activation each operative assembly is assigned at least one digital-analog converter, a voltage signal generator assembly, a lowpass filter and a power amplifier.
8. Device according to claim 1, wherein the switches, ADCs and DACs are connected to a microprocessor.
9. Method for operating a Coriolis mass flowmeter with at least one measuring pipe, wherein at least two operative assemblies are attached to the measuring pipe and each of these operative assemblies are operated as an exciter (actuator) and/or as a measuring signal pickup (sensor).
10. Method according to claim 9, wherein the operative assemblies are operated as plunger-type armature sensors.
11. Method according to claim 9, wherein each operative assembly is switched over between sensor operation and actuator operation by a switch controlled by a microprocessor.
12. Method according to claim 11, wherein, when operated as a sensor, the signal voltage is amplified, filtered and digitized in an ADC and is fed to a microprocessor.
13. Method according to claim 9, wherein, when operated as an actuator, a sinusoidal signal is generated by a DAC, band-limited with the air of a lowpass filter and after that is applied via a power amplifier to the coil of the operative assembly.
14. Method according to claim 13, wherein the armature current is measured by means of a further ADC and fed to the microprocessor.
15. Method according to claim 9, wherein the temperature of the pipe and/or housing is measured.
16. Method according to claim 9, wherein each individual operative assembly is operated simultaneously as an actuator and a sensor.
17. Method according to claim 16, wherein both the amplitude and the phase of the respective coil current are measured, and the amplitude ratio between the applied actuator voltage and the coil current is used to calculate the voltage induced by the oscillation of the measuring pipe.
18. Method according to claim 9, wherein the method is controlled by a microprocessor.
