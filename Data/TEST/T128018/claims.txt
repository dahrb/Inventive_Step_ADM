WHAT IS CLAIMED IS:

1. A method for managing data in an electronic device, the method
comprising:

Detecting (1403) a request for tagging a data record;

selecting (1405) a portion of the data record in response to the request;

identifying a content item based on the selected portion of the data record;
and

associating the content item with the data record.

2. The method of claim 1, further comprising identifying a subject
corresponding to the portion of the data record, wherein the content item is

identified based on the subject.

3. The method of claim 1, further comprising identifying a current
context of the electronic device in response to the request, wherein the content

item is identified based on the current context of the electronic device.

4. The method of claim 1, further comprising identify an emotion of a
person whose voice is recorded in the data record, wherein the portion of the data

record is selected based on the emotion.

5. The method of claim 1, further comprising:
outputting the content item; and

outputting the data record in response to a selection of the content item.

6. An electronic device (1701) comprising:
an input unit (1750) for receiving a request for tagging a data record; and
a processor configured to:

detect the request for tagging the data record;

select a portion of the data record in response to the request;
