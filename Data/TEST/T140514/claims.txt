CLAIMS

A portable computing device (12) antenna identification system (10), comprising:

an antenna (26) configured to be disposed in the portable computing device (12),
the antenna (26) having a first sensor connector (70) uniquely configured to trans-

mit a binary signal indicative of an identity of the antenna (26),

wherein the first sensor connector (70) comprises a plurality of sensor connector el-

ements (70, - 70s),

wherein one of the plurality of sensor connector elements (70;) is configured as a
ground element and the remaining connector elements (702 - 704) are configured as

signal elements couplable to a voltage source,

wherein selected ones of the signal elements (702 - 704) are bridged to the ground
element (701) to transmit a unique binary signal indicative of the identity of the an-

tenna (26), and

wherein the first sensor connector (70) is couplable to a second sensor connector

(72) disposed in the portable computing device (12).

The system (10) of Claim 1, wherein the a second sensor connector (72) is disposed

in a display member (18) of a portable computing device (12).

The system (10) of Claim 1, wherein the second sensor connector (72) is disposed

in a notebook computing device (12).
