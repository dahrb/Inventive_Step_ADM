NEW MAIN REQUEST — CLAIMS FOR APPEAL - MARKUP

1. A switch-mode power supply (SMPS), comprising:
at least one power switch (316; 318) coupled to a voltage source;
a power inductor (312) coupled to the at least one power switch (316; 318);

a filter capacitor (310) coupled to a load side of the power inductor (316; 318)
that provides a regulated voltage output (Vour) of the SMPS;

at least one driver (464) coupled to the at least one power switch (316; 318);

a pulse width modulation (PWM) generator (326; 458) having at least one output
coupled to and controlling the at least one driver (464), the at least one output of the
PWM generator (326; 458) providing at least one PWM signal comprising a plurality

of pulses;

a digital processor (462) having a memory, the digital processor (462) is coupled
to and provides operating parameters to the PWM generator (326; 458) during operation
thereof;

characterized by

a voltage comparison circuit (322) for comparing the regulated output voltage
(Vout) to a reference voltage (Vrer), wherein the voltage comparison circuit (322) has
an error signal output representative of a difference between the regulated output voltage

(Vout) and the reference voltage (Vrzr);

a comparator (324) having a first input coupled to the error signal output from
the voltage comparison circuit (322), a second input coupled to a ramp signal (Isensz;

Vramp), and an output coupled to an input of the PWM generator (326; 458);

a PWM averaging filter (328; 468), wherein the PWM averaging filter (328;
468) receives the PWM signal and produces an analog signal representative of a relative

efficiency thereof; and

an analog-to-digital converter (ADC) (470) having an analog input coupled to
the PWM averaging filter (328; 468) for receiving the analog signal representative of
the relative efficiency of the PWM signal, and a digital output coupled to the digital

processor (462) having the memory;

wherein the digital processor (462) is designated for storing in the memory
digital efficiency values from the digital output of the analog-to-digital converter (ADC)
(470) and wherein the digital processor (462) optimizes operation of the SMPS by
adjusting operating parameters of the PWM generator (326; 458) by using the stored

2. The SMPS according to claim 1, wherein the highest relative efficiency is when

the analog signal representative of the relative efficiency is at a lowest value.

3. The SMPS according to claim 1, wherein the ramp signal represents current values

of the power inductor (312).

4. The SMPS according to claim 1, wherein the ramp signal represents a generated

voltage ramp.

5. The SMPS according to one of the preceding claims, wherein the digital processor
(362) stores in the memory digital efficiency values from the digital output of the ADC (470),
wherein the digital processor uses the stored digital efficiency values to adjust the operating
parameters of the PWM generator to provide the PWM signal resulting in optimum relative

efficiency of the SMPS.

6. The SMPS according to one of the preceding claims, wherein the operating
parameters of the PWM generator (326; 458) are selected from any one or more of the group
consisting of on and off percents of the at least one power switch, drive current from the at least

one driver to the at least one power switch, and PWM pulse rate per second (frequency).

7. The SMPS according to one of the preceding claims, wherein the at least one power
switch (316; 318) is at least one power transistor, in particular at least one power metal oxide

semiconductor (MOS) field effect transistor (FET).

8. The SMPS according to one of the preceding claims, wherein the PWM averaging
filter (468) is coupled to the at least one driver (464) for receiving the PWM signal, wherein the
PWM averaging filter (468) is an analog low-pass filter, in particular a resistor-capacitor low-

pass filter.

11. A method for optimizing efficiency of a switch-mode power supply (SMPS)

according to one of the preceding claims, said method comprising the steps of:

driving the at least one power switch (316; 318) of the SMPS with the PWM

signal;

determining an average of on pulses of the PWM signal from the PWM
generator (326; 458) with the PWM averaging filter (328; 468) by receiving the PWM

signal and producing an analog signal representative of a relative efficiency thereof;

receiving by the analog-to-digital converter (ADC) (470) the analog signal

representative of the relative efficiency of the PWM signal;

storing by the digital processor in the memory digital efficiency values from the

digital output of the analog-to-digital converter (ADC) (470); and

adjusting by said processor (426) operating parameters of the PWM generator
(326; 458) by using the stored digital efficiency values to minimize the average of the
on pulses of the PWM signal and thereby increase efficiency of the SMPS.

12. The method according to claim 11, wherein the operating parameters of the PWM
generator (326; 458) are selected from any one or more of the group consisting of percent on
of the pulses of the PWM signal, drive current to a power switch, and PWM pulse rate per

second (frequency).

wherein the step of driving comprises driving a first power switch (316) of the
SMPS with the first PWM signal, and driving a second power switch of the SMPS with
