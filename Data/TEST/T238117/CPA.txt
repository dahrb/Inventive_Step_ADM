Title: Agents containing bioquinone and purine or purine derivatives

Abstract: The invention relates to cosmetic agents, in particular hair treatment agents, containing at least one purine and/or purine derivative and at least one bioquinone. Treating the hair with said agents activates hair growth, strengthens the internal hair structure and increases ATP synthesis.
