MAIN REQUEST

1. The use of albumin and sodium chloride for rendering a thrombin formulation capable of
being doubly nanofiltered through filters of a nominal pore size of up to 35 nm with good
productivity of up to 15 million IU of thrombin/m? of nanofiltration area without observing any

significant loss of activity, which means with a thrombin recovery of more than 90%,

wherein human albumin and sodium chloride are mixed with a thrombin formulation so
that they are present in an amount of between 0.05 and 1% w/v of albumin and at least 0.05

molar of sodium chloride in the thrombin formulation.

2. The use of claim 1, wherein the nominal pore size of the filters for double nanofiltration is

between 15 nm and 35 nm.

3. The use of claim 1 or 2, wherein the nanofiltration capacity per filter is between 5 and 30
Vm?.
4. The use of any of claims 1 to 3, wherein the nanofiltered formulation has a nominal

strength of at least 500 IU of thrombin per ml.

5. The process for the preparation of a thrombin composition comprising purified thrombin,

human albumin and sodium chloride,

wherein, a thrombin composition is filtered by double filtration through filters of a pore
size of up to 35 nm, wherein the concentration of human albumin in the thrombin composition
prior to nanofiltration is between 0.05 and 1% w/v and the concentration of sodium chloride in

the thrombin composition prior nanofiltration is at least 0.05 molar,

wherein, the thrombin composition prior to nanofiltration has a specific activity of 1500 !U
of thrombin/mg of protein or more and a nominal strength of at least 500 IU of thrombin/ml of the

composition; and

wherein, the nanofiltered material is not subsequently treated for adjustment of the

composition, thereby avoiding any risk of cross-contamination.
