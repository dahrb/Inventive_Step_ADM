Title: Optical recording system

Abstract: PURPOSE:To make one spot in charge of two types of information and to improve an information recording density by changing the length and width of a spot area to be in charge of a recording information signal in accordance with the recording information signal. CONSTITUTION:A driving control circuit 10 inputted ternary recording signal generates a driving current and an exciting current level-controlled in accordance with an action mode command and a recording signal and supplies respective to an optical beam generator 1 and an exciting coil 4. First, at the time of writing, the circuit 10 controls the intensity of an optical beam in accordance with the level of a recording signal, modulates the width of a magnetizable invertable spot area and modulates the length of a spot area in accordance with the pulse width of a recording signal. At the time of reproduction, a weak optical beam irradiates the spot area and the reflecting light is converted to the electric signal. At the part where the width of the spot area is increased, a reflecting light quantity in accordance with the width is obtained. Consequently, the reproducing signal is level-discriminated and an original ternary recording signal is demodulated.

Type: Japan Patent

Claims: 

[Claims] (1) An optical recording method for recording information by forming a spot area that carries a recorded information signal on a recording medium, characterized in that the length and width of the spot area are changed according to the recorded information signal. Optical recording method. (2) The recording medium is a magneto-optical recording medium, and the intensity of the light beam irradiating the magneto-optical recording medium and the intensity of the magnetic field applied to the magneto-optical recording medium are simultaneously modulated by the recording information signal to spot the spot. 2. The optical recording system according to claim 1, wherein the length and width of the area are changed.
