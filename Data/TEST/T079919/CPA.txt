TITLE:
System and method of providing conversational visual prosody for talking heads

TYPE:
US Patent

ABSTRACT:
A system and method of controlling the movement of a virtual agent while the agent is listening to a human user during a conversation is disclosed. The method comprises receiving speech data from the user, performing a prosodic analysis of the speech data and controlling the virtual agent movement according to the prosodic analysis.

CLAIMS:
1. A method of providing movements by an animation during a conversation with a user, the method comprising:
determining a culture associated with the user;
receiving speech from the user;
selecting from a database a set of listening movements associated with the determined culture; and
based on the set of listening movements, controlling via a processor listening movements of the animation when the animation listens to the user such that the listening movements are consistent with the determined culture and according to an analysis of the received speech.
2. The method of claim 1, wherein controlling the listening movements of the animation further comprises controlling the listening movement of the animation while the animation is transitioning between listening to speaking using transitional movements.
3. The method of claim 1, wherein the analysis of the received speech is a prosodic analysis.
4. A device for providing movements by an animation during a conversation with a user, the device comprising:
a processor;
a first module controlling the processor to determine a culture associated with the user;
a second module controlling the processor to receive speech from the user;
a third module controlling the processor to select from a database a set of listening movements associated with the determined culture; and
a fourth module controlling the processor, based on the set of listening movements, to control movements of the animation when the animation listens to the user such that the controlled movements are consistent with the determined culture and according to an analysis of the received speech.
5. The device of claim 4, wherein the fourth module configured further controls the movement of the animation while the animation is transitioning between listening to speaking using transitional movements.
6. The device of claim 4, wherein the analysis of the received speech is a prosodic analysis.
7. A non-transitory computer-readable medium storing instructions for controlling a computing device to provide movements by an animation during a conversation with a user, the instructions comprising:
determining a culture associated with the user;
receiving speech from the user;
selecting from a database a set of listening movements associated with the determined culture; and
based on the set of listening movements, controlling via a processor movements of the animation when the animation listens to the user consistent with the determined culture and according to an analysis of the received speech.
8. The computer-readable medium of claim 7, wherein controlling the movements of the animation further comprises controlling the movement of the animation while the animation is transitioning between listening to speaking using transitional movements.
9. The computer-readable medium of claim 7, wherein the analysis of the received speech is a prosodic analysis.
