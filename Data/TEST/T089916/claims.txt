MAIN REQUEST

Claims:

1. The use of dill oil as a potentiator for a pyrethrin insecticide, wherein the dill
oil is obtained from Anethum sowa.

2. The use of dill oil for reducing or preventing the development of resistance
to a pyrethrin insecticide by the target pest, wherein the dill oil is obtained

from Anethum sowa.

3. The use according to claim 1 or claim 2 wherein the dill oil is obtained from

seeds of Anethum sowa.

4. The use according to any one of the preceding claims wherein the pyrethrin

insecticide comprises a pyrethrin.

5. The use according to any one of claims 1 to 3 wherein the pyrethrin
insecticide is a pyrethroid.

6. The use according to any one of the preceding claims wherein the dill oil
and the pyrethrin insecticide are in a composition.

7. The use according to claim 6 wherein the composition is arranged so that
administration of a single composition will result in the administration of the

dill oil in advance of the pyrethrin insecticide.

8. The use according to claim 6 or claim 7 wherein the composition further

comprises a solvent.

9. The use according to any one of claims 6 to 8 wherein the dill oil is present in

the composition in an amount of from 5 to 8% w/w.

MAIN REQUEST

A pesticidal composition which comprises a pyrethrin insecticide and dill oil,
wherein the ratio of pyrethrin insecticide: dill oil is in the range of from 1:6
w/w to 5:1 w/w, wherein the dill oil is obtained from Anethum sowa.

A pesticidal composition according to claim 10 wherein the pyrethrin

insecticide comprises a pyrethrin.

The composition according to claim 10 or claim 11 wherein the dill oil is
obtained from seeds of Anethum sowa.

A composition according to any one of claims 10 to 12 wherein the dill oil is

arranged to be released in advance of the pyrethrin insecticide.

A method for the control of pests in agriculture which comprises administering
to the pest or its environment a pesticidally effective amount of a combination
of dill oil and a pyrethrin insecticide wherein the ratio of pyrethrin insecticide :
dill oil is in the range of from 1:6 w/w to 5:1 w/w, wherein the dill oil is
obtained from Anethum sowa, wherein the pests in agriculture are pests on

plants, including trees, and/or stored products.

A pesticidal composition which comprises a pyrethrin insecticide and dill oil,
for use in human and veterinary medicine and in public health control, wherein

the dill oil is obtained from Anethum sowa.
