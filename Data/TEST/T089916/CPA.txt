TITLE:
Evaluation of dillapiole and dihydrodillapiole as synergists for pyrethrins in dust formulations

TYPE:
Academic paper

ABSTRACT:
[4,5-dimethoxy-6-(2-propenyl)-1,3-benzodioxole] from Anethum sowa and its dihydro-derivative [4,5-dimethoxy-6-propyl-1,3-benzodioxole] were tested for synergistic activity with pyrethrins in dust formulations against Tribolium castaneum (Hbst.). The ratios used of insecticide to synergist were 1:0, 1:5, 1:10 and 1:15. Both dill apiole and dihydro dill apiole gave a maximum synergistic effect (a factor of 2.69) at the 1:10 ratio. The results also revealed that dihydro dill apiole had superior synergistic activity to dill apiole.
