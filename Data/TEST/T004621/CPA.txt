TITLE:
A digital relay having an improved man-machine interface

TYPE:
European Patent

ABSTRACT:
A digital relay, for regulating the operation of a power distribution apparatus, comprising a computerised interface, which includes at least a computerised module providing HTTP (Hyper Text Transfer Protocol) services.

CLAIMS:
A digital relay, for regulating the operation of a power distribution apparatus, comprising:
a control unit for generating, based on predefined processing strategies, signals for controlling the operation of said apparatus;
a computerised interface for exchanging information with said control unit; characterised by the fact that said computerised interface comprises at least a computerised module providing HTTP (Hyper Text Transfer Protocol) services.
A digital relay according to claim 1, characterised by the fact that said computerised module is connected to a communication network, which is coupled at least to a computer.
A digital relay according to claim 2, characterised by the fact that said communication network is an ethernet network.
A digital relay according to the previous claims, characterised by the fact that said computer is connected, through said communication network, to one or more partner computers for processing and/or exchanging information.
A digital relay, according to one or more of the previous claims, characterised by the fact that said computer comprises a Web browser.
A digital relay according to the previous claims, characterised by the fact that said computerised module comprises:
a microprocessor; and
computerised storage means for storing data and/or information.
A digital relay according to claim 6 characterised by the fact that said computerised module comprises an ethernet communication interface.
A digital relay according to claim 5 characterised by the fact that computerised module comprises a TCP/IP protocol slack.
A digital relay according to the previous claims, characterised by the fact that said computerised module comprises first computerised means for representing, in a virtual format, the appearance and the functionalities of a man-machine interface, related to said digital relay.
A digital relay according to the previous claims, characterised by the fact that said computerised module comprises second computerised means for performing a remote exchange of information.
An integrated protection system, for regulating the operation of a plurality of power distribution apparatuses, characterised by the fact of comprising a plurality of digital relays according to one or more of the previous claims.
An integrated protection system, according to claim 11, characterised by the fact that the computerised modules of said plurality of digital relays, are connected through a common communication network to a common computer, comprising a Web browser.
A power distribution apparatus such as a circuit breaker and/or a disconnector and/or the like characterised by the fact of comprising a digital relay according to one or more of the claims from 1 to 10.
A power distribution network, characterised by the fact of comprising one or more digital relays according to one or more of the claims from 1 to 10.
A power distribution network, characterised by the fact of comprising one or more integrated protection systems according to one or more of the claims from 11 to 12.
