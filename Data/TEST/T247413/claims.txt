CLAIMS — Main Request

1. A peptide having an amino acid sequence comprising [E/D/N/Q]-[S/T]-
[D/E/Q/N]-[V/L] for use in the treatment, optionally wherein the treatment is
prophylactic, of the damaging effect of stroke exacerbated by fever in a subject
having a fever of at least 38 degrees Celsius on initiating treatment.

2. A peptide for use as claimed in claim 1, wherein the fever is of at least 39
degrees Celsius before or up to 24 hours after initiating treatment, for example
between initiating treatment and 6 hours afterward.

3. A peptide for use as claimed in claim 1 or 2 wherein [E/D/N/Q] is E, [D/E/Q/N]
is D and [V/L] is V.

4. A peptide for use as claimed in claims 1 or 2 which comprises an amino acid
sequence selected from the group consisting of ETDV, ESDV, ESEV, ETEV, DTDV
and DTEV.

5. A peptide for use as claimed in any of claims 1 to 4, wherein the peptide
comprises ESDV or ETDV.

6. A peptide for use as claimed in claim 5, wherein the peptide comprises
KLSSIETDV or KLSSIESDV.

7. A peptide for use as claimed in claim 6, which comprises or consists of an
amino acid sequence YGRKKRRQRRRKLSSIETDV or
YGRKKRRQRRRKLSSIESDV.

8. A peptide for use as claimed in any of claims 1 to 7, wherein the fever is due
to (i) concurrent infection, (ii) location of the stroke in a region of the brain affecting
temperature control or set point, (iii) heat exposure of the subject.

9. A peptide for use as claimed in any of claims 1 to 8, wherein the stroke is
ischemic stroke.

10. A peptide for use as claimed in any of claims 1 to 8, wherein the subject also
has hyperglycemia due to concurrent diabetes.
