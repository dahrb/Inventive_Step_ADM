NEW CLAIMS - MAIN REQUEST

1.

A computer-implemented scheduling opportunity previewer system comprising means
adapted to perform the steps of:

receiving (202) a scheduling opportunity;

retrieving (204) a context of one or more surrounding appointments to the scheduling
opportunity; and

displaying (206) the scheduling opportunity in a mini-calendar format with the context of

surrounding appointments.

The system of claim 1, further comprising means adapted to perform the steps of:
receiving (208) input from a user to view further calendar details.

The system of claim 1, further comprising means adapted to perform the steps of:
receiving (208) input from a user to accept or decline the scheduling opportunity.

The system of claim 1, wherein the mini-calendar format is shown in an e-mail preview
pane (326) or in a separate window (350), wherein the mini-calendar format can be
toggled on and off (372) and/or adjusted in size.

The system of claim 1, wherein any conflicts occurring between the scheduling
opportunity and the surrounding appointments are indicated visually (266).

The system of claim 1, wherein when multiple conflicts occur between a scheduling
opportunity and the surrounding appointments, allowing a user to navigate among the
multiple conflicts (268).

The system of claim 1, wherein the scheduling opportunity is received in an email inbox
(324).

The system of claim 1, wherein the context of surrounding appointments is shown for
multiple users (104).
