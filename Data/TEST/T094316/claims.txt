NEW CLAIMS — MAIN REQUEST

A computer implemented method, comprising:

receiving user history data including queries that were issued by users and search

results provided to the users in response to the queries; and

generating an indication of quality of a tier assignment used to store searchable digital
items in a tiered storage system, wherein higher tiers have faster access and retrieval
times when compared to lower tiers, and wherein the indication is based at least in part

upon a subset of the user history data:

wherein the tier assignment indicates to which of several tiers the searchable digital

items are assigned;
wherein the tiered storage system is a search engine index; and

wherein the method further comprises generating an improved tier assignment based at
least in part upon the indication of quality, and assigning digital items to the tiered

storage system based at least in part upon the improved tier assignment.

The computer implemented method of claim 1, wherein the user history data further

comprises:
digital items in the search results selected by one or more users; and/or
a sequence of digital items accessed by a user after the user submits the queries.

The computer implemented method of claim 1, wherein generating the improved tier
assignment is further based at least in part upon the user history data, and wherein
generating the improved tier assignment comprises optimizing or substantially optimizing

the improved tier assignment with respect to a tier assignment quality metric.

The computer implemented method of claim 3, further comprising updating a tiering
policy used to assign digital items to tiers of the tiered storage system based at least in
part upon the improved tier assignment, and using machine learning techniques to
parameterize the tiering policy.
