TITLE:
Thin display device

TYPE:
Japanese patent

ABSTRACT:
PROBLEM TO BE SOLVED: To increase the size of a thin display device without adding an exhaust fan for discharging heat.
A display panel 4, a substantially flat metal structure 3 that supports the display panel 4 from the back side, and a control circuit 11 that is attached to the back of the structure 3 and drives the display panel 4 and the like. In the thin display device including the front and rear cabinets 1 and 2 for storing and holding the respective parts from the front and rear, the heat radiating fins 12 or the heat radiating fins 21 to 24 integral with the structure 3 are formed on the back surface of the structure 3. . These radiating fins 21 to 24 are installed on all or part of the upper, lower, left and right sides of the control circuit 11. Moreover, the holes 31 and 32 are formed in the part of the rear cabinet 2 above the radiation fins 21 to 24 to expose the radiation fins 21 to 24 to the outside air side.
