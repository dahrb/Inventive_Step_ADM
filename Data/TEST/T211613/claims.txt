MAIN REQUEST

1. A method of killing plant parasitic nematodes, said method comprising the step of
applying to at least a portion of, preferably all of, a volume of soil that is infested with
nematodes an effective amount of a nematicidal composition comprising a terpene
component,
wherein the nematicidal composition comprises hollow glucan particles which
encapsulate the terpene component,
wherein the terpene component comprises thymol or citral , and
wherein the nematicidal composition further comprises a surfactant in association

with the terpene component.

2. The method according to claim 1 wherein the surfactant is selected from the group
consisting of sodium lauryl sulphate, polysorbate 20, polysorbate 80, polysorbate 40,
polysorbate 60, polyglyceryl ester, polyglyceryl monooleate, decaglycery!
monocaprylate, propylene glycol dicaprilate, triglycerol monostearate,
polyoxyethylenesorbitan monooleate, sorbitan monolaurate, sorbitan monopalmitate,
sorbitan monostearate, sorbitan monooleate, polyoxyethylene (4) lauryl ether or a

mixture of two or more thereof.
3. The method according to claim 2 wherein the surfactant is sodium lauryl sulphate.

4. The method according to any preceding claim wherein the nematicidal composition

comprises citral as a teroene component.

5. The method according to any preceding claim wherein the nematicidal composition
has a pH of less than 7.

6. The method according to any preceding claim wherein the nematicidal composition
has a pH from pH 38 to less than 7.

7. The method according to any preceding claim wherein the nematicidal composition
has a pH from pH 3 to 5.

8. The method according to any preceding claim wherein the nematicidal composition
comprises the terpene component at a concentration that selectively kills root-knot
nematodes over saprophagous nematodes.
