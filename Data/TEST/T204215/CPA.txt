Title: X-ray isocentric support for four rotation axes

Abstract: The invention relates to X-ray supports of the type with isocentric exploration movements. <??>The invention is based on the fact that, in a support with three axes of rotation (1, 2 and 3) intersecting at the isocentre O, it is intended to turn the isocentre about a fourth axis (axis 4) perpendicular to the second axis (axis 2) and parallel to the third axis (axis 3). This makes it possible to move the isocentre (O) in the horizontal plane containing it, particularly in straight lines intersecting the third axis. <??>The invention is applicable to X-ray installations. <IMAGE>

Type: EPO Patent

Claims: 
1. Isocentric radiology stand comprising an X-ray source (11) and an X-ray detector (12) carried by an arch (13) and defining an image chain axis (RX) passing through an isocenter (O), said hoop (13) being movable in rotation about a first axis (Axis 1) perpendicular to the plane of the hoop (13) and passing through the isocenter as well as around a second axis (Axis 2) in the plane of the arch, or in a parallel plane, perpendicular to the first axis and also passing through the isocenter, said arch being supported by a rigid structure (17) which pivots around a third axis (Axis 3) perpendicular to the first and second axis and passing through the isocenter (O), characterized in that it comprises rotation means for rotating the isocenter around a fourth axis (axis 4) perpendicular to the second axis and parallel to the third axis. 2. Isocentric radiology stand according to claim 1, characterized in that the rigid structure (17) has the shape of an arc of a circle, one end of which is pivotally fixed to the ground and the other end of which supports the arch (13 ) and its associated means of rotation around the first and second axes as well as the means of rotation of the isocenter (O) around the fourth axis. 3. radiology stand according to claim 2, characterized in that the rigid structure (17) comprises a first horizontal part (24) which rests and moves on the ground by pivoting around the axis 3 and a second vertical part ( 25) which supports the arch (13) and its associated means of rotation around the first and second axes as well as the means of rotation of the isocentre O around the fourth axis (Axis 4). 4. Radiology stand according to any one of the preceding claims, characterized in that means are provided for effecting coordinated angular displacements around the third (Axis 3) and fourth axes (Axis 4) so as to move the isocenter ( O) along a curve determined in the horizontal plane passing through the isocentre (O). 5. radiology stand according to claim 4 characterized in that the displacement curve of the isocenter is a line intersecting the third axis which is obtained by an angular displacement around the fourth axis which is double that around the third axis. 6. Radiology stand according to any one of the preceding claims 1 to 5 characterized in that the X-ray detector (12) comprises means for rotating it around the RX axis of the image chain so as to compensate the rotation of the image during angular movements around the third and fourth axes. 7. radiology stand according to claim 6 characterized in that the means of rotation of the X-ray detector (12) about the axis RX of the image chain are of the mechanical / electrical type. 8. radiology stand according to claim 6 characterized in that the means for rotating the image around the RX axis of the image chain are of electronic type.
