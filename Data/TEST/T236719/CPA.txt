Title: Mobile information terminal

Abstract: A mobile information terminal (10) includes a first case (1) having an operation unit (11), a second case (2) having a display unit (21) and is partially overlapped on the first case (1), and a rotary connection mechanism (3) for connecting the first and the second case in such a manner that the second case (2) can be rotated relatively to the first case (1). The center of rotation is substantially identical to the center of the overlap portion (4) of the first and the second case.
