Title: Arrangement for reducing power consumption in a matrix display based on image change detection

Type: EPO Patent

Claims: 

A display arrangement comprising:
a display section (8) for presenting visual images, said display section (8) having an after image effect;
memory means (13) for storing image signals supplied to the display section (8);
comparison circuit means (14) for determining whether the image signals supplied to the display section (8) are those for a constant image or for a changing image display by comparing the image signals to be supplied to the display section (8) with the image signals read out of the memory means (13);
drive circuit means (9) for driving the display device (8); and
control circuit means (15) responsive to the comparison circuit means (14) for controlling the drive circuit means (9) so that, when the comparison circuit means (14) determines that the image signals to be supplied to the display section (8) are for a constant image display, the drive circuit means (9) drives the display section (8) in a reduced-power drive mode;
   characterized by the reduced-power drive mode being
   a spread-out drive mode by means of which the application of drive signals to the display section is spread out spatially or temporally down to the lower limit level of the drive required to maintain the image display or to such an extent as to be at a minimum display level.
A display arrangement according to claim 1,
characterized in that portions of the image to which spread out drive signals are applied are shifted every pixel, line or field.
A display arrangement according to claim 1 or 2, characterized in that the image signals supplied to the display section (8) are stored in the memory means (13) as image signals of a first field; and the comparison circuit means (14) determines whether the image signals of a second field subsequently supplied to the display section (8) are those for a constant image display or for a changing image display by comparing the image signals of the second field with the image signals of the first field stored in the memory means (13).
A display arrangement according to any one of the preceding claims, characterized in that the display section (8) comprises an LCD panel and the memory means (13) comprises a nonvolatile memory.
A display arrangement according to any one of the preceding claims, characterized in that the comparison circuit means (14) determines the image signals to be for a constant image if more than 50% of the compared image signals coincide with each other.
