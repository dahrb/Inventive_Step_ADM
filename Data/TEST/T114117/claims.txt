MAIN REQUEST

1. A system for a marketer who is selling a specific product to customers using a first
communications device having a unique Dialed Number Identification Service (DNIS)
linking number identification, comprising;

a) a second communications device utilized by a prospective customer having an
Automatic Number Identification (ANI) unique identification number;

b) acomputer operated by the marketer;

c) a transmission device to automatically transmit the ANI unique identification
number and the DNIS unique dialed number identification to the computer;

d) a tangible memory apparatus having a database containing customer
information associated with the unique identification numbers;

e) said computer operably connected to the tangible memory apparatus;

f) a plurality of scripts in the tangible memory apparatus, to be used by the
marketer in communicating with the customer, the scripts divided into a plurality of
customer value segments, being high, medium, low or risk or a combination thereof;

g) said computer locating the customers modeling score based upon the
customer’s information associated with the ANI unique identification number of the
customer’s communication device and upon the unique DNIS dialed number identification
of the marketer’s first communication device;

h) said computer selecting a script, based on the customer’s modeling score, from
the tangible memory apparatus, contemporaneously with the time the customer's
communications device provides its ANI unique identification number;

i) the displayed script used by the marketer to communicate with the customer,

prior to any communication with the customer.

2. The system of Claim 1 in which the unique ANI identification number is a
prospective customer telephone number, radio frequency identification number, television

cable identification number, television satellite identification number or Internet address.

3. The system of Claim 1 in which the prospective customer contacts a marketer
advertising a product or service for sale or in which a marketer or a marketer's

representative contacts a prospective customer about a product or service for sale.

4. The system of Claim 1 in which, based upon the results of the communication with
the prospective customer using the first script chosen, the computer chooses another
script from the tangible memory apparatus for offering additional products or services to
the prospective customer.

5. The system of Claim 1 in which the prospective customer modeling scores are

6. The system of Claim 1 in which the information in the database determines
subsequent marketing actions with the prospective customer.

7. The system of Claim 1 in which a salesperson is operating said computer.

8. The system of Claim 7 in which the time that the salesperson spends in
communication with the prospective customer depends upon the modeling score of the

9. The system of Claim 7 in. which a particular salesperson is chosen to
communicate with the prospective customer, based upon the prospective customer's

information in the database.

10. The system of Claim 7 further including the step of updating the prospective
customer activity information based upon the results of the salesperson’s interaction with

the prospective customer.

11. The system of Claim 10 in which the prospective customer's modeling score is

updated.

12. The system of Claim 6 in which the marketing actions determined by the database

script is given to the salesman, which subsequent offers are made, which package inserts
are sent, which delivery container is used, which method of delivery is used, and which
advertising is sent to the prospective customer.
