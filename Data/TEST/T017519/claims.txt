Main Request

Claims:
1. A fluid composition comprising:

a) a hyaluronan polymer, wherein the hyaluronan polymer is present at a
concentration of 11.5 mg/mL to 15.5 mg/mL, and wherein the hyaluronan
polymer is uncrosslinked and constitutes at least 95% by weight of the total
hyaluronan polymer present in the compasition; and

b) a mannitol; wherein the mannitol is present at a concentration of 2.0%

(w/v) to 5% (w/v) of the composition.
2. The fluid campositian of claim 1, wherein the hyalurenan polymer is entirely free of a

crosslinked hyaluronan polymer.

3. The fluid composition of claim 2, wherein the hyaluronan polymer is present at a

concentration of 13.5 mg/mL.

4. The fluid composition of claim 1, wherein the hyaluronan polymer has a mean

molecular weight greater than 2,000,000 Da and less than 5,000,000 Da.

5. The fluid composition of claim 1, wherein the hyaluronan polymer comprises both

high molecular weight hyaluronan and low molecular weight hyaluronan.

6. The fluid composition of claim 5, wherein the high molecular weight hyaluronan has a

molecular weight greater than 2,000,000 Da and wherein the low molecular weight

hyaluronan has a molecular weight of less than 1,000,000 Da.
