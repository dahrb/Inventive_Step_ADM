Claims (Main Request)
1. Acomposition comprising alpha-ketoglutaric acid or a pharmaceutically acceptable salt
thereof (AKG), wherein the composition further comprises:
200-20000 USP units of lipase per mmol AKG;
500-50000 USP units of protease per mmol AKG; and
200-20000 USP units of amylase per mmol AKG.

2. The composition according to claim 1, wherein the lipase is a mammalian lipase, a

microbial lipase or a bacterial lipase.

3. The composition according to claim 2, wherein the lipase is a pancrelipase lipase, a
liprotamase lipase, a Pseudomonas lipase, a human or other mammalian bile-salt
stimulated lipase (BSSL), a human or other mammalian bile-salt dependent lipase
(BSDL), a Rhizopus oryzae lipase, a Chromobacterium viscosum lipase, a Rhizopus
delemar lipase, a Burkholderia lipase, a Burkholderia cepacia lipase or a Burkholderia

cepacia lipase as cross-linked lipase crystals.

4. The composition according to any of the preceding claims, wherein the protease is a

mammalian protease, a microbial protease or a fungal protease.

5. The composition according to claim 4, wherein the protease is a pancrelipase protease, a

liprotamase protease, an Aspergillus protease or an Aspergillus melleus protease.

6. The composition according to any of the preceding claims, wherein the amylase is a

mammalian amylase, a microbial amylase or a fungal amylase.

7. The composition according to claim 6, wherein the amylase is a pancrelipase amylase, a

liprotamase amylase, an Aspergillus amylase or Aspergillus oryzae amylase.

8. The composition according to any of the preceding claims, comprising 400-10000 USP

units of amylase per mmol AKG.

9. The composition according to claim 1, comprising 200-20000 USP units of Burkholderia
cepacia lipase per mmol AKG as cross-linked lipase crystals, 500-50000 USP units of
Aspergillus melleus protease per mmol AKG and 200-20000 USP units of Aspergillus

oryzae amylase per mmol AKG.
