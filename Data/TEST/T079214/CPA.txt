Corrosion-inhibiting compositions and use thereof

ABSTRACT:
A composition of matter of formula TG13-R (I) and a composition of matter of formula TG14-R (II) where R is H or C1-C6 alkyl, is described and claimed. These compositions of matter have been found to be useful in a Coating Mixture that is applied to aluminium or aluminium alloys.
