EP 05 734 009.3

CLAIMS
(MAIN REQUEST)

1. A system characterised by being configured to gather data concerning usage of a publication (20; 40;
62; 100; 120; 140; 160; 300; 400) by an individual, exposure of the individual to a product within a
commercial establishment and exposure of the individual to media data having an acoustic component,
the system comprising a portable monitor (24; 200; 500; 1204) capable of being carried on the person of
the individual, the portable monitor comprising an acoustic publication usage monitor, an acoustic

product exposure monitor and an acoustic media data monitor.

2. Amethod of gathering data concerning usage of a publication (20; 40; 62; 100; 120; 140; 160; 300;
400) by an individual, exposure of the individual to a product within a commercial establishment and
exposure of the individual to media data having an acoustic component, comprising:

gathering data indicating exposure of the individual to the product within the commercial
establishment with the use of an acoustic product exposure monitor of a portable monitor (24; 200;
500; 1204) carried on the person of the individual,

gathering data indicating usage of the publication by the individual with the use of an acoustic
publication usage monitor of the portable monitor, and

gathering data indicating exposure of the individual to media data with the use of an acoustic

media data monitor of the portable monitor.
