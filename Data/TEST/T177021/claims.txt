Structural component (1), in particular for an aircraft, spacecraft or rocket
(10), comprising:

wherein the first carbon nano tube mat (3) and the metallic layer (4) are

wherein the hybrid lightning strike protection layer (5) comprises a second
carbon nano tube mat (6), wherein the metallic layer (4) is arranged be-
tween the first carbon nano tube mat (3) and the second carbon nano tube

mat (6).

Structural component (1) according to claim 3,

reinforced polymer and/or wherein a dielectric coating or paint (7) is applied

on the second carbon nano tube mat (6).
