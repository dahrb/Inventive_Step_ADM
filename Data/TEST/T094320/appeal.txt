The appeal of the applicant is against the decision of the examining division to refuse European patent application No. 17 158 196.0 under Article 97(2) EPC. II. The examining division reached the conclusion that none of the requests pending before it fulfilled the requirement of Article 83 EPC and that the subject-matter of claim 1 according to the main request was rendered obvious by a combination of the following two documents D1 and D4 with the common general knowledge of the person skilled in the art: D1 : CN 104821715 A together with its family member US 2016/315535 A1 as translation into English D4 : Ban Wong et al.: "Nano-CMOS Circuit and Physical Design", 1 January 2005, ISBN: 978-0-471-46610-9, Retrieved from the Internet on 23. December 2014 under the following URL: https://inst.eecs.berkeley.edu/~ee105/fa14/lectures/Lecture24-Digital%20Circuits-CMOS%20Inverters.pdf. III. The appellant initially requested that the contested decision be set aside and that a patent be granted based on the claims of their main request or based on the claims of one of their first and second auxiliary requests, all filed together with the statement setting out the grounds of appeal. The claims of the main request and of the first and second auxiliary requests correspond to those on which the contested decision is based. IV. In a communication under Article 15(1) RPBA the board informed the appellant of its preliminary opinion that it was not convinced by the grounds for refusal under Article 83 and 56 EPC as set out in the contested decision, but that it nevertheless considered none of the pending requests to fulfil the requirement of Article 83 EPC for other reasons and that it had concerns under Article 84 EPC regarding the clarity of claim 1 of each pending request. V. With a reply dated 10 February 2023 the appellant filed a third auxiliary request. During the oral proceedings before the board on 30 March 2023 the appellant filed several versions of claims according to a fourth auxiliary request. With respect to version 4 of the fourth auxiliary request the board during the oral proceedings arrived at the conclusion that this request met the requirements of the EPC. The appellant withdrew all other pending requests, so that their final request was that the decision under appeal be set aside and a patent be granted on the basis of the fourth auxiliary request, version 4 filed during the oral proceedings before the board. VI.
