TITLE:
Buck-boost switching circuit and control method thereof

TYPE:
Chinese Patent

ABSTRACT:
The invention provides a buck-boost switching circuit, which adopts a fixed-duration on circuit or a fixed-duration off circuit to control a switch in the buck-boost switching circuit. The buck-boost switching circuit: in the voltage reduction mode, a fixed-duration on circuit or a fixed-duration off circuit is adopted to control a voltage reduction switch pair; in the boosting mode, a fixed-duration on circuit or a fixed-duration off circuit is adopted to control a boosting switch pair; in the voltage boosting and reducing mode, a fixed-duration on circuit or a fixed-duration off circuit is adopted to simultaneously control the voltage reducing switch pair and the voltage boosting switch pair; therefore, the energy transmission from the input end to the output end of the buck-boost switching circuit is adjusted. The buck-boost switching circuit provided by the invention does not need a loop compensation circuit, and is simple in circuit structure. Meanwhile, the buck-boost switching circuit provided by the invention is convenient to switch each working mode, can realize seamless butt joint of each working mode, and has better transient characteristics.
