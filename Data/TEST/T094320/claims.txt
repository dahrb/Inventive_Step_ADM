CLAIMS:

1. A DC-DC converter (540) comprising:

a first switch (M1);

a second switch (M2) connected to the first switch (M1);

a mode selecting circuit (MSC) configured to select a converting mode from one
of at least a first mode and a second mode based on an input voltage (VIN); and

a controller (PC) configured to generate a first switching control signal (CONS1)
for controlling the first switch (M1) based on the converting mode, and a second
switching control signal (CONS2) for controlling the second switch (M2) based on the
converting mode, wherein the mode selecting circuit (MSC) is configured to select the
converting mode from one of at least the first mode, the second mode, and a third mode,
wherein, in the third mode, the first switch (M1) is configured to be repeatedly turned on
and off in response to the first switching control signal (CONS1), and the second switch
(M2) is configured to maintain a turned on state in response to the second switching
control signal (CONS2), and wherein in the third mode, the second switching control

signal is the input voltage (VIN).

2. A DC-DC converter (540) according to claim 1, wherein, in the first mode,
the first switch (M1) is configured to be repeatedly turned on and off in response to the
first switching control signal (CONS1), and the second switch (M2) is configured to be

repeatedly turned on and off in response to the second switching control signal (M2).
