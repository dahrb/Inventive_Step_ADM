Main Request

Claims

1. A computer-implemented method, comprising:
at a portable electronic device with a touch screen display,
displaying at least a portion of a web page on the touch screen display,
wherein the web page comprises a plurality of boxes of content (6006);
detecting a first gesture at a location on the displayed portion of the web page (6010);
determining a first box in the plurality of boxes at the location of the gesture (6012);
enlarging and translating the first box so as to substantially center the first box on the
touch screen display (6020);
while the first box is enlarged, detecting a second gesture on a second enlarged box
other than the first box (6040); and,
in response to detecting the second gesture, substantially centering the second enlarged

box on the touch screen display (6042).

2. The method of claim 1, including:
prior to displaying at least a portion of a web page, determining borders, margins,
and/or paddings for the plurality of boxes that are specified in the web page (6002); and
adjusting the borders, margins, and/or paddings for the plurality of boxes for display on

the touch screen display (6004).

3. The method of claim | or 2, wherein:
the web page has a document width and a document length;
the touch screen display has a display width; and
displaying at least a portion of the web page comprises scaling the document width to

fit within the display width independent of the document length (6008).

4. The method of claim 3 wherein:
the touch screen display is rectangular with a short axis and a long axis;
the display width corresponds to the short axis when the web page is seen in portrait

view; and
