CLAIMS (MAIN REQUEST)

1, Input device (1) for an electronic apparatus, comprising:

- a printed circuit board (200) with a top side (T), a bottom side (B), and an opening (201);

- a rotary wheel module (100) that has a rotary wheel (20);

- a signal generator (210) for generating electrical signals corresponding to an operation of the rotary
wheel, said signal generator being connected to the printed circuit board (200),

characterized in that the rotary wheel module (100) is mounted in the opening (201) with its rotation axis
perpendicular to said top and bottom sides (T, B) of said printed circuit board (200) and that the rotary module
(20) is located above the top side of the printed circuit board (200).

2, The input device (1) according to claim 1,
characterized in that the rotary wheel module (100) comprises an undercarriage (30) to which the rotary wheel
(20) is rotatably mounted.

3. The input device (1) according to claim 2,
characterized in that the undercarriage (30) comprises at least one adjustment pin (32) for placement in a
corresponding hole of the printed circuit board (200). |

4, The input device (1) according to claim 2,
characterized in that the undercarriage (30) is attached to the bottom side (B) of the printed circuit board (200).

5. The input device (1) according to claim |,
characterized in that the signal generator comprises a lever switch (210) with a lever (211) that can be actuated
by teeth (21) of the rotary wheel (20).

6. The input device (1) according to claim 5,
characterized in that the lever switch (210) ts attached to the bottom side (B) of the printed circuit board (200).

7. The input device (1) according to claim 1,
characterized in that it comprises a button (10) that is mounted on an undercarriage (30).

8. The input device (1) according to clair 7,
characterized in that the button (10) is non-rotatably mounted in a central opening of the rotary wheel (20).

9. The input device (1) according to claim 1,
characterized in that the rotary wheel module (100) comprises a circuit layer (40, 50) with at least one switch
(41) that can be operated by the rotary wheel (20) and/or by a button (10).

10. The input device (1) according to claim 9,
characterized in that the circuit layer comprises at least one metal dome (41).

ll. The input device (1) according to claim 9,
characterized in that the circuit layer comprises a flexible circuit board (50).
