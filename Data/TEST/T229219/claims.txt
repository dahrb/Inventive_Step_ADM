MAIN REQUEST — CLAIMS

A plastic container containing a pharmaceutical composition comprising sodium

benzoate and meloxicam or a pharmaceutical acceptable salt thereof, characterised

in that

(i) | the container material is selected from one or more members of the group
consisting of a homopolymer of polypropylene (PP), a copolymer of
polypropylene (PP), a homopolymer of polyethylene terephthalate (PET) and a
copolymer of polyethylene terephthalate (PET), and optionally one or more
non-polymeric components; and

(ii) | the plastic container has a volume of 3 ml to 11 ml; preferably 3 ml to 10 ml,
more preferably 3 ml to 8 ml, more preferably 3 ml to 5 ml, more preferably 3.5
ml to 4.5 ml, more preferably 3 ml to 4 ml; and

(iii) | the pharmaceutical composition has a volume of 2 ml to 10 ml, preferably 2.5
ml to 8 ml, more preferably 2.5 ml to 5 ml, more preferably 3.5 ml to 4.5 ml,
more preferably 3 ml to 4.5 ml.

The plastic container according to claim 1 comprising sodium benzoate in the
concentration range of 0.8 mg/ml to 2.0 mg/ml, preferably in a concentration of 1.5

mg/ml.

The plastic container according to any one of claims 1 to 2 comprising meloxicam in
a concentration of 0.2 mg/ml to 20 mg/ml, preferably 0.5 mg/ml to 15 mg/ml, more
preferably 0.5 mg/ml, 1.5 mg/ml or 15 mg/ml.

The plastic container according to any one of claims 1 to 3, wherein the

pharmaceutical composition is a suspension.

The plastic container according to claim 1, characterised in that the plastic container
has a volume of 8 ml and the pharmaceutical composition has a volume of 3.5 ml to
4 ml and in that it comprises meloxicam with a concentration of 0.5 mg/ml and

sodium benzoate with a concentration of 1.5 mg/ml.
