Claims:

1. A method of protecting a payment instrument in non-face-to-face transactions, the
payment instrument being issued by an issuing entity (20) and associated with an
authorized instrument holder (1), the authorized instrument holder being subject to
authentication by a trusted third party (15) with whom the payment instrument holder has
previously registered by submitting personal information, the method comprising:

the authorized instrument holder (1) communicating with the issuing entity (20) to
block, on a default basis, authorization of the payment instrument for non-face-to-face
transactions unless authorized to unblock the payment instrument by the trusted third party
(15);

prior to a non-face-to-face transaction (S5), the authorized instrument holder (1)
communicating (S2) with the trusted third party (15) to subject him or herself to
authentication based on the personal information and to request that the payment instrument
be unblocked for the non-face-to-face transaction (S5); and

the trusted third party (15) authenticating the authorized instrument holder (1), and
if the authentication result is positive, communicating (S4) with the issuing entity (20) to

request unblocking of the payment instrument for the non-face-to-face transaction (S5).

2. A method according to claim 1, wherein said communicating step is made by the
authorized instrument holder (1) registering with the trusted third party and the trusted third
party (15) sending a block request to the issuing entity.

3. A method according to claim 1, wherein the non-face-to-face transaction is a
transaction with a merchant (10) and further comprising the step of the issuing entity (20)
forwarding a payment authorization (S8) to the merchant in response to receipt of a request

for authorization from the merchant and the unblocking request from the trusted third party

(15).

4. A system for strongly authenticating that a customer (1) attempting to effect a
payment transaction with a merchant (10) using a payment instrument is an authorized user
of the payment instrument, the payment instrument being issued by an issuing entity (20)

and associated with the customer (1), wherein the customer has previously blocked use of
