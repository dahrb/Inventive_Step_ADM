Main Request
Claims
1. An eyeglass lens in which an anti-reflection film (5) and an oil-repellent

film (2) are disposed, and wherein an anti-slip film (6) is further disposed on
the lens surface in that order from the side of the lens surface, and
characterized in that the anti-slip film (6) comprises a mixture of minute
particles of a first group of a metal oxide, silicon oxide or antimony oxide or
minute particles of a second group of a fluoride, or minute particles of both
groups, and a resin consisting of an organic compound, with this mixture being
disposed on the surface of the oil-repellent film (2), as a single mixed layer

comprising particles and resin.

2. The eyeglass lens according to Claim 1, wherein the mean particle size
of the minute particles of a metal oxide, silicon oxide or antimony oxide and

minute particles of a fluoride is 100 nm or less.

3. The eyeglass lens according to Claim 1, wherein the metal oxide is a
metal oxide of Al, Sn, Ta, Ce, La, Fe, Zn, W, Zr, In, Ti or Nb, or a complex

metal oxide constructed from two or more of these metal oxides.
