New claims

1. A system of distributing electronic content comprising:

a network operations center (214) forming a composite broadcast signal
having digital electronic content therein;

a content delivery system (210) receiving said composite broadcast signal from
said network operations center and broadcasting said composite broadcast signal;

a base station (220) receiving said composite broadcast signal and forming a
wireless local area network (228), said base station (220) rebroadcasting the digital
electronic content at least a portion of said composite signal as a rebroadcast signal
using said wireless local network (228); and

a plurality of user appliances (232) positioned with said local area network,
said plurality of user appliances has conditional access software therein, said condi-
tional access software allowing the user appliances access to said rebroadcast signal;
and

said wireless local area network (228) formed between said base station (220)
and said plurality of user appliances (232) communicating the rebroadcast signal to
the user appliances (232) from the base station (220), characterized in that said
plurality of user appliances has conditional access software therein, said conditional

access software allowing the user appliances access to said rebroadcast signal.

2. A system as recited in claim 1 further comprising a television coupled
to said base station, said television receiving at least a portion of said composite

broadcast signal.

3. A system as recited in claim 2 wherein said base station couples said

primary channel to said television.
