NEW MAIN REQUEST
CLAIMS

1. A sterilization wrap for wrapping an article (36) to be sterilized, comprising:

at least a first panel of sterilization material (32,48) comprising fibrous or non-
fibrous material that is capable of inhibiting pathogens from passing therethrough; said
first panel having an outer periphery and a central portion; and

an additional panel (34,52) of material being bonded to the central portion of
said first panel; the additional panel being the inside panel and made of an absorbent
material; the perimeter of said additional panel being smaller than the perimeter of said
first panel; wherein said additional panel of absorbent material is adapted to receive the
article (36) to be sterilized thereon; said additional panel of absorbent material (a)
providing sterility protection for the article in addition to said first panel of sterilization
material, and (b) wicking moisture away from the article after sterilization has taken
place;

wherein the first panel and the additional panel permit sterilant to pass
therethrough, but inhibit pathogens from passing therethrough; said sterilization wrap
further including a second panel of sterilization material (46); said second panel of

sterilization material bonded to said first panel (48) of sterilization material.

2. A sterilization wrap as set forth in Claim 1, wherein said panel (34,52) of

absorbent material is made of an absorbent synthetic.

3. A sterilization wrap as set forth in Claim 2, wherein said absorbent synthetic is
taken from the group consisting of hydrophillic spunmelt polyolefins, polyester, and

nylon, and polyrayons and bicomponent fibers.

4. A sterilization wrap as set forth in Claim 1, wherein the perimeter of said panel
(34,52) of absorbent material is at least 25% less than the perimeter of said first panel

of sterilization material.

5. A sterilization wrap as set forth in Claim 1, wherein said first and second panels
of sterilization material (46,48) are made of a three-ply laminate consisting of a layer of
melt blown polypropylene sandwiched between two layers of spun bond polypropylene
(SMS).

6. A sterilization wrap as set forth in Claim 1, wherein said panel of absorbent

material (34,52) is bonded to said first panel (32,48) of sterilization material by glue.

7. A sterilization wrap as set forth in Claim 1, wherein said panel of absorbent
material (34,52) is bonded to said first panel (32,48) of sterilization material by heat and

pressure.

8. A sterilization wrap as set forth in Claim 1, wherein the color of said first
panel of sterilization material (32,48) is different from the color of said panel (34,52) of

absorbent material so that a sterile field may be readily identified.

9. A sterilization wrap as set forth in Claim 1, further including a chemical
visual indicator (42,56), said chemical visual indicator being in one visual state prior to
exposure to sterilant and being in another visual state after exposure to sterilant; said
chemical visual indicator being attached to said wrap so as to indicate whether or not

the article (36) has been exposed to adequate sterilization conditions.

10. A sterilization wrap as set forth in Claim 9, wherein said chemical visual

indicator (42, 56) meets the requirements of ISO 11140-1 indicator.

11. A sterilization wrap as set forth in Claim 9, wherein said chemical visual

indicator (42,56) is attached to said absorbent material.
