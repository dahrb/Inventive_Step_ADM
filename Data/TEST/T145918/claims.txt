Claims

1. A mass spectrometry apparatus, comprising:

an ion source (12) arranged in a substantially horizontal orientation and from which a
quantity of ions may be sourced, wherein the ions from the ion source (12) are
extracted and arranged to flow along a first intended path of travel (16A);

an ion filter device (20) arranged for receiving a stream of ions for filtering thereof;
and

an ion guide (26) arranged so as to guide ions sourced from the ion source (12)
toward the ion filter device (20), wherein the ions receivable by the ion filter device
(20) are arranged to flow along a second intended path of travel (16B) so as to be
received by an ion analysis device (44) for spectrometry analysis;

wherein the distance traveled by ions along the first intended path of travel (16A) is
substantially smaller than the distance traveled by ions along the second intended
path of travel (16B)

and wherein the flow of ions moving along the second intended path of travel is
directed with the action of gravity or against the action of gravity,

so that the profile of the apparatus is reduced so as to minimise the effective
footprint of the apparatus;

wherein the ion filter device (20) comprises a mass analyzer (36) arranged to receive
a stream of ions from the ion guide (26) for filtering purposes, and

wherein the mass analyzer (36) is a quadrupole mass analyzer having four spaced

apart but parallel metallic rods.

2. A mass spectrometry apparatus according to claim 1, wherein the first and
second intended paths of travel (16A, 16B) are generally linear, whereby the first
intended path of travel (16A) may be a path having a desired direction within a first
plane, and the second intended path of travel (16B) may be a path having a desired

direction within a second plane.

3. A mass spectrometry apparatus according to claim 2, wherein the first and

second planes are aligned so as to be substantially orthogonal to one another.

4, A mass spectrometry apparatus according to claim 2 or claim 3, wherein the
first plane is aligned so as to be substantially parallel with a horizontal plane,
and the second plane is aligned so as to be substantially parallel with a vertical

plane.

5. A mass spectrometry apparatus according to any one of claims 1 to 4,
wherein the ion guide (26) comprises an ion optics lens (25) arrangement capable of
directing the ion beam from the first intended path of travel (16A) to the second
intended path of travel (16B).

6. A mass spectrometry apparatus according to any one of claims 1 to 5,
wherein the ion guide (26) comprises curved elements arranged so as to guide or
direct the ion stream (22) between the first and second intended paths of travel (16A,
16B).

7. A mass spectrometry apparatus according to claim 6, wherein the curved
elements are shaped in a manner that is commensurate with a portion or segment of

the intended path of the ion stream (22).

8. A mass spectrometry apparatus according to any one of the preceding claims,
wherein the apparatus further comprises one or more collisional cells (30) arranged
for filtering interfering particles from the ion stream (22), thereby serving to improve
the signal strength of the ion stream (22) at the ion analysis device (44).

9. A mass spectrometry apparatus according to claim 8, wherein the or each
collisional cell (30) is placed at any desired location along the first and or second
intended paths of travel (16A, 16B) of the ion stream (22) so as to remove unwanted

particles from the ion stream (22).

wherein the ion source (12) may be provided by an inductively coupled plasma,
