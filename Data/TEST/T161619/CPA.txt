TITLE:
Authentication means

TYPE:
World International Patent Organisation

ABSTRACT:
There is described a biaxially oriented polypropylene (BOPP) film which has dispersed therein rigid rod like UV absorbers compatible with the polymer such as . (E,E)-1,4- diphenylbuta-1,3-diene (DPBD); (E,E)-1,6-diphenylhexa-1,3,5-triene (DPHT); and beta, beta-carote n e (carotene) at typical concentrations of about 0.01% w/w. These absorbers preferentially align along one of the two orientation axes of the film and allow anisotropy in the film to be covertly detected by the difference in the UV fluorescence spectra of the film in along each axis. This provides a covert means to authenticate the film, which is useful as a substrate for security applications

CLAIMS:
1 A biaxially oriented polymeric film suitable for use as a substrate to provide improved authentication, the film comprising from about 0.001 % to about 5% by weight of a colourless additive dispersed in at least one layer of the film; where the additive exhibits a detectable difference in the spectral response when illuminated with polarised IR or UV radiation for which the axis of polarisation is parallel with one of the principal orientation axes (MD or TD).
2 A film as claimed in the preceding claim, where the film is a biaxially oriented polypropylene film and optionally has a balanced oreintation.
3 A film as claimed in either preceding claim, where the additive possesses a marked anisotropy in its interaction with UV irradiation such that, when largely immobilised in the host substrate, irradiation of the substrate with UV light gives an absorption spectrum or a fluorescence intensity which is indicative of a preferred orientation of the guest additive with respect to any of the principal directions attributable to the substrate, such as the machine direction, transverse direction or normal direction.
4 A film as claimed in any preceding claim, where the additive has an elongated molecular geometry and is selected from one or more (optionally two or more) UV absorbers, UV fluorescent materials, materials capable of intermolecular non-radiative energy transfer (NRET), phosphorescent materials and/or any mixtures thereof..
5 A film as claimed in any preceding claim, where the additive is selected from: phenanthrenes, fluorenes, fluoresceins, flavins and/or aromatic hard-resins.
6 A film as claimed in any preceding claim, where the additive is selected from: (E,E)-1 ,4-diphenylbuta-1 ,3-diene (DPBD), (E,E)-1 ,6-diphenylhexa-1 ,3,5-triene (DPHT); and/or beta, beta- carotene (carotene).
7 A method of authenticating a product comprising the steps of:
(a) illuminating a film as claimed in any preceding claim with electromagnetic radiation (optionally UV radiation) with polarisation axis aligned in one of two direction along the film; and
(b) detecting a difference in the spectra of the film in each direction to authenticate the film.
8. A method as claimed in claim 6, comprising the steps of a) irradiating the film with a source of polarised (optionally UV) radiation to give a (optionally UV) absorption spectrum or UV fluorescence from the film b1 ) detecting the reflected , transmitted and/or fluoresced radiation from the film b2) authenticating the film by comparing the i) intensity of the radiation from the film with a known profile of radiation dependent on the relative orientation of the of the machine direction of the substrate and/or the polariser used for polarising the source irradiation and/or ii) distinguishing the polarisation of the irradiation from the film received by the detection system.
9 The use of UV absorbers, UV fluorescent materials, materials capable of intermolecular non-radiative energy transfer (NRET) and/or phosphorescent materials in a film as claimed in any of claims 1 to 5 and/or a method as claimed in either claim 6 or 7 for the purpose of reflecting a preferred orientation distribution of the polymer chain trajectories in the film via appropriate detection methods to determine the orientation history of the film.
