MAIN REQUEST

1. A method of identifying an authentic birefringent polymer film
comprising

measuring the birefringence of the film by measuring the variation
in the intensity of light passing through crossed polarisers when the film is
placed between the polarisers and either the film is rotated or the
polarisers are rotated whilst maintaining the same rotational configuration
with respect to one another, and

determining whether said film is authentic on the basis of the film
meeting a specified pre-defined criterion of birefringence based upon the

measurement obtained in said measuring step.

2. A method according to claim 1 wherein the film is a multi-layer film.

3. A method according to claim 2 wherein birefringence measurement

is made on a core layer of the film.

4. A method according to any one of claims 1 to 3 additionally
comprising measuring the thickness of a layer therein by white light

interferometry.

5. A method according to claim 4 wherein the measurement of

thickness by white light interferometry is made on a core layer of the film.
6. A method as claimed in claim 4 or claim 5 wherein the
measurement by white light interferometry is carried out using a pulsed or

detuned light source, or an LED light source.

f. A method as claimed in any preceding claim wherein measurement

is carried out on more than one layer.
