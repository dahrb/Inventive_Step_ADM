TITLE:
Method and apparatus for controlling access to mobile devices

TYPE:
US Patent

ABSTRACT:
According to one embodiment, the invention discloses a method comprising determining a position of a mobile device; and controlling access to said mobile device based on said position. According to another embodiment of the invention there is provided a mobile device comprising a positioning mechanism to determine a position a mobile device; and a controlling mechanism to control access to said mobile device based on said position.

CLAIMS:
1. A method comprising:
determining a position of a mobile device; and
controlling access to said mobile device based on said position.
2. The method of claim 1, wherein said position is a global position defined relative to a global coordinate system.
3. The method of claim 1, wherein said position is a local position defined relative to a local coordinate system
4. The method of claim 1, wherein controlling access to said mobile device comprises selecting a security zone associated with said position; and
applying an access protocol defined for said security zone to said mobile device, said access protocol configures access control parameters for said mobile device.
5. The method of claim 4, further comprising first defining a plurality of security zones for particular geographic areas and defining an access protocol for each security zone.
6. The method of claim 1, wherein said position is determined by transmitting a triangulation signal from said mobile device, which signal is triangulated by a network operator; and receiving a result of said triangulation.
7. The method of claim 1, wherein determining said position is achieved using a position sensor which forms part of said mobile device.
8. The method of claim 4, wherein said security zone is selected from a group comprising a work zone, a home zone, a hotel zone and an airport zone.
