CLAIMS

1. Assisted manual mail sorting system for assisting an operator in manual
sortation of mailpieces (30) in a sorting station (10) including a plurality of
destination bins (100 - 109), the system comprising a headset wearable by the
operator and connected to a computer (18) comprising an image analysis
software for extracting from an image of one of the mailpieces, a name, an
address or other relevant parameter required by a sorting application
determining a corresponding relevant destination bin, characterized in that the
headset comprises:

- a high resolution camera (16B) for capturing the image of the mailpiece,

- a transparent display (16A) for signalling to the operator the relevant
destination bin where the mailpiece is to be placed by highlighting the
destination bin or overlaying a visual artefact (36) on a view of the sorting
station while the operator is looking at the sorting station through the
transparent display.

2. Assisted manual mail sorting system of claim 1, wherein the headset and the
computer are integrated in a single wearable unit (24) and wirelessly connected
to a remote server (26).

3. Assisted manual! mail sorting system of claim 1 or claim 2, further comprising a
scale (22) connected with the computer or with the remote server.

4. Assisted manual mail sorting system of claims 1 to 3, further comprising
acquiring a spatial position of the relevant destination bin by analysing an image
of the sorting station captured by the high resolution camera (16B) with respect
to markers (110 — 118) of the sorting station.

5. Assisted manual mail sorting system of claims 1 to 4, wherein the headset
further comprises 3D sensors (16D) for acquiring a spatial position of the
destination bin by positioning a determined point of the destination bin within a
3D image of the sorting station.

6. Assisted manual mail sorting system of claims 1 to 5 wherein each of the
destination bins bear a name or a tag identifier (100A — 108A).

7. Assisted manual mail sorting system of claim 6 wherein the determination of
the relevant destination bin is based on reading its name or tag identifier with
the high resolution camera (16B).

8. Assisted manual mail sorting system of claims 1 to 7, wherein the transparent
display comprises a target (34) to be positioned on a graphic element of the
mailpiece or on the names or tag identifiers of the destination bins before
capturing an image on the mailpiece or reading the name or tag with the high
resolution camera.

9 Assisted manual mail sorting system of claim 8 wherein the target is displayed
when the graphic element is not located within a predetermined duration.

10. Assisted manual mail sorting method for assisting an operator in manual
sortation of mailpieces (30) in a sorting station (10) including a plurality of
destination bins (100 - 109), the method comprising:

- capturing with a high-resolution camera (16A) an image of the mailpiece,

- extracting from the captured image, with an image analysis software of a
computer (18), a name, an address or other relevant parameter required by a
sorting application determining a corresponding relevant destination bin, and

- signalling to the operator with a transparent display (16A) the relevant
destination bin where the mailpiece is to be placed by highlighting the
destination bin or overlaying a visual artefact (36) on a view of the sorting
station while the operator is looking at the sorting station through the
transparent display,

wherein the high-resolution camera and the transparent display are included in a
headset (16) wearable by the operator and connected to the computer.

11. Assisted manual mail sorting method of claim 10, further comprising:
- notifying an indication or confirmation to the operator that the article has been
placed in the relevant destination bin.

12. Assisted manual mail sorting method of claim 10 or claim 11, wherein the
relevant destination bin is determined according to information previously
recorded in the sorting application during a setup phase.
