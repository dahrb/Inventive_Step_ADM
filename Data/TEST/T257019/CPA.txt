Title: Sorting system using wearable input device

Abstract: Methods for sorting mail pieces and corresponding systems and computer-readable mediums. A method includes determining that a camera of a wearable device is directed at a mail piece and capturing an image of the mail piece by the camera of the wearable device. The method includes transmitting the image from the wearable device to a mail processing system and notifying a user wearing the wearable device of a sort location of the mail processing system that corresponds to the mail piece.

Type: US Patent

Claims: 

What is claimed is:
1. A method for sorting mail pieces, comprising:
determining that a camera of a wearable device is directed at a mail piece;
capturing an image of the mail piece by the camera of the wearable device;
transmitting the image from the wearable device to a mail processing system; and
notifying a user wearing the wearable device of a sort location of the mail processing system that corresponds to the mail piece.
2. The method of claim 1, wherein the mail processing system processes the image to extract mail piece information and determines the sort location from the mail piece information.
3. The method of claim 1, wherein the mail processing system notifies the user of the sort location by activating a lamp on a case proximate to the sort location.
4. The method of claim 1, wherein the mail processing system processes the image to extract mail piece information, determines the sort location from the mail piece information, and transmits information indicating the sort location to the wearable device.
5. The method of claim 1, wherein the wearable device displays coordinates indicating the sort location to the user.
6. The method of claim 1, wherein the wearable device determines that the camera is directed at a mail piece using a position sensor.
7. The method of claim 1, wherein the wearable device determines that the camera is directed at a mail piece by detecting a mail piece image using the camera.
8. The method of claim 1, wherein the wearable device determines that the camera is directed at a mail piece when the user is looking at the mail piece.
9. The method of claim 1, wherein the wearable device is implemented in an eyeglass-type apparatus.
10. The method of claim 1, wherein the mail processing system processes the image to extract mail piece information, the mail piece information including physical characteristics of the mail piece.
11. A wearable device configured to wirelessly communicate with a mail processing system, the wearable device comprising:
a processor;
an accessible memory; and
a camera, the wireless device configured to
determine that the camera is directed at a mail piece;
capture an image of the mail piece by the camera; and
transmit the image to the mail processing system, wherein a user wearing the wearable device is notified of a sort location of the mail processing system that corresponds to the mail piece.
12. The wearable device of claim 11, wherein the mail processing system processes the image to extract mail piece information and determines the sort location from the mail piece information.
13. The wearable device of claim 11, wherein the mail processing system notifies the user of the sort location by activating a lamp on a case proximate to the sort location.
14. The wearable device of claim 11, wherein the mail processing system processes the image to extract mail piece information, determines the sort location from the mail piece information, and transmits information indicating the sort location to the wearable device.
15. The wearable device of claim 11, wherein the wearable device displays coordinates indicating the sort location to the user.
16. The wearable device of claim 11, wherein the wearable device determines that the camera is directed at a mail piece using a position sensor.
17. The wearable device of claim 11, wherein the wearable device determines that the camera is directed at a mail piece by detecting a mail piece image using the camera.
18. The wearable device of claim 11, wherein the wearable device determines that the camera is directed at a mail piece when the user is looking at the mail piece.
19. The wearable device of claim 11, wherein the wearable device is implemented in an eyeglass-type apparatus.
