Kyocera Corporation

New Claims

(Main Request)

1, An input apparatus comprising:

an input unit (12) for receiving a pressure input;

a load detection unit (13) for detecting a pressure load on the input unit (12);

a vibration unit (14) for vibrating the input unit (12); and

acontrol unit (15) for driving the vibration unit (14) with a drive signal for providing a
click sensation, wherein the click sensation is provided by:

a) the control unit (15) driving the vibration unit (14) with the drive signal when
the pressure load detected by the load detection unit satisfies a predetermined standard,

b) the drive signal having a frequency between 140 Hz and 250 Hz, and

c) the drive signal being used for a period determined in a range from 1/4 period
to 5/4 period of the drive signal,

wherein the load detection unit comprises plural sensors; and

wherein the pressure load is detected by the output of the plural sensors.

2. A control method of an input apparatus comprising an input unit (12) for receiving a
pressure input, a load detection unit (13) for detecting a pressure load on the input unit (12)
and a vibration unit (14) for vibrating the input unit (12), the control method comprising the
step of:

driving the vibration unit (14) with a drive signal for providing a click sensation,
wherein the click sensation is provided by:

a) driving the vibration unit (14) with the drive signal when the pressure load
detected by the load detection unit (13) satisfies a predetermined standard

b) the drive signal having a frequency between 140 Hz and 250 Hz, and

c) the drive signal being used for a period determined in a range from 1/4 period
to 5/4 period of the drive signal,

wherein the load detection unit comprises plural sensors; and

wherein the pressure load is detected by the output of the plural sensors.
