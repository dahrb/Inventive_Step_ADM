Title: Keypad

Abstract: A thin keypad including a top surface layer, a light guide layer, a capacitive sensing layer and a piezo layer provides for touch input, pressure input and haptic feedback.

Type: 
WIPO

Claims: 
1. A mobile device comprising a keypad and a processor, said keypad comprising a top surface layer, a light guide layer below the top surface layer one or more optical sources controlled by the processor and coupled to the light guide, a capacitive sensor coupled to said processor, and a piezo element coupled to said processor, said capacitive sensor being configured to sense touch contact and location on said top surface layer, and said piezo element being configured to sense pressure applied to said top surface.
2. A mobile device according to claim 1, wherein said processor is configured to detect a keystroke via a signal from the piezo element.
3. A mobile device according to claim 2, wherein said processor is configured to activate said piezo element upon detection of a keystroke.
4. A mobile device according to claim 3, wherein said piezo element is configured for vibrating said keyboard.
5. A mobile device according to claim 1, wherein said top surface layer is monolithic
6. A mobile device according to claim 1, wherein said top surface layer comprises a plurality of discrete keys.
7. A mobile device according to claim 6, wherein said top surface layer is a keymat .
8. A mobile device according to claim 1, wherein a part of said capacitive sensor is formed a semi-conductive layer and the non-conductive top surface layer.
9. A mobile device according to claim 2 or 3, wherein said piezo element comprises a piezo sensor and a piezo actuator.
10. A mobile device according to claim 1, wherein said light guide layer is configured to emit light received from said optical source through said top surface layer in at least two patterns with different graphical layouts, and said light guide and said one or more optical sources being configured to selectively emit said light in one of said at least two patterns.
11. A non-mobile device according to claim 10, wherein said patterns represent groups of different keypad icons.
12. A mobile device according to claim 10, wherein said light guide is provided with embossed gratings or with micro-prisms for coupling out light through said top surface layer.
13. A mobile device according to claim 12, wherein the top layer includes a layer that reduces reflections of ambient light.
14. A mobile device according to claim 10, further comprising a diffusive layer in the path of the light leaving the light guide layer.
15. A mobile device according to claim 11, wherein said keypad icons are arranged in a grid with columns and rows .
16. A mobile device according, to claim 15, wherein a light source is coupled to each of said columns and to each of said rows.
17. A mobile device according to claim 1, wherein said processor is configured to use the signal from the capacitive sensor layer for detecting objects proximate to the keypad or touchpad.
18. A mobile device according to claim 17, wherein said processor is configured to light up otherwise invisible user-interface elements when an object is detected in the proximity of the keypad or touch pad.
19. A mobile device according to claim 1, wherein said processor is configured to use a signal from the capacitive sensor layer for handwriting input, drawing input or navigation input.
20. A mobile device according to claim 9, wherein said piezo actuator is configured to provide vibration feedback.
21. The mobile device according to claim 20, wherein said processor is configured to adapt the vibration feedback in accordance with the state of the mobile device.
22. A capacitive keypad construction comprising: an electrically non-conductive top surface layer that is at least locally transparent to light,
a capacitive sensor layer under the top surface layer,
a piezo layer, and
an electrically non-conducting light guide layer disposed between the capacitive sensor layer and the piezo layer.
23. A capacitive keypad according to claim 22, wherein said piezo layer includes a piezo sensor.
24. A capacitive keypad according to claim 22 or 23, wherein said piezo layer includes a piezo actuator.
25. A capacitive keypad according to claim 22, wherein said light guide is provided with embossed gratings or with micro-prisms for coupling out light through said top surface layer.
26. A capacitive keypad according to any of claims 20 to 25, further comprising an upper light guide insulator above the light guide layer and a lower light guide insulator layer under the light guide.
27. A capacitive keypad according to claim 26, wherein the indexer for a fraction of the insulator layers is substantially different from the index of refraction of the light guide.
28. A capacitive keypad according to claim 26, wherein a narrow gap is formed between the respective upper and lower light guide insulators and the light guide.
29 A capacitive keypad according to claim 26, wherein a layer of liquid gel is disposed between the upper and lower light guide insulator layer and the light guide.
30. A method for user input and feedback with a keypad or touchpad that includes a capacitive touch sensor, a pressure sensor and an actuator, said method comprising:
determining the position on the keypad or touch pad at which an object touches by means of a signal from the capacitive touch sensor,
determining the pressure at which the keypad or touchpad is touched by means of the pressure sensor, accepting user input in accordance with the detected position and pressure, and
providing feedback to the user by activating the actuator in accordance with the detected user input.
31. A method according to claim 30, wherein a moving low to medium pressure contact with the keypad or touchpad is interpreted as a navigational input.
32. A method according to claim 30 or 31, wherein a substantially nonmoving medium to high pressure contact with the keypad or touchpad is interpreted as a keystroke.
33. A method according to claims 32, wherein said actuator is activated upon detection of a keystroke for providing the user with haptic feedback concerning the acceptance of the keystroke.
34. A method according to any of claims 30 to 33, wherein said actuator is activated during detection of navigational input.
35. A method according to claim 33 or 34, wherein the type of activation of the actuator is dependent on the type of input and/or on the type of application in which the input is used and or on the other operating conditions of the device in which the keypad is used.
