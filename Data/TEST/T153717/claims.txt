Claims

1. A tubing mounted completion assembly (15) for running at an end of a
completion string (10); the assembly (15) comprising a substantially tubular body
for connection in a tubing string (10) below a production packer (16), the assembly
having a through bore (40) with a first inlet (44) and a first outlet (48) each coaxial
with the tubing string (10); a downhole electronic actuating mechanism (32), a
battery arranged to provide a remote power supply to the electronic actuating
mechanism, a downhole hydraulic pump (34) and a hydraulically operated valve
member (30); wherein the actuating mechanism (32) operates the hydraulic pump
(34) to pump fluid through at least one hydraulic control line (56) to control
movement of the valve member (30) from a first position, where the valve member
(30) is open and a through bore (40) is created between the inlet (44) and outlet
(48) of the assembly, to a second position, where the valve member (30) seals the
through bore (40), and back to the first position.

2. A tubing mounted completion assembly according to Claim 1 wherein the
valve member (30) is a ball (50) including an aperture (52) running there through
which is positioned substantially coaxially with the through bore (40) when the
valve member is in the first position.

3. A tubing mounted completion assembly according to Claim 1 or Claim 2
wherein the actuating mechanism (32) includes a pressure sensor located above
the valve member (30).

4. A tubing mounted completion assembly according to any preceding claim
wherein the actuating mechanism (32) is set to operate at fixed time periods.

5. A tubing mounted completion assembly according to any preceding claim
wherein the actuating mechanism (32) includes a processor and a memory unit for
storing measured pressure values.

6. A tubing mounted completion assembly according to any preceding claim
wherein there are two hydraulic control lines (56), a first to close the valve member
(30) and a second to open the valve member (30).

f. A method of controlling fluid flow in a completion string, the method
comprising the steps:

(a) locating a completion assembly (15) according to any one of Claims 1 to
6 at an end of a tubing string (10);

(b) running the tubing string (10) into the well bore (20) with the valve
member (30) in the first position for fluid to flow in the inlet and out of the outlet as
it fills the string;

(c) actuating the valve member (30) to move to the second position and
setting the production packer (16) to thereby provide a downhole barrier; and

(d) actuating the valve member (30) back to the first position to allow
produced fluids to flow in the inlet and out of the outlet up the string (10).

8. A method of controlling fluid flow in a completion string according to Claim 7
wherein the method includes the step of undertaking a pressure test against the
valve member (30) in the second position.

9. A method of controlling fluid flow in a completion string according to Claim 7
or Claim 8 wherein the completion assembly is in accordance with Claim 3 and the
step of actuating the valve member (30) to move to the second position comprises
the steps:

(a) monitoring hydrostatic pressure using the pressure sensor on the
assembly during run in;

(b) starting a timer when a predetermined value of hydrostatic pressure is
reached; and

(c) operating the hydraulic pump (34) to move the valve member (30) to the
second position at the end of a predetermined time period.

10. Amethod of controlling fluid flow in a completion string according to Claim 9
wherein the method includes the step of pulling the string (10) so that the
monitored hydrostatic pressure reduces to be below the predetermined value and
thereby resets the timer.

11... A method of controlling fluid flow in a completion string according to any
one of Claims 7 to 10 wherein the completion assembly is in accordance with
Claim 3 and the step of actuating the valve member (30) to move from the second
position back to the first position comprises the steps:

(a) monitoring hydrostatic pressure using the pressure sensor on the
assembly to set a reference pressure value;

(b) determining an applied pressure value using a measurement from the
pressure sensor and the reference pressure value; and

(c) operating the hydraulic pump (34) to move the valve member (30) to the
first position when the applied pressure meets a pre-determined condition.

12. A method of controlling fluid flow in a completion string according to any
one of Claims 7 to 11 wherein the completion assembly is in accordance with
Claim 3 and the method includes the additional step of detecting a pressure
change event in the wellbore using the pressure sensor.

13. A method of controlling fluid flow in a completion string according to Claim
12 wherein the method includes the step of categorising the pressure change
event as one of a group comprising: a variation in pressure due to a natural
change in the wellbore environment; and effected change due to a pressure
applied at the surface.

14. A method of controlling fluid flow in a completion string according to any
one of Claims 11 to 13 wherein the reference pressure value is the lowest
pressure value measured during a preceding time interval.

15. A method of controlling fluid flow in a completion string according to any
one of Claims 7 to 14 wherein the pre-determined condition is that the applied
pressure falls within a predetermined range for a specified time period.
