CLAIMS
1. Apparatus for didactic dentistry operations comprising at least a base structure
(11) conformed so as to define at least a supporting plane (13) raised from the
floor, the supporting plane (13) being usable as the top of a didactic table and
allowing the user to sit and insert the legs freely under the supporting plane (13),
and a dentistry simulation apparatus (12) pivoted to said supporting plane (13), so
as to be able to rotate with respect to the latter at least between a first operating
position, in which it extends laterally from the bulk of said supporting plane (13),
and a second inactive position in which it is substantially comprised in the bulk

of said supporting plane (13), characterized in that said simulation apparatus (12)

is pivoted to said supporting plane (13) in correspondence with a median zone of —

the latter, said supporting plane (13) comprising a housing seating (22) made
inside the thickness of the supporting plane (13) in correspondence with said
median zone, and able to completely accommodate said simulation apparatus
(12) in its inactive position.

2. Apparatus as in claim 1, characterized in that said housing seating (22) is
conformed so that when said simulation apparatus (12) is in said second position
it is substantially comprised inside the thickness of said supporting plane (13).

3. Apparatus as in claim 1 or 2, characterized in that said base structure (11)
comprises at least two supporting columns (15) disposed as a lateral support to
said supporting plane (13), so as to define a central compartment (16) below said
supporting plane (13).

4. Apparatus as in claim 3, characterized in that each of said supporting columns
(15) comprises at least a technical compartment (26), inside which at least some
of the dentistry equipment is disposed in'a removable condition, in order to effect
said didactic dentistry operations.

5. Apparatus as in any claim hereinbefore, characterized in that-it comprises at
least a drive member (31) mounted on said base structure (11) and kinematically
connected to said simulation apparatus (12), so as to command and guide the
rotation thereof in a desired manner, and its stopping between the first operating
position, the second inactive position and possible intermediate positions.

6. Apparatus as in any claim hereinbefore, characterized in that said supporting

plane (13) comprises a closing portion (23), able to be selectively actuated in
