Title: Dental device

Abstract: 
The invention relates to a dental device having a phantom structure (9, 20, 25, 41) which comprises a phantom head (11, 26a) mounted on a supporting component (10) corresponding to or resembling the backrest of a treatment chair and, if appropriate, a phantom body (25b), which structure is mounted on a support part (1, 44) so as to be pivotable by means of a pivoting device with horizontal axle bearing (12, 29, 47). In order to permit both laboratory work at the worktable and work on the phantom structure under conditions resembling those encountered in practice it is proposed that the phantom structure be held with the supporting component on a worktable and that the pivoting device be designed or arranged such that the phantom structure can be shifted from a non-use position in which it is arranged in a vacant space (7) underneath the tabletop (2, 22, 27, 42) into a use position in which it is arranged so as to represent an extension of its longitudinal axis (17) adjoining the front edge (18, 26) of the table. The device is intended for dental workplaces used for teaching and training purposes. <IMAGE>
