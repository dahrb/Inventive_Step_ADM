CLAIMS

1. A non-contact transmission module (Ma) comprising:

a transmission antenna module (Ma1); and

a transmitter (200a) configured to transmit a digital signal,

wherein the transmission antenna module (Ma1) includes:

a transmission antenna (100a) constituted by a metal plate, or by a conductor
formed on an insulator, the transmission antenna (100a) being configured to be
electromagnetically or magnetically coupled to a reception antenna (100b) of a non-contact
reception module (Mb); and

at least one resistor (400a) connected in series between the transmission antenna
(100a) and the transmitter (200a);

characterized in that

a distance between the transmitter (200a) and the transmission antenna (100a) is
between 2 millimeters and 200 millimeters, and

a resistance value of the at least one resistor is between 2 ohms and 100 ohms,
such that applying the digital signal to the at least one resistor (400a) entirely blunts a
waveform of the digital signal so as to reduce or suppress ringing occurring in the digital

signal, and the blunted digital signal is to be inputted into the transmission antenna (100a).

2. The non-contact transmission module (Ma) according to claim 1, wherein

the transmitter (200a) includes a first port (210a) and a second port (220a),

the transmission antenna (100a) includes a first portion (110a) and a second
portion (120a),

the at least one resistor (400a) comprises a plurality of resistors (400a) including
at least one first resistor (400a) and at least one second resistor (400a),

the at least one first resistor (400a) is connected in series between the first port
(210a) of the transmitter (200a) and the first portion (110a) of the transmission antenna
(100a), and

the at least one second resistor (400a) is connected in series between the second
