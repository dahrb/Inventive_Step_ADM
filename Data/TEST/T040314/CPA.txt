TITLE:
Combined standard video and high-speed camera

TYPE:
World International Patent Organisation

ABSTRACT:
The invention relates to a camera, suitable for the simultaneous generation of video sequences with two different image frequencies fV and fHS, where fHS > fV, comprising a sensor unit (1), suitable for recording video sequences by means of an optical-imaging system with an image frequency of fHS, a memory (5), suitable for storage of video sequences taken by the sensor unit (1) with an image frequency of fHS and a converter device (4), suitable for converting video sequences taken with the sensor unit (1) and image frequency fHS into video sequences of image frequency fV.
