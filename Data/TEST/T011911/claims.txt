NEW CLAIMS (MAIN REQUEST)

1. A method for managing an electronic record for compliance with a policy in a

distributed computing environment, the method comprising:

recording the electronic record in a central database, the central database
adapted to be accessed by the distributed computing environment;

creating an electronic tag that uniquely identifies the electronic record, the
electronic tag being created using information associated with the policy,
where the information is obtained by:

analyzing a user profile;

analyzing a workstation specification; and

generating a reference code;

storing the electronic tag in the distributed computing environment;

sending the electronic record to a recipient device via the distributed computing

environment;
wherein the electronic record is an electronic email message, and the

information used in the creation of the electronic tag enables analysis of the

electronic record for compliance with the policy.

2. The method of claim 1, further comprising deleting the electronic record and
selectively deleting the electronic tag.
