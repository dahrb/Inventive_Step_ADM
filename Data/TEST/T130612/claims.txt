Main Request

' Patent Claims

1. Automobile equipped with a reader to be placed inside of the automobile, wherein the
reader is able to read purchasing instrument data on a mobile phone, on which the
purchasing instrument data are stored, the reader being further connected to a
wireless communication interface of the automobile, wherein the wireless
communication interface is a Bluetooth interface, the wireless communication
interface being able to transmit the purchasing instrument data to a point of sale,
wherein the automobile comprises a display device, wherein from the point of sale an
information related to a purchasing operation is transmitted by means of the wireless
communication interface and wherein the information related to the purchasing

operation is displayed on the display device.

2. Automobile according to claim 1, wherein the wireless communication interface is

located in the side view mirror of the automobile.

3. Automobile according to claim 1, wherein the wireless communication interface is

located in the door panel of the automobile.

4. Reader to be placed inside of an automobile according to one of the preceding
claims, wherein the reader is able to read purchasing instrument data on a mobile
phone, on which the purchasing instrument data is stored, the reader being further
connected to a wireless communication interface of the automobile, wherein the
wireless communication interface is a Bluetooth interface, the wireless
communication interface being able to transmit the purchasing instrument data to a

point of sale.

5. Method to facilitate the use of purchasing instrument data inside of an automobile
according to one of the claims 1 to 3, wherein in a first step the purchasing instrument
data on a mobile phone, on which the purchasing instrument data are stored, are
read by a reader and wherein in a second step the purchasing instrument data are
transmitted to a point of sale via a wireless communication interface of the
automobile, wherein the wireless communication interface is a Bluetooth interface.

6. Method according to claim 5, wherein in addition to the transmission of the
purchasing instrument data, at least one of a vehicle identification number or a

license-plate-number is transmitted.

7. Method according to claim 5, wherein prior to transmitting the purchasing instrument
data to the point of sale, the payment needs to be initiated by an occupant of the

automobile.

8. Method according to claim 5, wherein after the transmission of the purchasing
instrument data a navigation process of the automobile to the point of sale is initiated.
