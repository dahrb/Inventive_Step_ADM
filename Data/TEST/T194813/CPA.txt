Title: The utility of capsule endoscopy in obscure gastrointestinal bleeding

Abstract: Capsule endoscopy is a new wireless endoscopic exam of the small intestine. Several studies have shown capsule endoscopy to be superior to other modalities in the evaluation of patients with obscure gastrointestinal bleeding. Capsule endoscopy has been shown to be superior to push enteroscopy and to small bowel series. Reading a capsule study can be stressful and it has been compared to the piloting a plane over long distances or administering anesthesia during a long surgical case. Specific steps can be taken to lessen this workload especially when combined with software in the reading system that shortens reading time. It is clear that with the advent of capsule endoscopy, practice guidelines for the evaluation of patients with obscure gastrointestinal bleeding will change. Literature suggests that health care costs in the evaluation of obscure bleeding could be lessened by the early use of capsule endoscopy. Capsule endoscopy will be first-line in the evaluation of patients with obscure bleeding, once upper endoscopy and colonoscopy are negative.
