CLAIMS

(clean)

1. A method of making a product, the method comprising:
providing a material comprising a carbohydrate produced by a process comprising pretreating a
biomass feedstock with radiation and sonication; and
contacting the material with a microorganism having the ability to convert at least a portion of

the material to the product.

2. The method of claim 1 wherein a high-frequency, rotor-stator device is utilized to pro-

vide the sonication pretreatment.

3. The method of claim 1 wherein the biomass feedstock comprises a cellulosic and/or lig-

nocellulosic material.

4. The method of any one of the above claims, wherein the biomass feedstock is selected
from the group consisting of paper, paper products, paper waste, wood, particle board, sawdust,
agricultural waste, sewage, silage, grasses, rice hulls, bagasse, cotton, jute, hemp, flax, bamboo,
sisal, abaca, straw, corn cobs, corn stover, switchgrass, alfalfa, hay, rice hulls, coconut hair, cot-

ton, synthetic celluloses, seaweed, algae, and mixtures thereof.

5. The method of any one of the above claims, wherein the product comprises one or more

of hydrogen, alcohols, organic acids and hydrocarbons.

6. The method of any one of the above claims, wherein the microorganism comprises a

bacterium, e.g. a cellulolytic bacterium.

7. The method of any one of the above claims, wherein converting comprises contacting

the material with a cellulase to produce sugars.

8. The method of claim 7 further comprising fermenting the sugars to the product.

9. The method of any one of the above claims, wherein the microorganism comprises a

yeast.

10. The method of any one of the above claims, wherein converting comprises fermenting

the material to the product.

11. The method of any one of the above claims, wherein the irradiation is performed utiliz-

ing an ionizing radiation, wherein the ionizing radiation comprises electron beam radiation.

12. The method of claim 11 wherein pretreating with radiation comprises pretreating with
electron beam radiation, and pretreating with sonication comprises pretreating with a high-

frequency, rotor-stator device.

13. The method of claim 11, wherein the radiation is applied at a total dose of between
about 10 Mrad and about 150 Mrad, such as at a dose rate of about 0.5 to about 10 Mrad/day, or
1 Mrad/s to about 10 Mrad/s.- -- *

14. The method of any one of the above claims wherein irradiating includes applying two

or more radiation sources.

15. The method of any of the preceding claims wherein irradiating comprises applying

radiation to the lignocellulosic material with more than one electron beam device.

16. The method of claim 11 wherein the electron beam device delivers electrons having an

energy of 0.3 MeV to 2.0 MeV.

17. The method of any one of the above claims, wherein sonication is performed at a fre-

quency of between about 15 kHz and about 25 kHz, such as between about 18 kHz and 22 kHz.

18. The method of any one of the above claims, wherein the biomass feedstock is prepared

by shearing a biomass fiber source to provide a fibrous material.
