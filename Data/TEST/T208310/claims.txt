THE TRUSTEES OF PRINCETON UNIVERSITY

Amended Claims

1. A method of depositing an organic material, comprising:

ejecting a carrier gas carrying an organic material from the nozzle at a flow
velocity that is at least 10 % of the thermal velocity of the carrier gas, such that the
organic material is deposited onto a substrate;

providing a guard flow around the carrier gas.

2. The method of claim 1, wherein the method is performed with a background

pressure of at least about 101,325 Pa (about 760 Torr).

3. The method of claim 1, wherein the method is performed in a glove-box

without the use of a vacuum apparatus.

4. The method of claim 1, wherein a dynamic pressure in a region between the
nozzle and the substrate surrounding the carrier gas is at least 1333 Pa (10 Torr), and
the background atmosphere is ambient atmosphere at about 101,325 Pa (about 760
Torr).

5. The method of claim 1, wherein the pressure in a region between the nozzle
and the substrate surrounding the carrier gas is at least 1333 Pa (10 Torr), and is

affected by the guard flow, which is ejected from the nozzle.

6. The method of claim 5, wherein the background pressure is the base pressure

of a vacuum chamber, and is less than about 13.33 Pa (about 0.1 Torr).
