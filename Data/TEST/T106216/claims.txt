CLAIMS

1, An oral care composition comprising
a. an effective amount of arginine, in free or salt form; and
b. a fluoride ion source, wherein the amount of fluoride ion source is 0.01 wt% to
0.1% by weight of the composition, and an anionic surfactant wherein the anionic
surfactant is selected from sodium lauryl sulfate, sodium laureth sulfate and

mixtures thereof.

2. The oral care composition of claim 1, wherein the fluoride ion source provides from

25 to 600 ppm of fluoride ions, and wherein the fluoride ion source is a fluoride salt.

3. A composition according to claim 1 or claim 2 wherein the arginine is in salt form and

selected from arginine phosphate, arginine bicarbonate, and arginine hydrochloride.

4, A composition according to claim 1 or claim 2 wherein the anionic surfactant is
present in an amount of 0.01 to 10 weight % of the total composition weight, optionally
wherein the anionic surfactant is present in an amount of 0.3 to 4.5 weight % of the total

composition weight.

5. A composition according to claim | or claim 2 comprising a fluoride ion source

selected from sodium fluoride and sodium monofluorophosphate.

6. A composition according to claim 1 further comprising an antibacterial agent,

optionally wherein the antibacterial agent is triclosan.
7. A composition according to claim 1 or claim 2 further comprising an anionic polymer,

optionally wherein the anionic polymer is a copolymer of methyl vinyl ether and maleic

anhydride.
