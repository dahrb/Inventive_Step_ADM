Main Request — December 2018

Applicant: Nokia Corporation
Application No.: 05024336.9
Claims
1. A method of ordering, paying for, and delivering goods and services using a mobile

station, comprising:

accessing a gateway by the mobile station and transmitting an
identification code for the mobile station to the gateway, the gateway being
capable of generating variables usable in verifying the identity of the mobile
station by accessing an authentication center and comparing the generated
variables with variables computed by the mobile station;

verifying the legitimacy of the gateway by the mobile station by
comparing the variables computed by the gateway with the variables computed
by the mobile station;

requesting a digital certificate by the mobile station from the gateway to
be used to order and pay for a product or service from a seller;

receiving a digital certificate at the mobile station from the gateway
when the identity of the mobile station has been verified by the gateway;

requesting by the mobile station a product or service from the seller; and

transmitting a digital signature by the mobile station accompanied by the

digital certificate for a signature verification key as payment to said seller.

The method recited in claim 1, wherein the verifying the legitimacy of the gateway
by the mobile station by comparing the variables computed by the gateway with the
variables computed by the mobile station, further comprises:
transmitting from the mobile station to the gateway a session
identification and a mobile subscriber identifier, the mobile subscriber identifier
for transmission from the gateway to an authentication center;
receiving a variable M1 and a computed random number from the
gateway;

computing a variable M1' by the mobile station;

using the formula M3 = M3'= MAC (K, C), where MAC is a message

The method recited in claim 1, wherein transmitting the digital signature,

accepting delivery of the product or service by a buyer.
