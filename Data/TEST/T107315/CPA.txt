TITLE:
Presence-based access control

TYPE:
European Patent

ABSTRACT:
To access services (121, 131) on a device (120, 130), such as a computer, a user has a portable device (170) in two parts: a plug (140) adapted to be inserted in a USB port and a transponder (150) that remains about his person. In a preferred embodiment, an access manager (110) verifies that first the plug (140) and then the transponder (150) are identified (405; 410). If so, the access manager (110) verifies if plug (140) and transponder (150) have to be paired (415) and if they have the proper access rights for the desired service (424). Only then is access given (425). In a further embodiment, more than one transponder is needed to access a certain service. It can thus be appreciated that the invention provides a flexible and secure way to secure access to services.

CLAIMS:
A system (100) for controlling access to a service (121, 131) on a device (120, 130) in the system that further comprises an access manager (110), a plug (140), and a transponder (150),
the transponder (150) being adapted for communication with the plug (140);
the plug (140) being adapted for insertion in the device (120, 130); and
the access manager (110) being adapted to provide access to the service (121, 131) upon successful verification that the plug (140) is inserted in the device (120, 130), that the transponder (150) is in presence of the plug (140), and that the transponder (150) is authorised for access to the service (121, 131),
the system being characterised in that the access manager (110) is adapted to verify that the plug (140) is authorised for access to the service (121, 131).
The system according to claim 1, further characterised in that the plug (140) and the transponder (150) are paired.
The system according to claim 1, further characterised in that the transponder (150) and the plug (140) are adapted to be carried upon a user without encumbrance or bother to the user.
The system according to claim 3, further characterised in that the transponder (150) and the plug (140) are adapted to be joined securely, and easily separated once thus joined.
The system according to claim 1, further characterised in that the transponder (150) is in presence of the plug (140) as long as the plug (140) repeatedly receives a signal from the transponder (150) before a specified time interval lapses.
The system according to claim 5, further characterised in that the transponder (150) sends a signal to the plug (140) in response to a request from the plug (140).
The system according to claim 1, further characterised in that the transponder (150) comprises an identification interface (155) that requires user identification before communication with the plug (140).
The system according to claim 1, further characterised in that the plug (140) is adapted to provide the verification of the presence of the transponder (150) to the access manager (110).
A method for controlling access to a service (121, 131) on a device (120, 130) in a system (100), the system (100) further comprising an access manager (110), a plug (140), and a transponder (150), the transponder (150) being adapted for communication with the plug (140), the method comprising the steps, at the access manager (110), of:
verifying:
that the plug (140) is inserted in the device (120, 130);
that the transponder (150) is in presence of the plug (140);
that the transponder (150) is authorised for access to the service (121, 131);
and, upon successful verification, providing access to the service (121, 131), characterised in that the method comprises the step of verifying, by the access manager (110), that the plug (140) is authorised for access to the service (121, 131) before providing access to the service (121, 131).
An access manager (110) for controlling access to a service (121, 131) on a device (120, 130) in a system (100), the system (100) further comprising a plug (140), and a transponder (150), the access manager (110) being adapted to:
verify:
that the plug (140) is inserted in the device (120, 130);
that the transponder (150) is authorised for access to the service; and
that the transponder (150) is in presence of the plug (140); and upon successful verification, provide access to the service (121, 131),
characterised in that the access manager (110) is adapted to verify that the plug (140) is authorised for access to the service (121, 131) before providing access to the service (121, 131).
