Claims

1. A method for controlling user’s access to a protected resource, the method

comprising:

detecting a plug-in token (111, 112) connected to a device (100, 200) that
controls user access to the protected resource, wherein the token (111, 112) is
associated with one or more authorized users;

identifying one or more authorized users associated with the detected token
(111, 112) who are authorized to access the protected resource, including at least one
supervising user;

authenticating whether a first user requesting access to the protected resource is
associated with the detected token (111, 112) and authorized to access the protected
resource;

detecting one or more wireless transponders (121, 122) of one or more
authorized users associated with the token (111, 112), including at least a transponder
(121) of the first user and a transponder (121, 122) of the supervising user of the first
user;

applying different combinations of rules to grant or deny the first user to access
different types of protected resources based at least on a current system state including
tokens (111, 112) connected to the device (100, 200) and the transponders (121, 122)
tied to the tokens (111, 112), and a detected change of a number of transponders
accessing the protected resources within a token reception area, the different types of
protected resources include at least protected applications, protected data and
protected devices, and the rules are determined based on at least authentication of the
first user associated with the detected token (111, 112), detection of the transponder
(121, 122) of the first user, and detection of the transponder (121, 122) of the

supervising user; and

--- PAGE 2 ---
providing access to the protected resource to the first user when an applicable

combination of rules allows the first user to access the protected resource.

2. The method of claim 1, wherein the wireless transponder (121, 122) is operable
to communicate wirelessly with one of the token (111, 112) and the device (100, 200),
and wherein detecting one or more wireless transponders (121, 122) of one or more
authorized users associated with the token (111, 112), includes detecting wireless

signal transmissions from one or more transponders (121, 122).

3. The method of claim 2, wherein the wireless signal transmissions from the

transponder (121, 122) are encrypted.

4. The method of claim 2, further comprising:

measuring signal strength or transmission delay of the wireless signal
transmission from the transponder (121, 122); and

determining approximate location of the wireless transponder (121, 122) based

on the measured signal strength or transmission delay.

5. The method of claim 1, wherein providing access to the protected resource to
the first user further includes:

determining whether at least one of the transponder (121, 122) of the first user
and the transponder (121, 122) of the supervising user was active for less or more than
a predetermined period of time; and

blocking access to the protected resource to the first user when at least one of
the transponder (121, 122) of the first user and the transponder (121, 122) of the

supervising user was active for less or more than a predetermined period of time.

6. The method of claim 1, wherein providing access to the protected resource to

the first user further includes:

7. The method of claim 1, further comprising:

determining a priority hierarchy for each of the rules to grant or deny a user to
access at least a portion of each of the different types of the protected resources, each

resources in an order of the priority hierarchy; and

applying one of the rules upon detecting the one or more conditions associated

with the one of the plurality of rules.
