TITLE:
Led package having an array of light emitting cells coupled in series

TYPE:
World International Patent Organisation

ABSTRACT:
Disclosed is a light emitting diode (LED) package having an array of light emitting cells coupled in series. The LED package comprises a package body and an LED chip mounted on the package body. The LED chip has an array of light emitting cells coupled in series. Since the LED chip having the array of light emitting cells coupled in series is mounted on the LED package, it can be driven directly using an AC power source.

CLAIMS:
[I] A light emitting diode (LED) package, comprising: a package body; and an LED chip mounted on the package body and having an array of light emitting cells coupled in series. [2] The LED package as claimed in claim 1, wherein the LED chip comprises: a substrate; and light emitting cells formed on the substrate and electrically insulated from the substrate. [3] The LED package as claimed in claim 2, wherein the LED chip comprises wires connecting the light emitting cells to one another in series. [4] The LED package as claimed in claim 2, further comprising a submount interposed between the LED chip and the package body, the submount having electrode patterns corresponding to the light emitting cells, wherein the light emitting cells are connected to one another in series through the electrode patterns. [5] The LED package as claimed in claim 1, wherein the LED chip further comprises a rectification bridge unit for applying predetermined rectified power to the array of light emitting cells coupled in series. [6] The LED package as claimed in claim 1, wherein the LED chip further comprises one or more arrays of light emitting cells coupled in series, and the arrays of light emitting cells coupled in series are connected in reverse parallel to one another. [8] The LED package as claimed in claim 7, wherein the package body is a printed circuit board with lead electrodes, and the LED chip is electrically connected to the lead electrodes.
