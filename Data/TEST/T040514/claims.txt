CLAIMS

1. A self-testing speed sensor (10) comprising:

a sensing element (28) configured to sense a motion parameter of an object (12), the
sensing element (28) being a Hall-effect sensing device or a magneto resistive sensing device;

a signal conditioning device (30) in communication with an output of the sensing
element, the signal conditioning device configured to generate a motion signal related to the
sensed motion parameter of the object;

a magnetic induction device (26) configured to induce a magnetic field upon the sensing
element (28) to simulate the motion parameter of the object (12); and

a driving circuit (24) configured to supply the magnetic induction device (26) with a
periodic signal causing the magnetic induction device to induce a periodic magnetic field upon
the sensing element (28);

wherein, when the periodic magnetic field is induced upon the sensing element (28), the
signal conditioning device (30) is further configured to generate an operability indication signal
indicating the operability of the sensing element; and

characterised in that:

the operability indication signal is generated independently of the presence of the object
(12); and

the sensing element (28), the signal conditioning device (30), the magnetic induction device

(26), and the driving circuit (24) are all contained in a housing (18).

2. The self-testing sensor of claim 1, wherein the signal conditioning device (30) is configured

to provide the motion signal and the operability indication signal to an indicator device (16).

3. The self-testing sensor of any of the preceding claims, wherein the magnetic induction device

(26) comprises one or more coils positioned in proximity to the sensing element.

4. The self-testing sensor of any of the preceding claims wherein the operability indication

signal is further indicative of the operability of the signal conditioning device (30).

5. The self-testing sensor of any of the preceding claims, wherein the operability indication

signal is generated independently of the motion parameter of the object (12).

6. The self-testing sensor of any of the preceding claims, further comprising a self-test enable
switch (32) connected between the driving circuit (24) and a power supply (14), wherein, when the
self-test enable switch is activated, the power supply supplies power to the driving circuit to cause

the driving circuit to supply the periodic signal.

7. The self-testing sensor of claim 6, wherein the power supply (14) is external to the housing,
wherein the self-test enable switch is at least partially contained in the housing, and wherein the
housing comprises a power supply terminal (20) connecting the external power supply to both the

self-test enable switch and the signal conditioning device.

8. The self-testing sensor of any of the preceding claims, wherein the object is a rotating object

(12) comprising ferrous metal.

9. The self-testing sensor of any of the preceding claims, wherein the self-testing sensor is
configured to operate in any of three modes, a first mode being a self-test mode occurring before the
self-testing sensor is installed near the object, a second mode being a self-test mode occurring when
the self-testing sensor is installed near the object but when the object is stationary, and a third mode

being an object-testing mode to sense the motion parameter of the object.

10. A method of performing a self test, the method comprising:

supplying (34) a periodic magnetic field upon a sensing element of a speed sensor that is
configured to sense a parameter of an object, the sensing element being a Hall-effect sensing
device or a magneto resistive sensing device, wherein supplying the periodic magnetic field
comprises providing, via a driving circuit, a predetermined pulse train to a signal induction
device to induce the periodic magnetic field upon the sensing element to simulate an effect of the
parameter of the object; and

receiving (36) an output from the sensing element indicating the operability of the
sensing element;

wherein the output is received independently of the parameter of the object and
independently of the presence of the object; and

wherein the sensing element, the signal induction device, and the driving circuit are all

contained in a housing.
