CLAIMS:
1. A mobile power plant system (10), characterized by:

a first mobile body (12) supporting both a gas turbine engine (22) and an
auxiliary skid (32) communicatively and fluidly coupled to the turbine engine (22),
both during transportation and after installation, wherein the first mobile body (12)

has a plurality of first wheels (20),

wherein the gas turbine engine (22) comprises support systems including a
lubrication system (140, 170), a hydraulic start system (172) and a water wash system
(72, 174),

wherein the auxiliary skid (32) includes components configured to regulate
and/or monitor the gas turbine engine (22), the lubrication system (40, 170), the
hydraulic start system (172) and the water wash system (42), the components
including water wash components (180) communicatively and/or fluidly coupled to
the water wash system (42, 174) of the gas turbine engine (22) to regulate a flow of
water for cooling the gas turbine engine (22), lubrication system components (182)
configured to provide a source of lubricant utilized by the lubrication system (40, 170)
of the gas turbine engine (22) to circulate the lubricant through the gas turbine engine
(22), and hydraulic start components (184) configured to facilitate regulating a
hydraulics motor (176) of the hydraulic start system (172); and

wherein the auxiliary skid (32) comprises a turbine control panel (150) which
includes various support wiring and/or instruments configured to support operations
of each of the gas turbine engine (22), the lubrication system (40, 170), the hydraulic
start system (172) and the water wash system (42, 174) and configured to interface
with a higher level control system (70) of the mobile power plant system (10) to
provide feedback from the gas turbine engine (22) to the control system (70); and

a second mobile body (14) supporting a generator (52), wherein the second

mobile body has a plurality of second wheels (20), and the first and second mobile
