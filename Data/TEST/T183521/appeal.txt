The appeal was filed by the Applicant against the decision of the Examining Division to refuse the application in suit. In its decision, the Examining Division held i.a. that the subject-matter of claim 1 according to the main request and auxiliary requests 1 - 3 did not involve an inventive step in the light of D1 and D6. Claim 1 of auxiliary request 4 was found to introduce unsearched subject-matter, Rule 137(5) EPC. II. In a communication pursuant to Rule 15(1) RPBA, the Board expressed the preliminary opinion that although the subject-matter of claim 1 according to the main request seemed to meet the requirements of Article 56 EPC, it appeared to extend beyond the content of the application as originally filed, Article 123(2) EPC. The same seemed to apply to all auxiliary requests for corresponding reasons. Furthermore, the description did not seem to meet the requirements of the EPC. III. In response to the communication the Appellant filed with letter of 29 November 2023 a new main request including a set of claims and an adapted description. IV. The Appellant requests that the decision under appeal be set aside and that a patent be granted on the basis of the main request filed with letter of 29 November 2023 or one of auxiliary requests 1 - 4, on which the Examining Division's decision is based. Further auxiliarly oral proceedings are requested. V.
