Claims

A self-supporting structural unit (10) to form a wall, or roof of a building comprising:

(i) two power generation members (12/14), the first comprising photovoltaic (PV) device,
adhered to a thermal collector 14 by a layer of adhesive 16, characterized in that, the second
power generation member is positioned in a different plane from the first power generation
member and substantially parallel with the first, with the second power generation member,
being .

(a) a metal thermal collector of high thermal conductivity, so to allow rapid conduction of
the heat to the transmission fluid held in the metal pipe of the thermal collector so
maximising heat capture situated beneath the first power generation member, or

(b) a substantially transparent thermal collector secured over the first generation member,
with fluid passing through the thermal collector so that heat may be absorbed from the first

power generator;

(ii) a support member to which the power generation members are attached, the support
member comprising insulating material laid within a structure formed from a supporting
material made of wood, or metal, or concrete or plastic or glass reinforced plastic, the unit

being adapted to be attached to a framework to form a wall or roof of a building.

A structural unit according to claim 1, wherein the power generating members further
comprises at least one protective layer, the protective layer being secured over the energy
converter or the energy converter in the protective layer.

A structural unit as claimed in claim 2, wherein the protective layer is transparent.

A structural unit as claimed in claim 2, wherein the protective layer has good transmittance

characteristics in the solar spectrum.

A structural unit as claimed in claim 2, wherein the protective layer comprises at least one

layer of low emissivity coating.
