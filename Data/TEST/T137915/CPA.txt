TITLE:
Device for the insulation of stator slots

TYPE:
European patent

ABSTRACT:
The invention relates to a device for insulating stator of an electric machine, which has a stator with a plurality of hammers and located between the hammers Statornuten, with an insulating body, which has adapted to the shape of Statornuten formations (14 to 20) and in axial Direction to the stator is pushed on and a cover (42) which is connectable to an end face of the insulating body to guide and cover the windings (36) on the end face of the stator after the application of windings (36) on the insulating body.
