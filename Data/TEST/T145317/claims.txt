CLAMS (Main request)

1. An electronic communication network for securities trading and for
providing real time, or near real time, execution quality indications, comprising:

an electronic data connection between a trader and an executing broker
for transmission of order information electronically from the trader to the
executing broker and for transmission of execution information electronically
from the executing broker to the trader;

a message interceptor for without interfering with the data transmissions
(a) intercepting in real time or almost real time an electronic order
communication from the trader to the broker and accessing order data therefrom
and (b) for intercepting in real time or in almost real time an electronic
execution communication from the broker to the trader and accessing execution
data;

a generic market data source; and

an execution quality calculation module (EQCM) in communication
with the generic market data source, the message interceptor and the trader for
electronically receiving on a real-time basis market data from the generic
market data source, electronically receiving copy order data and copy execution
data from the message interceptor, calculating one or more execution qualities
corresponding to one or more of said electronic market order executions using
said real-time or near real-time market data, and electronically transmitting to
the trader information related to the one or more execution qualities for real-

time or near real-time quality assessment of the market trade transactions.

2. The network of claim 1, wherein the executing broker is configured to
send execution data to the trader immediately as the relevant transactions occur
and the message interceptor is configured to aggregate execution data and to
transmit the data to the trader in larger lots so that multiple small executions are

bunched into a smaller number of larger executions.

3. The network of claim 1 or 2, further comprising a display for displaying

to the trader information related to the one or more execution qualities.

4. The network of any preceding claim, wherein said information that is
transmitted from the EQCM to the trader comprises at least one of said one or
more execution qualities which is an indication of deviation of a value
describing said at least one of said one or more execution qualities from one or

more predetermined limits.

5. The network of any preceding claim, wherein said EQCM is configured
to assess at least one of said one or more execution qualities for one of said one
or more electronic market orders by calculating a difference between a volume-
weighted average price of said one or more electronic market order executions
for said one of said one or more electronic market orders, and a volume-
weighted average price of said real-time or near real-time market data related to

a security for said one of said one or more electronic market orders.

6. The network of any preceding claim, wherein said information related to
the one or more execution qualities comprises a comparison of performance of
all or selected brokers of said one or more brokers using said one or more

execution qualities for a selected electronic market order.

7. The network of any preceding claim, wherein said information related to
said one or more execution qualities is accumulated over a predetermined

period of time.

to provide information related to said one or more execution qualities to said
trader via a display, said information comprises one or more of the following:
