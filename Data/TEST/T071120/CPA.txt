TITLE:
Article processing system

TYPE:
Japanese Patent

ABSTRACT:
PROBLEM TO BE SOLVED: To provide an article processing system capable of further efficiently processing an article.SOLUTION: An article processing system 2 comprises a supply conveyor 3 (carrying means) for carrying articles 1 in a plurality of rows, and a robot 5 having a head 5b having holding means HA-HD for holding the articles on the supply conveyor. Detection means 6 recognizes the supply conveyor 3 as a plurality of rows of carrying areas SA and SB divided along the carrying direction, and control means 7 respectively allocates the respective holding means HA-HD of the robot to any carrying areas. When the detection means recognizes the carrying area in which the articles are positioned by detecting the articles on the supply conveyor, the control means the robot to hold the articles by holding means allocated to the carrying area in which the articles are positioned, in order from the article positioned on the downstream side among the carried articles. The articles can be efficiently held by shortening a movement route of the head.
