CLAIMS (Clean

1. A method for identifying keypoints in a digital image (/) comprising a set of
pixels, each pixel having associated thereto a respective value of an image representative
parameter, said method comprising:

- approximating (108), without explicitly computing, a filtered image (Z(o), /02) of
said digital image (/), said filtered image being obtained from said digital image (/) by
means of a filtering function, said filtering function being a Laplacian of Gaussian or a
Difference of Gaussians and having a filtering parameter (o) being the standard deviation
(oc) of the Gaussian function, said approximating comprising:

a) generating (102) a set of base filtered images (Z(o;)) of said digital image (/), each
base filtered image (L(o;)) being the digital image (/) filtered by means of said filtering
function with a respective value (0;) of the filtering parameter;

b) for each pixel (x,y) of at least a subset of said set of pixels of the digital image
(J), approximating (106) the filtered image (L(x1,0)) obtained from said digital image (J)
by means of said filtering function having a filtering parameter value by means of a
respective approximation function based on the base filtered images, said approximation
function being a function of the filtering parameter (o) within a predefined range (104) of
values of the filtering parameter (0), wherein:

- said approximating the filtered image, obtained from said digital
image (/) by means of said filtering function having a filtering parameter
by means of a respective approximation function based on the base
filtered images, comprises calculating said approximation function based
on a linear combination of said base filtered images, such linear
combination providing for having each base filtered image multiplied by
a corresponding weight; and

- said approximation function being a polynomial having the
filtering parameter as a variable, wherein the coefficients of said
polynomial are calculated based on the base filtered images and based on
an approximation of the weights of said linear combination;

- for each pixel of said at least a subset, identifying (120) such pixel as a candidate

keypoint if, in such pixel, the approximation function has a local extreme (110) which is

2. The method of claim 1, wherein said approximation function is based on a further

3. The method of any one among the preceding claims, further comprising

4. The method of any one among the preceding claims, further comprising:

- for each selected pixel, calculating the main curvature and the secondary curvature
of the surface formed by the filtering functions in the pixels of the image contained in a
patch centered at such selected pixel;

- discarding / maintaining such pixel from / in the selected pixels based on the ratio

between the main curvature and the secondary curvature.

5. The method of any one among the preceding claims, further comprising:

- for each selected pixel, calculating the value assumed by the second derivative of
the approximation function with respect to the filtering parameter at the corresponding
global extreme, and

- discarding / maintaining such pixel from / in the selected pixels based on such

value assumed by the second derivative.

6. The method of any one among the preceding claims, wherein said identifying
keypoints is further repeated on at least a scaled version of the image, using the same

predefined range of the filtering parameter.

- at least one of the values of the filtering parameter of the base filtered images is

filtered image in the pixel with respect to such a change of coordinates, said further
approximation function being based:

1) on the filtering function of such selected pixel at the value of the filtering
parameter corresponding to the global extreme of the selected pixel, and

2) on the filtering functions of the pixels adjacent to the selected pixel in the image
at the value of the filtering parameter corresponding to the global extreme of the selected
