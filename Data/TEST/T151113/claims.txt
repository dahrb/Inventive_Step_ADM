Claims:

1. An optical device having at least one interconnection tab, comprising:

a lens (26) comprising a pair of opposed substrates (650A, 50B) having a gap (56)
therebetween filled with an electro-optic material (58), each said substrate having a facing
surface with a substrate electrode (652A, 52B) covered by a polyimide alignment layer (54A,
54B) disposed thereon;

a sealing material (60) disposed between said pair of opposed substrates to contain
said electro-optic material; and

at least one interconnection tab (500) interposed between said substrates, said
interconnection tab comprising:

an insulator layer (502) having opposed surfaces (512, 514), each insulator layer
surface comprising a tab electrode layer (522, 552) having at least a portion (524, 556)
interposed between said substrates (560A, 50B) and being in contact with the corresponding
alignment layer (54A, 54B) on the substrate electrode (52A, 52B) facing said tab electrode
layer, and each insulator layer surface further comprising a pad electrode (532, 564) being
electrically connected to the tab electrode layer (552, 522) on the opposite surface of the

insulator layer (502).

2. The optical device according to claim 1, wherein each said tab electrode layer (522,
552), and said pad electrode (564, 532) on the opposite surface of the insulator layer are

electrically connected by at least one conductive via (580) extending therethrough.

3. The optical device according to claim 1, further comprising:
a conductive adhesive disposed on at least a portion of each said tab electrode

layer.

4. The optical device according to claim 1, wherein said insulator layer has a lens edge
(504) received between said pair of opposed substrates (50A, 50B) of said lens (26)
opposite a terminal edge (506) extending from said substrates and at the opposite end of
the insulator layer (502) such that the portion (524, 556) of the tab electrode layer (522, 552)
interposed between said substrates (560A, 50B) extends primarily along the lens edge (504)
and the pad electrode (532) is positioned along a portion of the terminal edge (506).

5. The optical device according to claim 1, wherein each said tab electrode layer is L-

shaped.

6. The optical device according to claim 1, wherein each said pad electrode (532, 564)
is spaced apart from said tab electrode layer (522, 552) on the same surface of the insulator

layer.

7. The optical device according to claim 1, wherein said interconnection tab has a

thickness that is greater than the gap between said pair of opposed substrates (50A, 50B).

8. The optical device according to claim 1, wherein said interconnection tab is flexible.

9. The optical device according to claim 1, wherein said interconnection tab is

removably insertable between said pair of opposed substrates (50A, 50B).

10. The optical device of claim 1, wherein the pair of substrates (50A, 50B) is flexible.
