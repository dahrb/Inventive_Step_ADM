TITLE:
An approximation of data points by piecewise polynomial functions and their dual orthogonal functions

TYPE:
Academic paper

ABSTRACT:
The fluency theory provides a powerful model for describing various types of analog signals, by introducing a set of piecewise polynomial functions and their dual orthogonal functions which are classified in terms of their continuous differentiability. In this paper, we propose a method to approximate a set of given data points based on the fluency theory and give an example of its application. Results show the possibility of better approximations by adaptively employing fluency functions of different classes.
