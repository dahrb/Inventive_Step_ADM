TITLE:
Shampoo containing a gel network

TYPE:
World International Patent Organisation

ABSTRACT:
The compositions of the present invention relate to improved shampoo compositions having from about from about 5 to about 50 weight percent of a detersive surfactant, at least about 0.05 weight percent of a fatty alcohol gel network and at least about 20.0 weight percent of an aqueous carrier.

CLAIMS:
1. A shampoo composition comprising: a) from about 5 to about 50 weight percent of a detersive surfactant, b) at least about 0.05 weight percent of a fatty alcohol gel network, and c) at least about 20.0 weight percent of an aqueous caπier.
2. The composition of Claim 1 further comprising a deposition aid.
3. The composition of Claim 2 wherein said deposition aid is a cationic polymer.
4. The composition of Claim 3 wherein said cationic polymer has a molecular weight of from about 10,000 to about 10,000,000 and a charge density from about 0.9 meq/gm to about 7.0 meq/gm.
5. The composition of Claim 1 wherein said fatty alcohol gel network is present in an amount from about .05 weight percent to about 14 weight percent, preferably from about 1 weight percent to about 10 weight percent.
6. The composition of Claim 1 wherein said fatty alcohol gel network is formed using a cationic surfactant.
7. The composition of Claim 6 wherein said cationic surfactant is selected from the group consisting of quaternium-8, quaternium-14, quaternium-18, quateπιium-18 methosulfate, quaternium-24, behenyltrimethylammonium chloride, and mixtures thereof.
8. A method of treating hair by administering a safe and effective amount of the composition according to Claim 1.

A process for preparing a cleansing composition comprising: a) combining a fatty alcohol and a surfactant in a premix at a temperature sufficient to allow partitioning of the surfactant into the fatty alcohol, b) cooling the mixture below the chain melt temperature of the premix to form a gel network, c) adding the gel network to a detersive surfactant and an aqueous caπier to form a cleansing composition.
