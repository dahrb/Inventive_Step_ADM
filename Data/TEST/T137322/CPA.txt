TITLE:
Process and a device for the isolation of cell components such as nucleic acids from natural sources

TYPE:
US Patent

ABSTRACT:
Described is a method of isolating cell components, such as nucleic acids, from natural sources by filtering a sample of the digested natural sources such as cells or cell fragments. The method is characterized in that the sample is passed through a filter, the pore size of which decreases in the direction of flow of the sample through the filter.

CLAIMS:
1. A process for the isolation of cell components such as nucleic acids from natural sources by removing the digested natural sources such as cells or cell fragments in a sample by filtration, characterized in that the sample is passing a filter, wherein the pore size of the filter decreases in the direction of sample flow through the filter.
2. The process according to
claim 1
, wherein sample flow through the filter is supported by applying elevated pressure, reduced pressure or gravity or gravity enhanced by centrifugation and combinations of these measures.
3. The process according to
claim 1

4. The process according to at least one of
claims 1
to
3

5. The process according to at least one of
