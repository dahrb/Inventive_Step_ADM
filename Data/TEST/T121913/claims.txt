Main request
CLAIMS

1. An extraction method, wherein it comprises the flowing steps:

crushing and soaking the raw material, and then extracting, wherein the extracting
step is carried out under a 18~33 kHz nonlinear vibration, using water as a solvent, under
a pressure of 25~35 MPa and at the temperature of 0~50°C, thus obtaining a extract

which contains the active components of the raw material.

2. An extraction method of claim 1, wherein the extraction temperature is

20°C~50°C.

3. An extraction method of claim 1, wherein the extraction time is 1~3 hours.

4. An extraction method of claim 1, wherein the ratio of raw material to water

solvent by weight is 1:3~5.

5. An extraction method of claim 1, wherein the raw material is a plant.

6. An extraction method of claim |, wherein said method further includes the step

of carrying out the direct liquid package to the said extraction liquid.

7. An extraction method of claim 1, wherein said method further includes the

steps of the concentrating and drying the said extraction liquid.

8. An extraction apparatus for carrying out the extraction method according to
claim | comprising: an extracting can (5) comprising a can body (9) and a top lid (10), a
sealing structure to seal the can body and the top lid, and a pipeline connection (N4, NS,
N6, N7) to input the water into the can body and output the extraction liquid; wherein
said apparatus further comprises a high pressure pump (6), which is linked to the

extracting can (5) and maintain the high pressure in the extracting can under a pressure of

emitting nonlinear vibration with the frequency in the range of 18 KHz~33 KHz are set in

9. An extraction apparatus of claim 8, wherein said raw material can (8) is a
cylindrical bracket (25) enwrapped with filter cloth bag (26), and there is a hook (27) set

(11) and a seal washer (19) between the can body (9) and the top lid (10), wherein said

11. An extraction apparatus of claim 8, wherein the extracting can (5) is further
equipped with a temperature-controlling apparatus externally, to maintain the temperature

in the range of 20°C~50°C.
