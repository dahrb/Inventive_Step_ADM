CLAIMS

1. A group of associated gaming devices (10) each having a lottery drawer (25)
comprising a database (18) having a plurality of entries (30, 35), each entry (35) containing
one or more winning numbers associated with a predetermined time interval, and

a pseudo random number generator (20) for generating pseudo random numbers from a seed
supplied by the database (18), and

a clock (19) for extracting the winning number or numbers from an entry (35) of the database
(18) for the time interval indicated by the clock (19), and

a comparator (22) for at least one receiving of the extracted winning number or numbers and
at least one pseudo random number generated by the pseudo random generator (20), wherein
the comparator (22) is adapted for generating a win signal when the extracted winning
number and the generated pseudo random number match; and

wherein all of the associated gaming devices include identical databases (18) having the
plurality of entries (30) and pseudo random number generators (20) whereby win signals are

generated simultanously.

2. A group as claimed in claim 1 wherein each database entry (35) includes a

header (36), which contributes or contains the seed for the pseudo-random generator.

3. A group as claimed in claim 1 or claims 2 wherein the time interval is

twentyfour hours.

4. A group as claimed in anyone of the preceding claims wherein the number of
data entries is such that the same winning number will not be extracted on the same date for

at least a two year period.
5. A group as claimed in claim 4 wherein the number of data entries is such that
the same winning number will not be extracted on the same day of the week as previously

occurred within at least a two year period.
