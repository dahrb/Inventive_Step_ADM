TITLE:
Integrated insurance customer management system and method using the Internet

TYPE:
South Korean patent

ABSTRACT:
The present invention relates to a system and method for comprehensively managing an insurance customer using the Internet,

Conventional insurance customer management is performed independently for each insurer, so when an incident occurs, the insured should notify the insurance company individually and claim the payment. As a result, insurers with multiple insurers have a significant inconvenience and time to carry out complicated procedures against each insurer, and sometimes they do not remember, or do not know, their family members. If only a missing or insufficient family survives, the insured may not be able to exercise his or her rights by not claiming benefits, so it is convenient for the insured and prevented from being disadvantaged by not claiming benefits. in order to,

It manages the insurance details of members and members' families, helps members to view their insurance portfolios, and in case of incidents, retrieves whether the member is insured from the DB of all insurers and acts as a collective claim and necessary procedure. It is characterized by.

The present invention informs the member or his / her family of all the insurance details that can be claimed, so that it is difficult for the subscriber to remember or track one's own insurance, such as free or indirect insurance, which is paid as a giveaway. This is especially useful if you do not know this, and it is effective to increase profits by paying all the procedures necessary for claim payment.

CLAIMS:
In the integrated Internet insurance customer management system comprising a terminal, a server, an open network connecting the terminal and the server to each other, and an insurance company DB accessible from the server, The server may include an event occurrence accepting module that receives an event occurrence; An insurance company DB search module for accessing the DB of each insurance company and searching and documenting the insurance details of the members; A corresponding insurance discrimination module for distinguishing the applicable insurance from among the found member's insurances; An indirect insurance tracking module for tracking applicable insurance by analyzing the circumstances and background of the case; A claim generation module for preparing a claim for payment according to the form of each insurer and sending it in a batch; A data input module; A data output module; Internet insurance customer integrated management system comprising a member DB having information on the personal details and insurance details of the member and family. The method of claim 1, And the case is a situation where the member or his / her family determines that a claim is required due to mental or physical damage or time, material or money damage of the member or his / her family. In the integrated method of Internet insurance customer management, The client accessing a website or a mobile site; The client applying for membership on the website; Recording personal and family details; Approving the membership of the website; Searching the DB of each insurance company to secure and document the current insurance subscription details for the member and family, and The client accessing the website or mobile site; The client notifying the occurrence of an event on the website or the mobile site, and delegating a billing right to act on the billing process; Retrieving the insurance details of the member received from each insurance company DB using an insurance company DB search module; A corresponding insurance discrimination step of classifying the insurance which can be claimed in the insurance details of the searched member; Analyzing incurred incidents to track indirect insurable claims; Notifying the member of all relevant insurance details, requesting the data required for the claim and receiving the data; A payment invoice preparation step of preparing and bundling payment invoices according to each insurer's payment request form; Receiving benefits; And after the fee is deducted, transmitting the insurance money to the member along with the insurance payment details.
