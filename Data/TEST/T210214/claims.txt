Main Request

CLAIMS:

1.

A nozzle (410) for guiding molten metal comprising: an inlet (106) at an
upstream first end; at least one outlet (210) towards a downstream second end;
an inner surface (117) between said inlet (106) and said at least one outlet (210)
defining a bore (118) through the nozzle (410); the bore (118) having a throat
region (200) adjacent the inlet (106); an annular channel (420) being provided in
the inner surface (117) of the nozzle (410); and a fluid supply means (900) being
arranged to introduce fluid into the bore (118) via the annular channel (420) or
downstream thereof; wherein the throat region (200) has a convexly curved
surface and the annular channel (420) is located within or adjacent the convexly
curved surface of the throat region (200) such that the inner surface of the

nozzle immediately upstream of the annular channel is curved.

A nozzle (410) according to claim 1 wherein the channel (420) is located within

the convexly curved surface of the throat region (200).

A nozzle (410) according to any preceding claim wherein the throat region (200)
has a seating surface (220), which contacts a stopper rod (100) in use to stop the
flow of molten metal through the nozzle (410), and wherein the channel (420) is

positioned downstream of the seating surface (220).

A nozzle (410) according to any preceding claim wherein the width of the
channel (420) is within a range of 0.5% to 95% of the distance between the first

and second ends of the nozzle (410).

A nozzle (410) according to any one of claims 1 to 3 wherein the width of the
channel (420) is no more than 5% of the distance between the first and second

ends of the nozzle (410).

A nozzle (410) according to any preceding claim wherein the depth of the
channel (420) is within a range of 0.1% to 50% of the thickness of the nozzle
(410) at the point immediately upstream of the channel (420).
