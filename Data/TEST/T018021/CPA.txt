TITLE:
Method for controlling the temperature in a climate chamber and climatic device

TYPE:
European patent

ABSTRACT:
The invention relates to a method for controlling the temperature in a climate chamber (21), in which a flow of air is guided through the climate chamber (21), the temperature of which air flow is controlled with the aid of a heat exchanger, and the air flow is brought to the desired temperature with the aid of a first heat exchanger prior to or during the introduction of the air flow into the climate chamber, and in which method the air flow in the climate chamber (21) is forced past a second heat exchanger, during which process any heating or cooling of the air flow is negated. The invention also relates to a device for carrying out the above method.

CLAIMS:
1. Method for controlling the temperature in a climate chamber (21), in which a flow of air is guided through the climate chamber (21), the temperature of the air flow is controlled with the aid of a heat exchanger, and the air flow is brought to the desired temperature with the aid of a first heat exchanger prior to or during the introduction of the air flow into the climate chamber (21), characterized in that the air flow in the climate chamber is forced past a second heat exchanger, during which process any heating or cooling of the air flow is negated.
2. Climatic device (20) comprising: a climate chamber (21), having a bottom wall (2), a top wall (3), a front and rear side wall and a first (4) and a second (5) end wall, means (52) for heating or cooling the air in the climate chamber (21), the said means being accommodated in the said chamber (21), and - ventilation means (27) for generating an air flow through the climate chamber
(21), in which device the end walls (4, 5) are air-permeable, the ventilation means (27) being used to generate an air flow from the first end wall (4), through the climate chamber (21), towards the second end wall (5), and the device comprising preheating or precooling means for bringing the air flow to the correct temperature prior to or during the passage of the air flow through the first end wall (4), characterized in that at least one air-permeable partition (22) is incorporated between the end walls (4, 5), in which partition the heating or cooling means (52) are accommodated.
3. Climatic device according to Claim 2, characterized in that the preheating or precooling means are accommodated in the first end wall (4).
4. Climatic device (20) according to Claim 2 or 3, characterized in that the device is provided with control means (70) for setting the temperature of the heating or cooling means (52) and of the preheating or precooling means.
5. Climatic device (20) according to Claim 2, 3 or 4, characterized in that the ventilation means (27) are in communication, by means of a flow passage, with the first end wall (4), the extent of air permeability of the first end wall (4) being lower than that of the first (in the direction of flow) partition (22).
6. Climatic device (20) according to one of Claims 2 to 5, characterized in that the air permeability of the second end wall (5) is lower than that of the last (in the direction of flow) partition (22a).
7. Climatic device (20) according to one of Claims 2 to 6, characterized in that the air permeability of the first end wall (4) is preferably 5 to 25%, more preferably 5 to
15%, and most preferably 10%, while the air permeability of the first (in the direction of flow) partition (22) is preferably 20 to 50%, more preferably 25 to 40%, and most preferably 30%.
8. Climatic device (20) according to one of Claims 2 to 7, characterized in that the air permeability of the second end wall (5) is preferably 5 to 25%, more preferably
5 to 15%, and most preferably 10%, while the air permeability of the last (in the direction of flow) partition (22a) is preferably 20 to 50%, more preferably 25 to 40%, and most preferably 30%.
