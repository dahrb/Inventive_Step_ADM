Main Request

A method for measuring a capacitance for use in detecting the development of
a pressure ulcer at a target location of a patient’s skin, comprising:

positioning a flexible substrate (16) adjacent the target location of the patient’s
skin,

wherein on the flexible substrate (16) are embedded an array (14) of bipolar
radiofrequency (RF) sensors (24, 26), wherein each sensor comprises a first
electrode (36) and a second electrode (38) and wherein each sensor (24, 26) is
individually coupled to an interface electronic package (22), wherein the
electronics package (22) is configured for independent interrogation and
excitation of each of the bipolar RF sensors (24, 26), and data retrieval from
each of the bipolar RF sensors (24, 26):

conforming the flexible substrate (16) to the patient’s skin at the target location;
exciting the bipolar RF sensors (24, 26) to emit RF energy into the patient’s skin
derma; and

measuring a capacitance at the target location of the patient's skin as an
indicator of the Sub-Epidermal Moisture (SEM) at the target location.

The method of claim 1, wherein the capacitance is an equivalent sub-epidermal
capacitance.

The method of claim 1, wherein the array of bipolar RF sensors (24, 26) is
spaced across the flexible substrate (16) to define a scanning area of the
surface of the patient’s skin derma without using a scanning motion.

The method of claim 3, wherein the array of bipolar RF sensors (24, 26) is
configured to generate an SEM image of the surface of the patient’s skin
without using a scanning motion.

The method of claim 1, further comprising transmitting, by the interface
electronic package (22), data retrieved from the bipolar RF sensor (24, 26) toa
signal database.

An apparatus for measuring a capacitance for use in detecting the development
of a pressure ulcer at a target location of a patient’s skin, the apparatus
comprising:

a first electrode (36) and a second electrode (38) is individually coupled to the
interface electronics package,

sensors (24, 26) for emitting and receiving RF energy to interrogate the
patient's skin, so as to measure a capacitance at the target location of the
