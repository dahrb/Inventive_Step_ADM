1. An mobile device (102), comprising:

-

a high-definition multimedia interface, HDMI, connector

(106) configured to be coupled to a peripheral (104), the

HDMI connector (106) for transferring audio data in an audio
channel and video data in a video channel between the mobile
device (102) and the peripheral (104);

a processor configured to execute a power manager

application (152), the power manager application (152) being

-

configured to timeout the HDMI connector after a period of
time is exceeded without detecting user interaction with the
mobile device (102) such that transfer of data via the HDMI
connector is interrupted; and

a media data monitor (150) configured to detect whether
audio data (144) is in an audio channel (156) of the HDMI
connector (106), and responsive to the audio data being
detected in the audio channel of the HDMI connector (106),
to communicate a reset signal to the power manager
application (152),

wherein the power manager application (152) in response
to the reset signal is configured to reset the system
timeout such that the period of time is not exceeded while

audio data is detected.

2. The mobile device as recited in claim 1, wherein the
period of time is a screensaver setting controlled by the
power manager application (152) that is executed on the

mobile device (102).
