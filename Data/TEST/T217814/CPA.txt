Title: Catalyst for production of ethylene oxide

Abstract: 
A catalyst for oxidizing ethylene and producing ethylene oxide, said catalyst being prepared by pretreating a porous carrier with a solution containing a lithium compound and a cesium compound, thereafter, impregnating a solution containing a silver compound and a cesium compound in the pretreated porous carrier and then heat-treating the impregnated porous carrier.
The use of the catalyst of this invention provides high selectivity in the production of ethylene oxide by the vapor contact oxidation of ethylene.

Type: EPO Patent

Claims: 

A catalyst for oxidizing ethylene and producing ethylene oxide, said catalyst being prepared by pretreating a porous carrier with a solution containing a lithium compound and a cesium compound, thereafter, impregnating a solution containing a silver compound and a cesium compound in the pretreated porous carrier and then heat-treating the impregnated porous carrier.
A catalyst according to Claim 1, wherein said catalyst contains silver of 5 to 30 % by weight.
A catalyst according to Claim 1 or 2, wherein said catalyst contains lithium of 100 to 2000 ppm and cesium of 250 to 2000 ppm.
A catalyst according to any of Claims 1 to 3, wherein a weight ratio (Li/Cs) of lithium to cesium is in a range of 0.1 : 1 to 4 : 1.
A catalyst according to any of Claims 1 to 4, wherein 10 to 80 % of the total cesium to be impregnated in said porous carrier is impregnated at the same time as said lithium compound.
A catalyst according to any of Claims 1 to 5, wherein said carrier has a surface area in a range of 0.6 to 5 m2/g.
A catalyst according to any of Claims 1 to 6, wherein a main component of said carrier is α-alumina.
A catalyst according to any of Claims 1 to 7, wherein said lithium compound is lithium carbonate or lithium bicarbonate.
A catalyst according to any of Claims 1 to 8, wherein said lithium compound is lithium carboxylate.
A catalyst according to any of Claims 1 to 9, wherein said pretreatment comprises the steps of impregnating a solution containing a lithium compound and a cesium compound in said porous carrier and then heat-treating the impregnated porous carrier.
A catalyst according to Claim 10, wherein superheated steam is employed to perform the heat treatment in said pretreatment.
A catalyst according to any of Claims 1 to 11, wherein said silver compound and said cesium compound are both impregnated by using an aqueous solution containing a silver compound, a cesium compound, and amine as a complex forming agent.
A catalyst according to any of Claims 1 to 12, wherein superheated steam is employed to perform said step of impregnating a silver compound and a cesium compound in the porous carrier and then heat-treating the impregnated porous carrier.
