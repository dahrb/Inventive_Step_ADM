Claims

1. An X-ray computed tomography imaging method comprising:

scanning a subject with cone beam X-ray using an X-ray tube (13) and an X-ray detector
(14),

reconstructing input image data based on output data from the X-ray detector;

extracting a cone beam artifact component contained in the input image data based on a
typical shape and typical direction of the cone beam artifact such that an intermediate image data
is generated that comprises an edge component in a body axis direction of the subject as the cone
beam artifact component based on at least one of the output data and the input image data, by
performing high-pass filtering for the input image data in the body axis direction as the typical
direction of the cone beam artifact;

performing weighting with a distribution of weights changing in accordance with posi-
tions in the body axis direction for the cone beam artifact component; and

subtracting the weighted cone beam artifact component from the input image data to gen-

erate output image data in which the cone beam artifact is reduced.

2. The method according to claim 1, wherein the reconstructing step reconstructs the input

image data by performing Feldkamp reconstruction of the output data.

3. The method according to any of claims 1 to 2, wherein the extracting step extracts the
cone beam artifact component by performing low-pass filtering in a plane substantially perpen-
dicular to the body axis direction of the subject for intermediate image data comprising an edge

component in the body axis direction of the subject.

4. The method according to any of claims 1 to 2, wherein the extracting step extracts the
cone beam artifact component by performing threshold processing for intermediate image data
comprising an edge component in the body axis direction of the subject on the basis of a pixel

value which the cone beam artifact component has.

5. The method according to claim 1, wherein the weighting performing step performs the
weighting with the distribution of weights gradually increasing from a center to edges in the

body axis direction.
