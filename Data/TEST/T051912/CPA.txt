TITLE:
Metal layer containing transaction card and method of making the same

TYPE:
World International Patent Organisation

ABSTRACT:
Metal-containing transaction cards, useful for the purchase of goods and/or services, and methods of making the same are provided. The metal-containing transaction card may have a magnetic stripe, an embedded microchip, a signature panel, a holographic image, or any other feature typically contained on or within a transaction card. The transaction cards have at least one layer of metal with, optionally, other layers of substrates, such as thermoplastic polymers, other metals or adhesives. In addition, the metal-containing transaction card may be anodized, coated, or laser-engraved. Preferably, the metal is titanium or stainless steel.

CLAIMS:
1. A transaction card comprising: a first surface and a second surface; at least one layer of metal forming the first surface; a first pocket disposed in the at least one layer of metal in the first surface of the transaction card.
2. The transaction card of claim 1 further comprising: an inset fill panel adhered within the pocket.
3. The transaction card of claim 1 further comprising embossed characters punched into the transaction card within the pocket and protruding from the second surface of the transaction card.
4. The transaction card of claim 1 further comprising a transaction card feature disposed within the pocket.
5. The transaction card of claim 4 wherein the transaction card feature is selected from the group consisting of a signature panel, a magnetic stripe, a microchip, and a holographic image.
6. The transaction card of claim 4 wherein the transaction card feature disposed within the pocket of the transaction card is coplanar with the first surface of the transaction card.
7. The transaction card of claim 1 further comprising an adhesive material disposed within the pocket.
8. The transaction card of claim 7 wherein the adhesive material comprises polyvinyl chloride.
9. The transaction card of claim 1 wherein the at least one metal layer comprises titanium.
10. A method of making a transaction card having a first surface and a second surface comprising the steps of: providing at least one layer of metal forming the first surface; and disposing a pocket in the at least one layer of metal in the first surface of the transaction card.
11. The method of claim 10 further comprising the step of adhering an inset fill panel within the pocket.
12. The method of claim 10 further comprising the step of punching characters within the pocket so as to protrude from the second surface of the transaction card as embossed characters.
13. The method of claim 10 further comprising the step of disposing a transaction card feature within the pocket.
14. The method of claim 13 wherein the transaction card feature is selected from the group consisting of a signature panel, a magnetic stripe, a microchip and a holographic image.
15. The method of claim 13 wherein the transaction card feature disposed within the pocket of the transaction card is coplanar with the first surface of the transaction card.
16. The method of claim 10 further comprising the step of disposing an adhesive material within the pocket.
17. The method of claim 16 wherein the adhesive material comprises polyvinyl chloride.
18. The method of claim 10 wherein the at least one metal layer comprises titanium.
