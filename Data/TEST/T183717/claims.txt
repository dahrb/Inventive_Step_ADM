Amended claims - Main Request

1. A position detection system (100), comprising:
a detection area (104);
at least one source (110; 112) of electromagnetic radiation for outputting an
electromagnetic radiation over at least a portion of the detection area (104);
an unpinned prismatic film (108) positioned along a periphery of at least a
portion of the detection area (104), comprising:
a retroreflective substrate (132) including a plurality of triangular cube
corner retroreflective elements (140) and a metallized layer (142) disposed
over at least a portion of the retroreflective substrate (132),
wherein base edges (210) of the cube corner retroreflective elements
(140) are linear and in a common plane perpendicular to the detection area
(104), and wherein each cube corner retroreflective element (140) forms an
isosceles triangle cube shape with two of the base edges (210) being
approximately the same length,
wherein the cube corner retroreflective elements (140) have a cant ina
range of 8 to 35 degrees in a face-more-parallel direction and a cube depth
between 25.4 and 304.8 micrometers (0.001 and 0.012 inches) in relation to
the common plane, and
wherein a principal reflected ray is nominally perpendicular to the common
plane, and a cube corner retroreflective element (140) is face-more-parallel or
edge-more-parallel depending upon whether the face angle with respect to
the principal reflected ray that is most different from 35.26° is respectively
greater or less than 35.26° so that for a face-more-parallel cube one cube
face (202) is more parallel to the common plane than any face of an uncanted
cube, and wherein the cube corner retroreflective elements (140) are joined
together in one or more tiles and a retroreflective sheeting with one tile or
multiple tiles all having the same cube corner orientation is referred to as
unpinned; and
at least one camera (117; 118) positioned to receive electromagnetic radiation
reflected from the prismatic film (108).
