TITLE:
Money tape used in cassette - with different denomination segments retained usable in lash-point machine

TYPE:
German patent

ABSTRACT:
The cash handling system replaces coins in transactions. The system has a money tape (1) in which specific value elements (4) are secured by overlapping edges of the tape. Each element represents a specific denomination value and one tape may carry more than one denomination. Two such tapes can be wound onto spools in a cassette that has displays to indicate the amount. The cassette is inserted into a reader for any transaction to take place. The cassette may be used together with an identification code. ADVANTAGE - Increased security. Can be automatically counted.
