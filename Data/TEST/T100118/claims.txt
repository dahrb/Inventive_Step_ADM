MAIN REQUEST

1. Coins assembly (20), for use in a vending de-
vice for vending coins (2,3,4..., 21,25,26...), comprising
coins for use as value-representing coins in a closed pay-
ment environment, comprising a number of mutually distin-
guishable coins (2,3,4..., 21,25,26...), wherein mutually
adjacent coins are connected to each other in a manner
such that they are ordered in mutually separable manner
relative to each other, characterised in that the material
of the coins (2,3,4..., 21,25,26...) form the assembly be-
ing formed by means of an extrusion process and is suita-
ble for placing in the vending device for dispensing a
variable number of tokens input into the dispensing de-
vice, wherein the coins roll are formed from a plastic ma-
terial and have a thickness usual for tokens of for in-
stance 0.1-5 millimetres and the assembly is arranged in

the form of a roll.

2. Coins assembly as claimed in claim 1, wherein
the separable transition between adjacent coins is formed
by a notch (12) in the material from which the assembly is

manufactured.

3. Coins assembly as claimed in claim 2, wherein
the notch (12) is arranged on the inner side of the roll

in the rolled-up position of the roll.

4. Coins assembly as claimed in one or more of the
foregoing claims 2-3, wherein the notch (12) is made by
means of a cutting operation or a punching operation

through a part of the thickness of the material.
