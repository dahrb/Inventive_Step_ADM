CLAIMS

1. A method of defining a testing procedure for collecting biomarker values, the method

comprising the steps of:

determining similar biomarker values based on contextualized data associated with received

biomarker values ;

determining a first criterion based on the similar biomarker values and determining a testing
procedure, wherein said testing procedure includes a schedule of testing events for collecting

biomarker values ;

calculating an expected value based on similar biomarker samples ;
determining a second criterion based on said calculated expected value ;

defining a structured collection protocol or testing procedure according to said second

criterion.

2. The method of claim 1, further comprising the step of modifying the first criterion.

3. The method of claims 1 or 2, further comprising the step of modifying the first criterion

according to said second criterion.

4. The method of any one of the preceding claims, wherein the testing procedure is performed

until the second criterion is satisfied.

5. The method of any one of the preceding claims, wherein the first criterion is an entry

criterion and wherein the second criterion is an exit criterion.

6. The method of claim 1, wherein the method comprises the steps of:
receiving prior biomarker samples,

wherein said prior biomarker samples comprise at least one value based on a

measurement of a body fluid and
