TITLE:
Hand held electronic device with multiple touch sensing devices

TYPE:
US Patent

ABSTRACT:
Hand held devices with multiple touch sensing devices are disclosed. The touch sensing devices may for example be selected from touch panels, touch screens or touch sensitive housings.

CLAIMS:
1. A hand held electronic device, comprising:
a first touch sensing device located in a first region of the hand held electronic device; and
a second touch sensing device located in a second region of the hand held electronic device, the second region being at a different location than the first region of the hand held electronic device.
2. The hand held electronic device as recited in claim 1 further comprising a display that forms a viewing region of the hand held electronic device, and wherein the first touch sensing device is a touch screen positioned over the display in the viewing region of the display.
3. The hand held electronic device as recited in claim 2 further comprising a second display that forms a second viewing region of the hand held electronic device, and wherein the second touch sensing device is a second touch screen positioned over the second display in the second viewing region of the second display.
4. The hand held electronic device as recited in claim 3 wherein the first display is positioned on a first side of the hand held electronic device, and wherein the second display is positioned on a second side of the hand held electronic device, the second side being located opposite the first side.
5. The hand held electronic device as recited in claim 4 wherein the first side is the front side of the hand held electronic device, and wherein the second side is the back side of the hand held electronic device.
6. The hand held electronic device as recited in claim 2 wherein the second touch sensing device is a touch sensitive surface located on the hand held electronic device in a region other than the viewing region of the display.
7. The hand held electronic device as recited in claim 6 wherein the display is positioned on a first side of the hand held electronic device, and wherein the second touch sensing device is positioned on a second side of the hand held electronic device, the second side being located opposite the first side.
8. The hand held electronic device as recited in claim 6 wherein the touch sensitive surface is provided by a touch panel.
9. The hand held electronic device as recited in claim 8 wherein the touch panel is a touch pad.
10. The hand held electronic device as recited in claim 6 wherein the touch sensitive surface is formed by a touch sensitive housing.
11. The hand held electronic device as recited in claim 1 wherein the first touch sensing device is located on a first side of the housing, and wherein the second touching device is located on a second side of the housing, the second side being opposite the first side.
12. The hand held electronic device as recited in claim 1 further comprising a visual feedback system capable of providing visual stimuli to a touch surface of at least one of the touch sensing devices.
13. The hand held electronic device as recited in claim 12 wherein the visual stimuli is mapped to the sensor elements of the touch surface.
14. The hand held electronic device as recited in claim 12 wherein the at least one of the touch sensing devices is selected from a touchpad or touch sensitive housing of the hand held electronic device.
15. A hand held electronic device, comprising:
a housing;

a touch sensing device located on a second side of the housing, the second side being opposite the first side.

17. The hand held electronic device as recited in claim 15 wherein the sensing nodes of the touch sensing device are mapped to the display.

27. The hand held device as recited in claim 18 wherein the hand held device includes a front surface, and wherein the front surface does not include any physical actuators.
28. The hand held device as recited in claim 18 further comprising a visual feedback system capable of providing visual stimuli to a touch surface of at least one touch sensing device.

a housing; and
a sensor arrangement disposed underneath an outer surface of the housing, the sensor arrangement being configured to detect the presence of an object on at least a portion of the housing.
30. The handheld device as recited in claim 29 wherein the sensor arrangement covers the entire housing so that the entire housing is touch sensitive.
31. The handheld device as recited in claim 29 wherein the sensor arrangement is a multipoint sensor arrangement capable of detecting the presence of more than one object at the same time.

34. A method for operating a handheld device, comprising:
determining if a user is touching the handheld device;
determining the user based on the user's touch;
configuring the handheld device based on the user.
35. The method as recited in claim 34 wherein the step of determining the user includes determining the handedness of the user.
36. The method as recited in claim 34 wherein the step of determining the user includes determining the identity of the user.
37. The method as recited in claim 34 wherein the step of configuring the handheld device includes setting the buttons of the handheld device.
38. The method as recited in claim 37 wherein the button settings are based on the handedness of the user.
39. The method as recited in claim 37 wherein the button settings are based on the identity of the user.
40. The method as recited in claim 34 wherein the buttons are based on relative mapping or absolute mapping.
41. The method as recited in claim 40 wherein absolute mapping includes, providing one or more button zones;
generating a hand signal;
determining which button zone was selected based on the hand signal; and
generating a button signal based on the selected button zone.
42. The method as recited in claim 40 wherein relative mapping includes, generating a first hand signal;
generating a second hand signal;
determining the difference between the first and second hand signals; and
generating a button signal based on the difference between the first and second hand signals.
43. A user determination method for a handheld device, comprising:
providing baseline hand signals;
generating a current hand signal when a user grabs the handheld device;
comparing the current hand signal to at least one baseline hand signal; and
determining a characteristic of the user based on the current and baseline hand signals.
44. The method as recited in claim 43 wherein the step of determining includes determining if the current hand signal is similar to one of the baseline hand signals.
45. The method as recited in claim 43 wherein the step of determining includes determining if the current hand signal is different than one of the baseline hand signals.
46. The method as recited in claim 43 wherein the baseline hand signals correspond to right and left hand signals, and wherein the characteristic of the user corresponds to the handedness of the user.
47. The method as recited in claim 43 wherein the baseline hand signals correspond to a particular user's hand signal, and wherein the characteristic of the user corresponds to the identity of the user.
