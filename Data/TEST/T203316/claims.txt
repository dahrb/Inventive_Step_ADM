A display system, comprising:

a pixel circuit (24a, 44a, 64a, 84a) including a light emitting device (106), a driving
transistor for driving the light emitting device (106), the driving transistor (102) being
coupled between a power supply (110) and the light emitting device (106), and a switch
transistor (104) having a gate terminal, a first terminal and a second terminal, wherein one
of the first and second terminals of the switch transistor is coupled to the gate terminal of
the driving transistor and the other one of the first and second terminals is connected to a

common node (A01) of the driving transistor and the light emitting device;

a gate driver (28, 48, 68, 88) adapted to drive the switch transistor in the pixel circuit via

an address line (30, 50, 70, 90) connected to the gate terminal of the switch transistor;

a capacitor (14, 46, 86, 108) coupled between a data line (32, 52, 92) and a gate terminal
of the driving transistor; and

a ramp voltage generator (12) coupled to the data line and adapted to provide a time-
variant voltage to the capacitor, where the capacitor converts the time-variant voltage to a

current,
characterized by

a controller (29, 49, 69, 89) adapted to control and schedule programming and driving of
the pixel circuit by controlling the generation of the time-variant voltage in the ramp voltage

generator and the operation of the gate driver such that:

during a first stage (122) of a programming cycle (120) of the pixel circuit, the ramp voltage
generator provides the time-variant voltage to the capacitor and the address line (90) is
operated to switch the switch transistor on so that a bias current is provided by the

capacitor via the switch transistor; and

during a second stage (124) of said programming cycle (120), applying a programming
voltage to the data line while the switch transistor is off so as to store said programming

voltage in the capacitor;
