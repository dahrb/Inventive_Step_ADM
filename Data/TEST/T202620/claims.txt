(350) for power generation to form an expanded CO: containing stream;

injecting at least a portion of the CO» containing stream into the formation including

formation and at least a portion of the CO2 stream flow from the formation and into a
recovery well (200), wherein the CO2 containing stream injected into the formation

receiving from the recovery well (206) a recovery stream (251) comprising the fuel
material and the COz;
