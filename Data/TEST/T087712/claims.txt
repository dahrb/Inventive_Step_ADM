MAIN REQUEST

1. A laryngoscope (1) comprising a body portion (2) and a blade portion (3), the blade
portion (3) being separable from the body portion (2), and the laryngoscope (1) being
provided with a spoiling means, the spoiling means comprising a transmitting means and a
receiving means in the body (2), for sending and receiving signals respectively to and from a
receiving and transmitting means in the blade (3), and the spoiling means being adapted to
indicate if the blade portion has already been attached to the said body portion (2) or to
another body portion (2) comprising a transmitting means and receiving means for sending
and receiving signals respectively to and from the receiving and transmitting means in the

blade (3) previously.

2. A laryngoscope according to Claim 1, wherein the spoiling means will prevent
reattachment of a blade (3) that has already been attached to any said body portion (2)

previously.

3. A laryngoscope according to Claim 1, wherein the spoiling means will alert a user to a

blade that has already been attached to any said body portion previously.

4. A laryngoscope according to any preceding claim, wherein the receiving means in the
body (2) can determine from the transmitting means in the blade (3), if the blade has

previously been brought into close proximity of any said body portion previously.

5. A laryngoscope according to any preceding claim, wherein the transmitting means is a

radio frequency transmitter.

6. A laryngoscope according to any preceding claim, wherein the receiving means is a radio

frequency receiver.

7. A laryngoscope according to any preceding claim, wherein the receiving means in the

body (2) will count the number of blades it contacts with.

8. A laryngoscope according to any preceding claim, wherein the spoiling means comprises

a device for tripping electrical contacts to prevent their continued use.

9. A laryngoscope according to any preceding claim, wherein the spoiling means comprises
elements that cause a physical change if the blade portion has already been attached to the

body portion previously.

10. A laryngoscope according to any preceding claim, wherein the transmitting and receiving
means in the body (2) comprises a mother chip (4) and the transmitting and receiving means
in the blade (3) comprises a radio frequency tag (5), the radio frequency tag being provided
with a unique serial number, and the mother chip being adapted, when the radio frequency
tag and the mother chip are brought into close contact, to read the serial number and to

indicate to the user if the serial number is recognised from a previous occasion.

11. Alaryngoscope according to any preceding claim, wherein the transmitting and receiving
means in the body (2) comprises a mother chip (4) and the transmitting and receiving means
in the blade (3) comprises a radio frequency tag (5), and wherein the mother chip is adapted,
when the radio frequency tag and the mother chip are brought into close contact, to mark
electronically the radio frequency tag so that it can be recognised as having previously been

in contact with the said body portion.

12. A laryngoscope according to any one of Claims 1 and 3—11, wherein the spoiling means

is adapted to allow a certain number of re-uses of the blade.

13. A laryngoscope according to any one of Claims 1 and 3-12, wherein the spoiling means

is adapted to allow at least one re-use of the blade.
