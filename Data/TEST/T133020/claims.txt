Main Request

1. A dissolving microneedle DNA rabies virus vaccine composition, which elicits in an animal a
protective immune response against subsequent virulent rabies virus challenge, comprising a vector
expressing a rabies antigen, wherein the vector is a plasmid DNA vector or an attenuated recombinant

viral vector, wherein the composition is stable for at least three weeks at 4 °C.

2. The vaccine composition of claim 1, wherein the rabies antigen is rabies G protein.

3. The vaccine composition of claim 2, wherein the rabies G protein has the amino acid sequence as
set forth in SEQ ID NO: 1.

4. The vaccine composition of any one of claims 1 to 3, wherein the plasmid DNA vector is present in

an amount between about 5 ug and 50 ug.

5. The vaccine composition of any one of claims 1 to 3, wherein the viral vector is vaccinia, canarypox,

fowlpox, orthopox or avipox virus.

6. The vaccine composition of any of claims 1 to 5, which provides sufficient immunostimulation to
protect from subsequent challenge greater than 80% of the animals receiving a single dose, and
greater than 95% of the animals receiving a second, booster dose.

7. The vaccine composition of any one of claims 1 to 6, wherein the microneedles dissolve in the
animal's skin, thereby releasing the composition, in less than about 15 minutes, preferably in less than

about 10 minutes, more preferably in less than about 5 minutes.

8. The vaccine composition of any one of claims 2 to 7, wherein the vaccinated animals produce more
than twice as many serum-neutralizing antibodies as animals vaccinated with an identical amount of
the vector expressing the rabies G protein, but delivered as a single injection, instead of as a

microneedle vaccine composition.

9. The vaccine composition of any one of claims 1 to 8 for use in a method for eliciting a protective
immune response against rabies in an animal, wherein the vaccine composition additionally comprises

at least one polymer and at least one sugar.
10. The vaccine composition for use according to claim 9, wherein the method comprises the step of

placing the composition on the animal's skin, thereby piercing the animal’s skin with the microneedles,

and releasing the vaccine.

11. The vaccine composition for use according to claim 9 or claim 10, wherein the method is a dose-
sparing method wherein the composition contains less than one-half of the amount of antigen required

to elicit a protective immune response using a conventional single needle administration.

12. The vaccine composition for use according to claim 11, wherein the vector comprises a modified

live vaccinia virus.

13. The vaccine composition for use according to claim 12, wherein the rabies G protein is derived

from an ERA strain.

14. The vaccine composition for use according to claim 13, wherein the vaccinia virus is a

Copenhagen strain and/or has a tk— phenotype.

15. The vaccine composition for use according to any one of claims 12 to 14, wherein the modified live
vaccinia virus is RABORAL V-RG®.

16. The vaccine composition for use according to any one of claims 9 to 15, wherein a first dose is
followed by a second booster dose, of the same composition as the first dose, administered to the

animal about 28 days after the first dose.

17. The vaccine composition for use according to any one of claims 9 to 16, wherein the rabies G

protein is encoded by a polynucleotide having the sequence as set forth in SEQ ID NO: 2.

18. The vaccine composition for use according to any one of claims 9 to 17, wherein the animal is a
Canidae, such as a dog, a bitch or a puppy, a Felidae, such as a cat or a kitten, Homo sapiens or a

Suidae, such as a sow, a pig or a piglet.

19. The vaccine composition for use according to any one of claims 9 to 18, wherein the animal is
protected against rabies for at least about one year after administration of the composition, preferably

wherein the animal is protected against rabies for at least about three years.

20. The vaccine composition for use according to any one of claims 9 to 19, wherein the dose is
administered by hand-pressure application of a microneedle patch, and in a dose range of about 10 ug
to about 50 ug, preferably wherein the dose is about 30 ug to about 50 ug, more preferably wherein
the dose is about 50 yg.

21. The vaccine composition for use according to any one of claims 9 to 20, wherein the method is a
prime-boost method comprising administering the vaccine composition to the animal on two separate
occasions, preferably wherein the two administrations provide protection against rabies for at least one

year following the second administration of the vaccine .
