What is Claimed:
1. Meter electronics (120) for a flow measurement apparatus, said meter
electronics (120) comprising:

a processing system, characterized in that the processing system is
configured to:

sample a signal representing flow information generated by said flow
measurement apparatus during a zero flow state of said flow measurement
apparatus to define a plurality of data points representing said signal;

establish deviation limits for at least one of said plurality of data points based
on a plurality of previously sampled data points;

determine whether each data point is within said deviation limits;

sample a data point within said deviation limits to define spurious flow
information for said zero flow state;

sample a data point outside of said deviation limits to define information
representing a true material flow of said flow measurement apparatus;

prevent said spurious flow information from being applied as to an output of
said flow measurement apparatus during the sampling of data points within
deviation limits;

determine that a data point that was sampled latest in time relative to other
sampled data points is outside of said deviation limits and thereby represents
information for a true material flow of said flow measurement apparatus; and

generate an output signal representing said true material flow information
represented by said data point that was sampled latest in time relative to other

sampled data points.

2. The meter electronics (120) of claim 1 characterized in that said flow

measurement apparatus defines a Coriolis flowmeter.

3. The meter electronics (120) of claims 1 or 2 characterized in that said
processing system is configured to:

specify a low flow cutoff limit representing a material flow below which said
flow measurement apparatus will not generate an output signal representing a true

material flow;
