CLAIMS

1. A portable USB autorun device operable to communicate with a computer terminal,
the device including a USB microcontroller; and in which the autorun device is adapted to be
detachable from a surrounding support by an end-user to enable a USB interface plug on the
autorun device to be inserted into a female USB receptacle in the computer terminal, and
wherein the support is detachably connected to sections of the edge of the autorun device and
the edge thickness of those sections of the device is substantially the same as the edge
thickness of adjacent portions of the surrounding support and wherein the device is operable

to cause the computer terminal to auto-connect to a web site.

2. The portable USB autorun device of Claim 1 in which the device is die-cut from the

surrounding support.

3. The portable USB autorun device of any preceding Claim in which the sections of the

perimeter of the device exclude a USB interface plug portion of the device.

4, The portable USB autorun device of any preceding Claim, in which the device include

portions made of paper, card or plastic.

5. The portable USB autorun device of any preceding Claim, in which the surrounding

support comprises card or plastic.

6. The portable USB autorun device of any preceding Claim in which the device is
printed with graphics.
7. The portable USB autorun device of any preceding Claim in which the surrounding

support is printed with graphics.

8. The portable USB autorun device of any preceding Claim which is operable to cause

the computer terminal to auto-connect to a web site and open a specific page in that web site.

9. The portable USB autorun device of any preceding Claim which initialises or presents
itself as a HID keyboard and then sends to the terminal a first predefined sequence of

keycodes automatically without manual interaction; the keycodes complying with HID

10. The portable USB autorun device of any preceding Claim which, following connection

user can be identified.
