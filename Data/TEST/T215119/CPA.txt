Title: Apparatus used in production of wood fiber-reinforced plastics granulate, for extrusion or injection molding e.g. to molding or structural component, has twin-screw extruder with 2 feed ports and underwater perforated plate granulator

Abstract: 
In wood fiber-reinforced plastics granulation apparatus with 2-feed compounder, underwater perforated plate granulator, driven cutter for plasticized strands, water flow chamber and granulate separator, compounder is extruder (10) with 2 screws turning in same direction, each with length/diameter ratio 44-48; and apparatus can be heated by passing water at >= 80[deg]C through cutter chamber (20) and has centrifugal separator (22) beside or above this. An independent claim is also included for process for producing a granulate from wood fiber-reinforced plastics.

Type: Germany Patent
