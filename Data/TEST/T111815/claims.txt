Main request

CLAIMS

1. A threadable fiber optic connector (32) being configured to be connectable with a
separate non-threadable fiber optic adapter (26) and a separate threadable fiber optic
adapter (34), the non-threadable fiber optic adapter and the threadable fiber optic
adapter each including at least one retention latch (376, 250), the threadable fiber optic
connector (32) comprising:

a ferrule (100) aligned along a central longitudinal axis (A1);

a connector housing (39) including a plug portion (56) having an interface end
sized to fit within the non-threadable fiber optic adapter (26) and the threadable fiber
optic adapter (34), the ferrule (100) being positioned at the interface end of the plug
portion (56), the plug portion (56) having a first catch structure (55) for engaging the
retention latch of the non-threadable fiber optic adapter (26) to retain the plug portion
(56) within the non-threadable fiber optic adapter (26) and a second catch structure
(132, 134) for engaging the retention latch of the threadable fiber optic adapter (34) to
retain the plug portion (56) within the threadable fiber optic adapter (34); and

a threadable retention member (40) mounted on the connector housing (39), the
threadable retention member (40) engaging the threadable fiber optic adapter (34) to
retain the plug portion (56) within the threadable fiber optic adapter (34),
wherein the threadable fiber optic adapter (34) includes threads (76), and wherein the
threadable retention member (40) includes a threaded member (75) rotatably mounted
about the connector housing (39) that engages the threads (76)of the threadable fiber
optic adapter (34) to retain the plug portion (56) within the threadable fiber optic adapter
(34).

2. The threadable fiber optic connector of claim 1, wherein the non-threadable fiber
optic adapter includes a pair of the retention latches, and wherein the first catch
structure includes first and second catches (55) that engage the pair of the retention
latches.

3. The threadable fiber optic connector of claim 2, wherein the plug portion has a
first side and an opposite second side, wherein the plug portion also has a third side and
an opposite fourth side, the third and fourth sides extending between the first and
second sides, the central longitudinal axis being positioned between the first and second
sides and the central longitudinal axis also being positioned between the third and fourth
sides, the first catch of the first catch structure being provided at the first side, the
second catch of the first catch structure being provided at the second side, and the
second catch structure being provided at the third side.

4. The threadable fiber optic connector of claims 1-3, wherein the plug portion has a
generally rectangular cross-sectional shape.

5. The threadable fiber optic connector of claims 3 and 4, wherein the first and
second catches of the first catch structure are formed by recesses (55) provided at the

first and second sides of the plug portion of the connector housing.

6. The threadable fiber optic connector of claim 5, wherein the recesses are defined
at least in part by recessed latch engagement surfaces (57) that engage the retention
latches of the non-threadable fiber optic adapter to retain the plug portion within the non-
threadable fiber optic adapter, the recessed latch engagement surfaces being angled
relative to the central longitudinal axis such that the recessed latch engagement
surfaces extend radially outwardly relative to the central longitudinal axis as the
recessed latch engagement surfaces extend in a direction of insertion of the threadable
fiber optic connector.

7. The threadable fiber optic connector of claims 3-6, wherein the second catch
structure includes a projection (132) provided at the third side of the plug portion of the

connector housing.

8. The threadable fiber optic connector of claim 7, wherein the projection includes a
protruding latch engagement surface (254) that engages the retention latch of the
threadable fiber optic adapter to retain the plug portion within the threadable fiber optic
adapter, the protruding latch engagement surface being angled relative to the central
longitudinal axis such that the protruding latch engagement surface extends radially
outwardly relative to the central longitudinal axis as the protruding latch engagement
surface extends in the direction of insertion of the threadable fiber optic connector.

9. The threadable fiber optic connector of claims 6-8, wherein the fourth side of the
connector housing defines a connector alignment slot (133) for rotationally aligning the
plug portion within the threadable fiber optic adapter, the connector alignment slot
extending in a direction parallel to the central longitudinal axis and including a tapered
portion (137) that widens as the connector alignment slot extends in the direction of

10. The threadable fiber optic connector of claims 1-9, wherein the second catch

12. The threadable fiber optic connector of claim 11, wherein locking portions (390)

of the locking member are wedge shaped.

13. The threadable fiber optic connector of claims 1-12, wherein a sealing member
(49) is used to provide a seal between the threadable fiber optic connector and the

threadable fiber optic adapter.
14. The threadable fiber optic connector of claims 1-13, further comprising a

converter (190) for making the threadable fiber optic connector backward compatible
with a second threadable fiber optic adapter (188).
