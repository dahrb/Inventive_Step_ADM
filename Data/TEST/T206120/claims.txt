CLAIMS

A method of manufacturing an automatic dishwashing detergent product, said product
being provided as a discrete dosage unit and comprising a continuous, non-aqueous
gel phase, comprising manufacturing said gel phase by a method comprising free
radical polymerisation of monomer(s) in a non-aqueous reaction mixture to form a
polymeric builder without an intermediate step of drying the reaction mixture to form
a solid comprising the polymeric builder;
wherein the polymeric builder is in a dissolved state in the non-aqueous gel phase,
wherein said reaction mixture comprises:

- 10 wt.% to 50 wt.% of at least one non-ionic surfactant,

- 10 wt.% to 60 wt.% of at least one non-water solvent; and

- one or more Monomers comprising acrylic acid
wherein no more than 0.1 wt. % of the total amount of the monomer(s) are

crosslinking monomer(s).

Method according to claim 1, wherein said at least one non-ionic surfactant is an end-
capped alcohol alkoxylate, preferably wherein said alcohol alkoxylate non-ionic
surfactant is liquid at 20 °C and/or comprises ethoxylate groups;

more preferably wherein said surfactant is of the formula R1-O-(EO)x(AO)y-
R4, in which: R1 is an alkyl group; R4 is H or an optionally substituted alkyl group; x
is 1 to 100; y is 0 to 100; EO is ethylene oxide; and each AO, when present, is
independently propylene oxide or butylene oxide; wherein x and y represent the
average degrees of respective alkoxylation per molecule, and the order of EO and AO
groups may be varied but the sum of x and y is at least 3; preferably wherein:

i) if y is non-zero, x is greater than y, preferably at least double y;

ii) if AO is propylene oxide, y is 18 or less, preferably 17 or less, preferably
15 or less, preferably 10 or less; and/or

iit) R1 is an alkyl group and R4 is H or an optionally alkoxylated alkyl group,
preferably wherein R1 is an alkyl group with at least 3 C atoms, preferably at least 5

C atoms.

Method according to claim 1 or claim 2, wherein said one or more monomers

comprise one or more further a,B8-ethylenically unsaturated acids, preferably a
