Title: Process for preparing functionalized polymeric compositions

Abstract: Functionalized polymeric compositions are prepared by polymerizing ethylenically unsaturated functional monomer in a solvent including a reactive compound. The functional groups of the reactive compound and the functional monomer react during or subsequent to the polymerization of the functional monomer. The reactive compound can be a hydroxy-terminated, poly(ethyleneoxy)ated hydrophobe-bearing compound. The functional monomer can be a polymerizable carboxylated monomer, the functionalization in this case being a condensation. Removal of the water of condensation by azeotropic distillation drives the reaction to completion. The resulting high solids tunctionalized polymeric compositions are soluble or dispesible in an appropriate solvent, and can be used as is, diluted with a solvent, or isolated. They can be used as builders in detergent compositions, as pigment dispersants in coating compositions, as tanning agents for leather, as associative thickeners and rheology modifiers for coatings compositions.

Claims: 

1. A process for preparing a functionalized polymeric composition which comprises dissolving an ethylenically unsaturated monomer containing a first functional group in a solvent including a compound containing a second functional group, polymerizing the monomer, and reacting the first and second functional groups to provide functionalized side chains, some of the reacting taking place during the polymerizing.
2. A process according to claim 1 wherein no substantial reaction of the first and second functional groups occur before the polymerization of the monomer has begun.
3. A process according to claim 1 or claim 2 including dissolving an ethylenically unsaturated nonfunctional monomer in the solvent, the nonfunctional monomer being copolymerizable with the first functional group and substantially nonreactive with the second functional group.
4. A process according to any preceding claim wherein the first and second functional groups react in a condensation reaction to yield water.
5. A process according to claim 4 wherein the solvent includes an organic compound which forms an azeotrope with water, and the azeotrope is distilled from the reaction mixture to remove the water.
6. A process according to claim 5 wherein the organic compound is toluene, xylene, or methyl amyl ketone.
7. A process according to any preceding claim wherein the first functional group is carboxyl or carboxylic acid anhydride.
8. A process according to any preceding claim wherein the second functional group is hydroxyl or primary or secondary amino.
9. A process according to any preceding claim wherein the reactive compound includes both a hydrophobic portion and a hydrophilic portion.
10. A process according to claim 9, the hydrophilic portion including a poly(alkyleneoxy) group, the hydrophobic portion a (C₁-C₂₂) hydrocarbyl group.
11. A process according to any preceding claim including adding a chain transfer agent to the reactive mixture.
12. A process according to claim 11 wherein the chain transfer agent includes a second functional group.
13. A process according to claim 11 wherein the chain transfer agent is isopropanol, benzyl alcohol, a mono (C₁-C₄)alkyl ester of ethyleneglycol or a mono (C₁-C₄)alkyl ester of diethylene glycol.
14. A process according to claim 1 wherein the first functional group is isocyanato, and the second functional group is selected from primary and secondary hydroxyl and primary and secondary amino.
15. A process according to claim 1 wherein the first functional group is epoxy, and the second functional group is hydroxyl, carboxyl, or tertiary amino.
16. A process according to claim 1 wherein the first functional group is tertiary amino, the second functional group is epoxy, reactive halo in alkyl halide, or reactive sulfate in alkyl sulfate.
