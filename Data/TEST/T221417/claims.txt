Main Request

1. An antibody or antibody fragment for use in a method of reducing cholesterol level or
maintaining previously reduced cholesterol level ina human in need thereof, wherein the antibody is
evolocumab and said human comprises (i) an |GHG2*01 human heavy chain constant region gene
segment and (ii) a nucleotide sequence encoding proprotein convertase subtilisin/kexin type 9 (PCSK9)

that comprises a C-terminal domain comprising a mutation E670G in SEQ ID NO: 1.

2. The antibody or antibody fragment for use according to claim 1, the method comprising, before
administration of said antibody or fragment, selecting a human comprising said nucleotide sequence of

(ii), wherein the human is the human of claim 1.

3. The antibody or antibody fragment for use according to claim 1 or claim 2, wherein the human
has been determined to comprise the nucleotide sequence that encodes a PCSK9 comprising a C-
terminal domain comprising said mutation E670G in SEQ ID NO: 1 and/or a proprotein convertase

subtilisin/kexin type 9 (PCSK9) variant protein encoded by the nucleotide sequence of SEQ ID NO: 30.

comprising the step of determining that the human comprises the nucleotide sequence that encodes a

subtilisin/kexin type 9 (PCSK9) variant protein comprising said mutation E670G, optionally, wherein the

determining step is performed before administration of the antibody to the human.

determining comprises assaying a biological sample from the human for a nucleotide sequence
encoding the PCSK9 that comprises the C-terminal domain comprising the mutation E670G in SEQ ID
NO: 1.
