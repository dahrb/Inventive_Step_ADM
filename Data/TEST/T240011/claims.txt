transmission through the pitch side optical fiber (126); and

for the one diode laser light to develop;

2. A sensing apparatus of claim 1 wherein the length of the transmission
optical fiber is selected to maintain mode noise below a select level.

3. A sensing apparatus of claim 1 wherein the un-multiplexed input

optical fiber.

4. A sensing apparatus of claim 2 wherein the length of the transmission
optical fiber is equal to or less than three meters.

5. A sensing apparatus of claims 1 through 4 further comprising a motor
having a shaft substantially parallel to the catch side multimode optical fiber,
which is attached to said fiber for providing a twisting motion around the
longitudinal axis to minimize catch side mode noise.

6. A sensing apparatus of claim 5 wherein the catch side multimode
optical fiber has a longitudinal axis parallel to the fiber length and the
mechanical manipulation comprises twisting the catch side multimode optical
fiber around the longitudinal axis.

7. A sensing apparatus of claim 5 wherein the means to mechanically
manipulate the section of the catch side multimode optical fiber comprises a
motor operatively associated with the catch side multimode optical fiber such

10. A sensing apparatus of claim 1 through 4 further comprising a catch

providing for the alignment of the catch optic with respect to a direction of the

11. A sensing apparatus of claim 10 wherein the catch side alignment

12. A sensing apparatus of claim 11 wherein the means to tilt the catch
optic comprises a stepper motor.

13. A sensing apparatus of claim 10 further comprising: an alignment

side alignment mechanism, the data processing system receiving data from the
detector related to the strength of the alignment beam coupled to the detector
and further causing the catch side alignment mechanism to align the catch side
optics with the projection beam to maximize the strength of the alignment
beam coupled to the detector.

14, A sensing apparatus of claim 10 further comprising a pitch side
alignment mechanism providing for alignment of the pitch optic and

adjustment of the direction of the projection beam.
