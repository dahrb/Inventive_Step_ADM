This appeal is against the examining division's decision posted on 8 February 2021 refusing European patent application No. 17183144.9. The application was refused on the grounds that a main request and first and second auxiliary requests did not meet the requirements of Article 56 EPC in view of the disclosure of: D1: US 2008/184284, in combination with D2: EP 1 631 004 II. Notice of appeal was received on 8 April 2021, and the appeal fee was paid on the same date. The statement setting out the grounds of appeal was received on 9 June 2021. The appellant requested that the decision under appeal be set aside and a patent be granted based on the claims of the main request or the first or second auxiliary request on which the decision was based and which were refiled with the statement setting out the grounds of appeal. Oral proceedings were requested if the main request was not allowed. III. A summons to oral proceedings was issued on 5 July 2022. In a communication pursuant to Article 15(1) RPBA, sent on 17 May 2023, the board gave its preliminary opinion that the main request and the first and second auxiliary requests did not meet the requirements of Article 56 EPC in view of D1 in combination with D2. IV. By letter dated 30 June 2023, the appellant submitted third to sixth auxiliary requests. V. Oral proceedings were held on 17 July 2023. The appellant filed a new main request and a new first auxiliary request. The appellant then withdrew the main request and the first to sixth auxiliary requests filed during the written appeal proceedings and requested, after withdrawing the new first auxiliary request, that the decision under appeal be set aside and a patent be granted on the basis of the main request filed during oral proceedings before the board. The board's decision was announced at the end of the oral proceedings. VI. Claim 1 of the main (single) request reads as follows: "A set-top-box (501) for receiving broadcast media content and processing said content for presentation on a display, the set-top-box comprising one or more processors (304) for processing received broadcast data containing video and audio information and converting this information into video and audio streams for provision to a display; wherein the set-top-box further comprises: a modem circuit (718), or a connection (512) for connecting to a modem; a router circuit (719), or a connection (513) for connecting to a router; and a processor (511) configured to: - receive, from between the router circuit (718) or the connection (513) for connecting to the router, and the modem circuit (719) or the connection (512) for connecting to the modem, data packets, sent over a local network, to or from a local user device via the router circuit; - analyse said data packets by inspecting the payload of the data packets to determine whether they contain undesired content; and - if undesired content is found, to overlay an indicator containing at least one property of the undesired content on top of received media content being output by the set-top-box to the display." The main request contains further independent claims directed to a corresponding method (claim 13) and a corresponding computer program (claim 14).
