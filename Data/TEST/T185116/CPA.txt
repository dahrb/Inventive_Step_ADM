Title: 
Adaptive grip

Abstract: A shoe sole and stud assembly are provided in which studs are mounted on a spring member of the sole which provides cushioning to the user during use. In a preferred embodiment, the spring member (15-5) includes at least one primary stud (7-5) and one or more secondary studs (9-5, 11-5) which operate to engage with the ground when an impact force causes the primary stud to deform towards the sole of the shoe. In this way, additional grip can be provided on varying surface conditions.
