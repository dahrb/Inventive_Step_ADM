CLAIMS — Main Request

A resource management architecture including multiple resource providers (104) and a
resource manager (102), said resource management architecture being implemented by

a computing system, wherein:

said multiple resource providers (104) are associated with resources adapted to be

utilized by multiple consumers (32); and

said resource manager (102) is adapted to arbitrate access to the resources provided by
the resource providers on behalf of the consumers by using one of a plurality of resource
configurations that are specified for an activity (122) to be performed by said consumers,
wherein each of said plurality of resource configurations is adapted to successfully
complete the activity and wherein each resource configuration identifies at least one

resource provider of the multiple resource providers.
The architecture of claim 1, wherein said consumers are applications.

The architecture of any of claims 1 or 2, wherein the resource providers and the

resource manager reside at a kernel level.

The architecture of one of the claims 1 to 3, wherein at least one of the consumers

comprises an application program.

The architecture of one of the claims 1 to 4, wherein at least one resource provider is
adapted to act as a consumer of a resource provided by at least one other resource

provider.
