CLAIMS:

1. An input method for an electronic device using a pen input device, the
method comprising:

determining contact information of a contact point between the pen input
device and the electronic device; and

varying expressive effects of a pen input, based on the contact information.

2. The method of claim 1, further comprising receiving status information of
the pen input device, and wherein the determining contact information comprises

generating the contact information based on the received status information.

3. The method of claim 1,

further comprising detecting a contact point defined by an area of contact
between the pen input device and the electronic device;

wherein the determining contact information comprises generating the contact

information of the contact point.

4. The method of claim 3,

further comprising calculating status information of the pen input device,
based on the contact information; and

wherein the varying the expressive effects of the pen input is based on the

status information.

5. The method of claim 2 or 4, wherein the status information includes tilt

information of the pen input device.

6. The method of claim any preceding claim, wherein the contact information

includes at least one of a size of the contact point and a shape of the contact point.

7. The method of claim 6, wherein the contact information includes both the
size and shape of the contact point, the size increases as a function of the tilt of the pen

input device, and the shape changes from generally circular at a generally upright
