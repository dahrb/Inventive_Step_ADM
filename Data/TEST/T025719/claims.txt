CLAIMS
1. A colored structure applicable to a road surface to indicate a bicycle riding route along the
road, characterized in that the structure is formed by seven parallel painted strips of the rainbow
colors, which are arranged side-by-side and extend with identical widths in a longitudinal
direction along the road.
2. The colored structure of claim 1, characterized in that said seven parallel painted strips
comprise:
a) red color painted strip (R) disposed on the road surface ;
b) an orange color painted strip (O) disposed next to the red color painted strip on the road
surface;
c) a yellow color painted strip (Y) disposed next to the orange color painted strip on the road
surface;
d) a green color painted strip (G) disposed next to the yellow color painted strip on the road
surface;
e) a blue color painted strip (B) disposed next to the green color painted strip on the road surface;
f) an indigo color painted strip (1) disposed next to the blue color painted strip on the road
surface; and
g) a violet color painted strip (V) disposed next to the indigo color painted strip on the road
surface.
3. The colored structure of claim 2, wherein the width of each color painted strip is 300mm and

the total width of the bicycle riding route is 2100mm.
