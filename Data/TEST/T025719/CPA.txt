TITLE:
Tourist road

TYPE:
South Korean patent

ABSTRACT:
The present invention relates to a tourist road, the road that a conventional car, such as the road is black when the road surface is composed of, or when it is made of concrete is gray, the long road in such a road ride by car If you feel bored quickly and drowsiness was a problem.

In addition, there is no characteristic road tour course when foreigners come to Korea, and there is a problem that conventional roads cannot provide aesthetics, glamour, or various fun.

The tourist road of the present invention is to solve the above problems, and when foreign tourists travel to the country, it is interesting to see the tourist road and drive by car, or to get bored when the driver rides a car for a long time In order to prevent the configuration of the tourist road of the present invention is characterized in that different colors are formed for each of the plurality of intervals of the road surface continuous in the longitudinal direction.

CLAIMS:
And a road surface forming a tourist road and the road surface expressing various colors for each of a plurality of section distances. The tourist road as claimed in claim 1, wherein a picture member is formed on a plurality of section distance road surfaces. The road of claim 2, wherein the painting member can form various paintings, characters, cartoon characters, and other various paintings. The road surfaces constituting the tourist road are formed with paint lanes partitioning a plurality of driving lanes at predetermined intervals, and different colors are formed on the road surfaces of the plurality of driving lanes on the basis of the paint lanes. . The tourist road according to claim 4, wherein a picture member is formed on the road surface of the plurality of driving lanes. The tourist road as claimed in claim 5, wherein the figure member can alternatively form various figures, cartoon characters, and various other figures.
