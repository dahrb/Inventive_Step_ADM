TITLE:
Anti-theft device is for a fuel tank in or above ground or one which belongs to vehicle or boat

TYPE:
Dutch patent

ABSTRACT:
The anti-theft device (1) is for a fuel tank in or above the ground or one which belongs to a vehicle or boat. Mostly the device is fixed in, on or to the neck or filling aperture of the tank. It is provided with fixture devices (2) with one or more parts projecting out of its periphery, of which two, three or more are fitted over the outer periphery of the device. They are simple to produce and support up to a low integral weight of the device. The filling aperture of the tank is provided with screw thread or bayonet closures. The projecting parts can be cut or stamped out of the material of the device. These parts are suitabel for engagement with the screwthread devices on the filling aperture of the tank. They can also be welded onto the cylindrical outer periphery (3). Over the device can be fitted a closure cap (4). Preferably, the projecting parts are fixed by spot welding.
