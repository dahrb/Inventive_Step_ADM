The appellant lodged an appeal against the decision of the examining division refusing the European patent application. II. With the statement setting out the grounds of appeal, the appellant requested that the decision under appeal be set aside and that a patent be granted on the basis of the claims according to the main request or, in the alternative, one of the nine auxiliary requests, all filed with the statement of grounds of appeal. III. In a communication pursuant to Article 15(1) RPBA 2020 the board presented its preliminary assessment on certain aspects of the case. IV. With the letter dated 21 September 2023 the appellant provided further arguments and filed two further auxiliary requests. V. On 24 October 2023 oral proceedings took place during which the appellant filed a new main request and an adapted description and withdrew all other requests. VI. The appellant's sole and final request is that the appealed decision be set aside and that a patent be granted based on the claims of the new main request. VII.
