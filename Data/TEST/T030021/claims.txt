1.1 Main request 1.1 (Based on main request exam. item 14.4)

CLAIMS
1. An eye model (10) comprising an eye body (12) having:

a first flattened area (14) forming a cover surface of the eye model (10);

a sclera simulation which is made of a bright plastic material, the plastic material
containing a polyvinyl chloride at least as the main component, the sclera simulation
forming an annular surface in accordance with the shape of a spherical or ellipsoidal
surface, the sclera simulation surrounding the flattened area (14) and joined thereto;

a pattern (28) which contrasts in color with the sclera simulation, the
pattern simulating an eye pupil and/or an iris structure, wherein the pattern
(18) is situated on at least a portion of the flattened area (14) forming the
cover surface;

wherein the material of the sclera simulation is configured to appear
white, and the pattern (28) is configured to contrast in colour with the sclera
simulation under infrared illumination, and

a second flattened base area (20) on the side of the annular surface
opposite from the pattern, the region of the flattened base area (20) provided
with a threaded hole (30), the threaded hole used to arrange the eye model to

a positioning device (62).

2. The device according to Claim 1, wherein the plastic material is white in the

area of the sclera simulation.

3. The device according to Claim 1, wherein the transition from the annular

surface to the flattened area is formed by a ring-shaped edge (16).
4. The device according to one of Claims 1 to 2, wherein the pattern is formed
by imprinting or painting of the eye body, or by adhering to the eye body an adhesive

element which bears the pattern.

5. The device according to one of Claims 1 to 4, wherein the surface of the eye

body in the area of the sclera simulation forms a convexly curved annular surface.
