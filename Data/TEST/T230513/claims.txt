MAIN REQUEST

1. An electrophoretic medium (102; 202; 302) comprising a plurality of pigment
particles (108; 218; 320) suspended in a suspending fluid (106; 206), the pigment particles
having a polymer chemically bonded to the pigment particles (108; 218; 320), the
electrophoretic medium (102; 202; 302) being characterized by two types of pigment
particles (108; 218; 320) differing in at least one optical characteristic and having differing
electrophoretic mobilities and bearing charges of opposite polarity, both types of the
pigment particles (108; 218; 320) having from about 1 to about 15 per cent by weight of the

pigment, of the polymer chemically bonded to the pigment particles (108; 218; 320).

2. An electrophoretic medium (102; 202; 302) according to claim 1 characterized in that
the pigment particles (108; 218; 320) have from 4 to 15 per cent by weight of the pigment of

the polymer chemically bonded to the pigment particles.

3. An electrophoretic medium (102; 202; 302) according to claim 2 characterized in that
the pigment particles (108; 218; 320) have from 8 to 12 per cent by weight of the pigment of

the polymer chemically bonded to the pigment particles.

4. An electrophoretic medium according any one of the preceding claims characterized

in that the particles (108; 218; 320) comprise titania or carbon black.

5. An electrophoretic medium (102; 202; 302) according to any one of the preceding

claims characterized in that the polymer comprises charged or chargeable groups.

6. An electrophoretic medium (102; 202; 302) according to any one of the preceding
claims characterized in that charged or chargeable groups are bonded to the pigment

particles separately from the polymer.

7. An electrophoretic medium (102; 202; 302) according to any one of the preceding

claims, characterized in that the polymer comprises a main chain and a plurality of side

8. An electrophoretic medium (102; 202; 302) according to any one of the preceding

encapsulated in a plurality of capsules (104; 204; 304).

9. An electrophoretic medium (102; 202; 302) according to claim 8 characterized in that

the two types of particles (108; 218; 320) comprise titania and carbon black respectively.

10. An electrophoretic medium (102; 202; 302) according to claim 8 or 9 characterized in

that the capsules are held within a polymeric binder.

11. An electrophoretic medium (102; 202; 302) according to any one of claims 8 to 10

12. An electrophoretic display (100; 200; 300) comprising an electrophoretic medium (102;
202; 302) and at least one electrode (110; 112) arranged adjacent the medium and capable
of applying an electric field to the medium, the display being characterized in that the
electrophoretic medium (102; 202; 302) is a medium according to any one of the preceding

claims.
