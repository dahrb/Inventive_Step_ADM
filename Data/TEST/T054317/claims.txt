CLAIMS:

1. A method of forming a silicon carbide semiconductor device (110), comprising:

forming a silicon carbide epitaxial layer (140) on a surface of a silicon carbide substrate
(100), the epitaxial layer having a thickness greater than 3 um;

forming a semiconductor device at a first surface of the epitaxial layer (140) opposite the
silicon carbide substrate (100);

connecting a carrier substrate (105) to the first surface of the epitaxial layer (140);

removing the silicon carbide substrate (100) to expose a second surface of the epitaxial
layer (140) opposite the first surface while the carrier substrate (105) is providing mechanical
support to the epitaxial layer (140);

forming a metal layer on the second surface of the epitaxial layer (140); and

locally annealing the metal layer to form an ohmic contact (107) on the second surface
of the epitaxial layer (140), wherein locally annealing the metal layer comprises locally heating
the metal layer to a temperature that is sufficient to cause the metal layer to form the ohmic
contact (107) on the second surface of the epitaxial layer (140) but that is lower than a

temperature at which the carrier substrate (105) will detach from the epitaxial layer (140).

2. The method of claim 1 further comprising:

separating the epitaxial layer (140) from the carrier substrate (105).

3. The method of claim 2 further comprising:

dicing the epitaxial layer (140) to provide singulated semiconductor devices (150).

4. The method of any of claims 1 to 2 wherein removing the silicon carbide
substrate comprises grinding and/or lapping the silicon carbide substrate while the carrier

substrate (105) is providing mechanical support to the epitaxial layer (140).

5. The method of any of claims 1 to 2 wherein removing the silicon carbide
substrate comprises grinding the silicon carbide substrate using an in-feed and/or creep feed
grinder while the carrier substrate (105) is providing mechanical support to the epitaxial layer
(140).

6. The method of any of claims 1 to 2 wherein removing the silicon carbide
substrate comprises etching the silicon carbide substrate while the carrier substrate (105) is

providing mechanical support to the epitaxial layer (140).

7. The method of any of claims 1 to 2 wherein providing the semiconductor device
comprises forming a passivation layer on the first surface of the epitaxial layer (140), and
wherein connecting the carrier substrate (105) to the first surface of the epitaxial layer

comprises attaching the carrier substrate (105) to the passivation layer.

8. The method of claim 1 wherein heating the metal layer comprises laser annealing

the deposited metal layer.

9. The method of claim 1 wherein the epitaxial layer (140) comprises 4H SiC or 6H
SIC.

10. The method of claim 1 wherein heating the metal layer comprises directing an

electron beam at the metal layer.

11. The method of any of claims 1 to 2 further comprising forming a metal overlayer
on the ohmic contact (107), wherein the metal overlayer comprises an adhesion layer
comprising Ti, a barrier layer comprising Ni and/or Ti/W, and a bonding layer comprising Ag

and/or Au.
