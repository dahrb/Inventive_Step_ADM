TITLE:
Integrated circuit device having a built-in thermoelectric cooling mechanism

TYPE:
US patent

ABSTRACT:
An integrated circuit device having a built-in thermoelectric cooling mechanism is disclosed. The integrated circuit device includes a package and a substrate. Contained within the package, the substrate has a front side and a back side. Electric circuits are fabricated on the front side of the substrate, and multiple thermoelectric cooling devices are fabricated on the back side of the same substrate. The thermoelectric cooling devices are utilized to dissipate heat generated by the electric circuits to the package.

CLAIMS:
What is claimed is:
1. An integrated circuit device comprising:
a package; and
a substrate, contained within said package, having a front side and a back side which is opposite to the front side, wherein a plurality of electric circuits is fabricated on said front side of said substrate and a plurality of thermoelectric cooling devices is fabricated on said back side of said substrate, wherein said plurality of thermoelectric cooling devices dissipates heat generated by said plurality of electric circuits to said package and wherein there is no electrical contacts between said front side and said back side of said substrate.
2. The integrated circuit device of claim 1, wherein said plurality of thermoelectric cooling devices are Peltier devices.
3. The integrated circuit device of claim 1, wherein said plurality of thermoelectric cooling devices include a first conductive layer, a Peltier material layer, and a second conductive layer.
4. The integrated circuit device of claim 3, wherein said Peltier material layer is made of Bismuth Telluride.
5. The integrated circuit device of claim 3, wherein said Peltier material layer is made of Lead Telluride.
6. The integrated circuit device of claim 3, wherein said Peltier material layer is made of Chalcogenide.
7. The integrated circuit device of claim 3, wherein said integrated circuit device further includes a barrier layer between said first conductor layer and said substrate.
8. The integrated circuit device of claim 7, wherein said barrier layer is a nitride.
9. The integrated circuit of claim 7, wherein said barrier layer is a diamond-like carbon.
