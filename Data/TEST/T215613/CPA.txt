Title: 
Contact lens with prism

Abstract: 
The present invention aims to improve the optical performance of a contact lens, such as a bifocal contact lens or a toric contact lens, in which a lens is oriented by adding a prism. It is a thing. [Structure] Astigmatism generated by adding a prism is predicted in advance, and a toric surface having a refracting power and an axial direction sufficient to cancel it is configured as a front curve to cancel the astigmatism. In the front toric lens, astigmatism obtained by combining the astigmatism set in the front curve and the astigmatism for correcting the aberration caused by the prism is obtained, and the toric surface corresponding thereto is set in the front curve.

Type: Japan Patent

Claims: 

1. A contact lens having a prism for orienting the lens when worn, wherein a front curve forming an optical region of the contact lens has a rotation axis in a base direction of the prism or a direction perpendicular thereto. A contact lens with a prism, wherein a toric surface is formed to correct the aberration caused by the prism. 2. The contact lens with a prism according to claim 1, which is a multifocal contact lens. 3. The contact lens with a prism according to claim 1, which is a back toric lens. 4. A contact lens having a prism for orienting the lens at the time of wearing, wherein the contact lens is a toric lens having a toric surface at least in the front curve, and the astigmatism of the front curve determined from the prescription. A contact lens with a prism, characterized in that an astigmatism for correcting the aberration generated by the prism is combined in advance, and a toric surface according to the result is formed in the front curve.
