MAIN REQUEST

CLAIMS

1. A process for separation of an olefin from a paraffin in a product stream from a
dehydrogenation system having a steam turbine driven dehydrogenation reactor product
compressor (50, 80b), comprising the steps of:

feeding a stream (12) comprised of a mixture of an olefin and a paraffin to a product
splitter column (14) having a product splitter column reboiler (30) to produce an overhead
stream (16) and a bottoms stream (18);

splitting the bottoms stream (10) into a recycle stream (26) and a bottoms return
stream (27)

splitting the bottoms return stream (27) into a first bottom return stream (38) and a
second bottom returns stream (28) and feeding the first bottoms return stream (38) to the
product splitter column reboiler (30);

feeding the second bottoms return stream (28) to a heat exchanger (40);

heating the second bottoms return stream (28) by heat exchange contact with a
refrigerant circulated through a refrigerant loop having a steam turbine driven refrigeration
compressor (60, 80a);

feeding the heated second bottoms return stream (28) to the product splitter column
(14); and

feeding the exhaust steam from the steam turbine driven dehydrogenation reactor
product compressor (50, 80b) and the exhaust steam from the steam turbine driven
refrigeration compressor (60, 80a) to the product splitter column reboiler (30) to provide heat

to the product splitter column (14).

2. The process of claim 1, further comprising the step of:

combining the exhaust steam from the steam turbine driven dehydrogenation reactor
product compressor (50, 80b) and the exhaust steam from the steam turbine driven
refrigeration compressor (60, 80a) and feeding the combined stream (54) to the product

splitter column reboiler (30).

3. The process of claim 2, wherein the product splitter column (14) is operated at a
pressure of between about 1.38 MPag and 2.59 MPag (200 psig and 375 psig) and a
temperature of between about 26.7°C and 71.1°C (80°F and 160°F).
