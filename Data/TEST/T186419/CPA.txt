Title: 
Dual fuel injection system and motor vehicle comprising such injection system

Abstract: 
A dual fuel injection system for an internal combustion engine, comprises:
- a first fuel pump (1) for pumping a first fuel,
- a second fuel pump (4) for pumping at least a second fuel,
- a supply duct (26) for supplying the second fuel to the second fuel pump (4), the supply duct (26) comprising a junction (33) connected to an outlet of the first fuel pump (1), to join the first fuel supplied by the first fuel pump (1) into the supply duct (26),
- a high pressure pump (12), downstream of the second fuel pump (4), for pressurizing the first and/or second fuel, and
- an injector (16) for injecting the pressurized first and/or second fuel into a combustion chamber of the internal combustion engine.

Type: EPO Patent

Claims: 
1. A dual fuel injection system for an internal combustion engine, comprising:
- a first fuel pump for pumping a first fuel, the first fuel comprising a liquid fuel,
- a second fuel pump for pumping at least a second fuel, the second fuel comprising a gas fuel or a liquefied gas fuel,
- a supply duct for supplying the second fuel from a second fuel reservoir to the second fuel pump, the supply duct comprising a junction connected to an outlet of the first fuel pump, to join the first fuel supplied by the first fuel pump into the supply duct,
- a high pressure pump, downstream of the second fuel pump, for pressurizing the first and/or second fuel, and
- an injector for injecting the pressurized first and/or second fuel into a combustion chamber of the internal combustion engine.
2. The dual fuel injection system according to claim 1, wherein a one-way valve is provided between the outlet of the first fuel pump and the junction.
3. The dual fuel injection system according to claim 1 or 2, wherein a bypass duct comprising a one way valve is provided in parallel to the second fuel pump to bypass the second fuel pump.
4. The dual fuel injection system according to any of the preceding claims, further comprising a control device to control the first and second fuel pumps, the control device being arranged to:
operate the first pump to supply the first fuel;
operate the second pump to supply the second fuel, and
operate the first and second pumps simultaneously upon a transition from the second fuel to the first fuel, to pressurize first fuel so as to propel a remainder of second fuel in or towards the high pressure pump.
5. The dual fuel injection system according to claim 4, wherein the control device is further arranged to operate the first fuel pump upon a deactivation command, to flush at least the high pressure pump with the first fuel.
6. The dual fuel injection system according to any of the preceding claims, wherein one-way valves are provided at an inlet and an outlet of the high pressure pump, to retain fuel in the high pressure pump in a non operating state.
7. The dual fuel injection system according to any of the preceding claims, wherein a pressure sensor is provided to sense a fuel pressure downstream of the high pressure pump, a sensitivity of the pressure sensor being adapted substantially proportionally to a combustion value of the first and second fuels or being adapted to correct for a combination of density and lower combustion value of the first and second fuels.
8. The dual fuel injection system according to any of claims 4 - 7, wherein an additional first fuel pump is provided in series with the first fuel pump, the control device being further arranged to operate the first fuel pump, the additional first fuel pump and the second fuel pump simultaneously at an engine start.
9. A dual fuel injection system preferably according to any of the preceding claims, comprising:
- a first fuel pump for supplying a first fuel,
- a second fuel pump for supplying a second fuel,
- a high pressure pump, downstream of the first and second fuel pumps, for pressurizing the first and/or second fuel, and
- an injector for injecting the pressurized first and/or second fuel into a combustion chamber of the internal combustion engine,
a return duct being provided from the high pressure pump to a reservoir of the second fuel, the return duct for returning an excess of the second fuel back into the reservoir.
10. The dual fuel injection system according to claim 9, wherein the return duct is provided from a bypass of the high pressure pump, the bypass being located upstream of a high pressure part of the high pressure pump.
11. The dual fuel injection system according to claim 9 or 10, wherein the return duct is provided with a back pressure valve.
12. The dual fuel injection system according to any of the preceding claims, comprising an accumulator, a supply duct towards the accumulator being connected downstream of the second fuel pump, an outlet duct from the accumulator being connected upstream of the second fuel pump, the inlet duct and outlet duct each comprising respectively an accumulator inlet closing valve, and an accumulator outlet closing valve, the control device being arranged to open the accumulator outlet closing valve at the transition from the second fuel to the first fuel.
15. The dual fuel injection system according to any of the preceding claims, wherein an overpressure release valve is provided from a return duct of a high pressure injector rail, to a return duct towards the second fuel reservoir.
14. The dual fuel injection system according to claim 13, wherein a one way valve is provided from the return duct of the high pressure pump to an inlet side of the overpressure release valve.
15. A motor vehicle comprising a dual fuel injection system according to any of the preceding claims.
