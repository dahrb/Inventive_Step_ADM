Claim

1. The fuel supply system for internal combustion engines equipped with pumps, valves is
characterized by the fact that has a fuel type switch (1) upstream of the pump (2) that
supplies fuel to the high pressure pump (3), the connecting pipe supplying fuel to the
damping nozzle (6) activated by a second valve (7).

2. The fuel supply system for internal combustion engines according to claim 1 is
characterized by the fact that a damping nozzle (6) activated by a second valve (7) forces
the flow of LPG fuel, whereas when switching to petrol /Diesel oil, the second valve (7)
opens a flow path, that circumvents damping nozzle (6),which causes the fuel system to

empty of LPG.
