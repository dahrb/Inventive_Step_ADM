The appeal was filed by the Applicant against the decision of the Examining Division to refuse the patent application in suit (hereinafter "the application"). In this decision, the Examining Division held i.a. that - the application did not disclose the invention in a manner sufficiently clear and concise to be carried out; - the subject-matter of claim did not involve an inventive step; and - claim 1 of the sole request was not clear. II. In a communication pursuant to Rule 100(2) EPC (hereinafter "the communication"), the Board expressed the preliminary opinion that the invention was sufficiently disclosed to be carried out. With regard to the remaining issues, lack of inventive step and of clarity, the Board supported the findings of the Examining Division. III. In response to the communication, the Appellant submitted with a letter dated 30 September 2021 amended claims 1 and 2 and an adapted description without further commenting the Board's preliminary opinion or providing any substantive arguments, such as the basis of the amendments and how these established inventive step and clarity. IV. Since oral proceedings were not requested, the Board decides in written proceedings. V. The Board understands that the Appellant requests to set aside the decision under appeal and to grant a patent on the basis of their sole request filed with letter of 30 September 2021. VI. Amended independent claim 1 of the sole request reads as follows (amendments with regard to the previous version of claim 1 indicated by the Board): "The fuel supply system for internal combustion engines equipped with pumps, valves[deleted: , sensors, switch] is characterized by the fact that [deleted: is equipped with the] has a fuel type switch (1) [deleted: which is placed before] upstream of the pump (2) that supplies fuel to the high pressure pump (3), [deleted: behind which the valve (4) is placed, wherein valve (4) receives impulses of fuel pressure measurement from the sensor (8) and fuel temperature from the sensor (9), while valve (4) is located on the return pipe connecting the high pressure pump (3) with ]the connect[deleted: or]ing pipe [deleted: (5)] supplying [deleted: the] fuel to the damping nozzle (6)[deleted: ,] activated by [deleted: the] a second valve (7)." Newly introduced dependent claim 2 of the sole request reads as follows: "The fuel supply system for internal combustion engines according to claim 1 is characterized by the fact that a damping nozzle (6) activated by a second valve (7) forces the flow of LPG fuel, whereas when switching to petrol /Diesel oil, the second valve (7) opens a flow path, that circumvents damping nozzle (6), which causes the fuel system to empty of LPG." VII. In the present decision, reference is made to the following documents: D1:    DE 10 2006 0001 616 A1 D2:    EP 2 143 916 A1 D3:    WO 2012/177128 A1. VIII. The Appellant's arguments can be summarised as follows: The application satisfied the requirements of Article 83 EPC with regard to sufficiency of disclosure. It met furthermore the requirements of Article 84 EPC, as the independent claim was sufficiently supported by the description. Finally, the prerequisite of inventive step was also met, since none of the cited documents D1 - D3 disclosed a fuel switch point located before the high pressure pump. Accordingly, a patent had already been granted for the priority application in Poland, where in contrast to the EPO the Polish Patent Office had not concentrated on marginal issues and not raised corresponding objections.
