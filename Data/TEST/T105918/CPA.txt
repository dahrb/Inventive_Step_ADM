TITLE:
Apparatus and method for determining mobility state in wireless communication system

TYPE:
World International Patent Organisation

ABSTRACT:
An apparatus and method of determining a mobility state is provided. A user equipment determines a size of a cell to be reselected by performing cell reselection, and determines the mobility state on the basis of the number of cell reselections and a size of the cell. Unnecessary cell reselection can be avoided even if the user equipment enters an area where a micro cell and a macro cell coexist.

CLAIMS:
A method of determining a mobility state of a user equipment in a wireless communication system, the method comprising:
determining a size of a cell to be reselected by performing cell reselection; and
determining the mobility state on the basis of the number of cell reselections and a size of the cell.
The method of claim 1, wherein the mobility state is one of a normal mobility, a medium mobility, and a high mobility.
The method of claim 2, wherein the determining of the mobility state comprises:
determining the mobility state to the normal mobility if the number of cell reselections during a mobility period is less than a medium mobility threshold;
determining the mobility state to the medium mobility if the number of cell reselections during the mobility period is between the medium mobility threshold and a high mobility threshold; and
determining the mobility state to the high mobility if the number of cell reselections during the mobility period is greater than the high mobility threshold.
The method of claim 3, further comprising determining whether to increment a counter for indicating the number of cell reselections according to the cell size.
The method of claim 4, wherein, if the reselected cell is a micro cell, the counter is not incremented.
The method of claim 5, wherein the micro cell is a closed subscriber group (CSG) cell.
The method of claim 3, further comprising regulating a reselection time on the basis of the mobility state.
The method of claim 1, wherein the cell size is determined on the basis of a physical cell identity (PCI) of a measured cell.
The method of claim 7, wherein the cell size is determined on the basis of a cell size indicator.
The method of claim 7, wherein the cell size is determined on the basis of a CSG indicator.
A user equipment for determining a mobility state, comprising:
a radio frequency (RF) unit for transmitting and receiving a radio signal; and
a processor, coupled to the RF unit, for determining the mobility state,
wherein the processor is configured to
determine a size of a cell to be reselected by performing cell reselection, and
determine the mobility state on the basis of the number of cell reselections and a size of the cell.
The user equipment of claim 11, wherein the mobility state is one of a normal mobility, a medium mobility, and a high mobility.
The user equipment of claim 12, wherein the processor is configured to
determine the mobility state to the normal mobility if the number of cell reselections during a mobility period is less than a medium mobility threshold,
determine the mobility state to the medium mobility if the number of cell reselections during the mobility period is between the medium mobility threshold and a high mobility threshold, and
determine the mobility state to the high mobility if the number of cell reselections during the mobility period is greater than the high mobility threshold.
The user equipment of claim 13, wherein the processor is configured to determine whether to increment a counter for indicating the number of cell reselections according to the cell size.
The user equipment of claim 14, wherein, if the reselected cell is a micro cell, the processor is configured no to increment the counter.
