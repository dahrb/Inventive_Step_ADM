CLAIMS:

1. A surgical kit comprising:

a suture (100) comprising a biocompatible elongate weave of fibers having a first length
(112) with a first pick count and a second length (114) with a second pick count that is greater
than the first pick count, wherein the first length (112) defines a first pattern and the second
length (114) defines a second pattern that differs from the first pattern and wherein the first
length (112) has a reduced bending stiffness as compared with the second length (114) such that,
upon application of a bending force, the first length (112) of the suture (100) will more readily
deform, resulting in a reduced cross-section of the first length (112); and

a cannulated anchor (600) having an inner lumen (622) configured to receive the weave
of fibers;

characterized in that a doubled over portion of the first length (112) of the weave of fibers

is received in the inner lumen (622).

2. The surgical kit of claim 1, wherein the first length (112) has a resting outer diameter (dr)
that is greater than or equal to an outer diameter (dz) of the second length (114), and the first

length (112) is configured to collapse in diameter when the bending force is applied thereto.

3. The surgical kit of claim 2, wherein the collapsed outer diameter (dc) of the first length
(112) is less than the outer diameter (dz) of the second length (114).

4. The surgical kit of any preceding claim, wherein the second length (114) has a flexibility
that is greater than a flexibility of the first length (112).

5. The surgical kit of any preceding claim, wherein the elongate weave includes a hollow

lumen (18) extending at least partially therethrough.

6. The surgical kit of any preceding claim, wherein the first and second patterns each

comprise an uninterrupted series of repeating units (212, 214).

7. The surgical kit of claim 6, wherein the repeating unit (212) on the first pattern has a

length that is greater than a length of the repeating unit (214) on the second pattern.
