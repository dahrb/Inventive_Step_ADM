New Claims:

An electronic ticket providing system for providing electronic ticket information
from an electronic ticket vending apparatus to a mobile telephone through a

communication network, wherein:

the electronic ticket vending apparatus comprising

control means for controlling distribution of electronic ticket information to a
mobile telephone, the electronic ticket information containing: formal ticket data
comprising authentication information for admission, a ticket notation item, and
authentication information for acquisition of information for a ticket owner; and
provisional ticket data comprising the ticket notation item and authentication

information for acquisition of information for a ticket purchase requester; and

the mobile telephone comprises: internal memory; means for reading on and
writing to a removable storage medium; and storage control means for
controlling storage of the formal ticket data in the storage medium and the

provisional ticket data in the internal memory,

wherein the electronic ticket vending apparatus comprises authentication means
for confirming storage of the provisional ticket data in the internal memory for
the mobile telephone in response to an acquisition request for information for the
ticket purchase requester from the mobile telephone according to the
authentication information about the provisional ticket data to transmit
information for the ticket purchase requester to the mobile telephone based on

the authentication check result.
An electronic ticket providing system for providing electronic ticket information

from an electronic ticket vending apparatus to a mobile telephone through a

communication network, wherein:
