CLAIMS (MAIN REQUEST, CLEAN)

1. A method for displaying, on an avionics display system (100), cues for executing an
aircraft landing after a loss of engine thrust by the aircraft, the method comprising:

(a) receiving avionics data (602);

(b) responsive to detecting the loss in engine thrust, processing the avionics data (604)
to determine a current aircraft configuration and displaying (606), on a three dimensional
display (106) on the avionics display system (100), a unique conformal cue for each of:

(i) a first potential landing site associated with not altering the current aircraft

configuration (210),

(i1) a second potential landing site associated with deploying only landing gear

(214), and

(iii) a third potential landing site associated with deploying the landing gear

and flaps (218);

(c) updating terrain (304) in the three dimensional display (106), indicative of aircraft
travel (302); and

repeating (a) — (c) until the aircraft has landed.

2. The method of Claim 1, wherein the second potential landing site represents an altered

lift-to-drag ratio based on extending landing gear.

3. The method of Claim 1, wherein the third potential landing site represents an altered

lift-to-drag ratio based on extending landing gear and deploying flaps.

4. The method of Claim 1, further comprising, responsive to detecting the loss in engine
thrust, processing the avionics data to:
add to the three dimensional display (106) on the avionics display system
(100),

(iv) a unique conformal cue for altering the lift-to-drag ratio based on an aircraft maneuver.

5. The method of Claim 1, wherein the step of displaying a cue comprises rendering the

cue in perspective form at its predicted location on the terrain.

6. The method of Claim 1, further comprising:
receiving user input data; and

altering, by a processor, aircraft configuration data according to the user input data.

7. The method of claim 1, further comprising displaying text “clean” proximate to the

cue at the first potential landing site.

8. The method of claim 7, further comprising displaying text “GEAR” or “G” proximate

10. The method of Claim 9, wherein each cue is a chevron and each chevron is oriented to

11. An avionics display system (100) for an aircraft, comprising:
a flight deck display (106) coupled to receive display commands and configured,
upon receipt thereof, to render images; and
a processor (102) operatively coupled to the flight deck display and adapted to receive
avionics data, the processor configured, upon detecting a loss of engine thrust, and upon
receipt of the avionics data, to:
(a) determine a current aircraft configuration,
(b) predict a first potential landing site for the aircraft based on not altering the
current aircraft configuration,
characterized by the processor being further configured to:
(c) predict a second potential landing site for the aircraft associated with
altering the current aircraft configuration by deploying only landing gear (214),
(d) predict a third potential landing site for the aircraft associated with altering

the current aircraft configuration by deploying the landing gear and flaps (218),
