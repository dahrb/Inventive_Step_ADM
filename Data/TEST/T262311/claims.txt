Main Request
NEW CLAIMS 1 TO 9

1. An organic thin film transistor comprising a substrate, a gate electrode, a dielectric layer,
a source electrode, a drain electrode, a semiconductor layer and a barrier layer, wherein the

barrier layer comprises a polymer, an antioxidant, and an inorganic particulate material.

2. The organic thin film transistor of claim 1, wherein the polymer is selected from the group
consisting of polyolefins, paraffin waxes, polyacrylates, polyimides, polyesters, polyvinyls,

polycarbonates, polysulfones and polyetherketones.

3. The organic thin film transistor of claim 1, wherein the antioxidant is selected from the
group consisting of derivatives of benzophenone, substituted benzophenones, triazine,

substituted triazines, benzotriazol, substituted benzotriazoles, phosphates and mixture thereof.

4. The organic thin film transistor of claim 1, wherein the inorganic particulate material is a

surface-modified particulate material.

5. The organic thin film transistor of claim 4, wherein the surface-modified inorganic

particulate material is an inorganic oxide.
6. The organic thin film transistor of claim 5, wherein the inorganic oxide is a metal oxide.

7. The organic thin film transistor of claim 5 or 6, wherein the inorganic oxide is aluminum
oxide (Al,Os), silicon oxides (SiO,) including silicon monoxide (SiO) and silicon dioxide (SiOz), or

silicon oxynitrides.

8. The organic thin film transistor of claim 4, wherein the surface-modified inorganic
particulate material comprises particles which are surface-modified with a silane reagent of

formula

wherein R and X each independently represents an alkyl group of from about 1 to about 60

carbon atoms, an aryl group of from about 6 to about 60 carbon atoms, a substituted alkyl group
