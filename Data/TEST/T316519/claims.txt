1. An antibody or an antigen-binding fragment thereof that specifically binds and
inhibits proprotein convertase subtilisin/kexin type 9 (PCSKQY) for use in a method for
reducing cardiovascular risk in a high cardiovascular risk patient following an acute
coronary syndrome (ACS) event,

wherein said patient has hypercholesterolemia and exhibits inadequate control
of other atherogenic lipoproteins despite treatment with a maximum tolerated daily
dose statin therapy, wherein the antibody or the antigen-binding fragment thereof has
heavy and light chain CDR amino acid sequences having SEQ ID NOs: 2 (HCDR1), 3
(HCDR2), 4 (HCDR3), 7 (LCDR1), 8 (LCDR2) and 10 (LCDR3), and wherein the use
comprises administering the antibody or antigen-binding fragment thereof to the

patient at a dose of 75 mg or 150 mg.

2. The antibody or an antigen-binding fragment thereof for use according to claim

wherein the antibody or antigen-binding fragment thereof is administered to the
patient at a dose of 75 mg at a frequency of once every two weeks,

wherein the 75 mg dose is maintained if the patient's LDL-C measured after two
doses is lower than 50 mg/dl, and

wherein the 75 mg dose is discontinued if the patient's LDL-C measured after
two doses is greater than or equal to 50 mg/dl, and the antibody or antigen-binding
fragment thereof is subsequently administered to the patient at a dose of 150 mg ata

frequency of once every two weeks.

3. The antibody or an antigen-binding fragment thereof for use according to claim
2, wherein the antibody or an antigen-binding fragment thereof is administered to the

patient within 12 months following an acute coronary syndrome (ACS) event.

4. The antibody or an antigen-binding fragment thereof for use according to any

one of claims 1-3, wherein reducing cardiovascular risk means increasing the time to
