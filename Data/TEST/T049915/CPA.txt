TITLE:
Method for manufacturing containers having a peelable closure

TYPE:
European patent

ABSTRACT:
Method of manufacturing a container comprising a body having an opening, a bead of heat-weldable material arranged in the proximity of the opening and a lid sealed to the bead. The heat-weldable bead (6) is injected in the proximity of the opening during the production of the body of the container (1) or during the operation of sealing the lid. <IMAGE>

CLAIMS:
Method of manufacturing a container comprising a body having an opening, a bead of heat-sealable material arranged near the opening and a seal sealed on the bead, characterized in that the heat-sealable bead (6) is injected near the opening during the production of the container body (1) or during the sealing operation of the cover. A method according to claim 1, characterized in that the heat-sealable material is injected along one or more continuous and closed lines around the container. Method according to claim 1 or claim 2, characterized in that the heat-sealable material is injected onto the rim of the container. Container with peelable opening, characterized in that it is obtained by implementing the method according to any one of claims 1 to 3.
