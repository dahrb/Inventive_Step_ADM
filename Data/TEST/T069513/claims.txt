MAIN REQUEST

Claims:

1. A printed antenna assembly (10) for the transmission of electromagnetic
waves, the printed antenna comprising:

a substantially planar circuit board (16) having a first planar surface (22) and
a second planar surface (24), the first and second planar surfaces being parallel and
spaced by a material thickness (A), the circuit board including a mounting section
(26) and an antenna section (28) integrally formed with each other; characterised by

an antenna driving circuit (30) mounted to the first surface of the mounting
section for generating electromagnetic waves to be transmitted by the printed
antenna;

a coating (32) of electrically conductive material covering substantially all of
the second planar surface of the mounting section of the circuit board, wherein the
conductive material forms a ground plane for the circuit board and provides a
radiating element for transmitting the electromagnetic wave;

a radiating strip (46) formed on the antenna section of the circuit board and
coupled to the radiating element for aiding in the transmission of electromagnetic
waves; and

an impedance matching strip (56) formed on the antenna section of the circuit
board and coupled to the radiating strip, the impedance matching strip having an
overall length selected to substantially match the impedance of the antenna driving
circuit, wherein the impedance matching strip (56) and the radiation strip (46) are
formed on the second side (24) of the printed circuit board (16).

2. The printed antenna assembly (10) of claim 1 wherein the mounting section
(26) of the circuit board (16) extends along a center axis (33) and has a length
parallel to the center axis that is greater than a width perpendicular to the center axis.

3. The printed antenna assembly (10) of claim 2 wherein the radiation strip (46)

extends parallel to the center axis (33) of the mounting section (26).
4. The printed antenna assembly (10) of claims 1 or 3 wherein the impedance

matching strip (56) includes at least a first leg (58) formed on the antenna section
(28) and extending perpendicularly to the radiation strip (46).
