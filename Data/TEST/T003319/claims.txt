Main Request

1. An antibody for use in treating atypical hemolytic uremic syndrome (aHUS) in a patient,

wherein the antibody is eculizumab.

2. The antibody for use in treating aHUS in a patient according to claim 1, wherein the
antibody is to be administered to the patient for at least 7 weeks under the following

schedule:

900 mg of the antibody, once per week for four consecutive weeks;
1200 mg of the antibody once during the fifth week; and
1200 mg of the antibody bi-weekly thereafter.

3. The antibody for use in treating aHUS in a patient according to claim 2, wherein the
antibody is for use in a patient with a body weight that is greater than or equal to 40 kg.
