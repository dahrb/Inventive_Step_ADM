TITLE:
hopper structure

TYPE:
European patent

ABSTRACT:
A hopper structure for treating granular material, comprising an inner sidewall section arranged, in use, as an upper tubular sidewall, a tapered lower section having a delivery mouth (15) and an elongated insert body (8,9,10,80,90,100,105,122), wherein the insert comprises an upper portion and a lower portion (11,12,13,111,121) arranged at the lower tapered portion of the hopper and having its conicity facing away from the upper portion, the upper portion axially extending from the base of the lower portion up to at least one half of the upper tubular section at an angle λ≥ 0 with respect to a vertical line.

CLAIMS:
A hopper structure for treating granular material, comprising an inner sidewall section arranged, in use, as an upper tubular sidewall, a tapered lower section having a delivery mouth (15) and an elongated insert body (8, 9, 10; 80, 90, 100; 105 and 122), characterized in that said insert comprises an upper portion and a lower portion (11, 12, 13; 111, 121) arranged at said lower tapered portion of the hopper and having its conicity facing away from said upper portion, said upper portion axially extending from the base of the lower portion up to at least one half of the upper tubular section at an angle λ≥ 0 with respect to a vertical line.
A hopper structure according to claim 1, characterized in that said tapered lower section of said hopper comprises at least one sidewall with a tapering angle (α7) and said lower -portion (11, 12, 13; 111; 121) has a tapering angle (α1, α2, α3, β) equal to, or lower than a tapering angle (α7) of tapered lower section of the hopper.
A hopper structure according to claim 1 or 2, characterized in that the minimum distance (d1, d2, d3) between the lower tapered sidewall of the hopper and the lower conical or -truncated cone surface (11, 12, 13; 111; 121) of the insert (8, 9, 10; 80, 90, 100; 105 e 122) is greater than the "critical distance" which causes the formation of at least one blocking arc in the granular material.
A hopper structure according to any previous claim, characterized in that said lower truncated cone portion (11, 12, 13; 111 e 121) of insert (8, 9, 10; 80, 90, 100; 105 e 122) is foraminated.
A hopper structure according to any previous claim, characterized in that said upper portion of said insert (105) has a cone or truncated cone shape (110) with conicity facing away from its lower portion (111).
A hopper structure according to any previous claim 1 to 4, characterized in that said upper portion of said insert (8, 9, 10; 122) is tubular with a constant cross-section.
A hopper structure according to any claim 1 to 4, characterized in that said upper portion of said insert (TRa, TRb, TRc) is tubular with a constant cross-section and terminates with a conical or truncated cone top portion (21, 22, 23).
A hopper structure according to claim 7, characterized in that the distance (d23) between the vertex of said conical or truncated cone top portion (23) and a loading mouth (19) of said hopper is close to zero.
A hopper structure according to any claim 1 to 4, characterized in that said upper portion of said insert (TRc) is tubular with a constant cross-section and terminates with a concave top portion (23a, 23b) having the concavity thereof facing said portion (TRc) of said insert.
A hopper structure according to claim 9, characterized in that said concave top portion (23a, 23b) is semispherical, or formed by a truncated cone portion supporting a semispherical portion at the top thereof.
A hopper structure according to any previous claim, characterized in that said upper portion of said insert is connectable in fluid communication with a hot and dried source of air.

A hopper structure according to any previous claim, characterized in that said lower portion (11, 12, 13; 111; 121) of the insert is made of a material having a friction coefficient with respect to granular material, greater than the inner sidewall friction coefficient with respect to granular material.
A hopper structure according to claim 16, characterized in that the friction coefficient of said inner sidewall is smaller than the friction coefficient of said lower portion (11,12, 13; 111; 121) of the insert only at said insert (11, 12, 13; 111; 121).
