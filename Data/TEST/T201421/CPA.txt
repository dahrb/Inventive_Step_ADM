Title: 
Heat exchange equipment diagnostic system

Abstract: A heat exchange device diagnosis system for diagnosing deterioration of a heat exchange device by grasping a relationship between a flow rate of a fluid and a heat transfer coefficient or a change in the heat transfer coefficient.
In a heat exchange device diagnosis system that grasps the operating state of a heat exchange device by simulation, a tracking simulator that reproduces the state of the actual plant in real time based on the actual measured value of the actual plant is used, and this tracking simulator is used as a tracking parameter. Select the heat transfer coefficient, operate it in parallel with the actual plant, adjust the heat transfer coefficient until the measured value matches the simulator value, find the estimated value of the heat transfer coefficient, and based on the change trend of these estimated values The deterioration state of the heat transfer performance in the heat exchange device is determined.
