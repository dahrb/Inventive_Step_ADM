The appellant (applicant) lodged an appeal against the decision of the examining division refusing European patent application No. 11813672.0. II. During the first-instance proceedings reference was made to the following document: D1: US 2007/0024971 A1. In its decision the examining division held in respect of the main and the first to third auxiliary requests then on file that - the subject-matter of claim 1 of the main and the first and third auxiliary requests was not new in view of document D1, and - the subject-matter of claim 1 of the second auxiliary request did not involve an inventive step over the disclosure of document D1. In an obiter dictum the examining division also expressed the view that the subject-matter of claim 1 of the main request would, in any case, not involve an inventive step over the disclosure of document D1. III. With the statement setting out the grounds of appeal, the appellant submitted claims according to a main and first to fifth auxiliary requests. IV. In reply to the observations made by the board in a communication under Article 15(1) RPBA 2020 annexed to the summons to oral proceedings, the appellant filed by letter dated 14 January 2022, among other application documents, amended pages 5 to 14 of the description. V. In reply to a subsequent communication of the board dated 21 January 2022, the appellant filed by letter dated 26 January 2022 amended claims 1 to 5 according to a new Main Request and requested that the decision under appeal be set aside and that a patent be granted - according to the main request, i.e. on the basis of the claims 1 to 5 filed with the letter dated 26 January 2022, pages 1 to 4 and 15 to 17 of the description of the application as originally filed and pages 5 to 14 filed with the letter dated 14 January 2022, and drawing sheets 1/8 to 8/8 of the application as originally filed, or - on the basis of the claims of the first to sixth auxiliary requests filed as the main and first to fifth auxiliary requests with the statement of grounds of appeal, respectively. VI. In view of the main request submitted by the appellant, the oral proceedings were cancelled. VII. Claim 1 of the main request reads as follows: "An optical light mixer (101, 201, 301) providing a homogenized and uniform light beam, said optical light mixer (101, 201, 301) being formed of a solid transparent material, where light enters said optical light mixer (101, 201, 301) through an entrance surface and is reflected through said optical light mixer (101, 201, 301) to an exit surface where said light exits said optical light mixer (101, 201, 301) characterized in that said optical light mixer (101, 201, 301) comprises: - a first part formed as a pyramid frustum (107, 207, 307), said pyramid frustum (107, 207, 307) comprises plane side walls, a top surface (103, 203) and a bottom surface (111, 211), where said top surface (103, 203) of said pyramid frustum (107, 207, 307) has a smaller area than said bottom surface (111, 211) of said pyramid frustum (107, 207, 307) and where the top surface (103, 203) of said pyramid frustum (107, 207, 307) forms said entrance surface where part of said light is multiple reflected by said plane side walls; - a second part formed as a cone frustum (109, 209, 309), said cone frustum (109, 209, 309) comprises curved side walls, a top surface (113) and a bottom surface (105), where said top surface (113) of said cone frustum (109, 209, 309) has a smaller area than said bottom surface (105, 305) of said cone frustum (109, 209, 309) and the bottom surface (105, 305) of said cone frustum (109, 209, 309) forms said exit surface, where part of said light is reflected by said curved side walls; and wherein the top surface (113) of the cone frustum (109, 209, 309) circumscribes and is in contact with the bottom surface (111, 211) of the pyramid frustum (107, 207, 307) such that the area of the bottom surface (111, 211) of said pyramid frustum (107, 207, 307) is smaller than the area of the top surface (113) of said cone frustum (109, 209, 309)." The claims of the main request also include claim 3 directed to an illumination device comprising the optical light mixer defined in claim 1, and dependent claim 2 and dependent claims 4 and 5 referring back to claims 1 and 3, respectively.
