CLAIMS

An electricity supply apparatus (ESA) of an industrial site (TS) comprising a load

(TA), said electricity supply apparatus (ESA) comprising:

- a control device (CD) configured to calculate an amplitude (A) of a control
signal (Vc) according to a price information (InfoP) indicative of a price per
time unit of a first current (I2) drawn from a mains (Ms) and to generate said
control signal (Vc);

- an energy station (ES) configured to draw said first current (12) from said
mains (Ms), to provide at least a first part (14) of said first current (12) to said
load (TA), to receive said control signal (Vc) from said control device (CD), and
to reduce said first current (12) by an amount proportional to said amplitude (A)
of said control signal (Vc); and

- a battery (B) electrically connected to said energy station (ES) and to said load
(TS), said battery (B) being configured to recharge by drawing a second part
(13) of said first current (12) from said energy station (ES) and to discharge by
providing a second current (-I3) to said load (TA),

wherein said control device (CD) is further configured to calculate said

amplitude (A) of said control signal (Vc) also according to battery information

(InfoB) relating to at least one of a recharge condition and a discharge condition

of said battery (B), said amplitude (A) having an intermediate value of a range

delimited by a minimum value and a maximum value (Amax).

The apparatus (ESA) according to claim 1, wherein said price information
(InfoP) comprise a price table (PT), said price table (PT) comprising a number of
rows, each of said number of rows comprising an hour of the day and a price
per hour of said first current (12) during said hour.

The apparatus (ESA) according to claim 2, wherein said control device (CD) is
further configured to classify each hour comprised in said price table (PT) as
one of a peak hour, a neutral hour and an off-peak hour, according to said
battery information (InfoB).

The apparatus (ESA) according to claim 3, wherein said battery information
(InfoB) comprise an optimum charge level (BCLopt) of said battery (B), a
minimum charge level (BCLmin) of said battery (B) and an optimum discharge
current (I3opt-) of said battery (B), said optimum charge level (BCLopt) being
the maximum charge level allowing said battery (B) to store energy with a
