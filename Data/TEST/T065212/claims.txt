CLAIM REQUEST

1. An interleukin-6 (IL-6) antagonist for use in the treatment of chronic arthritides
diseases of childhood, wherein the IL-6 antagonist is a humanized monoclonal
antibody against human IL-6 receptor and wherein the chronic arthritides diseases of
childhood is systemic onset type juvenile rheumatoid arthritis in which symptoms can
not be controlled with nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs, long term bolus steroids

and immunosuppressants.

2. An interleukin-6 (IL-6) antagonist according to any claims 1, wherein the antibody
against IL-6 receptor is a recombinant antibody.

3. An interleukin-6 (IL-6) antagonist according to claim 1, wherein the monoclonal
antibody against human IL-6 receptor is the PM-1 antibody produced by the hybridoma
deposited as FERM BP-2998.

4. An interleukin-6 (IL-6) antagonist according to claim 1 or 2, wherein the antibody
against IL-6 receptor is a chimeric antibody, a humanized antibody, or a human
antibody against IL-6 receptor.

5. An interleukin-6 (IL-6) antagonist according to claim 4, wherein the antibody is a
humanized antibody of the PM-I antibody produced by the hybridoma deposited as
FERM BP-2998.

6. An interleukin-6 (IL-6) antagonist for use in the treatment of Still's disease, wherein

the IL-6 antagonist is a monoclonal antibody against human IL-6 receptor.

7. An interleukin-6 (IL-6) antagonist according to claim 6, wherein the antibody against

IL-6 receptor is a recombinant antibody.

8. An interleukin-6 (IL-6) antagonist according to claim 6, wherein the monoclonal
antibody against human IL-6 receptor is the PM-1 antibody produced by the
hybridoma deposited as FERM BP-2998.
