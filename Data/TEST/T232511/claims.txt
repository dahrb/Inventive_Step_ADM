MAIN REQUEST (December 2015)

1. An apparatus, comprising:

a layer of group III-nitride having a top surface, the top surface having a plurality
of pyramidal structures of the group II]-nitride, the pyramidal structures being located on
a part of the top surface having nitrogen-polarity; and

a metallic electrode located directly on a flat surface of the layer between portions
of the layer having the pyramidal structures, the pyramidal structures of the top surface

being physically separated from electrode.

2. The apparatus of claim 1, wherein the group III-nitride comprises gallium.

3. The apparatus of claim 1, wherein the pyramidal structures spatially
overlap.

4, The apparatus of claim 1, wherein the pyramidal structures have a random
distribution.

5. The apparatus of claim 1, further comprising a phosphor screen facing the

top surface.
