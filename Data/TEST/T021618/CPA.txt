TITLE:
Higher harmonic control apparatus for a helicopter

TYPE:
European patent

ABSTRACT:
A control system (24) has an HHC actuator (29) having a basal portion (40) and a movable portion (41). A load acting on the control system (24) is detected by a semiconductor gauge (31). The load detected by the semiconductor gauge (31) is compared with a threshold value in a computer (32). If the detected load is larger than the threshold load, the movable portion (41) is fixed to the basal portion (40) by the fixing means (33). By detecting the sign of stall of blades, the HHC actuator (29) is fixed in a neutral position, allowing safe flight. A fixing switch (56) is provided for allowing the fixing means (33) to operate regardless of the result of the comparison with the computer (32).

CLAIMS:
A higher harmonic control apparatus (20) for a helicopter rotor (21) comprising:
   a higher harmonic control actuator (29) having a basal portion (40) and a portion movable (41) with respect to the basal portion (40);
   a control system (24) for operating a pitch angle of the helicopter rotor (21);
   means (31) for detecting a load acting on the control system (24);
   means (32) for comparing the load detected by the load detecting means with a predetermined threshold of load; and
   fixing means (33), responsive to the output of the comparing means (32), for fixing the movable portion (41) to the basal portion (40) when the detected load is larger than the threshold load.
The higher harmonic control apparatus (20) of claim 1, further comprising command means (56) for commanding about the operation of the fixing means (33) regardless of the output of the comparing means (32).
The higher harmonic control apparatus (20) of claim 1, wherein an engagement hole (51) is formed in the movable portion (41) of the harmonic control actuator (29) and the fixing means (33) has a movable pin (50) which can be inserted into the engagement hole (51) when the movable portion (41) is put in a neutral position.
The higher harmonic control apparatus (20) of claim 1, further comprising display means (55) for displaying the detected load.
The higher harmonic apparatus (20) of claim 1, wherein a low-pass filter (35) having a predetermined cut-off frequency is provided between the detecting means (31) and the comparing means (32).
