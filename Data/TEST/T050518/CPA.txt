TITLE:
Mobile advertising method and system

TYPE:
European Patent

ABSTRACT:
In the present invention, advertisement is presented without putting signboard for advertisement at a place hoped for. Network navigation center 10 outputs guide access information GAI to personal computer 18 of each sponsor of the ad over Internet 17 and receives registration of advertisement information ADI to present advertisement at spot for presenting advertisement from personal computer 18 of sponsor of the ad according to guide access information GAI. In addition, network navigation center 10 receives current position information API from mobile communication device 12 carried by automobile 11 viamobile telephone base station 14 and public telephone network 16. Network navigation center 10 transmits advertisement information ADI based on current position information API to mobile communication instrument 12 via public telephone network 16 and mobile telephone base station 14.
