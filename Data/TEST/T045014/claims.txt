MAIN REQUEST
CLAIMS:

1. A reproduction test service apparatus which reproduces past operations of a medical
system, characterized by comprising:

a unit (404) configured to receive a plurality of past log files directly or indirectly from the
medical system;

a unit (405) configured to store the plurality of log files;

a pseudo medical system (407); and

a control unit (406) configured to control the pseudo medical system based on the log
files so as to allow a plurality of past operations of the medical system to be reproduced on the
pseudo medical system, wherein the unit (404) configured to receive the log files is configured
to receive pure raw data or raw data together with the log files, the pure raw data being data
converted from signals detected by the medical system, the raw data being data processed from
the pure raw data, and the control unit (406) is further configured to contro! the pseudo medical

system based on at least one of the pure raw data and raw data.

2. The apparatus according to claim 1, characterized in that the pseudo medical system
reproduces the past operations of the medical system while retracing back through them one

after another according to its operation history.

3. The apparatus according to claim 2, characterized in that the pseudo medical system
automatically stops its reproducing operation at the time when the same conditions as when a

fault occurred in the medical system are reproduced.

4, The apparatus according to any preceding claim, characterized in that the pseudo
medical system differs from the medical system at least in that it has neither gantry nor bed.

5. The apparatus according to any preceding claim, characterized in that the pseudo

medical system reproduces operations under the same reconstruction conditions as with the

past operations of the medical system.
