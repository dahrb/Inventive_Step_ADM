MAIN REQUEST CLAIMS:

1. A process of digital inkjet printing an image directly on a surface, the
process comprising:

contacting at least a part of the surface with a wetting composition so as to
provide a wet part of the surface, said wetting composition being capable of
interfering with the engagement of a liquid ink composition with at least one binding
site of the surface;

applying said liquid ink composition, by an inkjet printing head, on said wet
part of the surface;

optionally re-contacting said part of the surface with said wetting
composition subsequent to said applying; and

curing the image subsequent to said applying;

wherein:

said liquid ink composition comprises a colorant and a carrier;

said wetting composition is aimed at temporarily modifying at least one of
the mechanical property, physical property, and chemical property of the surface
during the application of said liquid ink composition thereon;

said surface is a textile fabric;

said contacting is effected prior to said applying;

said contacting is effected on at least pre-determined areas of said surface to
be subjected to said applying;

said contacting is effected in-line of said applying by means of a liquid
applicator selected from the group comprising a spraying nozzle, a dripping nozzle, a
droplet injector, a drop-on-demand piezoelectric inkjet nozzle and a continuous
piezoelectric inkjet nozzle; and

said applying is effected so as to form the image on said part of the surface;

characterized in that said wetting composition and said liquid ink
composition are selected such that a surface tension of said wetting composition is

lower than a surface tension of said liquid ink composition.

2. The process of claim 1, wherein said liquid ink composition is
selected from the group consisting of an aqueous-based ink composition and a non-

aqueous solvent-based ink composition.
