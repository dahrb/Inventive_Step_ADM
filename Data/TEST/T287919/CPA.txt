Title: Production of 2-hydroxy-4-phenylbutyric acid amide

Abstract: PURPOSE:To obtain the subject compound useful as a synthetic intermediate for angiotensin exchange enzyme inhibiting agent, etc., known as a hypotensor with simple operation in high yield by hydrolyzing 3-phenylpropionaldehyde cyanhydrin in the presence of hydrochloric acid. CONSTITUTION:The objective compound can be produced by hydrolyzing 3- phenylpropionaldehyde cyanhydrin (abbreviated as PPCH) at 15-50 deg.C (preferably 20-40 deg.C) using concentrated hydrochloric acid having a concentration of 20-36wt.%. The molar ratio of the hydrochloric acid to PPCH is 0.5-2, preferably 1-1.5. There is no particular restriction in the mixing method of PPCH and hydrochloric acid, however, it is preferable to perform a batch system by slowly adding PPCH to hydrochloric acid or a continuous system by continuously supplying PPCH and hydrochloric acid to the reaction system.

Type: Japan Patent

Claims: (1) A method for producing 2-hydroxy-4-phenylbutyric acid amide, which comprises hydrolyzing 3-phenylpropionaldehyde cyanohydrin at 15 to 50°C in the presence of hydrochloric acid.
