CLAIMS

1. A process for hydroprocessing a heavy hydrocarbon oil, comprising contacting a heavy
hydrocarbon oil in the presence of hydrogen with a mixture of hydroprocessing catalyst I and
hydroprocessing catalyst IT wherein catalyst I comprises a Group VIB metal component and
optionally a Group VII metal component on a porous inorganic carrier, said catalyst having a specific
surface area of at least 100 m?/g, a total pore volume of at least 0.55 ml/g, at least 50% of the total
pore volume in pores with a diameter of at least 20 nm (200 A) and at least 65% of the total pore
volume in pores with a diameter of 10-120 nm (100-1200 A), and catalyst II comprises a Group VIB
metal component and optionally a Group VIII metal component on a porous inorganic carrier, said
catalyst having a specific surface area of at least 100 m?/g, a total pore volume of at least 0.55 ml/g,
30-80% of the pore volume in pores with a diameter of 10-20 nm (100-200 A), and at least 5% of the
pore volume in pores with a diameter of at least 100 nm (1000 A), wherein the catalyst I has a larger
percentage of its pore volume in pores with a diameter of at least 20 nm (200 A) than catalyst IL.

2, The process of claim 1 wherein the carrier of catalyst I consists essentially of alumina and/or
wherein the carrier of catalyst II consists essentially of alumina and at least 3.5 wt.% of silica, and/or
wherein catalyst II comprises 0,.1-2 wt.% of a Group IA metal component.

3. The process of claim 1 or 2 wherein catalyst II has less than 50% of its pore volume in pores
with a diameter of at least 20 nm (200 A).

4. The process of any of the preceding claims wherein catalyst I and/or catalyst II comprise 7 to
20 wt.% of a Group VIB metal component, calculated as trioxide on the weight of the catalyst, and 0.5
to 6 wt.% of a Group VIII metal component, calculated as oxide on the weight of the catalyst.

5. The process of any of the preceding claims wherein the heavy hydrocarbon feed is a feed of
which at least 50 wt.% boils above 538° C (1000°F) and which comprises at least 2 wt.% of sulfur and
at least 5 wt.% of Conradson carbon.

6. The process of any of the preceding claims which is carried out in an ebullating bed.

7. A mixture of catalysts comprising a catalyst I which comprises a Group VIB metal component
and optionally a Group VIII metal component on a porous inorganic carrier, and 0.5 to 6 wt.% of a
Group VIII metal component, calculated as oxide on the weight of the catalyst, on a porous inorganic
carrier, said catalyst having a specific surface area of at least 100 m?/g, a total pore volume of at least
