The appeal is against the decision of the Examining Division refusing European patent application No. 12 196 250 on the grounds that the claimed subject-matter did not meet the requirements of Article 84 EPC (main request) and did not involve an inventive step within the meaning of Article 56 EPC (main request and auxiliary request). II. At the end of the oral proceedings held before the Board the appellant requested that the decision under appeal be set aside and that a patent be granted in the following version: Description: pages 1-22 filed in the oral proceedings before the Board, Claims: No. 1-6 filed in the oral proceedings before the Board, Drawings: sheets 1/6 to 6/6 as originally filed. All other requests, including the request for a reimbursement of the appeal fee, were withdrawn. III.
