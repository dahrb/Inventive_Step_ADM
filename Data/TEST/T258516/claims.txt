Request M’

1. A portable device (10) for detecting a radio frequency transmission of
electromagnetic radiation, wherein the portable device comprises:
a housing (12);
a controller (16) situated within the housing;
a storage medium (18) communicatively connected to the controller; and
an antenna (20, 50) connected to the controller and configured to receive the radio
frequency transmission, wherein the antenna comprises monopole antenna elements, and
wherein each of the monopole antenna elements is connected to a pair of detector diodes
that are connected in series to a signal ground plane;
wherein the controller is operative for:
sensing at least one of magnitude, duration and repetition of the radio
frequency transmission;
identifying a class of the radio frequency transmission based on one of the
magnitude, duration, and repetition associated with the radio frequency
transmission; and
outputting a visual and/or aural alert indicative of the occurrence of one or
more of the identified classes of radio frequency transmission having been sensed
at one or more of a threshold level.

2. The portable device (10) of claim 1, wherein the monopole antenna elements of the
antenna comprise a first (80a) and a second (30b) monopole antenna element oriented

along a substantially same plane as each other.

3. The portable device (10) of claim 2, wherein the monopole antenna elements of the
antenna further comprise a third monopole antenna element (30c) arranged in substantially
perpendicular relation to the first and second monopole antenna element, wherein the
controller is operative for identifying a highest signal level obtained from the first, second, or
third monopole antenna element to determine the polarization orientation of the radio

frequency transmission.

4. The portable device (10) of claim 3, wherein the controller is further operative for
establishing a plurality of threshold levels corresponding to ranges of at least one of
magnitude, duration, and repetition associated with the class of the radio frequency

transmission.

5. The portable device (10) of claim 4, wherein the identified class is selected from the
group consisting of: (a) continuous wave; (b) pulsed signals with repetition rates
substantially on the order of milliseconds; (c) transient signals with repetition rates
substantially on the order of seconds; and (d) random signals with no defined repetition

rate.

6. The portable device (10) of claim 4, further comprising visual indicia corresponding

to each of the plurality of threshold levels.

7. The portable device (10) of claim 4, wherein the visual and/or aural alert is indicative
of the occurrence of the one or more of the identified classes of radio frequency

transmission at one of the plurality of threshold levels.

8. The portable device (10) of claim 3, wherein the controller is further operative for
storing data representative of an occurrence of the one or more of the identified classes of

the radio frequency transmission.

9. The portable device (10) of claim 3, further comprising a computer interface
connector (28) communicatively connected to the controller.

10. The portable device (10) of claim 9, further comprising a timer (22) connected to the
controller, wherein the controller is operative for starting the timer when the controller
detects that an electrical current received from a computer via the computer interface

connector has ceased.

11. The portable device (10) of claim 10, further comprising a rechargeable battery
electrically connected to the computer interface connector, wherein the controller is
configured to charge the rechargeable battery from electricity received via the computer

interface connector.

12. The portable device (10) of claim 1, wherein the radio frequency transmission has a

frequency between 100 MHz and 10 GHz.

13. The portable device (10) of claim 1, further comprising a visual-indicating

component (82) or an audio generating component (24) connected to the controller.
