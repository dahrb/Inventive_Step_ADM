CLAIMS

1. Electronic device, comprising sigma-delta modulation means
(SDM) adapted to operate with a clock signal and having output means
adapted to deliver a digital data signal, first means (PLL) adapted to
deliver a radiofrequency transposition signal, and a notch filter
including at least two identical radiofrequency digital to analog
conversion blocks (RDCi), each having first input means coupled to said
output means, second input means adapted to receive said
radiofrequency transposition signal and second output means adapted to
deliver a radiofrequency analog signal (ARFSi), digital delay means
(DM) controlled by said clock signal and including a delay block
connected between the two first input means, the frequency of a notch of
said notch filter being related to the delay values of the delay block, and
summation means (SM) adapted to sum said radiofrequency analog
signals (ARFSi).

2. Electronic device according to claim 1, wherein said notch
filter comprises N identical radiofrequency digital to analog conversion
blocks, N being greater than or equal to 2, and said digital delay means
(DM) includes a chain of N-1 delay blocks respectively connected
between the N first input means.

3. Electronic device according to claim 1 or 2, wherein the
delay’s value of the delay means is smaller than 1/(30*BW), where BW
is the frequency band width of the radiofrequency analog signal.

4.Electronic device according to any one of the preceding
claims, wherein the delay means are programmable.

5. Electronic device according to any one of the preceding
claims, realized within an integrated circuit.

6. Wireless apparatus, including an electronic device according
to any one of the preceding claims.

7. Method for notch filtering a digital signal delivered by sigma-
delta modulation means operating with a clock signal, comprising
delaying (40) said digital signal using said clock signal for obtaining at
least one delayed digital signal, the frequency of a notch of said notch
