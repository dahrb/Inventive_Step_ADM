MAIN REQUEST, clean

CLAIMS

l. A composition for use in preventing mastitis in cows by forming a physical
barrier in the teat canal of the cow during the cow's dry period and simultaneously
preventing black spot defect in dairy products made with milk from the cow, the
composition comprising:

at least 30% by weight of a bismuth-free, non-toxic compound selected from
titanium dioxide, zinc oxide, barium sulfate, or combinations thereof, dispersed in a gel

base.

2. The composition for use in presenting mastitis according to claim 1, wherein

the composition does not contain antibiotics.

3. The composition for use in presenting mastitis according to claim 1,

comprising 50% to 75% by weight of the bismuth-free, non-toxic compound.

4. The composition for use in presenting mastitis according to claim 1,

comprising 65% by weight of the bismuth-free, non-toxic compound.

5. The composition for use in presenting mastitis according to claim 1, wherein

the gel base comprises aluminum stearate.

6. The composition for use in presenting mastitis according to claim 1, wherein

the gel base comprises liquid paraffin.
