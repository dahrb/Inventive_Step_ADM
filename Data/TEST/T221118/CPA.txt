Title: Spiral conveyor

Abstract: (57) [Summary] [Object] To provide a spiral conveyor that can sort and carry out articles to be sorted out of articles transported in a spiral transport path to the outside of the spiral transport path. A discharge path 33 is provided in a part of a spiral transport path 2 for transporting the article Y, and a sensor 37 detects the presence or absence of the article Y to be sorted among the articles Y transported through the transport path 2. Then, the sorting mechanism 32 having the endless belt 36 that shields the transport path 2 and biases the articles Y to be sorted toward the discharge path 33 side is provided. In addition, a sorting mechanism is provided which causes the rotating roller to ride the article and urges the article in the discharge path direction. Further, a merging mechanism for urging the article toward the transport path while the article is positioned higher than the transport surface of the spiral transport path by the rotating roller is provided.
