TITLE:
Viewing angle adjustment system for a monitor

TYPE:
US Patent

ABSTRACT:
This invention provides a support arm to couple a monitor to a surface such as a wall or ceiling to remotely adjust the viewing angle of the monitor. The support arm includes at least one motor along a pivot axis in the support arm. The motor may be activated by a processor based on the control signal provided by the remote control. The support arm may include a plurality of pivot axes with a motor in each of the pivot axis to adjust the viewing angle along the vertical and horizontal planes. The support arm may be also provided with a tracking device that follows the movement of the viewer to adjust the viewing angle of the monitor corresponding to the movement of the viewer. The support arm may also receive periodic signal from the remote control to determine the location of the remote control. Based on the periodic signal, the processor may adjust the viewing angle of the monitor towards the location of the remote control.

CLAIMS:
1. A system for adjusting the viewing angle of a monitor, the system comprising;
a support arm capable of coupling a flat monitor to a wall, where the support arm is capable of moving the flat monitor along a first plane based on a control signal to adjust the monitor along the first plane; and
a remote control capable of sending the control signal to the support arm to adjust the viewing angle of the flat monitor along the first plane.
2. The system according to claim 1, where the flat monitor is a plasma television.
3. The system according to claim 1, where the support arm has a plurality of channels, where each channel is capable of pivoting relative to each other to move the monitor from a first position to a second position.
4. The system according to claim 3, where each of the channels is capable of pivoting about a pivot axis with a motor, where activation of the motor causes the channel to pivot about the pivot axis.
5. The system according to claim 1, further including a processor to calculate the control signal to move the support arm along the first plane so that the flat monitor moves from a first position to a second position with minimal delay.
6. The system according to claim 1, further including a memory capable of storing a desired movement of the support arm to move the flat monitor from a first position to a second position along a predetermined path.
7. The system according to claim 1, where the remote control includes a transmitter to send a location signal from the remote control, where the support arm includes a tracking device capable of monitoring the location signal and adjusting the viewing angle of the flat monitor based on the location signal.
8. A method for adjusting the viewing angle of a monitor based on the movement of a viewer, the method comprising:
coupling a flat monitor to a wall;
tracking the location of a viewer; and
adjusting the viewing angle of the monitor based on the location of the viewer.
9. The method according to claim 8, where the tracking is through the movement of the viewer.
10. The method according to claim 8, where the tracking is through receiving a voice command from the viewer.
11. The method according to claim 8, where the tracking is through the location of the viewer's head motion.
12. The method according to claim 8, where the flat monitor is a video phone and the tracking is through the location of the voice of the viewer.
13. A method for adjusting the viewing angle of a monitor based on the location of a remote control, the method comprising:
coupling a flat monitor to a wall;
sending a periodic signal from the remote control; and
receiving the periodic signal from the remote control to track the location of the remote control; and
adjusting the viewing angle of the monitor based on the location of the remote control.
