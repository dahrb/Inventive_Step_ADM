1. A method of dispensing restricted products from an authorized vendor to an

approved purchaser which includes the steps of:

b)

providing a dispenser containing an inventory (20) of restricted goods;
providing an audio communication link (48,54) from the dispenser to the
authorized vendor;

providing means in the dispenser to enable the vendor to verify the
purchaser's status as an approved purchaser;

providing an inventory system that includes product storage in rows and
columns and a product identification system that identifies the location of
each product by its row and column;

providing a product selection device that verifies that the product selected
is correct and holds and carries the product from its storage location to one
or more of a printing location, viewing location and issue tray (60) all located
within the dispenser;

providing visual viewing means for the vendor to view the product (44A)
before it is placed in the issue tray (60);

providing a payment transaction system (43) in the dispenser to verify
payment for the product;

maintaining locked the issue tray (60) until the vendor releases the product
to the purchaser; and

the dispenser including a reject system (62,63, 65 to 68 and 70) that
securely removes the product (44A) to a reject hopper (70) at any time after
the product is held by the product selection device but prior to the vendor
releasing the product from the issue tray (60).

2. A method as claimed in claim 1 and including the steps of one or more of printing

a label for the product, a receipt and a repeat authorization, on one or more printers

(46,47,57).

3. A method as claimed in claim 1 which also includes a management server

having a database of authorized vendors, approved purchasers, purchaser locations

and dispensing terminals, the management server being programmed to assist

communication between a purchaser and the dispensing terminal.
