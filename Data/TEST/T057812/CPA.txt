TITLE:
Process of Dispensing Pharmaceuticals

TYPE:
Australian Patent

CLAIMS:
1. A process of dispensing a package of pharmaceuticals to a purchaser or the like, Z the process including: dispensing a package of pharmaceuticals from at least one dispensing machine which is adapted to dispense at least one of a plurality of packages of pharmaceuticals contained therein, the dispensing machine being in communication with an operator situated remote from the dispensing machine, the _dispensing machine including: at least one video capture device adapted to capture an image of at least one of the purchaser, a prescription or the like for transmission to the operator and a label which has been attached to a package, audio communications means for establishing communications between the operator and the purchaser, and means operable by the operator to release a package of pharmaceuticals to the purchaser.
2. A process as claimed in claim 1, in which the at least one dispensing machine includes means for printing consumer medical information.
3. A process as claimed in claim 1 or claim 2, in which the at least one dispensing machine further includes means adapted to issue, consequent on the presentation of a prescription, a printed repeat authorisation which authorises the dispensing of at least one further supply of the pharmaceutical.
4. A process as claimed in any one of the preceding claims, in which the at least one dispensing machine includes means for printing a label or the like and for fixing that label to the package of pharmaceuticals.
5. A process as claimed in any one of the preceding claims, in which the at least one dispensing machine includes means which is adapted for receiving a pharmaceutical prescription and for retaining the pharmaceutical prescription within the at least one remote vending machine..
