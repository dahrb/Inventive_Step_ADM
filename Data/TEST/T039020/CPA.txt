TITLE:
Vibration damping method applied to e.g. papermaking roller scraper fits vibration damper of low resonant frequency

TYPE:
German Patent

ABSTRACT:
Object of this invention is a method for damping the vibrations of a carrier-like piece (1). At the connection of the carrier-like piece (1) at least one vibration damper (2) is attached, whose natural frequency is chosen so that it is lower than the frequency or the frequency band of the vibration to be damped.

CLAIMS:
Method for damping vibrations of a carrier-like piece ( 1 ), characterized in that at the connection of the piece ( 1 ) at least one vibration damper ( 2 ) whose natural frequency is lower than the frequency or the frequency band of the vibration to be damped. A method according to claim 1, characterized in that as a vibration damper ( 2 ) on the piece ( 1 ) to be fastened, resilient and damping damping element ( 3 ) is used, in whose space a mass ( 4 ) is adjusted. A method according to claim 1 or 2, characterized in that the vibration damper ( 2 ) is attached to the vaulting point of the waveform to be damped. Any method according to claim 2, characterized in that as a damping element ( 3 ) a wire rope is used. Vibration-damped carrier-like piece ( 1 ), characterized, at least by one to the connection of the piece ( 1 ) adapted vibration dampers ( 2 ) whose natural frequency is chosen to be lower than the frequency or frequency band of the vibration to be damped. Piece ( 1 ) according to claim 5, characterized in that the vibration damper ( 2 ) on the piece ( 1 ) to be fastened, resilient and damping damping element ( 3 ), in whose space a mass ( 4 ) is adjusted. Piece ( 1 ) according to claim 5 or 6, characterized in that the vibration damper ( 2 ) is attached to the vaulting point of the waveform to be damped.
