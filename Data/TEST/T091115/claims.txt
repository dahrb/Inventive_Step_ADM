Main Request

1. A method for processing Hybrid Automatic Repeat Request, hereinafter
HARQ, feedback information in a mobile terminal of a mobile communication
system, wherein the mobile terminal (120) communicates with one base station (110)
via two or more sectors (yl-y3) associated with the one base station (110), the
method comprising:

transmitting from the mobile terminal (120) a data packet to the base station
(110) through two or more sectors (yl-y3);

receiving at the mobile terminal (120) a plurality of common ACK/NACK
signals for the transmitted data packet from each of the two or more sectors by
applying a HARQ transmission scheme, combining the plurality of common
ACK/NACK signals into one combined ACK/NACK information when the mobile
terminal knows that the mobile terminal is communicating with the one base station

through the two or more sectors.

2. The method of claim 1, further comprising:
decoding the combined ACK/NACK information.

3. Mobile terminal operated in a mobile communication system employing a
HARQ transmission scheme, the mobile terminal comprising:

a transmitter (121) adapted to transmit a data packet to the base station (110)
through two or more sectors (yl-y3) associated with the one base station (110);

a receiver (122) adapted to receive a plurality of common ACK/NACK
signals for the transmitted data packet from each of the two or more sectors (yl-y3)
of the one base station (110) by applying a HARQ transmission scheme, a controller
(124) adapted to combine the plurality of common ACK/NACK signals into one
combined ACK/NACK information, when the mobile terminal knows that the mobile

terminal is communicating with the one base station through the two or more sectors.

4, The mobile terminal of claim 3, wherein the controller further adapted to
decoding the combined ACK/NACK information.
