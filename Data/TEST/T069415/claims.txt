CLAIMS — Main Request

An arrangement (10, 30) for visual indication of an electrical quantity, said electrical
quantity being a level of current and/or power consumption of one or several
apparatuses, the arrangement comprising a power cable having a conductor (11,
12, 13) for transmitting current to said one or several apparatuses,

wherein the arrangement further comprises a wire shaped illuminator (14, 15, 16)
and a controller (34, 40), said controller (34, 40) comprising:

a measuring arrangement (43) for measuring said level of current and/or
power consumption;

a processing arrangement (42) for adapting a control signal to control at
least one illumination characteristic of said illuminator with respect to said electrical
quantity, wherein said at least one illumination characteristic is one or several of
intensity, colour or frequency, and

a driver (45a-45c) for driving said illuminator receiving the control signal from
said processing arrangement,

wherein said wire shaped illuminator is arranged along said conductor (11, 12, 13).

The arrangement of claim 1, wherein said wire shaped illuminator is

electroluminescent (14, 15, 16).

The arrangement of claim 1, wherein said wire shaped illuminator is an optical fibre.

The arrangement of claim 2, wherein said electroluminescent includes a central
conductive wire (141, 151, 161) coated with a phosphorus material coating (141,
152, 162) and transmitter wires wrapped around it, and covered by coating and a

colored vinyl coat.
