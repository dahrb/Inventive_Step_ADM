TITLE:
Power supply facility for airplane

TYPE:
Japanese Patent

ABSTRACT:
PURPOSE:To reduce installation space in the vicinity of each spot by converting a commercial power to D.C. power at a terminal building, and thereafter converting it to power for planes and power for battery units of service vehicles at several spots. CONSTITUTION:Inside an electric apparatus room in a terminal building 1, one common large capacity rectifier 3 for converting the commercial power to the D.C. power as well as transforming facility of commercial power 2 are arranged, respectively. The D.C. power from the rectifier 3 is distributed to a small capacity inverter 7 positioned in the vicinity of each of plural spots 6 via power cable 5 along a plurality of bridges, while it is distributed to a plane in a waiting position from an underground pit 9 through power cable 8 buried underground. A converter 11 mainly comprising chopper for converting the magnitude of D.C. power from the rectifier 3 is installed at each inverter 7. The D.C. power of the converter 11 is charged to a battery unit 13 of service vehicle for airplanes.

CLAIMS:
1. The terminal building side is equipped with power receiving and transforming equipment for commercial power and a rectifier that converts the commercial power into DC power,
An aircraft power supply facility comprising, near each spot, an inverter that converts the DC power into aircraft power, and a converter that converts the DC power into power for a battery unit of a service vehicle. 2. The terminal building side is equipped with commercial power receiving and transforming equipment and a rectifier that converts the commercial power into DC power, and near each spot is equipped with an inverter that converts the DC power into aircraft power. An aircraft power supply facility comprising a converter located at a remote location that converts the DC power into power for a battery unit of a service vehicle. 3. The terminal building side is equipped with commercial power receiving and transforming equipment and a rectifier that converts the commercial power into DC power, and near each spot is equipped with an inverter that converts the DC power into aircraft power. An aircraft power supply facility comprising converters for converting the DC power into power for the battery unit of a service vehicle both on the building side and near each spot.
