CLAIMS

1. A semiconductor based device, comprising:

a plurality of active semiconductor layers (14, 16, 18);

a field plate on said active semiconductors;

a gate (24), a source (20), and a drain (22) on said
semiconductor layers (14, 16, 18);

characterised by a negative ion region (56) within at least one of
said plurality of semiconductor layers (14, 16, 18) and arranged partially in a
region below said gate and primarily between said gate and said drain and
below a portion of said field plate.

2. The semiconductor device of claim 1, comprising a high electron
mobility transistor (HEMT).

3. The semiconductor device of claim 1, wherein said active
semiconductor layers comprise Group-lll nitride materials and wherein said

negative ion region comprises fluorine ions.

4. The semiconductor device of claim 1, wherein said negative ion region
has sections with different depths in said active semiconductor layers.

5. The semiconductor device of claim 1, wherein said negative ion region
comprises different concentrations of negative ions at different locations.

6. A method for fabricating a semiconductor based device, comprising:
providing a substrate (92);
depositing epitaxial layers on said substrate (94);
forming a negative ion region within at least one of said epitaxial layers
(96);
forming a source electrode, a drain electrode, and a gate;
forming a plurality of field plates; and
