NEW MAIN REQUEST

1. | A composition for use in a method of treatment of a patient with short stature but having
no achondroplasia and wherein said short stature is selected from the group consisting of:
(1) _ short stature caused by endocrine abnormalities,
(2) short stature caused by non-endocrine abnormalities, and
(3) secondary short stature caused by chemotherapy or radiation therapy,
wherein the composition comprises type C natriuretic peptide (CNP) as an active
ingredient and is administered by injection, wherein the CNP is CNP-53 from mammals,

including human, or birds.
2. The composition of claim 1, wherein the CNP is CNP-53 of SEQ ID NO: 2.
3. The composition of claim 1, wherein the CNP has an amino acid sequence having 95%

or more identity with the amino acid sequence of SEQ ID NO: 2 and has the activity of
increasing the body height of an individual.
