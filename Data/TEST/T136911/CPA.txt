TITLE:
A General Cartographic Labeling Algorithm

TYPE:
Academic paper

ABSTRACT:
Some apparently powerful algorithms for automatic label placement on maps use heuristics that
capture considerable cartographic expertise but are hampered by provably inefficient methods
of search and optimization. On the other hand, no approach to label placement that is based on
an efficient optimization technique has been applied to the production of general cartographic
maps — those with labeled point, line, and area features — and shown to generate labelings of
acceptable quality. We present an algorithm for label placement that achieves the twin goals of
practical efficiency and high labeling quality by combining simple cartographic heuristics with
effective stochastic optimization techniques.
