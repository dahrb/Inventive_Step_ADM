NEW CLAIMS - Main Request

1. A method executed on a computing device to provide large data management in a
communication application through multiple mailboxes, the method comprising:

detecting a predefined size threshold of a mailbox associated with a user being reached,
wherein the mailbox is one of a primary mailbox and a first archive mailbox;

upon detection that the predefined size threshold of the mailbox being reached, creating
an additional archive mailbox for the user in chained manner connected to one of the primary
mailbox and the first archive mailbox; and

storing communication application related data in the additional archive mailbox, wherein
data in the mailbox and in the additional archive mailbox are available for one or more
functionalities of the communication application in a transparent manner to the user; wherein
folders are stored in various mailboxes with pointers to those folders in other mailboxes being
placed in the primary mailbox or the first archive mailbox, so that a user may click on a folder that
exists in a different mailbox and open the folder and see its contents without a noticeable
difference; and

if inactivity is detected after a predefined period of time, placing the additional archive
mailbox on hold and removing the additional archive mailbox permanently upon expiration of a

hold period.

2. The method of claim 1, further comprising:
storing the primary mailbox, the first archive mailbox, and the additional archive mailbox
in one or more data stores, wherein the data stores include one of local data stores and online

data stores.

3. The method of claim 1, further comprising:
managing storage of the primary mailbox, the first archive mailbox, and the additional

archive mailbox through one or more servers.

4. The method of claim 1, further comprising:
for communication application related data not stored in the primary mailbox or the first
archive mailbox, creating one or more pointers to respective additional archive mailboxes in the

primary mailbox or the first archive mailbox.
