This appeal is against the examining division's decision posted on 11 February 2020, refusing European patent application No. 16 711 454.5. The application was refused for lack of inventive step (Article 56 EPC) of a main request and auxiliary requests I to VI in view of the disclosure of: D1: US 2011/264630. The following documents were also cited in the decision: D2: P. Cunningham: "How to Create an Archive Mailbox in Exchange Server 2010", 20 November 2010, pages 2 to 4, D3: "Archive Manager RESTful Web Service API", 31 January 2013, page 4, D5: US 2013/212200, D6: US 7505571, and D7: EP 0840491. II. Notice of appeal was received on 31 March 2020, and the appeal fee was paid the same day. The statement setting out the grounds of appeal was received on 8 June 2020. The appellant requested that the decision be set aside and that a patent be granted on the basis of the main request or auxiliary requests I to VI on which the decision was based, or auxiliary request VII  filed with the statement setting out the grounds of appeal. Oral proceedings were requested in the event that the main request was not allowed. III. A summons to oral proceedings was issued on 11 March 2022. In a communication pursuant to Article 15(1) RPBA, sent on 19 September 2022, the board gave its preliminary opinion, which was that the main request and auxiliary requests I to VI did not meet the requirements of Article 56 EPC in the light of the disclosure of D1 in combination with the disclosures of D5 and D7. Further, the board expressed the opinion that under Article 12(4) RPBA auxiliary request VII should not be admitted into the appeal proceedings. IV. Oral proceedings were held on 4 November 2022. The appellant requested that the decision under appeal be set aside and that a patent be granted on the basis of the main request or on the basis of any one of auxiliary requests I to VII, the main request and auxiliary requests I to VI as filed by letter dated 23 October 2019, auxiliary request VII as filed with the statement setting out the grounds of appeal. The board's decision was announced at the end of the oral proceedings. V. Claim 1 of the main request reads as follows: "A method executed on a computing device to provide large data management in a communication application through multiple mailboxes, the method comprising: detecting a predefined size threshold of a mailbox associated with a user being reached, wherein the mailbox is one of a primary mailbox and a first archive mailbox; upon detection that the predefined size threshold of the mailbox being reached, creating an additional archive mailbox for the user in chained manner connected to one of the primary mailbox and the first archive mailbox; and storing communication application related data in the additional archive mailbox, wherein data in the mailbox and in the additional archive mailbox are available for one or more functionalities of the communication application in a transparent manner to the user; wherein folders are stored in various mailboxes with pointers to those folders in other mailboxes being placed in the primary mailbox or the first archive mailbox, so that a user may click on a folder that exists in a different mailbox and open the folder and see its contents without a noticeable difference; and if inactivity is detected after a predefined period of time, placing the additional archive mailbox on hold and removing the additional archive mailbox permanently upon expiration of a hold period." The main request includes further independent claims directed to a corresponding computing device (claim 6) and a corresponding computer program product (claim 9). Due to the outcome of the appeal, there is no need to detail the claims of the auxiliary requests.
