Claims

1.

A method of reducing detectability of an encryption key used in a
communication network (1) to encrypt a message transmitted between a

base station (10) and a mobile station (2), the method comprising:

determining (S31) in the message one or more selected bits (e1-e11) at

random positions in the message; and

generating (S3) in the message random bit errors by inverting the selected

bits (e1-e11), prior to transmitting the message over the air.

The method of claim 1, wherein the selected bits (e1-e11) are determined

(S31) at the physical layer (110).

The method of one of claims 1 or 2, wherein the message is a known control

message.

The method of one of claims 1 to 3, wherein the message is a physical layer
data frame (L1) encoding a data link layer control frame (L2), and the
selected bits (e1-e11) are determined (S31) at random positions in the

physical layer data frame (L1).

The method of one of claims 1 to 4, wherein the selected bits (e1-e11) are
determined (S31) so that the message contains at least one random bit

error within any sequence (S) of consecutive bits having a length N.
