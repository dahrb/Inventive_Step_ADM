TITLE:
Bile acid conjugates, derivatives thereof with metal complexes and related uses

TYPE:
Patent

ABSTRACT:
The invention relates to novel paramagnetic metal ion chelates and their use as contrast agents in the diagnostic technique known as 'magnetic resonance imaging' (M.R.I.).
