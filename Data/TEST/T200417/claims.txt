European Patent Application no. 11182963.6

Main Request

4 August 2017

1. A device (100), comprising:

a touch screen display (112);

one or more processors (120);

memory (102); and

one or more programs, wherein the one or more programs are stored in the
memory (102) and configured to be executed by the one or more processors (120), the one or
more programs including instructions for:

displaying at least a first portion of an electronic document at a first
magnification (1102);

detecting a gesture on or near the touch screen display corresponding to a
command to zoom in by a user-specified amount (1104);

displaying decreasing portions of the electronic document at increasing
magnifications, in response to detecting the gesture (1106); and

characterized in that

the one or more programs further include instructions for:

displaying a respective portion of the electronic document at a predefined
magnification if, upon detecting termination of the gesture, the magnification exceeds the

predefined magnification (1112).

2. The device of claim 1, including instructions for:

displaying a last decreased portion of the electronic document at a first resolution,
immediately prior to detecting termination of the gesture; and

displaying the respective portion of the electronic document at a second resolution that is
greater than the first resolution, upon detecting termination of the gesture.

3. The device of claim 1, wherein the gesture comprises a de-pinching gesture.

4. The device of claim 1, wherein the device is a portable multifunction device.
