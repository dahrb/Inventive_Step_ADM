CLAIMS

A system for recommending wellness products, the system comprising:

means for receiving via the Internet at least one of a gender, age,
wellness goal, medical condition, and biometric indicator for a user; and

means, responsive to the one of the gender, age, wellness goal,
medical condition, and biometric indicator for the user and responsive to at
least one scientific efficacy rating for a product ingredient contained in one or
more ingestible wellness products in a database, for recommending a set of
two or more wellness products to the user via an electronic device.

A system according to claim 1, wherein:

the system is an ecommerce system for over-the-counter health and
fitness products;

the means for receiving is a means for collecting via the Internet at
least one of a gender, age, fitness goal, medical condition, and biometric
indicator for a user; and

the means for recommending is a means, responsive to at least one of
the collected gender, age, fitness goal, medical condition, and biometric
indicator for the user and responsive to at least one scientific efficacy rating
for a product ingredient contained in one or more health and fitness products
in a database, for recommending a subset of the health and fitness products
to the user via an electronic device, wherein two or more products in the
presented set are presented in rank order of efficacy for the user, wherein:

the one of the collected gender, age, fitness goal, medical condition
and biometric indicator may be a fitness goal and at least one of the two or
more products may be indicated as being proven to be scientifically effective
for pursuing or attaining the fitness goal of the user; OR

the one of the collected gender, age, fitness goal, medical condition
and biometric indicator may be a medical condition and at least one of the two
or more products in the presented set may be indicated as being scientifically
contraindicative or not recommended based on the medical condition of the
user and one or more ingredients of the one product.

The system of claim 2, further comprising:

means for accepting an online order for one or more of the presented
products; and

means for awarding loyalty points to the user for the online order; and

means for sharing a portion of a user payment for the online order with
a fitness club, personal trainer, massage therapist, or healthcare provider
identified by the user, wherein the fitness club, personal trainer, massage
therapist, or healthcare products are not retailers or wholesalers of the
presented products.
