Main Request

1. A method for administering a consumer direct pet care system for offering customized pet
care products according to an individual preference of a customer, said method comprising
the steps of:

gathering data characterising the pet from the customer, the data comprising age, size and
breed of the pet, pet care information and pet health information,

compiling this data to generate an individual pet profile (80);

accepting a pet care product order from the potential customer based upon information in the
individual pet profile (80); and

processing the pet care product order in accordance with the individual pet profile (80), which
comprises the steps of manufacturing (94) and packaging (96) the order in accordance with

data contained in the individual pet profile (80).

2. A method in accordance with Claim 1, wherein the pet care products include dry pet foods,
said step of gathering data characterizing the pet further comprises the step of obtaining
information on at least one of a desired nutrient level for the food, a desired flavor of the food,

a desired kibble size, a desired kibble shape, and a desired food supplement.

3. A method in accordance with Claim 2 wherein the step of gathering data characterizing the
pet comprises the steps of:

maintaining an electronic customer interface (76) including information fields for
completion by the customer indicating customer preference of selected product
characteristics;

and storing the completed information fields for future reference.

4. A method in accordance with Claim 1 wherein the step of accepting a pet care product
order comprises the step of accepting an order for at least one of a completely custom-made
food order, a custom-component food order, a custom-finish food order, and a custom-care

order.

5. A method in accordance with Claim 1 wherein said step of gathering data characteristic the
pet comprises the step of obtaining information from the customer regarding the desired

quantity of a pet care product.

6. A method in accordance with Claim 5 wherein the pet care product is dry pet food, and
wherein said step of gathering data characterizing the pet from the customer regarding the
desired quantity of a pet care product comprises the step of maintaining an electronic
customer interface (76) including information fields for completion by the customer
indicating customer preference of at least one of a desired number of cups of food, a desired
number of servings of food, a desired bulk quantity of food, and a desired expected time until

food depletion.

7. A method in accordance with Claim 1 further comprising the step of generating a customer

demand for consumer direct pet care products by advertising.

8. A method in accordance with Claim 7 wherein said step of generating a customer demand
further comprises the step of generating a re-order notice to the customer from information

contained in the individual pet profile (80).

9. A method in accordance with Claim 8 wherein said step of generating a customer demand
further comprises the step of renewing a pet care product subscription of the purchaser based

upon information contained in the individual pet profile (80).

10. A method in accordance with Claim 7 further comprising the step of generating reports
from a plurality of individual pet profiles of a plurality of customers for use to target other

potential customers.

11. A method in accordance with Claim 1 further comprising the step of shipping each

accepted order directly to the customer.

12. A method in accordance with Claim 11 wherein the step of shipping each accepted offer

further comprises the step of utilizing existing third party distribution systems.

13. A method in accordance with Claim 1 wherein the method further comprises the step of

generating an Individual Care Plan from information contained in the individual pet profile.

14. An apparatus for monitoring a production system of customized pet care products using a
process management and workflow system coupled to a data repository, said apparatus
comprising:

a data repository (16,18,24); and

a process management and workflow system (10,50) configured to be coupled to a production
facility by a communications link (24), said process management and workflow system
configured to:

compile an individual pet profile (80) from information provided by the customer, the
information comprising data characterizing the pet, the data comprising age, size and breed of
the pet, pet care information and pet health information,

receive customized orders (64) from a plurality of customers via an electronic customer
interface (76) and based upon information in the individual pet profile (80);

load data from the customized orders (64) into the data repository (16,18,24); and
communicate with the production facility via the communication link (24), control equipment

runs during production, and track products through delivery to the point of distribution.

15. An apparatus in accordance with Claim 14 wherein the communication link (24)

comprises at least one of a wide area network and a local area network.

16. An apparatus in accordance with Claim 14 wherein the customized orders (64) include
data related to at least one of a desired nutrient level for a pet food, a desired flavor of a pet
food, a desired kibble size for a pet food, a desired kibble shape for a pet food, and a desired

food supplement for a pet food.

17. An apparatus in accordance with Claim 14 wherein the customized orders include an
order for at least one of a completely custom-made food order, a custom-component food

order, a custom-finish food order, and a custom-care order.

18. An apparatus in accordance with Claim 14 wherein the customized orders include data
related to at least one of a desired number of cups of food, a desired number of servings of
food, a desired bulk quantity of food, and a desired expected time until food depletion.
