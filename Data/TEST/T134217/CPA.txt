TITLE:
A getter/ion atomisation combination pump for high vacuum and ultra-high vacuum systems

TYPE:
UK Patent

ABSTRACT:
A combination pump comprises a getter sorption pump (3) consisting of non-vapourising getter material for high and ultra-high vacuum systems and an ion atomisation pump (1). <IMAGE>

CLAIMS:
1. A combination pump for high and ultrahigh vacuum systems comprising an ion atomisation pump in combination with a getter sorption pump.
2. A combination pump as claimed in Claim 1, in which the getter material of the ion atomisation pump consists of titanium.
3. A combination pump as claimed in claims 1 or 2 in which the getter sorption pump comprises at least one getter element of zirconium, titanium, thorium, tantalum, platinum, niobium, cerium, palladium or mixtures or alloys thereof.
4. A combination pump as claimed in any preceding claim in which the getter element of the getter sorption pump is provided with a heating element.
5. A combination pump as claimed in any preceding claim connected t6 a receiver to be evacuated thereby, the getter sorption pump being disposed between the ion atomisation pump and the receiver.
6. A combination pump as claimed in claims 4 and 5 including means to activate the heating element while the ion atomisation pump is working but to deactivate the heating element when it is not.
7. A combination pump substantially as described with reference to the drawings.
