TITLE:
Treating cardiovascular diseases with a compound of formula (i) (cs 747 - prasugrel; rn 150322-43-4)

TYPE:
World International Patent Organisation

ABSTRACT:
A method of treating Cardiovascular Diseases comprising administering of a compound of formula (I) (chemical names i.a. CS747 or Prasugrel; registry number 150322-43-3) or a pharmaceutically acceptable salt, solvate, prodrug, active metabolite, racemate or enantiomer thereof, in conjunction with coronary and non-coronary intervention procedures is disclosed.
