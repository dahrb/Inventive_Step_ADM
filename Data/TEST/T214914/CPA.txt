Title: Method for producing a structural member from plates stacked on top of each other and soldered together

Abstract: The invention relates to a method for producing a structural member from plates (9, 10) stacked on top of each other, soldered together and provided with at least partial recesses (11, 12). At least one solder layer is provided between said plates in order to allow a solder diffusion process. According to the invention, the plates are stacked on top of each other, separated from each other by solder diffusion layers, and then compressed in a cold condition before the solder diffusion process. Thanks to this measure, the need for expensive compression tools can be avoided in the solder diffusion process itself.

Title: Heat exchanger, especially oil/coolant cooler

Abstract: The invention relates to a heat exchanger, especially a stacked plate oil cooler (1) having a plate-type design. The heat exchanger is characterized in that two adjacent heat exchanger plates (2) define a space between them through which the heat exchange medium or a second medium that is to be cooled or heated flows. At one end of said heat exchanger, a base plate (3) lies substantially flat against the adjacent outmost heat exchanger plate (2) of the heat exchanger. Said base plate (3) is provided with a recess (5) whose contour follows that of the heat exchanger plate (2).
