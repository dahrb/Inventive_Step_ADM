CLAIMS

1. A method of generating origin signals of an encoder having a scale(10) on
which an incremental pattern(12) and an origin pattern(14) are formed,

the method comprising initially detecting the origin pattern and generating a
corresponding origin signal

characterised in that the method further comprises

initially generating a first origin signal with preset width(P) at an origin position
actually detected by an initial operation after power-on;

then generating following origin signals with the preset width(P) using
software, the following origin signals being generated at positions of predetermined
counted value of DOWN pulses and UP pulses output from an internal counter (48)
based upon a main signal waveform obtained from the incremental pattern, wherein
the first origin pattern is actually detected in the first operation alone, and the
following origin signals are subsequently generated virtually by software according to
the counted value of the internal counter (48).

2. An apparatus for generating an origin signal of an encoder having a scale(10)
on which an incremental pattern (12) and an origin pattern (14) are formed,

the apparatus comprising:

means for obtaining an origin waveform from the origin pattern(14);

means for obtaining a main signal waveform form the incremental pattern
(12);

means for generating a first origin signal with preset width(P) at an origin
position actually detected by an initial operation after power-on;
an internal counter (48) for counting the main signal waveform and outputting
counted value of DOWN pulses and UP puslese;

means for generating following origin signals with preset width(P) using
software, wherein the organ pattern is actually detected in the first operation alone,
and the following origin signals are subsequently generated virtually by software
according to the counted value of the internal counter (48).
