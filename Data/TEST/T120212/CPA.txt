TITLE:
Electronic part and manufacturing method thereof

TYPE:
World International Patent Organisation

ABSTRACT:
An electronic part includes a conductor pattern (2) on an insulating substrate (1). A metal film is arranged by plating on the surface of the conductor pattern (2). The metal film is oxidized to obtain a metal oxide layer (3) on the surface of the conductor pattern (2). Thus, a thin uniform insulating film is arranged on the conductor pattern. Accordingly, the electronic part has a high reliability with the conductor pattern having a high aspect ratio.
