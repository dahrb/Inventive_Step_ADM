NITTO DENKO CORPORATION

New Claims 1 and 2

. A wired circuit board (1) comprising:

an insulating base layer (3);

a seed film (11) formed on the insulating baser layer (3);

a conductive pattern (4) formed on the seed film (11);

a metal oxide layer (5) formed to cover the conductive pattern (4); and

an insulating cover layer (6) formed over the insulating base layer (3) to cover the
conductive pattern (4) covered by the metal oxide layer (5);

characterized in that:

the seed film (11) comprises a thin chrome film formed on the insulating base layer (3)
and a thin copper film formed on the thin chrome film;

the material of the metal oxide layer (5) is chrome oxide;

the thickness of the metal oxide layer (5) is 5 to 300 nm;

a surface resistance of the metal oxide layer (5) is not less than 10’? Q/0; and

the oxide layer (5) functions as a barrier layer to the conductive pattern (4) to prevent the

metal for forming the conductive pattern (4) from diffusing in the insulating cover layer

(6).

. The wired circuit board (1) according to claim 1, wherein the thin chrome film has a

thickness of 10 - 60 nm and the thin copper film has a thickness of 50 - 200 nm.
