Claims
New Main Request

1. A display device (100) comprising:

a display panel (110);

a flat frame (130) positioned in the rear of the display panel (110);

a backlight unit (120) positioned between the display panel (110) and the flat
frame (130) and providing light for the display panel (110), wherein the backlight
unit (120) includes a substrate (122) and a light source (124, 203) mounted on the
substrate (122);

at least one coupling protrusion (410) formed on the flat frame (130),
wherein the coupling protrusion (410) fixes the backlight unit (120) to the flat frame
(130); and

at least one inclined protrusion (420) formed on the flat frame (130) and
positioned under one end of the substrate (122), the inclined protrusion (420)
providing an inclination of the end of the substrate (122) facing in a longitudinal
direction of the substrate (122).

2. The display device of claim 1, wherein the inclined protrusion (420) protrudes
from a surface of the flat frame (130) facing the display panel (110).

3. The display device (100) of claims 1 or 2, wherein the substrate (122) has a
coupling groove (410h), into which the coupling protrusion (410) is inserted.

4. The display device (100) of claim 3, wherein the coupling groove (410h)
elongates on the substrate (122) along the longitudinal direction of the substrate
(122), and

wherein the coupling groove (410h) has a circular insertion groove (410R) at
one end.

5. The display device (100) of claim 4, wherein the coupling protrusion (410) has
a head (H) smaller than a diameter (D) of the insertion groove (410R), and

wherein the head (H) of the coupling protrusion (410) is larger than a width
(W) of the coupling groove (410h).

6. The display device (100) of any one of claims 1 to 5, wherein the backlight
unit (120) includes a plurality of light sources (124, 203),
