Title: Backlight assembly and liquid crystal display device having the same

Abstract: 

A backlight assembly and a liquid crystal display device having the same backlight assembly are provided. The backlight assembly includes a bottom case, a plurality of printed circuit boards (PCBs) disposed on the bottom case, wherein substantially all of the PCBs have predetermined inclinations with respect to a front surface of the bottom case, and a plurality of light emitting diodes (LEDs) mounted on the PCBs.

Type: 

US Patent

Claims: 

1. A backlight assembly, comprising:
a bottom case;
a plurality of printed circuit boards (PCBs) disposed on the bottom case, wherein substantially all of the PCBs have predetermined inclinations with respect to a front surface of the bottom case; and
a plurality of light emitting diodes (LEDs) mounted on the PCBs.
2. The backlight assembly according to claim 1, wherein all of the PCBs have predetermined inclinations with respect to a front surface of the bottom case.
3. The backlight assembly according to claim 1, wherein each PCB is formed such that it has two inclines with respect to the front surface of the bottom.
4. The backlight assembly according to claim 3, wherein said inclines form substantially equivalent angles on both sides of a direction perpendicular to the front surface of the bottom case.
5. The backlight assembly according to claim 1, wherein each PCB is bent in a Λ-shape and divided into first and second substrates with reference to the bent portion.
6. The backlight assembly according to claim 1, wherein opposing side edges of the bottom case are provided with a plurality of supports for supporting said plurality of PCBs.
7. The backlight assembly according to claim 6, wherein the supports protrude from the bottom case and are formed in a Λ-shape to support the PCBs.
8. The backlight assembly according to claim 7, wherein the supports protrude from the front surface of the bottom case.
9. The backlight assembly according to claim 6, wherein the opposing side edges of the bottom case are provided with air circulation holes formed between the front surface of the bottom case and the supports.
10. The backlight assembly according to claim 1, wherein the front surface of the bottom case is provided with a plurality of protrusions extending from one side to the other side of the front surface and arranged at predetermined intervals.
11. The backlight assembly according to claim 10, wherein a rear surface of the bottom case is provided with grooves corresponding to the protrusions.
12. The backlight assembly according to claim 11, wherein said grooves enhance heat dissipation efficiency.
13. The backlight assembly according to claim 11, wherein the protrusions and grooves are integrally formed with the bottom case.
14. The backlight assembly according to claim 1, wherein the LEDs are red, green and blue LEDs.
15. A liquid crystal display device comprising:
a bottom case;
a plurality of printed circuit boards (PCBs) disposed on the bottom case, wherein substantially all of the PCBs have predetermined inclinations with respect to a front surface of the bottom case;
a plurality of light emitting diodes (LEDs) mounted on the PCBs; and
a liquid crystal panel for displaying an image using the light emitted from the LEDs.
16. The liquid crystal display device according to claim 15, wherein said display device does not have a mixing plate.
17. The liquid crystal display device according to claim 15, wherein all of the PCBs have predetermined inclinations with respect to a front surface of the bottom case.
18. The liquid crystal display device according to claim 15, wherein each PCB is formed such that it has two inclines with respect to the front surface of the bottom.
19. The liquid crystal display device according to claim 18, wherein said inclines form substantially equivalent angles on both sides of a direction perpendicular to the front surface of the bottom case.
