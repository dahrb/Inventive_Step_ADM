Claims — Main Request

1.

A synthetic motor oil characterized by:

at least one polyol ester; and at least one hexagonal boron nitride, wherein the

hexagonal boron nitride has a particle size of 1 micron or less,

wherein the lubricant is further characterized by: at least one base oil selected
from the group comprising: group III petroleum oils, polyalphaolefins (PAOs),

and mixtures thereof,

wherein the lubricant is further characterized by: at least one additive or
combination of additives selected from the group comprising: anti-oxidants,
corrosion inhibitors, pour point depressants, metal deactivators, friction
reducers, viscosity modifiers, anti-wear inhibitors, friction modifiers, and

extreme pressure additives.

The synthetic motor oil of claim 1, wherein the base oil is 67.35% to 72.40% by
weight of the motor oil, the hexagonal boron nitride is 0.10% to 0.15% by
weight of the motor oil, the polyol ester is 13% by weight of the motor oil, and
the additives are 16.65% to 20.50% by weight of the motor oil.

The synthetic motor oil of claims 1 or 2, wherein the base oil is a mixture of
PAO4 and PAO6.

The synthetic motor oil of claims 1 or 2, wherein the base oil is a group III oil.

The synthetic motor oil of claims 1-4, wherein the motor oil is further

characterized by polytetrafluoroethylene.
