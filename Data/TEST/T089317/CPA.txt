TITLE:
Improved self-service terminal

TYPE:
European Patent

ABSTRACT:
A self-service terminal such as an ATM (10) is co-located with a human-form recognition engine such as a camera (22) and appropriate software (23). If there is no user adjacent the ATM (10), the ATM screen 24 is operated as an advertising bill board. If one person or a short queue is present, the ATM (10) provides a full range of services on demand. For a longer queue, the services available are limited, e.g. one transaction per user or only one type of transaction such as cash dispense only. For a bank of ATMs 10A to 10D, different ATMs are arranged to provide different services. Each ATM has a service indicator 19A to 19D to indicate the service or services available.

CLAIMS:
A responsive self-service terminal system characterized by a self-service terminal (10) arranged to provide a plurality of services, a human-form recognition means (22,23) arranged to sense a number of persons A,B,C beside the terminal (10), and control means (26) to vary the services provided by the terminal (10) in accordance with the sensed number.
A responsive system according to claim 1, characterized in that if said sensed number is 0, the service provided is a promotional advertising service.
A responsive system according to claim 1 or claim 2, characterized in that if said sensed number is between 1 and a predetermined number the terminal (10) is arranged to provide a plurality of on-demand services.
A responsive system according to any preceding claim, characterized in that if said sensed number is equal to or greater than the predetermined number, said terminal (10) is arranged to provide a restricted on-demand service.
A responsive system according to any preceding claim, characterized by at least one further self-service terminal (10B) arranged to provide a plurality of services, the control means 26 being arranged to vary the services provided at each terminal 10A, 10B so that at least one terminal provides a different service to the other terminal.
A responsive system according to any preceding claim in which each self-service terminal is an automated teller machine having display means 12, input means 14, cash dispense means 30 and cash/cheque accept means 32.
A method of controlling a self-service terminal which provides a plurality of services characterized by sensing the number of persons adjacent the terminal and varying the services provided in accordance with the sensed number.
A method of controlling a system comprising a plurality of self-service terminals each providing a plurality of services, characterized by sensing the number of persons adjacent the terminals and varying the services provided so that at least one terminal provides a service which differs from the services provided by the other terminal.
