TITLE:
Cluster-based business process management through eager displacement and on-demand recovery

TYPE:
European patent

ABSTRACT:
Methods and apparatus, including computer program products, are provided for transporting processes within a distributed computing system, such as a cluster. In one aspect, the computer-implemented method receives an event at a first node. The event corresponds to a process instance for handling the received event. The process instance is transported from a second node to the first node. The process instance is transported from a persistence when the process instance is inactive and, when the process instance is active, the process instance is persisted to enable transport to the first node. Related apparatus, systems, methods, and articles are also described.
