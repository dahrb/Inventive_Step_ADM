CLAIMS

What is claimed is:

1. Asystem for conducting a video game tournament having a plurality of levels,

starting with a first level and ending with a last level, and involving a plurality of

competing players, the system comprising a tournament server connected toa

communications network; each player has a respective player device and game

machine; a hosting network also connected to the communications network,

wherein the game machines are connected via the hosting network, wherein the

player devices are connected to the tournament server via the communications

network, wherein the matches are played on the hosting network and the hosting

network is configured to transmit the results of each match to the tournament

server via the communications network, and wherein the tournament server

includes:

a record of competing players registered for the tournament indicating at

which level each player is eligible to play;

a receiver configured to receive indications from the players that they are

ready to play a match;

a ready list for each level which includes players who have indicated that

they are ready to play a match and who are eligible to play at that level;

a processor configured to match players on each ready list, output a signal to
the matched players of the match and remove the matched players from the
ready list;

a receiver configured to receive the results of each completed match;

a processor configured to analyze the results to determine whether each
player won or lost the match, wherein the second processor is further
adapted to update the record of competing players to indicate that a winning

player is eligible to play at a higher level when the player wins a match that

is not at the last level;

wherein the tournament server is configured to award a grand prize to the
winning player when the player wins a match at the last level, and wherein each
pair of matched players comprises two players who are selected to compete

against each other in a match.

. The system of claim 1, wherein the player device and the game machine are the

same device.

. The system of claim 1 wherein the system presents to a challenger, who is a player
at a level, a list of other players on the ready list for that level, and allows the
challenger to challenge one of the listed players, whereby the system matches the

challenger with the challenged player.

. The system of any one of claims 1 to 3 wherein the first processor matches players

on each ready list at pre-determined intervals.

. The system of any one of claims 1 to 3 wherein the first processor matches players
on the ready list for a level when a pre-determined number of players eligible to

play that level are on the ready list.
. The system of claim 5 wherein the pre-determined number of players is two.

. The system of any one of claims 1 to 6 wherein the server is adapted to restrict the
registration of players onto the record of competing players based on the

geographic location of the players.

. The system of any one of claims 1 to 7 wherein the tournament comprises a
plurality of rounds, each round comprising a pre-determined number of levels and
wherein the second processor is adapted to promote a player to play in a higher

round after winning the last level of a prior round.

. The system of any one of claims 1 to 8 wherein the tournament server further

provides a syndication feed that outputs a summary of the state of the tournament.
