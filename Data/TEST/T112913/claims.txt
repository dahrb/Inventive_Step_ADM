MAIN REQUEST

Claims

1. A storage area network associated data library partitioning system (200)
characterised by:

a plurality of partitions (215-217) comprising at least one secured partition,
each of said plurality of partitions comprising at least one set of at least one
storage element slots (205) adapted to store data storage media and at least
one set of at least one data transfer elements (201-204) that are adapted to
receive said data storage media and transfer data to and from said data
storage media; and

a library controller (213) that assigns a different logical element designation
(LUN1, LUN2, LUNS3) to a virtual controller for each of said plurality of
partitions, said virtual controllers directing movement of said media to and
from said at least one set of slots and at least one set of data transfer
elements assigned to a same of said plurality of partitions,

wherein access to the or each said secured partition is restricted to users of
said storage area network having a unique host device identifier that is listed
in a list of unique host device identifiers for access to a particular secured
partition.

2. The system of claim 1, wherein a blank listing of unique host device
identifiers for a secured partition (215-217) results in said partition being

secured from access by any users.

3. The system of claim 1 or 2, wherein at least one of said plurality of
partitions (215-217) is unsecured allowing access to said unsecured partitions
by any user of said storage area network.

4. The system of claim 1, 2 or 3, wherein at least one of said elements is

disabled and said at least one disabled element may not be accessed by any

users.

5. The system of any preceding claim, wherein at least one of said plurality of
partitions has at least one storage element slot that is defined as an
import/export slot.

6. The system of any preceding claim, wherein at least one of said plurality of
partitions (215-217) has at least two of said data transfer elements assigned
to it that are connected to different SCSI buses.

7. The system of any preceding claim, comprising a library management
interface cooperating with said library controller (213) to implement selected
partitioning in response to user input.

8. A method for partitioning a storage area network associated data library
(200) characterised by:

establishing a plurality of partitions (215-217) comprising at least one secured
partition in said data library, each of said plurality of partitions comprising at
least one storage element slot (205) and at least one data transfer element
(201-204), each of said slots adapted to store media, and each of said data
transfer elements adapted to receive said media and transfer data to and from
said media;

assigning a different logical element designation (LUN1, LUN2, LUN3) to each
of said plurality of partitions (215-217);

controlling movement of said media to and from said at least one slot and at
least one data transfer element assigned to a same partition; and

securing the or each said secured partition by assigning a list of unique host
device identifiers that may access a particular secured partition.
