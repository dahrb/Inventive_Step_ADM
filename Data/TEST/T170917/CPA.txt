TITLE:
Insect repellant

TYPE:
World International Patent Organisation

ABSTRACT:
The invention relates to insect repellent compositions which are free from N-N-diethyl-m-toluamide (DEET). The insecticides are based around compositions of one or more essential oils, and are effective against mosquitoes, flies, sandflies, leeches fleas and ticks. Particularly preferred compositions include synergistic mixtures of two essential oils. Most preferably, the compositions are mixtures of backhousia citriodora oil and melaleuca ericafolia oil. It has also been found that adding film-formers to the present invention maintains the efficacy for around two hours at levels which are compatible to commercial DEET formulations.
