EP 12 198 009.8 (C 3103 EP)

HILFSANTRAG
Neue Anspriche 1 bis 8 - Reinschrift

1. A cosmetic composition comprising

(a) a cross-polymer which is a copolymerisation product of trimethylpentanediol,
glycerol and adipic acid, and

(b) at least one fragrance
in a ratio by weight (a:b) of from about 1:99 to about 1:20.

2. The composition of Claim 1, wherein the natural fragrances extracts of blossoms,
stems and leaves, fruits, fruit peel, roots, woods, herbs and grasses, needles and, res-
ins and balsams or animal sources and their mixtures.

3. The composition of Claim 1, wherein the synthetic fragrances are selected from the
group of aldehydes, ketones, alcohols, ethers, esters, hydrocarbons and their mixtures.

4. The composition of Claim 1, wherein the component (a) and (b) are present in a ratio
by weight (a:b) of about 1:80 to about 1:30.

5. The composition of Claim 1, wherein the components (a+b) are present in an amount
of about 0.01 to about 5 % b.w. — calculated on the total composition.

6. A fragrance composition consisting of
(a) across-polymer

which is a copolymerisation product of trimethylpentanediol, glycerol and adipic
acid, and

(b) at least one fragrance
in a ratio by weight (a:b) of from about 1:99 to about 1:20.

7. A method for improving the lasting of perfume delivery, wherein a fragrance (b) is
blended with a working amount of a cross-polymer which is a copolymerisation prod-
uct of trimethylpentanediol, glycerol and adipic acid in a ratio by weight (a:b) from
about 1:99 to about 1:20 , and the mixture thus obtained is applied to human skin or
hair.

8. The use of a cross-polymer which is a copolymerisation product of trimethylpentane-
diol, glycerol and adipic acid as fixative for fragrances (b) in a ratio by weight (a:b) of
from about 1:99 to about 1:20.
