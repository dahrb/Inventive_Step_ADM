Title: Manual chemical dispersing device

Abstract: A manual chemical dispersing device capable of manually dispersing various chemicals easily and accurately in up to several lots of them in advance with a range of each lot being instantly noticeable. The device comprises a frame (44) which allows a preliminary dispersing cassette (20) formed with many partition rooms (21) to be set or removed via the top surface, a lid (47) consisting of a plate element opening/closing the top surface and formed with many charging ports (48) penetrating it and arranged corresponding to the partition rooms of the cassette (20), many lighting members (49) provided to the lid and arranged to respectively correspond to the charging ports (48), a screen display unit (41) for displaying the name of a chemical prescribed to be manually dispersed, and a control unit (42) receiving a selection relating to the name of the chemical and lighting those of the lighting members (49) belonging to the manually dispersing range of the selected chemical.

Type: WIPO
