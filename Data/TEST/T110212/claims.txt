CLAIMS

1. A method of drawing in a superimposed window comprising at least a transparent
overlying window (102) and at least an underlying window (100), the method
including the steps of:

drawing in the underlying window (100),

identifying the transparent overlying window (102) over the underlying
window,

sending a signal instructing (E) a client of the identified transparent
overlaying window (102) which may be affected to redraw foreground objects in at
least part of the identified transparent overlaying window (102), so that drawing

affecting the identified transparent overlying window (102) can be corrected.

2. A method according to claim 1, wherein the signal is sent following each drawing

operation.

3. A method according to claim 2, further comprising suppressing the signal for at

least one drawing operation.

4. A method according to claim 1, 2 or 3, wherein the signal is sent from a window

manager (2050).

5. A receiver/decoder (13) comprising means for drawing in a superimposed window
comprising at least a transparent overlying window (102) and at least an underlying
window (100), the receiver/decoder being characterized in that it further comprises:

means for identifying a transparent overlying window over the determined
underlying window, and

means for sending a signal instructing a client of the identified transparent
overlaying window (102) which may be affected to redraw foreground objects in that
part of the identified transparent overlaying window (102), so that drawing affecting

the identified transparent overlying window (102) can be corrected.
