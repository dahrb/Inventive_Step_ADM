Main Request

1. A field effect transistor including a film comprising a nitride layer, the nitride
layer comprising aluminum and gallium and being on a silicon substrate, the nitride layer having
a first side adjacent to the silicon substrate and a second side opposite the first side, wherein

the nitride layer is a continuously graded nitride layer having a higher aluminum fraction
adjacent to the first side than adjacent to the second side,

the aluminum fraction of the nitride layer is at least 20% adjacent to the first side and is
less than 20% adjacent to the second side, and

the grade of the nitride layer is carried out over a thickness of 20-100% of the total

thickness of the film.

2. The field effect transistor of claim 1, wherein the grade of the nitride layer is

carried out over a thickness of 20-80% of a one micrometer-thick film.

3. The field effect transistor of claim 1, wherein the nitride layer comprises AlyGaj.

4, The field effect transistor of claim 1, further comprising an Al,Gaj.,N or
InGaAIN layer on the nitride layer.

5. A method of forming a field effect transistor, comprising

forming a film comprising a nitride layer, the nitride layer comprising aluminum and
gallium and being on a silicon substrate, the nitride layer having a first side adjacent to the
silicon substrate and a second side opposite the first side, wherein

the nitride layer is a continuously graded nitride layer having a higher aluminum fraction
adjacent to the first side than adjacent to the second side,

the aluminum fraction of the nitride layer is at least 20% adjacent to the first side and is

less than 20% adjacent to the second side, and
