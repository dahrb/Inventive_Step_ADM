Title: High electron mobility AlGaN/GaN heterostructure on (111) Si

Abstract: Room-temperature Hall mobilities exceeding 900 cm2/V s are obtained for AlGaN/GaN heterostructures on (111) Si by single-temperature flow modulation organometallic vapor phase epitaxy.
