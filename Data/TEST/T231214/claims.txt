CLAIMS:

1. A silicon carbide power device, comprising:

an n-type silicon carbide drift layer (10, 12);

a p-type conductivity silicon carbide well region (14) on the n-type silicon carbide
drift layer (10, 12);

a buried p* conductivity silicon carbide region (18) in the p-type conductivity
silicon carbide well region (14);

an n* conductivity silicon carbide region (20) directly on the buried p* conductivity
silicon carbide region (14);

a channel region (29) of the power device comprising a first portion that is
adjacent the buried p* conductivity silicon carbide region (18) and n* conductivity silicon
carbide region (20) and a second portion comprising an n silicon carbide region (19’) on
the first portion of the channel region (29);

wherein the channel region (29) of the power device has a root mean square
(RMS) surface roughness of less than 0.1 nm (1.0 A).

2. The device of Claim 1, wherein the RMS surface roughness of the
channel region (29) is 0.07 nm (0.7 A).

3. The device of Claim 1, wherein the n silicon carbide region (19’)
comprises a portion of an n epitaxial layer on the first portion of the channel region (29)
and wherein the n silicon carbide region on the first portion of the channel region (29)
has a thickness of from 100 to 500 nm (1000 to 5000 A).

4. The device of Claim 3, wherein the thickness of the n’ silicon carbide
region on the first portion of the channel region is 150 nm (1500 A).

5. The device of Claim 1, further comprising;

a silicon carbide substrate (10), wherein the p-type silicon carbide well region
(14) is provided on the silicon carbide substrate (10); and

wherein the n-type silicon carbide drift layer (12) is between the silicon carbide

substrate (10) and the p-type silicon carbide well region (14),
