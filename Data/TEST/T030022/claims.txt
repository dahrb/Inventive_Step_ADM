Claims

Claim 1. Computer-implemented method of augmenting a simulation model of
a physical environment of a vehicle,
comprising
establishing, by means of a simulation encompassing modules such as

“environment-geography”, “environment-weather’, “traffic”, “surrounding
objects”, “sensors”, or “vehicle”, a deficiency of the simulation model, the
deficiency pertaining to a certain setting within the physical environment, the
setting being defined in terms of weather conditions,
issuing instructions for capturing a scan of the setting, the instructions calling
for certain sensor hardware to be employed in the capturing,
receiving the scan, and
remedying the deficiency by complementing the simulation model with the
scan,
characterized in that
the setting is further defined in terms of a certain location, terrain, and physical
object present within the setting and
the instructions further call for certain boundary conditions to be met by the
scan, such as relative height, speed, angle, or distance between the hardware
and the object.

Claim 2. Method as per Claim 1
wherein
the deficiency is established by a client and communicated to a server,
by issuing the instructions, preferably through a Web portal, to a community of
participants, the server mounts a field campaign among said community to
capture the scan, and
upon following the instructions, at least one of the participants uploads the
scan to the server.

Claim 3. Method as per Claim 2
wherein
prior to mounting the campaign, the server verifies the deficiency by
transposing a simulated sensor having an input/output interface comparable to

those of the defective model into the described setting containing the
