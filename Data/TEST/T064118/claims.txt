Claims

- 39-

Use of 1-(4-Chlorophenyl)-4-(4-morpholinyl)-2,5-dihydro-1H-imidazol-2-one or a
physiologically acceptable salt thereof as an active ingredient for the manufacture of a
medicament for the treatment of anxiety of dogs, wherein the daily dose of the active

ingredient is from 5 to 100 mg/kg.

The use of claim 1, wherein such treatment results in a reduced risk of undesirable

behavioral side effects, such as sedation.

The use of any one of claims 1 to 2, wherein such anxiety is fear behavior, such as

unexpected aggression against men or environment.

The use of any one of claims 1 to 3, wherein the medicament is for oral administration.

The use of any one of claims 1 to 4, wherein the medicament is administered 1-5 times

daily, preferably 1-3 times daily.

The use of any one of claims 1 to 5, wherein the daily dose of the active ingredient is
from 10 to 60 mg/kg, preferably 10 mg/kg, 20 mg/kg, 30 mg/kg, 40 mg/kg, 50 mg/kg or
60 mg/kg.

The use of claim 6, wherein the daily dose of the active ingredient is administered in two

separate dosages (twice daily administration).

The use of any one of claims 1 to 7, wherein the active ingredient is co-administered

with at least one further active ingredient.
