The appeal is against the decision of the examining division refusing European patent application No. 17 206 163 pursuant to Article 97(2) EPC. II. The examining division cited the following documents: D1    US 2015/0189160 A1 D2    WO 2016/134260 A1 D3    KR 101 712 793 B1 and decided that the claimed subject-matter according to the main request and the first auxiliary request did not involve an inventive step (Article 56 EPC) in view of D1 in combination with D2 or D3. The second and third auxiliary requests filed only several days prior to the oral proceedings before the examining division were not admitted (Rule 137(3) EPC), because they were late-filed and prima facie did not overcome the objections raised against the main request and the first auxiliary request. III. The appellant requests that the decision under appeal be set aside and a European patent be granted on the basis of claims filed with letter dated 6 December 2023 as a main request or on the basis of one of the first to third auxiliary requests underlying the impugned decision; the main request further including amended description pages 1, 1a and 2 filed with letter dated 23 May 2024, description pages 3 to 9 as originally filed and drawing sheets 1/4 to 4/4 as originally filed. IV. Claim 1 according to the main request has the following wording (amendments made with respect to claim 1 as originally filed indicated by the board): Apparatus (10) comprising: a frame (14) having a slot (32); a screen (18) supported by the frame for displaying an image; a first optical sensor (20) connected to part of the frame positioned behind the screen or to a back surface of the screen (18) for capturing an image; an extendable arm (12) connected to the frame (14); and a second optical sensor (22) connected to the arm (12) for capturing an image, wherein the extendable arm (12) can rest in the slot (32) when not extended and can sit and secure generally within the confines of the frame (14) when not extended. Claim 10 according to the main request has the following wording (amendments made with respect to claim 12 as originally filed indicated by the board): A method of manufacturing an apparatus (10) for displaying information on a screen, the method comprising: connecting a screen (18) to a frame (14) such that the screen (18) can be supported by the frame (14) with respect to a surface; connecting a first optical sensor (20) to [deleted: the apparatus]a back surface of the screen (18) or to a part of the frame (14) positioned behind the screen such that it can capture information to display on the screen (18); and connecting an extendable arm (12) to the frame (14) such that a first end (28) can extend above and/or to the side of the frame (14), the first end (28) comprising a second optical sensor (22), wherein the extendable arm (12) rests in a slot (32) in the frame (14) when not extended and wherein the extendable arm (12) sits and secures generally within the confines of the frame (14) when not extended. The wording of the claims according to the auxiliary requests is not relevant for the present decision. V. The appellant argued essentially that the claimed subject-matter involved an inventive step.
