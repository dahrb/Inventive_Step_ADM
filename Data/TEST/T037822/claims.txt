Main Request - CLAIMS:

1. Apparatus (10) comprising:

a frame (14) having a slot (32) ;

a screen (18) supported by the frame for displaying an image;

a first optical sensor (20) for capturing an image;

an extendable arm (12) connected to the frame (14); and

a second optical sensor (22) connected to the arm (12) for capturing an image,

wherein the extendable arm (12) can rest in the slot (32) when not extended and
can sit and secure generally within the confines of the frame (14) when not

extended.

2. The apparatus (10) of claim 1, and further comprising:
a third optical sensor (24) connected to the apparatus, wherein the first optical
sensor (20) is configured for capturing an image near to the apparatus (10)
and the third optical sensor (24) is configured for capturing an image at a

distance from the apparatus (10).

3. The apparatus (10) of any of the preceding claims, wherein the arm (12) is
rotatably connected to the frame (14).

4. The apparatus (10) of any of the preceding claims, wherein the arm (12) is
extendable to a use position such that the second optical sensor (22) is positioned to

capture an image to a side of the frame (14).

5. The apparatus (10) of any of the preceding claims, wherein the first optical
sensor (20) is connected to a back surface of the screen (18) or to a part of the frame

(14) positioned behind the screen.

6. The apparatus (10) of any of the preceding claims, wherein the apparatus (10) is

configured to be collapsible into a compact package.
