TITLE:
Mounting structure of exhaust gas sensor for cylinder head in internal combustion engine

TYPE:
Japanese Patent

ABSTRACT:
The present invention provides an attachment structure in which an exhaust gas sensor is attached to a cylinder head of an internal combustion engine in a protected state.
At the top of an internal combustion engine, intake / exhaust valves (56, 57) are arranged in a V-shape, and an exhaust port (55) from the exhaust valve (57) is taken in the direction intersecting the cylinder axis from the exhaust valve axial direction. In the cylinder head 41 of the internal combustion engine 6, which is formed to be bent in a direction opposite to the valve 56 and has a mounting flange surface of an exhaust system member at a downstream end of the exhaust port 55,
An exhaust gas sensor is disposed around the mounting flange surface of the exhaust port 55 and outside of the exhaust port 55 in a state inclined with respect to the cylinder axis.
