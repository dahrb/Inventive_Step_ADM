Claims of Main Request

1. A combination of a personal digital assistant (50) and an adapter unit (100) for removably

receiving said personal digital assistant (50),

the personal digital assistant (50) having a back surface, two side surfaces defining a width of the
personal digital assistant (50), a top surface and a bottom surface defining a length of the personal
digital assistant (50), a scanning window for reading a product identification code in a direction
which is outward from the top surface and a module for reading a product identification code, said

adapter unit (100) comprising:

a body portion having a bottom wall (108) and two side walls (110), said bottom wall (108) having a
top section and a bottom section, said top section of said bottom wall (108) being configured to
conform to the back surface of the personal digital assistant (50) and said two side walls (110)

configured to prevent side to side movement of the personal digital assistant (50); and

a handle grip (150) extending from said bottom section of said bottom wall (108), said handle grip
(150) being joined to said body portion at a selected angle with respect to said body portion to cause
a proximal end of said bottom section of said bottom wall (108) to rest on a radial surface of a user’s

hand when the user grasps the handle portion;

the combination comprising:

means configured to change the functionality of one or more application buttons on the personal
digital assistant (50) upon attaching the adapter unit (100) to the personal digital assistant (50) by

changing application button mapping within the personal digital assistant (50);

an interface circuit board (227) housed between a cover and said bottom wall of said body portion,

said interface circuit board (227) including circuitry; and

the personal digital assistant (50) being adapted to determine when the adapter unit (100) is attached
to the personal digital assistant (50).

2. The combination of claim 1 wherein said adapter unit includes a filler block (440) secured

between the back surface of the personal digital assistant (50) and the bottom wall (108).

3. The combination of claim 1, further comprising a flexibly secured connector interface (405)

for engaging the personal digital assistant (50).

4. The combination of claim 3 wherein said connector interface (405) engages the back surface

of the personal digital assistant (50).

5. The combination of claim 3 wherein said connector interface (405) engages at least one of

the side surfaces of the personal digital assistant (50).

6. The combination of any of the preceding claims, said personal digital assistant (50) having
an expansion connector (52), said adapter unit comprising:
an adapter connector (124) for creating a communications link with said expansion connector
(52) of the personal digital assistant (50); and
said handle grip (150) having a trigger (152) coupled to the personal digital assistant (50)

through said communications link.

7. The combination of any of the preceding claims, wherein said means configured to change
the functionality of one or more application buttons on the personal digital assistant (50) comprises a
configuration program including routines operable to change the functionality of one or more

application buttons on the personal digital assistant (50)

8. The combination of claim 1, wherein a reconfigured function of the application button is
related to a function that was added by the attachment of the adapter unit (100) to the personal
digital assistant (50).

9. The combination of claim 7 or claim 8, wherein said adapter unit (100) includes a non-

volatile memory that includes said configuration program.
