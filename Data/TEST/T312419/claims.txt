MAIN REQUEST
CLAIMS

1. A method of associating a plurality of objects, the method comprising the
steps of:
defining a physical space;
determining by use of a real-time locating system (RTLS) objects that are within the
physical space; and
automatically creating at least one association between the objects determined by the
RTLS to be within the physical space, wherein the objects include a caregiver, a patient, and
a medical device;
wherein said determining comprises:
determining that a patient is within the physical space; determining that a
medical device is within the physical space; and
determining that a caregiver is within the physical space;
wherein said automatically associating comprises:
automatically creating an association between the medical device and the
patient; automatically creating an association between the caregiver and the medical
device; and
recording, in a database connected to a real-time locating system (RTLS), the

association between the objects within the physical space.

2. The method of claim 1, further comprising the step of downloading one or
more operating parameters to the medical device from a database, wherein the operating

parameters are associated with a procedure that has been ordered for the patient.

3. The method of claim 1, further comprising the step of uploading one or more
data records from the medical device to a database, wherein the data records are associated

with a procedure that has been performed for the patient.
