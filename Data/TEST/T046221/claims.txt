A process for preparing concentrated recombinant humanized anti-IgE antibody
compositions, comprising:
a) a first ultrafiltering of a first antibody preparation to provide a second antibody
preparation;
b) a diafiltering of the second antibody preparation to provide a diafiltered
intermediate antibody preparation; and
c) asecond ultrafiltering of the diafiltered intermediate antibody preparation to
provide a third antibody preparation,
wherein one or more of steps a), b), and c) are carried out at a temperature of 30°C to
50°C; and wherein the antibody is rhuMAb E25.

The process of claim 1, wherein one or more of steps a), b), and c) are carried out at a
temperature of 35°C to 50°C.

The process of claim 1, wherein one or more of steps a), b), and c) are carried out at a
temperature of 45°C.

The process of claim 1, wherein one or more of steps a), b), and c) are carried out at a
temperature of 40°C to 50°C.

The process of claim 1, wherein one or more of steps a), b) and c) are carried out ata
temperature of 40°C.

The process of claim 1, wherein one or more of steps a) and b) and c) are carried out at a
temperature of 45°C + 5°C.

The process of any one of the preceding claims, wherein the process is accomplished in
from 1 to 10 hours, for example in from 2 to 5 hours or in 3 hours.

The process of claim 1, wherein the first antibody preparation has an antibody
concentration of 0.1 to 10 g/L.

The process of claim 1, wherein the first antibody preparation has an antibody
concentration of 1 to 5 g/L.

The process of any one of the preceding claims, wherein the second antibody preparation
has an antibody concentration of 10 to 50 g/L.

The process of claim 10, wherein the second antibody preparation has an antibody
concentration of 20 to 40 g/L.

The process of any one of the preceding claims, wherein the third antibody preparation has
an antibody concentration of 170 to 200 g/L.
