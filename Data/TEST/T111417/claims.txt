CLAIMS

1 — Laser sensor with integrated rotating mechanism which comprises: a camera (1); a
laser (2); and a chassis (3) configured to rotate on its own axis (4); characterised in
that it comprises an indexed rotating mechanism which comprises, in turn: a first
motorised set (14), which consists of a first rotating motor (5) positioned on an axis
parallel to the rotating axis (4), and connected to said rotating axis (4) by a set of gears
(11); and a second motorised set (6) which consists of a second locking motor (8)
coupled onto the rotation axis (4) in a first support (9) of the laser sensor; and where
the first support (9) comprises at the bottom locking rollers (10) and a spindle (13)
coupled to the aforementioned first support (9) in such a way that locking occurs as a
result of contact between balls housed in the chassis (3) and the rollers (10) adjacent

to the lower part of the support (9).

2 — The sensor in claim 1, in which said locking rollers (10) are inserted in a second
support (12), wherein the bottom locking rollers (10) and balls can be separated and

fitted together to lock and unlock the chassis (3).

3 — The sensor in either of the claims 1-2, in which the spindle (13) is coupled to the
first support (9) using a retaining stud coupled to the first support (9), which converts
the rotating movement of the motor into a linear movement which allows the rotating

axis (4) to be released or locked.

4 — The sensor in any of the claims 1-3, in which a connector is coupled to the upper
part of the rotating axis (4), configured for connection with the head of the machine or

arm.

5 — The sensor according to any of the claims 1 to 4, in which has been incorporated

means for monitoring the temperature and the energy consumption.

6 — The sensor according to any of the claims 1 to 5, in which an electronic control has
been incorporated, with this electronic control being configured for activation of the
secondary motorised element (6) to release the axis (4); activation of the principal

motor (14) for rotation of the sensor to the desired position; control of an encoder (7)
