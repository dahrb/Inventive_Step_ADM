TITLE:
Diesel combustion engine with direct injection and combustion chamber in the piston

TYPE:
European Patent

ABSTRACT:
The engine has pistons (1) with combustion cavities (3). Each cavity has a constriction (7) extending into a lower outer wall (9) via a transition (21) with a strongly rounded section (22). An inner wall (10) is concave up to a tear-off edge (30), which is located approx. at half the depth (13) of the combustion cavity. When seen in radial cross section, the shortest distance (33) between outer and inner walls decreases continuously from the tear-off edge to the lowest point (8) of the cavity.
