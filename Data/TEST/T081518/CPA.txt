TITLE:
Multi-service handling by a single mobile station

TYPE:
World International Patent Organisation

ABSTRACT:
A method for processing multiple data services (6) over a communications link (4) between a mobile station (2) and a base station (3) is disclosed. An RLC/MAC protocol layer (15) of the communications link (4) between a mobile station (2) and a base station (3) receives a plurality of radio bearer services (6) each including at least one service provided by the mobile station (2). The plurality of radio bearer services (6) are processed in such a manner that services (6) having substantially similar quantity of signal requirements are combined for transmission on a single logical channel (40). Data within the transmission blocks (145) may be prioritized to enable flexible control of transmission rates.
