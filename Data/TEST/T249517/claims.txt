CLAIMS

A method (40) for determining quality of experience parameters of an encrypted video
stream received at a client device, the method comprising:

extracting (410), from one or more encrypted video streams sent over a network from a
content server to a plurality of client devices, at least one stream-related feature, wherein
strearn-related features comprise at least one of a volume timeline, a mean throughput,
an effective throughput, a quartile of throughput, a video state estimate, a video duration,
a total number of high, mid and/or low bitrate peaks, a high, mid and/or low peak density,
and a total number of the one or more encrypted video streams; and

determining (430) at least one quality-related label based at least in part on applying (420)
a trained classifier to the at least one stream-related feature, wherein quality-related labels
correspond to quality of experience parameters of an encrypted video stream received at
a client device.

The method of claim 1, further comprising:
changing one or more parameters associated with the network;

extracting, from the one or more encrypted video streams, at least one stream-related

feature after changing the one or more parameters;

determining at least one quality-related label based at least in part on applying the trained
classifier to the at least one stream-related feature extracted after changing the one or

more parameters; and

analyzing the at least one quality-related label determined before and after changing the
one or more parameters to obtain a measure of an impact of changing the one or more
parameters on the quality of experience parameters of an encrypted video stream received
at a client device.

A method (30) of obtaining a trained classifier for determining quality of experience
parameters of an encrypted video stream received at a client device, the method
comprising:
