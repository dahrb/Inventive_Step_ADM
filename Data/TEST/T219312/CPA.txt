Title: 
Method for downloading data preceded by information signals

Abstract: The invention relates to a process for transmitting and a process for receiving update data. According to the invention, the update data are dispatched by a communication center to terminals with the aid of a broadcasting network. Next, the terminals report their update to the center through a bidirectional network. The center renews the campaign or stops the current campaign as a function of the number of terminals updated. When only a minimum number of terminals to be updated remains, the campaign is stopped, the last terminals not updated will be so via the bidirectional link.
The invention also relates to the center implementing a dispatch process and the receiver for receiving and processing the update data.

Type: US Patent

Claims: 

1. A process for transmitting update data by a communication centre to a plurality of terminals, characterized in that it comprises the following steps:
broadcasting of an update campaign consisting in dispatching update data;
of formulating of a bi-directional communication between the centre and at least some of the terminals, each terminal transmitting an indication of the state of its update to the centre;
determining at the level of the centre of the number of terminals actually updated;
as a function of the number of terminals updated, halting or triggering of a new update campaign;
transmission by a point-to-point link of the update data from the centre to a terminal subsequent to the dispatching by this terminal of the absence of update indication.
2. The transmission process as claimed in claim 1, characterized in that the bi-directional communication occurs following the actual updating of the terminal during the update campaign.
3. The transmission process as claimed in claim 1, characterized in that the bi-directional communication occurs on the initiative of a terminal outside of the update campaign, said terminal transmitting an absence of update indication at the request of the centre.
4. The transmission process as claimed in any one of the preceding claims, characterized in that the update data depend on the type of terminal, the determining of the number of terminal at the level of the centre is performed as a function of each type of terminal.
5. The transmission process as claimed in any one of the preceding claims, characterized in that the new update campaign is triggered as a function of the proportion between the number of terminals updated and the number of terminals not updated.
6. A process for receiving update data dispatched by a communication centre to terminals, the centre broadcasting an update campaign in the course of which some terminals receive and store the update data, characterized in that it furthermore comprises, subsequent to the actual updating of a terminal, a step of transmission from the receiver to the centre of a signal of proper running of the operation, and of a step of reception via a point-to-point link of the update data from the centre to a terminal subsequent to the dispatching by this terminal of the absence of update indication.
7. The reception process as claimed in claim 6, characterized in that the update data contain an indication of the type of terminal, and in that the update in a terminal is performed if said indication corresponds to the type of this terminal.
8. A communication centre (5) for dispatching update data destined for a plurality of terminals, comprising a memory (6) containing update data, a means (4) of broadcasting the update data for a specified duration; characterized in that it furthermore comprises a means of bi-directional communication (7) with at least some of the terminals receiving from each terminal an update performed signal; a counter (6) for determining the number of signals received; a comparator triggering the broadcasting means so as to broadcast the data once again when the value of the counter is less than a specified number, the comparator activating the means of bi-directional communication (7) with at least one terminal not updated so as to transmit the update data to it by this means.
9. The communication centre (5) as claimed in claim 8, characterized in that the centre comprises a means of bi-directional communication (7) so as to receive a request for update by a terminal not updated.
10. The communication centre (5) as claimed in claim 8 or 9, comprising, in memory (6), data blocks corresponding to types of terminals liable to process them, and counters (6) corresponding to each type of terminal, each counter determining the number of the update performed signals on each terminal corresponding to its type, the comparator triggering the broadcasting of the data block whose type corresponding to that of the counter.
11. The communication centre (5) as claimed in any one of claims 8 to 10, for which the comparator takes into account the proportion between the number of terminals updated and the number of terminals not updated.
12. A terminal (1) furnished with a memory (24) for receiving update data originating from a communication centre through a broadcasting network (4), and with a means of bi-directional communication (7) with the centre, characterized in that said means of bi-directional communication is activated subsequent to the storage of the update data and dispatches an update performed signal destined for the centre, and in that said means of bi-directional communication receives the update data originating from the centre when this terminal is not updated.
13. The terminal as claimed in claim 12, characterized in that the memory (24) contains a value of type of terminal, the means of bi-directional communication dispatching said value subsequent to the storage of the update data.
