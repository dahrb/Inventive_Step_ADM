New claims (main request) (clear copy)

1. An output driver to drive on a multi-drop bus, an output symbol representing
at least two bits including a most significant bit (MSB) and a least significant bit
(LSB), comprising:

a first driver circuit (952) to drive the LSB by generating an LSB symbol
component representing the LSB; and .

a second driver circuit (954) to drive the MSB by generating an MSB
symbol component representing the MSB, the LSB symbol component being
combined with the MSB symbol component to provide the output symbol, where-
in one of the MSB and LSB is driven with about twice as much current as the
other one of the MSB and LSB;

characterized in that

the first driver circuit (952) includes at least one driver transistor (960-1)
and the second driver circuit (954) includes at least one driver transistor (960-3),

- and in that

a respective driver transistor (960-4) in one of the first and second driver
circuits (952, 954) is about twice as large in size as a corresponding driver tran-
sistor (960-1) in the other one of the first and second driver circuits (952, 954).

2. The output driver of claim 1, wherein the first driver circuit (952) is

connected in parallel with the second driver circuit (954).

3. The output driver of claim 1, wherein the first driver circuit (952) includes

one or more first driver transistors (960-0, 960-1), the second driver circuit (954)
