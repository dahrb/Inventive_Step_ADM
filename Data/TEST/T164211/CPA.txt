TITLE:
Method of transferring data between subsystems of a computer system

TYPE:
US Patent

ABSTRACT:
Data transmission arrangement for transmitting data between integrated circuit chips in a computer comprises a driver circuit having inputs connected to two discrete data bits. The driver circuit converts the states of the two data bits to one of four possible output voltage levels on each of two data transmission conductors. A receiver circuit connected to the data transmission conductors converge the multi-level signals on the pair of transmission conductors into binary output signals for use in a receiving circuit chip. The driver circuit and receiver circuit are balanced circuits and symmetrically arranged such that essentially the same magnitude of current is drawn from the power bus independent of the value of the signal being transmitted, thereby eliminating Delta-I noise typically occurring on a power bus when binary data is transmitted.

CLAIMS:
1. In a computer system comprising a plurality of subsystems, a method of reducing Delta-I noise resulting from current passing on power supply leads as a result of the switching of binary signal gates between ON and OFF states defining the opposite states of binary signals in the transmission of adjacent bits of a multibit data word between subsystems, the method comprising the steps of:
storing a plurality of independent binary encoded data signals in a data register, each of the data signals defined by two discrete signal levels;
converting two of the independent binary encoded data signals occurring concomitantly in two independent bit locations of the data register into two separate four-level transmission signals working differentially and having differing signal swing levels, the two separate four-level transmission signals having differing signal swing levels for each combination of the two independent binary encoded data signals, each combination of the two separate four-level transmission signals defining a unique combination of states of the two binary encoded data signals;
transmitting from one of the subsystems to another of the subsystems the two separate four-level transmission signals in parallel on two fully differential output pair of lines such that each state change of any one or both of the inputs will move the signal levels on said output pair of lines in opposite directions to insure that each input state and a unique output state and that the changes in current in the differential outputs add up to zero; and
receiving the two separate four-level transmission signals at the other subsystem; and converting the received four-level transmission signals to first and second concomitantly occurring binary encoded data signals correspond to the two independent binary encoded data signals, whereby Delta-I noise spikes which typically occur when a binary output data transmission driver switches between binary states is reduced.
2. The method in accordance with claim 1 and wherein the step of converting the input data signals comprises assigning a voltage signal value to each level of the two separate four-level encoded transmission signals and further comprising the step of changing the voltage signal values of a level of each of the two separate four-level transmission signals in opposite directions in response to a change in combination of the input data signals.
3. The method in accordance with claim 2 wherein the step of changing the voltage signal values further comprises changing the voltage signal values of the level of each of the two separate transmission signals by the same magnitude in the opposite directions.
4. The method in accordance with claim 1 wherein the step of converting the received transmission signals comprises generating an output signal of a first discrete signal level when a first of the received transmission signals is greater than a second of the received transmission signals and generating an output signal of a second discrete level when the second of the received transmission signals is greater than the first of the received transmission signals.
5. The method in accordance with claim 4 wherein the step of converting the received transmission signals further comprises the step of generating an intermediate output signal when signals of the highest and signals of the lowest levels of the four levels of a transmission signal are received.
6. The method in accordance with claim 5 wherein the step of converting the received transmission signals further comprises the step of combining the signals of the first and second discrete levels and the intermediate output signal.
