MAIN REQUEST

An antenna having radiator elements arranged on different planes, comprising:
a reflective plate (1);

a first-position radiator element (21, 23, 25, 27) placed on a first plane at a first height

over the reflective plate (1);

a second-position radiator element (22, 24, 26) placed on a second plane at a second
height over the reflective plate, the second height being different from the first height;

and

power supply cables (211, 221) connected to the first-position radiator element and the

second-position radiator element;
a radiator element (11, 12, 13, 14) of another frequency band,
wherein:

a length difference between the power supply cables is determined according to a phase
difference between signals radiated in the air from the first-position radiator element and
the second-position radiator element by a phase difference between the power supply
cables according to a position difference between the planes on which the first-position

radiator element and the second-position radiator elements are placed,

the radiator element of another frequency band is a patch-type radiator element having a
top patch plate (11-1, 12-1, 13-1, 14-1) and a bottom patch plate (11-2, 12-2, 13-2, 14-
2),

the first-position radiator element (21, 23, 25, 27) and the second-position radiator

element (22, 24, 26) are of a dipole type, and

the first-position radiator element or the second-position radiator element is stacked on
the top patch plate of the radiator element (11, 12, 13, 14) of another frequency band,

and use the top patch plate as a ground.
