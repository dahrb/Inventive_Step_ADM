Claims

1. Method for remotely delivering a full subscription profile to a UICC over IP, said
UICC being installed in a terminal able to provide an IP connectivity to a remote server and give
access to said UICC, said UICC being pre-personalised with a unique serial number and with a
bootstrap application and bootstrap credentials allowing to establish a secure transport channel
with said remote server, said remote server hosting a stock of subscription profiles and acting
as a web server, said method consisting in:

- opening (101), at the request of said UICC, a data channel between said terminal and
said server;

- performing (102) a mutual authentication between said UICC and said server by using
said bootstrap credentials;

- requesting (103), from said UICC to said server, the delivery of a subscription profile
by using said unique serial number;

- if a subscription profile exists for said UICC, downloading (106, 107) said subscription
profile to said UICC.

2. Method according to claim 1, wherein http communication protocol is used between
said UICC and said remote server.

3. Method according to any of the claims 1 or 2, wherein said UICC and said terminal

communicate over a BIP channel.

4. Method according to any of the claims 1 to 3, wherein said UICC is a removable
UICC.

5. Method according to any of the claims 1 to 3, wherein said UICC is an embedded
UICC.
