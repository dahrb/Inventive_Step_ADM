TITLE:
Crystal growth method for nitride semiconductor, nitride light emitting device, and method for producing the same

TYPE:
US Patent

ABSTRACT:
A crystal growth method for growing a nitride semiconductor crystal on a sapphire substrate in a vapor phase, includes the steps of: providing a sapphire substrate having a substrate orientation inclined by about 0.05° to about 0.2° from a <0001> orientation; and allowing a nitride semiconductor crystal to grow on the surface of the sapphire substrate.

CLAIMS:
1. A nitride semiconductor light emitting device, comprising:
a first cladding layer of a nitride semiconductor having a first conductivity type;
a second cladding layer of a nitride semiconductor having a second conductivity type which is different from the first conductivity type; and
a quantum well active layer of a nitride semiconductor interposed between the first and second cladding layers, wherein
interfaces between the active layer and the first and second cladding layers are inclined by about 0.05° to about 0.2° from a <0001> orientation.
2. A nitride semiconductor light emitting device according to claim 1, wherein an average surf ace roughness of the cladding layer underlying the quantum well active layer is less than a thickness of the quantum well active layer.
3. A nitride semiconductor light emitting device according to claim 1, wherein an average surface roughness of the cladding layer underlying the quantum well active layer is less than about 1.8 nm.
4. A nitride semiconductor light emitting device according to claim 1, wherein an orientation for an epitaxial growth of the nitride semiconductor is inclined from the <0001> orientation toward a <11-20> orientation.
5. A nitride semiconductor light emitting device according to claim 1, wherein an orientation for an epitaxial growth of the nitride semiconductor is inclined from the <0001> orientation toward a <1-100> orientation.
6. A nitride semiconductor light emitting device according to claim 1, wherein a threading dislocation density of the quantum well active layer is equal to or less than about the order of 1010 cm−2.
7. A nitride semiconductor light emitting device, comprising:
a pair of light confinement layers which have conductivity types different from each other, and a device structure including a quantum well active layer of a nitride semiconductor interposed between the pair of light confinement layers, wherein the device structure is provided on a substrate having with an outer most surface inclined by about 0.05° to about 0.2° from a <1-100> orientation.
8. A nitride semiconductor light emitting device according to claim 7, wherein an average surface roughness of the light confinement layer underlying the quantum well active layer is less than a thickness of the quantum well active layer.
9. A nitride semiconductor light emitting device according to claim 7, wherein an average surface roughness of the light confinement layer underlying the quantum well active layer is less than about 1.8 nm.
10. A nitride semiconductor light emitting device according to claim 7, wherein an orientation for an epitaxial growth of the nitride semiconductor is inclined from the <1-100> orientation toward a <11-20> orientation.
11. A nitride semiconductor light emitting device according to claim 7, wherein an orientation for an epitaxial growth of the nitride semiconductor is inclined from the <0001> orientation toward a <1-100> orientation.
12. A nitride semiconductor light emitting device according to claim 7, wherein a threading dislocation density of the quantum well active layer is equal to or less than about the order of 1010 cm−2.
