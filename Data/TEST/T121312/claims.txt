New claims 1 to 12 — Main Request
(fair copy)

1. A method for producing a III nitride epitaxial substrate, comprising the
steps of:

employing a c-faced sapphire single crystal base material of which a
crystal orientation of a main surface is inclined from the <Q001>
orientation, that is, the c-axis orientation by a range within 0.02-0.3
degrees;

nitriding the main surface of the base material to form a surface
nitride layer thereon;

forming an III nitride underfilm including at least Al element directly
on said main surface of said sapphire single crystal base material via said
surface nitride layer by means of a MOCVD method in a state where the
base material is heated at a temperature in the range of 1200°C or over
and 1250°C or below; and

forming a III nitride film on the nitride underfilm,
wherein a full width at half maximum of the underfilm at (10-12) reflection
in X-ray rocking curve is 1800 seconds or below and of which a surface
roughness Ra within 5 um square area is 3,0 A or less.

2. The method as defined in claim 1, wherein said crystal orientation of said

main surface of said sapphire single crystal base material is inclined from
<0001> orientation by a range within 0.05-0.25 degrees.
