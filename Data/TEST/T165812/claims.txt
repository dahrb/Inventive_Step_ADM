MAIN REQUEST

1. A system to dispense at least one user-appropriate item on-site, said system
comprising:

a. coded instructions to dispense at least one user-appropriate item based on
predetermined criteria for particular user-specific information (210, 220, 230), that user
specific-information (210, 220, 230) having been obtained based on a user-identifier
(200) associated with a user;

b. at least one storage means (600, 602, 604, 606, 608) capable of storing a
plurality of items;

c. a dispensing means (500, 502, 504, 506, 508) coupled with respective at
least one storage means (600, 602, 604, 606, 608); and

d. a processor coupled to said dispensing means (500, 502, 504, 506, 508),
said processor (400) capable of executing said instructions to actuate the dispensing
means (500, 502, 504, 506, 508) to dispense at least one user-appropriate item based
on said user-specific information and said predetermined criteria.

2. The system of Claim 1 wherein said processor (400) further actuates said
dispensing means (500, 502, 504, 506, 508) based on information associated with said

items.
3. The system of Claim 1 wherein said system is a vending machine.
4. The system of Claim 1 further comprising a means for reading item-specific

information, said means to read item specific information coupled with the processor
(400), and said processor (400) further capable of executing said instructions to
actuate at least one dispensing means (500, 502, 504, 506, 508) to dispense at least
one user-appropriate item based on said item-specific information.

6. The system of Claim 1 wherein said dispensing means (500, 502, 504, 506,
508) comprises a flange coupled with a solenoid, said solenoid coupled with said
processor (400); or a shooting solencid operable to accelerate said items out of the
system.
