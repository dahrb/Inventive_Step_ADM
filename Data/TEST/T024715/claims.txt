Main request

Claims

1. A method of scheduling delivery of multiple advertisements selectively to a

plurality of online users having client devices (10), comprising:

— determining expected values relating to each user being online to access
information over the Internet during a given time period;

— generating an ordered list of the advertisements to be selectively delivered to the
users based on the expected values, said ordered list being prioritized to meet
delivery requirements associated with said advertisements;

— and generating an individual list of items of advertisements to be delivered to
each user based on the ordered list, wherein said individual list is dynamically
generated for each user on user login,

— transmitting advertisements according to the individual list of advertisements to

each user.

2. The method of claim 1 wherein said expected values include the probability of
each user being online during the given time period.

3. The method of claim 1 wherein said expected values include a length of time each

user is expected to be online during the given time period.

4. Tne method of claim 1 wherein each said user has known profile or preference
data, and said advertisement are selectively delivered to users based on their profile
or preference data.

5. The method of claim 1 wherein said ordered list is periodically generated based

further on prior content deliveries made.
6. The method of claim 5 wherein said ordered list is generated daily.

7. The method of claim 1 wherein said ordered list comprises list excluded
advertisements, said exclusion is based on the maximum number of times an

advertisement can be delivered to the user.

8. The method of Claim 1 wherein said advertisements are displayed on a display
device operated by each user.

14. A computer system for scheduling and delivering multiple advertisements

selectively to a plurality of online users having client machines (10), each

advertisement being associated with a delivery requirement, the system comprising:

— amaster server (18) for determining data relating to a probability of a given user
being online to access information over the Internet during a given time period,
and generating an ordered master list of advertisements to be delivered based on
a probability data and known prior deliveries of advertisements to users, said list
being prioritized to meet delivery requirements associated with said
advertisements; and

— a plurality of local computers (10) linked to said master server (18), each local
computer (10) generating an individual schedule on user login, said schedule
comprising an individual list of selected advertisements to be delivered to said
user in accordance with said ordered master list,

— said selected advertisements comprising content for which the user is eligible to
receive based on user profile or preference data.

15. The system of claim 14 wherein said master server (18) comprises a Web server.

16. The system of claim 14 wherein each of said local computers (10) comprises an

Internet service provider point-of presence server.
