CLAIMS

1. A molecule capable of downregulating a tissue level and/or activity of at least one
type of lysyl oxidase for use in treating fibrosis ina mammal, wherein said molecule is an
antibody or an antibody fragment directed against at least an antigenic portion of a
polypeptide of SEQ ID NO:2.

2. The molecule for use according toclaim 1, wherein the antibody or fragment is

generated using a C-terminal 200 amino acids fragment of the polypeptide of SEQ ID NO:2.

3. The molecule for use according toclaim 1or claim 2, wherein the antibody or

fragment is capable of inhibiting the activity of LOR-1.

4. The molecule for use according toany of claims 1-3, wherein the fibrosis is tumor
fibrosis.
5. A pharmaceutical composition for use in treating fibrosis ina mammal, the composition

comprising, as an active ingredient, a molecule capable of downregulating a tissue level and/or
activity of at least one type of lysyl oxidase present within the mammalian tissue, and a
pharmaceutically effective carrier, wherein said molecule is an antibody or an antibody

fragment directed against at least an antigenic portion of a polypeptide of SEQ ID NO:2.

6. The pharmaceutical composition for use according to claim 5, wherein the
antibody or fragment is generated using a C-terminal 200 amino acids fragment of the
polypeptide of SEQ ID NO:2.

7. The pharmaceutical composition for use according toclaim 5 or claim 6,

wherein the antibody or fragment is capable of inhibiting the activity of LOR-1.

8. The pharmaceutical composition for use according toany of claims 5-7, wherein

the fibrosis is tumor fibrosis.
