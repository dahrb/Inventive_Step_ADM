CLAIMS: Main Request

1. A data processing device comprising:

an operand storage circuit comprising at least two source registers for storing
operands and at least one result register for storing a result, each register being notionally
subdivided into a plurality of segments at respective positions in the register,

a functional unit for executing an instruction including an opcode and referring
to one or more source operands and to a single result register in the operand storage circuit,

said instruction causing the functional unit to execute at least two operations in
parallel and independently of one another, at least one operation of the at least two operations
differs from at least one of the other operations of the at least two operations, each of the at
least two operations combining segments from the referred one or more source operands, the
segments having mutually different positions in the referred one or more source operands,
said positions are predetermined from the opcode from said instruction, and to store each

result of the at least two operations in a respective segment of the single result register.

2. A data processing device according to claim 1 wherein the at least two

operations consist of addition and subtraction operations.

3. A data processing device according to any of the previous claims wherein said

instruction refers to at least two source operands.

4. A data processing device according to claim 3 wherein the at least two
operations combine a first segment from a first source operand with a second segment from a

second source operand.

5. A data processing device according to any of the previous claims, wherein the

register is a 64 bit register.
