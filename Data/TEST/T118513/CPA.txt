TITLE:
Method of delivering information over a communication network

TYPE:
US Patent

ABSTRACT:
A technique and apparatus for delivering information to the subscribers (terminals) on a communication network. The information is simultaneously displayed without interfering with the reception and/or display of subscriber's selected data or program.
