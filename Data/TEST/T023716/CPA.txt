TITLE:
Aqueous disinfecting compositions with rapid bactericidal effect

TYPE:
European patent

ABSTRACT:
An aqueous disinfecting composition based on hydrogen peroxide and a mixture of carboxylic and percarboxylic acids, wherein the weight ratio of the said mixture to hydrogen peroxide is from 0.002 to 0.08, and wherein the said composition further comprises an effective amount of a metal component as a source of metal ions, as well as the use of said aqueous disinfecting composition for cleaning, decontaminating, disinfecting or sterilizing a solid surface or a volume of a gas or liquid. A disinfecting product comprising a carrier impregnated with said aqueous disinfecting composition.

CLAIMS:
An aqueous disinfecting composition comprising by weight:
(a) from 0.1 to 55% of hydrogen peroxide,
(b) from 0.01 to 4% of an RCO3H/RCO2H mixture, wherein R is methyl or ethyl, and
(c) from 0.0004 to 0.5% of a metal component as a source of metal ions, and
(d) an amount of water to make up to 100%,
characterized in that the weight ratio of the RCO3H/RCO2H mixture to hydrogen peroxide in the said composition is from 0.002 to 0.08.
An aqueous disinfecting composition according to claim 1, characterized in that the RCO3H/RCO2H molar ratio in the RCO3H/RCO2H mixture is in the range from 1:4 to 5:1.
An aqueous disinfecting composition according to claim 1 or claim 2, characterized in that the weight ratio of the RCO3H/RCO2H mixture to hydrogen peroxide in the said composition is from 0.005 to 0.05.
An aqueous disinfecting composition according to any of claims 1 to 3, characterized in that the metal of the metal component (c) is a monovalent metal.
An aqueous disinfecting composition according to any of claims 1 to 4, characterized in that the metal of the metal component (c) is silver.
An aqueous disinfecting composition according to any of claims 1 to 5, characterized in that the metal of the metal component (c) is a source of Ag+ ions.
An aqueous disinfecting composition according to any of claims 1 to 6, characterized in that the metal component (c) is silver nitrate.
An aqueous disinfecting composition according to any of claims 1 to 7, characterized in that it further includes one or more stabilizers.
An aqueous disinfecting composition according to any of claims 1 to 8, characterized in that it further includes one or more corrosion inhibitors and/or one or more surfactants.
Use of an aqueous disinfecting composition according to any of claims 1 to 9 for cleaning, decontaminating, disinfecting or sterilizing a solid surface or a volume of a gas or liquid.
A disinfecting product comprising a carrier impregnated with an aqueous disinfecting composition according to any of claims 1 to 9.
