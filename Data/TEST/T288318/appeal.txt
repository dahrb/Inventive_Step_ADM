The appeal is directed against the decision of the examining division, dated 21 June 2018, to refuse application No. 10760868.9 for lack of inventive step over D4 (WO2007/007171 A1; introduced as D2 in the summons to oral proceedings before the examining division) in combination with common knowledge. II. A notice of appeal was received on 17 August 2018. The appeal fee was paid on the same day. With a statement of grounds of appeal received on 1 November 2018 the appellant re-filed the claims according to the main request and the first auxiliary request in the decision and filed new claims according to a second auxiliary request. III. In an annex to a summons to oral proceedings, the board informed the appellant of its preliminary opinion that the pending claims were deficient under Articles 123(2) and 56 EPC. IV. In a letter dated 19 June 2023 the appellant filed three new sets of claims, and arguments addressing the objections raised in the summons. V. Oral proceedings were held on 23 June 2023 during which the appellant filed an amended main request and withdrew all other requests. At the conclusion of the oral proceedings, the board announced its decision. VI. The appellant requests that the decision under appeal be set aside and that a patent be granted on the basis of claims 1-3 according to the (sole) main request filed during the oral proceedings before the board. The further application documents on file are: description pages 1, 3-24 as published, 2 as filed on 3 June 2016; drawing sheets 1-12 as published. VII. The sole independent claim reads as follows: "1. A computer implemented method comprising: in response to a third party application changing application data, receiving identifiers of records changed in the application data and temporarily storing the identifiers in an update list, launching an application indexer (330) to run in a process having privileges identical to privileges of a third party application (310) executable on the processor, wherein the process is separate from other processes involving other third party applications executable on the processor; instantiating, by the application indexer (330), an instance of an application search bundle (340) specific to the third party application (310) that executes with privileges identical to the privileges of the third party application (310), the third party application search bundle (340) specific to the third party application (310) to access records of application data (315); passing, by the application indexer (330), the updated identifications associated with records of application data in the update list to the application search bundle (340) to request the records from the instance of the application search bundle (340), the changed records corresponding to the updated identifiers, the application indexer (330) indexing the records received from the instance of the application search bundle (340) in a search database (380)."
