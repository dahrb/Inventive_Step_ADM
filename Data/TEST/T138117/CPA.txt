TITLE:
A flash memory cell

TYPE:
UK Patent

ABSTRACT:
A flash EEPROM memory cell comprises a silicon substrate 11 consisted of an insulating film 11A and a silicon film 11B in which a first and second channel regions 13A, 13B are formed and a pair of gate electrodes 15A, 15B formed on the first and second channel regions, respectively. Each channel region has a drain region 12A, 12B and a source region 12C, 12D formed at the sides thereof. The gate electrodes 15A, 15B are interconnected by a conductive film 18. Such a cell can be programmed, erased and read without the need for a negative voltage.

CLAIMS:
1. A flash memory cell comprising; a silicon substrate consisted of an insulating film and a silicon film in which a first and second channel regions are formed, said each channel region having a drain region and source region formed at both sides thereof, a pair of gate electrodes formed on said first and second channel regions, respectively, said each electrode isolated electrically with said silicon film by a dielectric film, and an insulating film formed on the entire structure including said gate electrodes, a conductive film formed on said insulating film to connect said gate electrodes from each other through contact holes formed in said insulating film.
2. The flash memory cell of claim 1, wherein said first channel region and second channel region are formed by injecting opposite type impurity ions from each other.
3. The flash memory cell of claim 1, wherein said drain and source regions are formed by injecting same type impurity ions.
