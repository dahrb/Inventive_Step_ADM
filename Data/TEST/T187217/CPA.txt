Title: Pigment dispersion for cosmetic

Abstract: A pigment dispersion for cosmetic products, which contains black oxide of iron, red oxide of iron and Prussian blue.

Type: EPO Patent

Claims: 

A pigment dispersion for cosmetic products, which contains black oxide of iron, red oxide of iron and Prussian blue.
A pigment dispersion for cosmetic products of claim 1, characterized in that it contains an anionic dispersing agent and a nonionic dispersing agent.
A pigment dispersion for cosmetic products according to claim 1 or 2, characterized in that the viscosity is not more than 20mPa·s.
A pigment dispersion for cosmetic products of claim 2 characterized in that the anionic dispersing agent contains at least one from poly-aspartic acid, poly-glutamic acid, styrene-acrylic acid copolymer, styrene-methacrylic acid copolymer and styrene-α-methyl·styrene-acrylic acid copolymer and the salts of each of these, and that the nonionic dispersing agent contains a polyoxyethylene alkylether having HLB of from 15 to 20.
A pigment dispersion according to any one of Claims 1 to 4, characterized in that it is a water type eyeliner use.
An eyeliner characterized in that the pigment dispersion as defined in any one of Claims 1 to 4 is filled in a pen type container.
