Main Request

1. A cosmetic water-based ink in form of a suspension containing at least one
acryl-based polymer, at least one polymeric ionic thickener, at least one
anionic or amphoteric-ionic surfactant, at least one material in particle form
and at least one non-ionic surfactant, wherein the non-ionic surfactant is a
compound which contains between 4 and 8 units of PEG or PPG and a C8-
C16 fatty acid residue.

2. An ink as set forth in claim 1 characterised in that the material in particle form

is a pigment.

3. An ink as set forth in claim 1 or claim 2 characterised in that the acrylic acid-

based polymer contains alkyl, aryl and/or alkaryl groups.

4. An ink as set forth in one of claims 1 through 3 characterised in that it contains
between 20 and 70% by weight, in particular between 40 and 60% by weight

of polymer.

5. An ink as set forth in one of the preceding claims characterised in that it
contains between 0.1 and 15% by weight, in particular between 0.2 and 0.5%

by weight of thickener.

6. An ink as set forth in one of the preceding claims characterised in that the
anionic or amphoteric-anionic surfactant is contained therein in an amount of

between 0.2 and 3% by weight, in particular between 0.3 and 1% by weight.

7. An ink as set forth in one of the preceding claims characterised in that the non-
ionic surfactant is contained therein in an amount of between 0.2 and 3% by

weight, in particular between 0.3 and 1% by weight.

8. An ink as set forth in one of the preceding claims characterised in that
between 0.1 and 50% by weight and in particular between 5 and 16% by

weight of pigments are included.
