Title: Mobile terminal and screen displaying method thereof

Abstract: A mobile terminal and method for displaying on a touch screen information associated with an item displayed on the touch screen when an object is in near-proximity to the displayed item. A sensing unit senses a real-touch and a proximity-touch of the touch screen and a controller displays the information associated with the specific item on the touch screen.

Type: 
EPO Patent

Claims: 

A mobile terminal (100), comprising:
a display (151) including a touch screen;
a sensing unit (140) for sensing an object in near-proximity to the touch screen; and
a controller (180) for displaying information associated with an item displayed on the touch screen when the object is in near-proximity to the item.
The mobile terminal of claim 1, wherein the controller executes a function associated with the item when the object contacts the item on the touch screen.
The mobile terminal of any one of claims 1 or 2, wherein the controller displays additional information associated with the item when the object contacts the item on the touch screen.
The mobile terminal of any one of claims 1 or 2, wherein the controller facilitates the information associated with the item to disappear from the display when the object is no longer in near-proximity to the touch screen.
The mobile terminal of claim 4, wherein the information disappears after a predetermined time from when the object is no longer in near-proximity to the touch screen.
The mobile terminal of any one of claims 4 or 5, wherein the item comprises an item displayed in a phone book list.
The mobile terminal of claim 6, wherein the information associated with the item displayed in the phone book list comprises at least one of:
an image;
recent call information;
message transceiving information;
a current position; and
a current state.
The mobile terminal of any one of claims 4 or 5, wherein the item comprises a group of contact addresses.
The mobile terminal of claim 8, wherein the information associated with the group of contact addresses comprises a plurality of contact names arranged in an order according to one of most recently called and most frequently called.
The mobile terminal of any one of claims 4 or 5, wherein the item is a menu item related to a specific function.
The mobile terminal of claim 10, wherein the information associated with the menu item comprises at least one of a sub-menu of the menu and an explanation of the menu.
The mobile terminal of any one of claims 4 or 5, wherein the information associated with the item comprises an option menu associated with the item, or a plurality of contents associated with the item wherein the controller displays the plurality of contents in an order according to one of most recently used and most frequently used.
The mobile terminal of any one of claims 4 or 5, wherein the item is a word displayed on the touch screen and the information comprises dictionary information for the word.
The mobile terminal of any one of claims 4 or 5, wherein the item is a virtual input button displayed on the touch screen and the information comprises a plurality of input symbols associated with the virtual input button.
A method for displaying information on a mobile terminal touch screen, the method comprising:
sensing an object in near-proximity to the touch screen; and
displaying information associated with an item displayed on the touch screen when the object is in near-proximity to the item.
