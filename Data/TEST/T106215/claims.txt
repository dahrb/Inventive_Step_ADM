1. An orthogonal frequency division multiplexing (OFDM) apparatus configured to

vector, the second complex number and the fourth complex number provide a second

second pilot subcarrier, the fourth complex number being used to generate the fourth
pilot signal to be transmitted using the second pilot subcarrier,

signal and the second OFDM signal comprises the second pilot signal and the fourth

pilot signal.
