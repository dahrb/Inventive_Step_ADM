CLAIMS

1. A pixel array (205) including a backside illuminated (“BSTI’) imaging sensor
pixel, the BSI imaging sensor pixel comprising:

a photodiode region (420) disposed within a semiconductor die for
accumulating an image charge in response to light incident upon a backside of the BSI
imaging sensor pixel, wherein the photodiode region (420) is an N or P type doped
region within a substrate (405) having the opposite conductivity type; and

pixel circuitry (430, 431) including transistor pixel circuitry disposed within the
semiconductor die between a frontside of the semiconductor die and the photodiode
region (420), wherein at least a portion of the pixel circuitry (430, 431) overlaps the
photodiode region (420);

wherein a portion (431) of the pixel circuitry (430, 431) is shared with an
adjacent pixel within the pixel array as shared pixel circuitry, wherein a first portion of
the shared pixel circuitry overlaps the photodiode region (420) of the BSI imaging
sensor pixel (400) and a second portion of the shared pixel circuitry overlaps a
photodiode region of the adjacent pixel;

wherein the shared pixel circuitry includes any one of: gain circuitry, ADC

circuitry (305), gamma control circuitry, and exposure control circuitry.

2. The pixel array (205) of claim 1, wherein the shared pixel circuitry includes
the ADC circuitry, and wherein the ADC circuitry includes an analog-to-digital
converter, “ADC”, coupled to convert the image charge to a digital value within the
BSI imaging sensor pixel (400), wherein at least a portion of the ADC overlaps the

photodiode region of the BSI imaging sensor pixel.

3. The pixel array (205) of claim 1, wherein the shared pixel circuitry includes
the gain circuitry, and wherein the gain circuitry is for amplifying image data derived
from the image charge within the BSI imaging sensor pixel (400), wherein at least a
portion of the gain circuitry overlaps the photodiode region of the BSI imaging sensor

pixel.

4. The pixel array (205) of claim 1, wherein the shared pixel circuitry includes

at least one of the gamma control circuitry or the exposure control circuitry, wherein at

a color filter (410) disposed between the microlens and the photodiode region to
