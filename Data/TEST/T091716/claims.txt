Main Request

1. An immunogenic composition comprising at least 2 different N. meningitidis
capsular saccharides, wherein one or more is/are selected from a first group
consisting of MenA and MenC, which is/are conjugated through a linker to a
carrier protein(s), and one or more different saccharides is/are selected from a
second group consisting of MenC, MenY and MenW which is/are directly
conjugated to a carrier protein(s) wherein the composition comprises MenA
and MenC capsular saccharides conjugated through a linker to a carrier
protein(s), and MenY and Men W capsular saccharides directly conjugated to
a carrier protein(s) or MenA capsular saccharide conjugated through a linker to
a carrier protein, and MenC, MenY and Men W capsular saccharides directly
conjugated to a carrier protein(s) and wherein each N. meningitidis capsular
saccharide is conjugated to the same carrier protein selected from the group
consisting of TT, DT, CRM197, fragment C of TT and protein D.

2. The immunogenic composition of claim 1 comprising MenA capsular
saccharide conjugated through a linker to a carrier protein, and MenC
capsular saccharide directly conjugated to a carrier protein.

3. The immunogenic composition of claim 1 comprising MenC capsular
saccharide conjugated through a linker to a carrier protein, and MenyY capsular
saccharide directly conjugated to a carrier protein.

4. The immunogenic composition of any preceding claim wherein each N.
meningitidis capsular saccharide is conjugated to TT.

5. The immunogenic composition of any preceding claim wherein at least one,
two or three N. meningitidis saccharide conjugate(s) is directly conjugated by
CDAP chemistry.

6. The immunogenic composition of any preceding claim wherein the linker is
bifunctional.

7. The immunogenic composition of any preceding claim further comprising a

H. influenzae b capsular saccharide (Hib) conjugated to a carrier protein, said
carrier protein being selected from the group consisting of TT, DT, CRM197,
fragment C of TT and protein D.

8. A vaccine comprising the immunogenic composition of any one of claims 1-7
and a pharmaceutically acceptable excipient.

9. A vaccine kit for concomitant or sequential administration comprising two
multi-valent immunogenic compositions for conferring protection in a host
