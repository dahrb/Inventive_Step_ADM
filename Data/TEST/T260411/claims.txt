CLAIMS

A power management system (10), comprising:

a power management module (30) configured to receive a requested duration of
time (80) for powering an electronic device (12) by a battery (16), the power man-
agement module (30) configured to control use of power-consuming applications
(40) that are executable on the electronic device (12) based on a prioritization (62)
of the power-consuming applications (40) to enable powering of the electronic de-
vice (12) by the battery (16) for at least the requested duration of time (80),

wherein the power management module (30) is configured to reduce the power
consumption of the electronic device (12) by automatically closing a particular

power-consuming application (40),

wherein the power management module (30) is configured to prevent opening
and/or otherwise initiating a particular power-consuming application (40),

wherein the prioritization (62) is dynamic and comprises at least two different prior-
ity levels of the power-consuming applications (40),

wherein one or more of the power-consuming applications (40) has a variable pri-
ority, and

wherein, in response to opening and/or otherwise initiating a particular power con-
suming application (40) having a variable priority, the priority level of the particular
power consuming application (40) is changed to a higher level.

The system (10) of Claim 1, wherein, in response to closing the particular power
consuming application (40), the priority level associated with the particular power
consuming application (40) returns to its default level.

The system (10) of Claim 1, wherein the power management module (30) is con-
figured to control use of at least one of the power-consuming applications (40) in
response to initiation of use of at least another of the power-consuming applica-
tions (40).
