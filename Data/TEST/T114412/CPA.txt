TITLE:
Electric current measuring device using a regularly shaped toroidal solenoid

TYPE:
European Patent

ABSTRACT:
The present invention relates to a device for measuring an electric current using a solenoid with regular steps in the shape of a torus. This device is characterized in that the toroidal solenoid (1) is interrupted over a portion of its length to form a gap (2) allowing the free passage of conductors and / or current lines and this solenoid (1) has, at at least one of its ends delimiting the breach, at least one compensation winding (5, 6) connected in series with the solenoid so that the voltages induced in this compensation winding (5, 6) and in the solenoid ( 1) are added.

CLAIMS:
1.- Device for measuring an electric current using a solenoid with regular steps in the shape of a torus, characterized in that the toroidal solenoid (1) is interrupted over a portion of its length to form a gap (2) allowing free passage of conductors and / or current lines and this solenoid (1) has, at at least one of its ends delimiting the breach, at least one compensation winding (5,6) connected in series with the solenoid so that the voltages induced in this compensation winding (5,6) and in the solenoid (1) are adjusted, the total number N c and the surface S c of the turns of the compensation winding (s) (5,6) satisfactory to the relation N c S c = nSd in which n is the number of turns of the solenoid (1) per unit of length, S is the surface of a turn of the solenold (1) and d is the length of the breach (2 ) i.e. the missing length to close the solenoid (1). 2.- Device according to claim 1 characterized in that the compensation coils (5,6) have a cylindrical shape and they are wound outside or inside the two end parts of the solenoid delimiting the breach (2) . 3.- Device according to claim 1 characterized in that the compensation coils (7,8) are made in the form of flat coils of the same axis as the end parts of the solenoid (1) or inclined relative to this axis. 4.- Device according to any one of the preceding claims, characterized in that the compensation windings (5,6; 7,8) are removable and associated with a length (d) determined of the gap (2). 5.- Device according to any one of the preceding claims, characterized in that the compensation coils (5,6; 7,8) are pluggable mechanically and / or electrically on the ends of the solenoid (1).
