1. A computer-implemented method (400) for determining content visibility
when a content item is included in a cross-domain iframe (308), comprising:

determining (402) a first estimate of a size of a webpage viewing area (206)
of a viewport (202) associated with a browser including (1) reading, from inside the
iframe (308), an outer bound for size of the viewport (202), (i) performing a
statistical analysis to determine an average size of one or more browser elements
(204, 208a, 208b) including determining a browser header (204) size based on
historical measurements that are gathered as statistical data, and (iii) subtracting from
the size of the viewport (202) the average size of the one or more browser elements
(204, 208a, 208b);

determining (404) a second estimate for a size of the content item for display
in the webpage viewing area (206), including reading iframe inside measurements
for the iframe (308) from the browser;

determining (406) content item visibility including determining of a location
of the content item relative to the webpage viewing area (206) by reading a location
of the viewport (202) and, from inside the iframe (308), a location of the iframe (308)
within the viewport (202) in screen coordinates and calculating how much of the
content item is visible by determining an area of overlap of two rectangles, the first
rectangle defined by the location of the viewport (202) and the estimate of the
webpage viewing area (206), and the second rectangle defined by the location of the
iframe (308) and the size of the content item; and

reporting (408) information about the visibility of the content item to a

content sponsor.
