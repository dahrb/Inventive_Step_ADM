MAIN REQUEST

CLAIMS

1. A pharmaceutical composition for intranasal administration consisting
essentially of:

0.15 (w/v) azelastine hydrochloride

0.1% (w/v) hypromellose;

0.05% (w/v) disodium edetate;

0.025% (w/v) benzalkonium chloride 50%;

0.15% (w/v) sucralose;

6.4% (w/v) sorbitol 70%;

0.068% sodium citrate;

and QS water.

2. The pharmaceutical composition of claim 1, wherein the osmolality of said

composition is from about 250 mosmol/kg to about 320 mosmol/kg.

3. A pharmaceutical composition as claimed in any one of claims 1 to 2 for
treating or preventing a physical disorder selected from the group consisting of allergic
rhinitis, non-allergic vasomotor rhinitis and allergic conjunctivitis in an animal suffering

from or predisposed thereto.

4. A pharmaceutical composition as claimed in claim 3, wherein said animal is a

mammal.

5. A pharmaceutical composition as claimed in claim 4, wherein said mammal is

a human.
