CLAIMS
What is claimed is:

1. A meter calibration system (600) configured to calibrate a meter
devoid of process connections (220), the system comprising a source (602) for
flowing a process material through the calibration system, a controller (622)
configured to process measurement signals derived from the calibration system
and the meter to derive calibration information, and process connection adapters
(216) for conveying process material between the meter under calibration and the
calibration system.

2. The meter calibration system of claim 1 wherein the process
connection adapters are sized and shaped to emulate a process connection that
may be subsequently attached to the meter.

3. The meter calibration system of claim 2 wherein the process
connection adapters (300) are sized and shaped to emulate flange process

connections.

4. The meter calibration system of claim 2 wherein the process
connection adapters (400) are sized and shaped to emulate sanitary process

connections.

5. The meter calibration system of claim 1 further comprising a master
meter (616) for supplying measurement signals to said controller (622) for use in

deriving said calibration information.
6. The meter calibration system of claim 1 further comprising a scale
(716) for supplying measurement signals to said controller (722) for use in deriving

said calibration information.

f. The meter calibration system as set forth in claim 1 wherein the meter

under test is a Coriolis meter.

14. | Amethod of calibrating a meter under test devoid of process
connections, the method comprising the steps of:

connecting a first process connection adapter to an input of said meter and a
second process connection adapter to an output of said meter to form a series
connection with said meter;

flowing a fluid serially through said first process connection adapter, said
meter, and said second process connection adapter;

generating measurement signals from the meter in response to said flow;
and

processing the generated measurement signals to derive calibration

information for the meter.
