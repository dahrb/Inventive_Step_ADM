Main Request 1 December 2021

Claims
1. A method of detecting and quantifying dental plaque in a test subject, comprising
the following steps;
(i) obtaining one or more images of one or more teeth of a conscious test

subject using an image taking device that is capable of detecting

fluorescence;

(ii) analysing the images; and

(ili) quantifying the dental plaque coverage on each tooth of the test subject,

wherein the test subject is a companion animal:

wherein the method is not a diagnostic method practised on the human or animal body.

2. The method of claim 1, wherein the one or more teeth are located in the upper jaw

of the test subject.

3. The method of any one of the preceding claims, wherein the companion animal is

a dog or a cat.

4. The method of any one of claims 1 or 2, wherein the test subject is a cat.

5. The method of any one of claims 1 or 2, wherein the test subject is a dog.
