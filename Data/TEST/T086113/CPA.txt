TITLE:
Concentric ring single vision lens designs

TYPE:
European Patent

ABSTRACT:
Concentric ring, single vision lens designs are disclosed which improve the depth-of-focus of the lens relative to current spherical contact lenses or intraocular lenses by placing at least one additional peripheral optical radius on the front or back surface of the lens. A plurality of concentric annular rings are provided in the periphery of the optic zone of the lens. The arrangement of optic powers in the concentric annular rings includes a mixture of the basic spherical refractive power and other annular rings with less plus or greater minus spherical refractive power, arranged to mediate spherical aberrations and improve visual acuity. The concentric annular ring design corrects peripheral, aperture-dependent ocular aberrations in a discrete, zonal fashion.

CLAIMS:
A concentric annular ring, single vision lens which focuses light rays passing through the periphery of the lens at the same focal plane as light rays passing through the center of the lens, thereby increasing the quality of the lens image and improving its depth-of-focus, comprising:
a. said lens having a front surface and an opposite back surface, wherein one of the front and back surfaces defines a central area comprising a circular disc having a surface corresponding to a basic prescription Rx spherical refractive power; and
b. a plurality of annular rings surrounding the central area which have a combination of the basic prescription Rx refractive power and a less plus or greater minus refractive power, to mediate spherical aberration and improve visual acuity.
A concentric annular ring, single vision lens with improved depth-of-focus as claimed in claim 1, wherein the plurality of annular rings comprise spherical annular rings.
A concentric annular ring, single vision lens with improved depth-of-focus as claimed in claim 2, wherein the circular disc has a spherical curve.
A concentric annular ring, single vision lens with improved depth-of-focus as claimed in claim 1, wherein the plurality of annular rings comprise aspheric annular rings.
A concentric annular ring, single vision lens with improved depth-of-focus as claimed in claim 4, wherein the circular disc has an aspheric curve.
A concentric annular ring, single vision lens with improved depth-of-focus as claimed in claim 1, wherein the lens comprises a contact lens to be worn on the cornea of the eye.
A concentric annular ring, single vision lens with improved depth-of-focus as claimed in claim 6, wherein the contact lens comprises a soft hydrogel contact lens.
A concentric annular ring, single vision lens with improved depth-of-focus as claimed in claim 1, wherein the lens comprises an intraocular lens.
A concentric annular ring, single vision lens with improved depth-of-focus as claimed in claim 1, wherein the plurality of annular rings have less plus spherical power for a hyperopic prescription.
A concentric annular ring, single vision lens with improved depth-of-focus as claimed in claim 1, wherein the plurality of annular rings have greater minus spherical power for a myopic prescription.
A concentric annular ring, single vision lens with improved depth-of-focus as claimed in claim 1, wherein the central area is surrounded by a plurality of alternating sphere power and sphere' power annular rings, where the sphere' power comprises a spherical power which is less plus or more minus than the spherical power of the central area.
A concentric annular ring, single vision lens with improved depth-of-focus as claimed in claim 11, wherein the widths of individual annular rings are different to generate a power profile which varies to generate different amounts of minus power with increasing distance from the center.
A concentric annular ring, single vision lens with improved depth-of-focus as claimed in claim 1, wherein the central area and the plurality of annular rings are formed on the back surface of the lens to minimize flare and glare problems.
A concentric annular ring, single vision lens with improved depth-of-focus as claimed in claim 1, wherein the plurality of annular rings have increasing values of optical power, increasingly more minus or less plus as the radius increases from the center to provide a systematic change of power.
A method of designing a concentric annular ring, single vision lens which focuses light rays passing through the periphery of the lens at the same focal plane as light rays passing through the center of the lens, thereby increasing image quality and improving its depth-of-focus, and comprises,
a. the lens having a front surface and an opposite back surface, wherein one of the front and back surfaces defines a central area comprising a circular disc having a surface corresponding to a basic prescription Rx spherical refractive power, and
b. a plurality of annular rings surrounding the central area which have a combination of the basic prescription Rx refractive power and a less plus or greater minus refractive power, to mediate spherical aberration and improve visual acuity, wherein the method comprises,
c. performing an in vivo image quality analysis, with an in vivo quality analysis instrument, of the lens on the eye to measure any residual aberrations,
d. reducing the measured residual aberrations by redesigning the lens to improve visual acuity and performance.
A method of designing a concentric annular ring, single vision lens as claimed in claim 15, wherein redesigning the lens includes aspherizing the surface opposite the surface defining the central area and the at least one annular ring.
A method of designing a concentric annular ring, single vision lens as claimed in claim 15, wherein redesigning the lens includes adding more minus power spherical annular rings.
A method of designing a concentric annular ring, single vision lens as claimed in claim 15, wherein said performing step comprises utilizing an aberroscope or MTF point spread measuring device to measure the modulation transfer function of the combination of the lens and eye.
