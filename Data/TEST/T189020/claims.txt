Claims
1. A display device comprising:

a plurality of display units that are equipped with a touch panel function and that
display an image that is information;

a coordinate conversion unit that judges whether an application that is currently
operating is displaying said image straddling said plurality of display units,

wherein the coordinate conversion unit, when the application is displaying said
image straddling said plurality of display units, uses a predetermined operation to convert
physical coordinates that indicate the position at which said display unit senses contact to virtual
coordinates; and

a coordinate notification unit that, when the application is displaying said image on
one display unit among said plurality of display units, reports the physical coordinates at which
said display unit senses contact to the application and, when the application is displaying said

image straddling said plurality of display units, reports said virtual coordinates to the application.

2. The display device as set forth in claim 1, wherein said coordinate conversion unit
converts to said virtual coordinates the physical coordinates on, from among said plurality of
display units, a display unit other than a primary display unit that includes the coordinates of the

origin that said application recognizes.

3. The display device as set forth in claim 2, wherein said coordinate conversion unit
converts, from among said physical coordinates, the coordinate of the direction along which said

primary display unit and a display unit other than the primary display unit are aligned.

4. The display device as set forth in claim 3, wherein said coordinate conversion unit
cafries out conversion to said virtual coordinates by adding a coordinate portion that accords
with the size of said primary display unit to the physical coordinates on display units other than

said primary display unit.

5. The display device as set forth in claim 1, wherein said coordinate conversion unit
carries out conversion of said physical coordinates to said virtual coordinates according to
movement of physical coordinates with respect to the origin that said application recognizes that

is caused by rotation of the length and width of the display device.

6. A coordinate notification method in a device that is equipped with a plurality of
displays having a touch panel function, comprising:

a step of sensing contact to said plurality of displays;

a step of judging whether an application that is currently operating is displaying said
image straddling said plurality of displays;

a conversion step of, when the application is displaying said image straddling said
plurality of displays, using a predetermined operation to convert the physical coordinates that
indicate the position at which said contact is sensed to virtual coordinates;

a step of, when the application is displaying said image on one display among said
plurality of displays, reporting the physical coordinates at which said contact is sensed to the
application; and

a step of, when the application is displaying said image straddling said plurality of

displays, reporting said virtual coordinates to said application.

7. The coordinate notification method as set forth in claim 6, wherein, in said
conversion step, physical coordinates on, from among said plurality of displays, a display other
than a primary display that includes the coordinates of an origin that said application recognizes

are converted to said virtual coordinates.

8. The coordinate notification method as set forth in claim 7, wherein, in said
conversion step, from among said physical coordinates, coordinates of the direction along which

said primary display and a display other than the primary display are aligned are converted.

9. The coordinate notification method as set forth in claim 8, wherein, in said
conversion step, conversion to said virtual coordinates is carried out by adding to physical
coordinates on a display other than said primary display a coordinate portion that accords with

the size of said primary display.
