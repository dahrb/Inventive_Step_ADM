CLAIMS (MAIN REQUEST)

1. A simulated cockpit training system, comprising:

at least one pane (304, 306) that is large, clear, seamless and sized and shaped to at least
partially present an approximation of physical portions of the cockpit;

at least one projector (302) to backproject, via an optical path comprising at least one
optical element (402), a simulation of the cockpit onto the at least one pane (304, 306);

at least one tactile aspect, discrete from the at least one pane (304, 306), interactively
provided in association with the simulation; and

at least one processor (110) having communicatively associated therewith non-transitory
computing code for at least partially providing the simulation and the interaction with the at least
one tactile aspect, and further for enabling at least one training feature to be at least partially
overlaid on the backprojected simulation.

2. The system of claim 1, wherein the at least one pane (304, 306) 1s flat.

3. The system of claim 1, wherein the at least one pane (304, 306) comprises one of
glass and an acrylic.

4. The system of claim 1, wherein the at least one optical element (402) comprises at
least one beam folding mirror that is planar.

5. The system of claim 1, further comprising at least one physical support (408) for
the at least one pane (304, 306), wherein at least one of the at least one projector (302), the at
least one optical element (402), and the at least one tactile aspect are associated with the at least
one physical support (408).

6. The system of claim 1, wherein the at least one projector (302) comprises an LED
high definition projector.

7. The system of claim 1, wherein the at least one tactile aspect comprises at least
one of a button, a light, and a knob extending through the at least one pane (304, 306).

8. The system of claim 1, further comprising at least one power supply,
communicative with and physically associated with the at least one pane (304, 306) and suitable
to provide power to at least the at least one tactile aspect.

9. The system of claim 8, wherein the at least one power supply comprises at least
