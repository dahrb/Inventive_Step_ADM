TITLE:
Method and apparatus for indicating mobile electronic device status or state

TYPE:
US Patent

ABSTRACT:
Apparatus for indicating mobile electronic device status comprising a database for storing mobile electronic device status information and associated colour information; a set of light emitting diodes (LEDs); a processor for retrieving the colour information based on the mobile electronic device status and transmitting a signal based on the colour information to the set of LEDs; wherein the set of LEDs provide a light corresponding to the colour information to illuminate an area of the mobile communication device to indicate the mobile electronic device status.

CLAIMS:
1. A method of indicating mobile electronic device status comprising the steps of:
determining status of said mobile electronic device;
retrieving colour information associated with a mobile electronic device status; and
displaying a colour by illuminating said mobile electronic device, representative of said colour information, on said mobile electronic device to provide an indication of said mobile electronic device status.
2. The method of claim 1 further comprising the step, occurring before said step of determining, of:
monitoring said mobile communication device to determine said mobile electronic device status.
3. The method of claim 1 further comprising the step, occurring after said step of retrieving, of:
transmitting a signal, representative of said colour information, to a set of light emitting diodes (LEDs).
4. The method of claim 1 wherein said step of displaying comprises the step of:
illuminating a trackball of said mobile electronic device.
5. The method of claim 1 wherein said step of displaying comprises the step of:
illuminating an area surrounding a trackball of said mobile electronic device.
6. The method of claim 1 wherein said step of displaying comprises the step of:
illuminating an area surrounding a set of buttons of said mobile electronic device.
7. The method of claim 1 wherein said step of retrieving comprises the step of:
searching a database for said colour information associated with said mobile electronic device status.
8. The method of claim 1 further comprising the step, occurring before said step of retrieving, of:
receiving said mobile electronic device status.
9. The method of claim 1 wherein said mobile electronic device status is selected from the group comprising trackball usability, user profiles, status of an application on said mobile electronic device, status of battery, connectivity status, device state and email status.
10. Apparatus for indicating mobile electronic device status comprising:
a database for storing mobile electronic device status information and associated colour information;
a set of light emitting diodes (LEDs);
a processor for retrieving said colour information based on said mobile electronic device status and transmitting a signal based on said colour information to said set of LEDs;
wherein said set of LEDs provide a light corresponding to said colour information to illuminate an area of said mobile communication device to indicate said mobile electronic device status.
11. The apparatus of claim 10 wherein said area is selected from a group including a trackball, an area surrounding said trackball and an area surrounding a set of buttons.
12. The apparatus of claim 10 wherein said mobile electronic device information and said associated colour information is stored in a table.
13. The apparatus of claim 10 wherein said set of LEDs comprises single-coloured LEDs.
14. The apparatus of claim 10 wherein said set of LEDs comprises multi-coloured LEDs.
15. The apparatus of claim 10 wherein said set of LEDs comprises at least one LED.
16. The apparatus of claim 10 further comprising at least one audible indicator for indicating mobile electronic device status.
17. The apparatus of claim 10 further comprising at least one tactile indicator for indicating mobile electronic device status.
