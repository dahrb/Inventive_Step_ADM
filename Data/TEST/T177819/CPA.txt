Title: 
Functional ceramics manufacturing method, functional ceramic resin manufacturing method, functional ceramics, functional ceramic resin and water purification apparatus

Abstract: <P>PROBLEM TO BE SOLVED: To provide functional ceramics which can prevent a deterioration in water conditioning function of ceramics even when used for a long time, functional ceramic resin, a functional ceramics manufacturing method, and a water purification method. <P>SOLUTION: The functional ceramic resin manufacturing method comprises a process for mixing granular material made of components extracted from hornblende andesite and having a grain diameter of 1 μm or lower, granular material of zirconia having a grain diameter of 1 μm or lower, and granular material of magnesia having a grain diameter of 1 μm or lower at a weight ratio of 2:1:2, a process for adding purified water to the granular material obtained by mixing and pressurizing it, and a process for drying after the pressurization. <P>COPYRIGHT: (C)2006,JPO&NCIPI
