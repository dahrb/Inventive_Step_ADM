TITLE:
Organic electro luminescence display (OELD), OELD motherboard and testing method

TYPE:
European Patent

ABSTRACT:
An Organic Electro Luminescence Display (OELD) includes a pixel unit including: a plurality of pixels adapted to receive a first power supply voltage (ELVDD), a second power supply voltage (ELVSS) and scanning signals to emit light; a scan driving unit adapted to supply the scanning signals to the pixel unit; and a test unit adapted to test whether or not a defect in the pixel unit is present. The OELD further includes a first wire group arranged in a first direction and having a floating end; and a second wire group arranged in a second direction and having a floating end. This structure allows a sheet unit test to be conducted without previously scribing each OELD, by supplying the power supply voltages and the signals for their tests to a plurality of the OELDs formed on a motherboard simultaneously.
