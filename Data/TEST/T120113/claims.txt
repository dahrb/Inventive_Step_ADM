CLAIMS (main request):

1. An organic light emitting display device comprising:

a pixel unit (110) comprising pixels (120) at crossing regions of scan lines ($1,
$2, Sn) and data lines (D1, D2, D3, D3n-2, D3m-1, D3m);

a scan driving circuit (130) configured to supply scan signals to the scan lines
(S1, S2, Sn); and

a lighting test circuit (160) configured to supply lighting test signals to the data
lines (D1, D2, D3, D3m-2, D3m-1, D3m),

the lighting test circuit (160) comprising a plurality of transistors (M1, M2, M3,
Mm-2; Mm-1, Mm), the plurality of transistors comprising source electrodes (168), drain
electrodes (166), and gate electrodes (164), the source electrodes (168) being
coupled, in common, to an input line (TD) to which the lighting test signals are input,
the drain electrodes (166) being coupled to the data lines (D1, D2, D3, D3m-2, D3m-1,
D3m), the gate electrodes (164) being coupled, in common, to an input line (TG) of
test control signals, and the gate electrodes (164) and the source electrodes (168)

being coupled through a resistor (R) composed of a semiconductor material,

characterized in that
the resistor (R) is integral with a channel layer (Ch) of the transistors (M1, M2,
M3, Min-2, Mrn-1, Mm), and

the resistor (R) has a resistance value of 100kQ to 1MOQ .

2. The organic light emitting display device as claimed in claim 1, wherein

the resistor (R) is composed of a high-resistance polysilicon semiconductor.

3. The organic light emitting display device as claimed in one of the

preceding claims, further comprising:

a data distribution circuit (140) coupled between the lighting test circuit (160)
and the data lines (D1, D2, D3, D3mn-2, D3m-1, D3m) and configured to distribute and
output the lighting test signals supplied from the lighting test circuit (160) to the data
lines (D1, D2, D3, D3m-2, D3m-1, D3m) during a lighting test period.

4. The organic light emitting display device as claimed in one of the
preceding claims, further comprising:

a driving integrated circuit (180) mounted in a chip form and overlapped with
the lighting test circuit (160), the driving integrated circuit (180) having a built-in data

driving circuit.

5. A method of fabricating an organic light emitting display according to
one of claims 1 through 4, comprising:

forming a lighting test circuit (160) in a mounted region (150) configured to
host a driving integrated circuit (180),

wherein the resistor (R) is formed integrally with the channel layers (Ch) of the

transistors (M1, M2, M3, Mm-2, Mm-1, Mm) of the lighting test circuit (160).

6. The method of claim 5, further comprising:

mounting a driving integrated circuit (180) in the mounted region (150)
overlapping with the lighting test circuit (160), after a positive lighting test,

wherein, during a lighting test period, the lighting test circuit (160) performs a
lighting test and simultaneously turns all the transistors (M1, M2, M3, Mm-2, Mm-1, Mm)
on by the test control signal (TG) supplied to the respective gate electrodes (164),
and

wherein the lighting test circuit (160) is maintained at a turn-off state during the

actual driving period after the lighting test is completed.
