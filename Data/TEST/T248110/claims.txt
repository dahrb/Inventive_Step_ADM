MAIN REQUEST
CLAIMS:-

1. A method (400) for displaying medical information on a display (350) to
display information for a plurality of users based on gaze detection, the
method (400) comprising:

determining a direction of a user’s gaze in relation to a reference
position (420) using a portable eyewear and eye tracking system;

identifying a user looking toward a display (350) to display information
for a plurality of users based on the determined direction of the user’s gaze in
relation to the reference position; and

updating user specific information on the display (350) based on the
direction of a user’s gaze (430) and on the identity of the user,

wherein the identified user is allowed control of the display, based on
the identity of the user, to allow display of specific information targeted to the

identified user.

2. The method (400) of claim 1, where the updating step further
comprises updating information on the display (350) based on at least one of

preferences and input from the user.

3. The method (400) of claim 1, further comprising arbitrating between a
plurality of users to determine control of the display (350).

4. The method (400) of claim 1, further comprising allowing multiple users
to control information on the display (350).

5. The method (400) of claim 1, further comprising triggering a function at
the display (350) using at least one of a voice command, a gesture, and a
gaze (440).

6. The method (400) of claim 1, further comprising projecting an image

into a user’s oculars.
