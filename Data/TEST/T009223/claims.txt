1. A wet piping system modular valve assembly (10, 310) comprising: an
upstream control assembly (12) in series with a downstream check valve assembly (14),

the check valve assembly (14) defining a single piece valve body (34, 334)
housing a check valve (30, 330), and fluidly connecting a test, drain and pressure relief
single module (32) and a flow detection switch (28, 328);

the check valve (30, 330) comprising an endless valve seat (30a) within the valve
body (34, 334) and a clapper disk (30b, 330b) removably and pivotably mounted within
the valve body (34, 334), the clapper disk (30b, 330b) being movable between a closed
position, wherein the clapper disk (30b, 330b) is in sealed engagement with the valve seat
(30a), and an open position, wherein the clapper disk (30b, 330b) is spaced away from
the valve seat (30a), according to a pressure differential across the clapper disk (30b,
330b);

a test valve, a drain valve and a pressure relief valve combined into the test, drain
and pressure relief single module (32) removably mounted directly to the valve body (34,
334) and fluidly connected with the valve body (34, 334) downstream of the check valve
(30, 330); and

the flow detection switch (28, 328) being a mechanically independent flow
detection switch (28, 328) removably mounted directly to the valve body (34, 334) and
fluidly connected with the valve body (34, 334) upstream of the drain valve and the check
valve (30, 330), and

assembly (14), the control assembly (12) including a valve body (18) having a butterfly

position, permitting fluid flow through the valve body (18), and a closed position,
substantially preventing fluid flow through the valve body (18),

ports (52a, 52b; 352a, 352b) extending through a sidewall of the valve body (34, 334)
