MAIN REQUEST

CLAIMS

1. A method for enhancing red blood cell quality and survival during storage, the method
comprising:

(a) depleting a red blood cell sample comprising an acidified additive solution having a
pH of between 5.5 and 7.0, of both oxygen and carbon dioxide prior to storage; and

(b) transferring said oxygen- and carbon dioxide-depleted red blood cell sample to an
oxygen- and carbon dioxide-impermeable environment; and

(c) storing said depleted red blood cell sample in said oxygen- and carbon dioxide-

impermeable environment at a temperature between 1°C and 6°C.

2. The method of claim 1, wherein the 2,3-diphosphoglycerate (2,3-DPG) acid levels are

maintained during storage.

3. The method of claim 1, wherein the red blood cell sample is stored for at least three
weeks.

4. The method of claim 1, wherein the red blood cell sample is stored for at least seven
weeks.

5. The method of claim 1, wherein the red blood cell sample is stored for at least nine
weeks.

6. The method of claim 1, wherein the red blood cell sample is depleted of oxygen to a level

of approximately 10 mmHg at 21-25°C.

7. The method of claim 1, wherein the red blood cell sample is depleted of carbon dioxide

to a level of approximately 5 mmHg at 21-25°C.

8. The method of claim 1, wherein said acidified additive solution has a pH between 6.25

and 6.75.

9. The method of claim 1, wherein the red blood cell sample is selected from the group
consisting of whole blood, anti-coagulated whole blood, packed red cells and red cells separated

from plasma.
10. The method of claim 1, wherein reducing carbon dioxide in the red blood cell sample
maintains 2,3-DPG levels relative to a red blood cell sample in which carbon dioxide was not

depleted.

11. | The method of claim 1, wherein the depletion step comprises a leukocyte depletion step,

a platelet depletion step, or a combination thereof.

12. The method of claim 1, further comprising storing said oxygen- and carbon dioxide-

depleted red blood cell sample for at least 4 weeks.

13. The method of claim 1, wherein the red blood cell sample stored for at least 9 weeks has

less than 0.7% hemolysis.

14. The method of claim 1, wherein the red blood cell sample stored for at least 3 weeks has

less than 0.2% hemolysis.
