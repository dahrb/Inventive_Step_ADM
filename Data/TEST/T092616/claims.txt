Claims (main request)

1. A device (1) for handling a liquid sample to be assayed, said device comprising
a non-porous substrate having a substrate surface;

at least one open fluid passage, said at least one open fluid passage having a first
end at which fluid is adapted to be added to said device and a second end opposite
said first end, wherein said at least one open fluid passage consists entirely of an
open lateral fluid passage (27) supported by substantially perpendicular projections,
said projections having a height (H), diameter (D) and a distance or distances (t1, t2)
between the projections, capable of generating capillary flow, lateral to said substrate
surface, of a liquid sample added to the first end of said at least one open fluid
passage; and

at least one absorbing zone in fluid contact with said second end of said at least one
open fluid passage,

characterized in that said at least one absorbing zone comprises projections
substantially perpendicular to said substrate surface, said projections having a
height, diameter and a distance or distances between the projections, capable of
generating capillary flow, lateral to said substrate surface, of a liquid sample added to
the first end of said at least one open fluid passage, and a foil (45) placed on the top
of the perpendicular projections of said at least one absorbing zone, said foil or an
adhesive of the foil having hydrophilic properties adapted to influence the flow
velocity of said liquid sample from said first end to said second end of said open fluid
passage and to accurately limit the fluid volume defined by said projections, and

in that the volume of liquid sample delivered along the open fluid passage is
determined by the absorbing capacity of said absorbing zone and not by the amount
of sample added to the device.

2. The device according to claim 1, wherein said device is a disposable assay
device or a part of such device.

3. The device according to claim 1, wherein said device comprises parallel open
fluid passages (3, 5, 7) leading to the same absorbing zone (9) or to sections of the
same absorbing zone (9).

4. The device according to claim 1, wherein said device comprises parallel open
fluid passages (11, 13, 15) leading to two or more absorbing zones (17, 19, 21),
exhibiting the same or different absorption capacity.

5. |The device according to any one of claims 1 - 4, wherein the capacity of the
absorbing zone is at least equal to the volume of liquid sample to be transported.

6. A method for handling transport of a liquid sample in an assay device (1)
according to claim 1, the method comprising the steps of:

providing at least one open fluid passage on a non-porous substrate having a
substrate surface; the at least one open fluid passage having a first end and a
second end opposite said first end, wherein said at least one open fluid passage
consists entirely of an open lateral fluid passage (27) supported by substantially
perpendicular projections, said projections having a height (H), diameter (D) and a
distance or distances (t1, t2) between the projections, capable of generating capillary
flow, lateral to said substrate surface, of a liquid sample added to the first end of said
at least one open fluid passage;

providing at least one absorbing zone in fluid contact with said second end of said at
least one open fluid passage,

characterized in that said absorbing zone comprises projections substantially
perpendicular to said substrate surface, said projections having a height, diameter
and a distance or distances between the projections, capable of generating capillary
flow, lateral to said substrate surface, of a liquid sample placed in contact with said
absorbing zone from said second end of said at least one open fluid passage; and

in that the method further comprises:

applying a foil (45) on the top of the perpendicular projections of said at least one
absorbing zone; and

placing a liquid sample in contact with said first end of said at least one open fluid
passage so the liquid sample is drawn through said at least one open fluid passage
into the absorbing zone;

wherein the flow velocity of fluid added to said device along said at least one open
fluid passage is influenced by the pre-selected hydrophilic properties of the foil or an
adhesive of the foil; and

wherein the volume of liquid sample transported along said passage is determined by
the absorbing capacity of the absorbing zone and not by the amount of sample
added to the device.

7. |The method according to claim 6, wherein said substrate is a part of a
disposable assay device.

8. The method according to claim 6, wherein said device comprises parallel open
fluid passages (3, 5, 7) leading to the same absorbing zone (9) or to sections of the
same absorbing zone (9), exhibiting the same or different absorption capacity.

absorbing zone is at least equal to the volume of liquid sample to be transported.

10. An analytical or diagnostic test device comprising a device according to any
one of claims 1 - 5.
