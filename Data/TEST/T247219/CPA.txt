Title: Coin cell battery

Abstract: The present invention relates to a coin-type battery in which a power generation element is liquid-tightly sealed by a positive electrode case, a sealing plate and a gasket, and the gasket is controlled by controlling the compressibility of the gasket at 3 points. The purpose is to eliminate breakage and improve liquid leakage resistance. By setting the compressibility of the gasket between the U-shaped folded-back tip portion C and the positive electrode case in this order, it is possible to prevent liquid leakage and ensure an excellent effect of not breaking the gasket.
