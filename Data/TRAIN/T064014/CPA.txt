TITLE:
Plastic gut perforated by a laser

TYPE:
World International Patent Organisation

ABSTRACT:
The present invention relates to a biaxially stretched, polyamide-based film tube comprising at least one layer and being able to shrink perforated by means of laser light, especially a plastic gut which can be used as an artificial sausage skin.

CLAIMS:
Claims 1. Single or multi-layer, biaxially stretched, shrinkable, polyamide-based film tube dipped with laser light. 2. Film tube according to claim 1, wherein the hole diameter of the individual dipped holes is between 10 and 1000 μm, in particular between 100 and 300 μm. 3. film tube according to claim 1 or 2, wherein the used for dipping Laser light is generated by a CO 2 laser. 4. film tube according to one of claims 1 to 3 with a total thickness of 30 to 70 microns. 5. Use of a film tube according to one of claims 1 to 4 as a plastic casing in the manufacture of sausages. 6. A method for producing a film tube according to one of claims 1 to 4, in which a biaxially oriented, polyamide-based film tube produced by the tube stretching method is guided past at least one focused CO 2 laser in a separate assembly step or preferably inline and thereby with laser light is treated with sufficient intensity to prick the film tube. A method according to claim 6, wherein the film tube is passed flat past at least one focused CO 2 laser.
