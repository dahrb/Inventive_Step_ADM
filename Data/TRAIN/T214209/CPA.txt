Title: Process and device for damaged vehicle remote expertise

Abstract: Process and device for the remote expert-assessment of a damaged vehicle, by use of a system (10, 14), installed at a repair shop, for acquiring three-dimensional digital data representative of a damaged part of a vehicle (12), transmission of these data and of a computer dossier to an expert or to an assessment centre by way of a digital data transmission network (16), and by displaying and processing a digital three-dimensional image on a computer system (18) installed at the expert's or at the assessment centre. <IMAGE>

Type: EPO Patent

Claims: 

1- Method for the remote assessment of an accidented or damaged vehicle, this process consisting in optically acquiring data on the state of the vehicle, in digitizing them and transmitting them to an expert or to a center of expertise, characterized in that it consists of: - to acquire three-dimensional digital data representative of at least one damaged part of the vehicle (12) by optical means, this data comprising coordinates of surface points of said part with respect to a predetermined system of reference axes, - to transmit these three-dimensional data and vehicle identification data to an expert or a center of expertise, - then, at the expert's or at the center of expertise, to make a representation of said damaged part from the transmitted data and to apply automatic processing to highlight and quantify the damage of the vehicle for the determination of operations vehicle repair and evaluation of their cost. 2- Method according to claim 1, characterized in that it consists in using an existing network (16) for transmitting digital data to transmit the aforementioned data to the expert or to the center of expertise. 3- Method according to claim 1 or 2, characterized in that the acquisition of three-dimensional digital data of said part of the vehicle (12) consists in projecting on this part the image of a test pattern (20) with sinusoidal variation of light intensity, to modulate in a predetermined way the phase of this sinusoidal variation, to be captured on a matrix array (30) of photodetectors, for example of the CCD type, the images of the test pattern formed on said part of the vehicle, recording the output signals from the photodetectors and processing them to deduce by calculation the coordinates of the surface points of said part with respect to three predetermined reference axes. 4- Method according to one of the preceding claims, characterized in that it also consists in taking a two-dimensional color video image of at least said part of the vehicle, in digitizing it and in transmitting it to the expert or to the center expertise for visualization. 5- Method according to one of the preceding claims, characterized in that the processing of three-dimensional digital data carried out at the expert or at the center of expertise comprises a comparison of this data with that of an identical undamaged part of a vehicle, the latter being previously stored in memory or accessible by connection to databases, and a determination of the differences between the compared data. 6- Method according to one of the preceding claims,
