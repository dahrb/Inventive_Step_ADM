TITLE:
Method and apparatus for detecting the presence and/or concentration of biomolecules

TYPE:
European Patent

CLAIMS:
Method for detecting the presence and/or the concentration of biomolecules (41a-41c), in particular desoxyribonucleic acid molecules, wherein said biomolecules directly or indirectly adsorb to, deposit on, or are physically or chemically bound to a boundary surface (39) between an optically denser and an optically rarer medium, said boundary surface (39) including a thin conductive or semiconductive film, preferably a metal film (37), comprising the steps of
(1.1) irradiating p-polarized electromagnetic waves, preferably in the spectrum of visible light, through said optically denser medium onto said boundary surface, wherein
(1.2) the angle of incidence of the electromagnetic waves impinging onto said boundary surface (39) is basically equal to the angle (ϑSPR) at which surface plasmon resonance occurs, the surface plasmon wave causing said biomolecules (41a-41c) at said boundary surface (39) to reflect or generate radiation in response to said surface plasmon wave,
(1.3) monitoring the radiation reflected or generated by said biomolecules (41a-41c) at said boundary surface (39) and determining their presence and/or their concentration by analysis of said reflected or generated radiation,
characterized by the steps of
(1.4) monitoring the electromagnetic waves reflected at said boundary surface (39) and detecting their intensity,
(1.4) controlling the angle of incidence (ϑ) of the impinging p-polarized electromagnetic waves in dependence of the intensity of the electromagnetic waves reflected at said boundary surface (39) such that said intensity is substantially kept at a minimum corresponding to the occurence of surface plasmon resonance.
Method according to claim 1, characterized in that said optically denser medium is a transparent prism, preferably a glass prism (35,56).
Method according to claim 1 and 2, characterized by acutating a rotatable support (60) carrying said transparent prism (56) in dependence of said intensity.
Method according to claim 3, characterized in that said support (60) is rotated in a first direction and then in a second direction opposite to said first direction in case an increase or a substantial increase in said intensity is detected, or in case a predetermined time period (Tmax) has expired.
Method according to any of claims 1 to 3, characterized in that said electromagnetic waves reflected at said boundary surface (39) are fed to an array (80) of monitoring elements comprising at least a central monitoring element (80a) and at least a lateral element (80b,80c) at either side of said central monitoring element (80a), and that a control signal causing adjustment of said angle of incidence is generated if the intensity recorded by one of said lateral elements (80b,80c) is lower than the intensity recorded by said central element (80a).
Method according to claim any of the preceding claims, characterized in that said p-polarized electromagnetic waves are passed through a thin dielectric film (36,38) before and/or after passing through said thin conductive film.
Method according to any of the preceding claims, characterized in that said optically rarer medium is a solvent (40) in which said biomolecules (41a-41c) are dissolved such that said biomolecules (41a-41c) adsorb to, deposit on, or are physically or chemically bound to the optically rarer side of said boundary surface (39).
Method according to any of the preceding claims, characterized in that said biomolecules (27) or labeled biomolecules complementary to said biomolecules (27) are marked with a fluorescent, phophorescent, chemiluminescent or electroluminescent substance (30).
Method according to claims 7 and 8, characterized in that
(9.1) the optically rarer side of said boundary surface (39) is coated with capture molecules (28) complementary to said biomolecules (27),
(9.2) said solvent contains said biomolecules (27) and
(9.3) labeled biomolecules (29) complemetary to said biomolecules (27).
Apparatus for detecting the presence and/or concentration of biomolecules (41a-41c), in particular desoxyribonucleic acid molecules, comprising
(10.1) a source (31,50) of electromagnetic waves, preferably in the spectrum of visible light,
(10.2) polarizing means (33,51) for p-polarizing the electromagnetic waves emitted by said source (31,50) of electromagnetic waves,
(10.3) means for directing said p-polarized electromagnetic waves through an optically denser medium onto the boundary surface (39) between an said optically denser and an optically rarer medium,
(10.4) a thin conductive or semiconductive film, preferably a metal film (37), on said boundary surface (39),
(10.5) first monitoring means (45;61) for monitoring radiation reflected or generated by said biomolecules (41a-41c) at said boundary surface (39),
characterized by
(10.6) second monitoring means (46;61) for monitoring electromagnetic waves reflected at said boundary surface (39),
(10.7) intensity detection means (47) for detecting the intensity of said electromagnetic waves reflected at said boundary surface (39),
(10.8) control means (48) controlling the angle of incidence of the impinging p-polarized electromagnetic waves in dependence of the intensity of the electromagnetic waves reflected at said boundary surface (39) such that said intensity is substantially kept at a minimum which corresponds to the occurence of surface plasmon resonance.
Apparatus according to claim 10, characterized in that said second monitoring means (46;61) comprises a detector, preferably a detector responsive in the spectrum of said p-polarized electromagnetic waves only.
Apparatus according to claim 10, characterized in that said first monitoring means (45;61) and said second monitoring means (46;61) are a detector selectively responsive to said p-polarized electromagnetic waves and/or said electromagnetic waves reflected at said boundary surface (39).
Apparatus according to any of claims 10 to 12, characterized in that said second monitoring means comprises an array (80) of monitoring elements, preferably photodiodes or phototransistors, with at least a central monitoring element (80a) and at least a lateral element (80b,80c) at either side of said central monitoring element (80a), and that control signal generating means (81,82) are provided generating at least a control signal (87,88) if the intensity recorded by one of said lateral elements (80b,80c) is lower than the intensity recorded by said central monitoring element (80a), said control signal (87,88) being fed to said control means.
Apparatus according to any of claims 10 to 13, characterized in that said means for directing said p-polarized waves onto the boundary surface (39) comprises a transparent prism, preferably a glass prism (35,56).
Apparatus according to claim 14, characterized in that said transparent prism (56) is mounted on a support (60) rotatable by operating means, preferably a motor, which are controlled by said control means.
Apparatus according to claim 15, characterized by transmission means which rotates said support (60) around a first angle (ϑ) and said first and/or said second monitoring means (61) around twice said first angle (2ϑ).
Apparatus according to any of claims 10 to 16, characterized by a thin dielectric film (36,38) at the optically denser and/or the optically rarer side of said boundary surface (39).
