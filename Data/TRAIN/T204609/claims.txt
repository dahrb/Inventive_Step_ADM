AMENDED CLAIMS of the Main Request

1. An isolated and purified human or mouse urocortin III protein, wherein said protein has the

amino acid sequence depicted in SEQ ID NO: 3 and SEQ ID NO: 5, respectively.

2. The urocortin III protein of claim 1, wherein the N-terminus of said protein is modified with
an acylating agent selected from a carboxyl-containing moiety, a sulfonyl-containing moiety,

and an isocyanate.

3. The urocortin III protein of claim 1, wherein the N-terminus of said protein is extended with

additional amino acids or peptides.

4. The urocortin II protein of claim 3, wherein the additional amino acids or peptides are

threonine-lysine, D-tyrosine, L-tyrosine, D-tyrosine-glycine, or L-tyrosine-glycine.

5. The urocortin III protein of claim 1, wherein the N-terminus of said protein is chemically

crosslinked to a toxin molecule.

6. The urocortin III protein of claim 1, wherein the N-terminus of said protein is extended with
additional amino acids or peptides selected from D-iodotyrosine, L-iodotyrosine, D-
iodotyrosine-glycine, and L-iodotyrosine-glycine and wherein the methionine residues at

positions 12 and 35 are replaced with Nle.

7. The urocortin III protein of claim 6, wherein the iodotyrosine residue is labelled with an 157

radioisotope.

8. The urocortin II protein of claim 1, wherein the first five, six, seven, or eight N-terminal

amino acids have been deleted.

9. The urocortin III protein of claim 8, wherein the N-terminus of said protein is blocked with
an acylating agent selected from a carboxyl-containing moiety, a sulfonyl-containing moiety,

and an isocyanate.

10. The urocortin II protein of claim 1, wherein the protein is linked to a toxin.
