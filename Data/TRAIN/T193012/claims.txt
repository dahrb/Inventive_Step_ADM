Main Request

1. An optical device which in use is positioned on a surface (24) and which
illuminates an area of said surface under the device; the device comprising a printed
circuit board (50) arranged parallel to said surface (24); a light source (28) and a
light guide for projecting light from the light source (28) onto said area; wherein
the light guide comprises a body of optically transparent material formed to provide
an prism (10;30) having an input face (12) facing the light source (28) and an output
face (14) spaced from and at an angle to the input face (12); characterised in that:

the light source is arranged horizontally with respect to the optical device's
frame of reference;

in use, light from the light source (28) passing through the input face
undergoes bending and passage through the output face provides further bending
such that light from the light source is refracted from the horizontal onto said
surface by the prism (10;30);

said light source (28) and light guide are provided beneath an underside
surface of said printed circuit board (50); and in that

the prism (10; 30) is formed with an extension (42) adapted to locate the
light source (28) in a given relationship to the input face (12).

2. An optical device according to claim 1, in which the input face (12) is a

light-gathering face.

3. An optical device according to claim 2, in which the input face (12) is

positively curved.

4, An optical device according to claim 2, in which the input face is formed as

a Fresnel lens.

5. An optical device according to any preceding claim in which, with reference
to the perpendicular to said surface as 0°, the input face (12) has an angle from -
10° to +60°, the output face (14) has an angle from -60° to +10°, and one of said

faces has an angle less than +5°.

6. An optical device according to claim 5, in which the input face (12) has and
angle between 25° and 45°, and the output face (14) has an angle between 0° and -
5°,

7. An optical device according to claim 6, in which the input face has an angle

8. An optical device according to claim 5, in which the input face (12) has an

9. An optical device according to claim 8, in which the output face (14) has an

10. An optical device according to any preceding claim, in which said body of

optically transparent material is an integral body (30).

11. An optical device according to any preceding claim, in which the extension
(42) is adapted to mount a light emitting diode (28) such that the input face (12) is

at an angle to the emission axis of the light emitting diode (28).

12. | Anoptical device according to any preceding claim, in which the prism (30)
and the extension (42) are formed as a unit with a lens (34) which is positioned to

view said area of the surface (24) on which light is projected by the prism (30).

13. An optical device according to claim 13, in which the prism (30), the
extension (42) and the lens (34) form part of a single moulding of optically

transparent material.

14. An optical mouse comprising a base (20) adapted for sliding movement
across a surface (24), an aperture (26) in the base (20), a printed circuit board (50)
arranged parallel to said surface (24), a light source (28), an optical assembly
arranged to deviate light from the light source (28) through the aperture (26) to
impinge on said surface (24), a lens (34) mounted in the mouse to view the light
projected on said surface (24), and an image sensor (40) positioned to receive an
image from the lens (34); characterised in that the optical device is in accordance
with claim 1 and said light source (28), optical assembly, lens (34) and image sensor

(40) are provided beneath an underside surface of said printed circuit board (50).
