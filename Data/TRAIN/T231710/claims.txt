CLAIMS

1. An audio and/or video generation system (101) which is arranged in
operation to generate audio and/or video material representative of an audio and/or
visual source, said audio and/or video generation system comprising

a camera means (102) which is arranged in operation to generate audio and/or
video signals representing said audio and/or video material, the camera means (102)
including a recording means (122) which is arranged in operation to record said audio
and/or video signals representing said audio and/or video material on a recording
medium (126), and a metadata generation processor (128) which is arranged in
operation to receive said audio and/or video signals, and to generate first metadata
automatically in association with said audio and/or video signals, and

a portable data processor (12, 112) connected to the metadata generation
processor (128) of the camera means (102) using an interface (18, 118) having a
predetermined format, wherein said first metadata includes time code data
representative of in and out points of one or more parts of the audio/video material,
and said first metadata includes a unique identification code for each of the parts of
the audio and/or video material, each unique identification code uniquely identifying
one of the parts of audio and/or video material, and the portable data processor (112)
is arranged to generate second metadata in response to user commands and to
communicate the second metadata to said metadata generation processor (128) via
said interface (18, 11), said recording means being arranged to record said second
metadata with said first metadata and said audio and/or video signals on said

recording media (126).

2. An audio and/or video generation apparatus (101) as claimed in Claim
1, wherein said metadata generation processor (128) is operable to generate an
identifier of the recording media (126) on which the audio and/or video material is
recorded, and to store said first and said second metadata and said identifier of the
recording media in a data store for communication separately from the recording

media (126).

3. An audio and/or video generation apparatus as claimed in Claim 1 or 2,
wherein the unique identification code is a Universal Material Identifier (UMID) or

the like.

4. A method of generating audio and/or video material representative of
an audio and/or visual source, said method comprising

generating audio and/or video signals representative of an audio and/or visual
source using a camera means,

recording said audio and/or video signals on a recording medium using a
recording means forming part of the camera means,

generating first metadata automatically in association with said audio and/or
video signals using a metadata generation processor (128) forming part of the camera
means,

using a portable data processor to generate, in response to user commands,
second metadata,

communicating the second meta data from the portable data processor to the
metadata generation processor, and

recording said second metadata with said first metadata and said audio and/or
video signals on said recording media using said recording means, wherein said first
metadata includes time code data representative of in and out points of one or more
parts of the audio/video material, and said first metadata includes a unique
identification code for each of the parts of the audio and/or video material, each of the
unique identification codes uniquely identifying each of the parts of audio and/or

video material.

5. A method as claimed in Claim 4, comprising

generating an identifier of the recording media on which the audio and/or
video material is recorded, and

storing said first and second metadata and said identifier of the recording

media in a data store for communication separately from the recording media.

6. A method as claimed in any of Claims 4 or 5, wherein the unique

identification code is a Universal Material Identifier (UMID) or the like.
