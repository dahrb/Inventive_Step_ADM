Claims

1. A gesture recognition simulation system (10) comprising:

a three-dimensional display system (16) that is configured to display a three-
dimensional image of at least one simulated object (360, 410) having at least one
functional component (412), wherein the at least one functional component (412) is
a portion of the at least one simulated object (360, 410) in which simulated
interaction can be performed such that a gesture performed at or near a location of
the at least one functional component (412) causes an automatic rendering of a
simulated action on at least the portion of the at least one simulated object (360,
410);

a gesture recognition interface system (12) configured to receive an input
gesture associated with one of a simulated tool (414) that is displayed by the three-
dimensional display system and is reactive to a user's bare hand (416) and a
sensorless tool (364) held by the user's bare hand (416), respectively, the input
gesture being determined by at least one motion of the one of the simulated tool
(414) and the sensorless tool (364), respectively, or changes in a physical location of
the one of the simulated tool (414) and the sensorless tool (364), respectively,
relative to the at least one functional component (412); and

a simulation application controller (14) configured to match a given input
gesture with a predefined action associated with the at least one functional
component (412) upon contact of the one of the simulated tool (414) and the
sensorless tool (364), respectively, with the at least one functional component (412)
based on correlating a physical location in three-dimensional space associated with
one of an image of the simulated tool (414) and the sensorless tool (364),
respectively, with a physical location in three-dimensional space associated with an
image of the at least one functional component (412), and to invoke the three
dimensional display system (16) to display the simulated action associated with the
predefined action on at least the portion of the at least one simulated object (360,
410) associated with the at least one functional component (412).

(12) comprises:
