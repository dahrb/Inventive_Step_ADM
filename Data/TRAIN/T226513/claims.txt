Amended claims
1. A method for preparing a shaped and filled cheese product,

comprising coextruding a first edible phase and a second edible phase, wherein
the second phase (filling) is completely enclosed by the first phase (covering),
which first phase comprises semi-hard and/or hard cheese, wherein the
coextruded phases are compressed with the aid of a diaphragm valve, such
that the first phase completely encloses the second phase and a discrete
product is formed.

2. A method according to claim 1 wherein, during the extrusion, at
least the first phase has a temperature of maximally 14°C.

3. A method according to claim 1 or 2, wherein the temperature of the
first phase is 4-12°C, preferably 4.0-7.0°C.

4, A method according to claim 1, 2 or 3, wherein the pH of the second
phase is between 2 and 6.

5. A method according to any one of the preceding claims, wherein the
pH of the second phase is maximally 4.5.

6. A method according to any one of the preceding claims, wherein the
pH of the first phase is at least 0.5 pH higher than the pH of the second phase.
7. A method according to any one of the preceding claims, wherein the
first phase comprises at least one hard or semi-hard cheese chosen from the
group consisting of: Gouda cheeses, Edam cheeses, Maasdam cheeses, cheeses
with a fat content of at least 20 wt.% in dry matter, cheeses with an age of
minimally 15 days, cheeses with added herbs and cheeses with added spices.
8. A method according to any one of the preceding claims, wherein the
second phase comprises at least one ingredient chosen from the group
consisting of mustard, pesto, mayonnaise, fruit paste, fruit concentrate,
membrillo, tomato paste, tomato purée, doughy pastes, cookies, crispy parts in

a viscous carrier, viscous pastes, purées, aromas, colorings, fruit pieces,
