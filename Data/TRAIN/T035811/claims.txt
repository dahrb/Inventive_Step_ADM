Claims

1. A photosensitive device structure (200), comprising:

a conductive core (202, 204) including a first electrode (204);

an organic layer (206) comprising a photoactive region for absorbing light to form
an exciton for being dissociated at a heterojunction formed by the juxtaposition of an
acceptor layer and a donor layer, wherein the thicknesses of the individual layers are
less than or equal to the diffusion distance of the exciton, and wherein the organic layer
(206) surrounds the core and is electrically connected to the first electrode; and

a transparent second electrode (208) surrounding and electrically connected to the

organic layer (206).

2. The structure of claim 1, wherein the core (202, 204) includes a non-conductive support
element and a conductive first electrode (204) surrounding the non-conductive support

element.

3. The structure of claim 2, wherein the support element comprises a nylon fiber.

4. The structure of claim 2, wherein the support element comprises an optical fiber.

5. The structure of claim 1, wherein the conductive core (202, 204) comprises a metal wire.

6. The structure of claim 1, further comprising an electrically conductive auxiliary conductor

(210) electrically coupled to the second electrode (208).

7. The structure of claim 6, wherein the auxiliary conductor (210) has an axis substantially

parallel to an axis of the core (202, 204).

8. The structure of claim 6, wherein the auxiliary conductor (210) is wound around the
second electrode (208) with a duty cycle of at least about the circumference of the second

electrode (208).

9. The structure of claim 6, wherein the auxiliary conductor (210) is one of a metallic wire, a

metallized wire, a metallic ribbon, a metallized ribbon, and a metallic coating.

10. The structure of claim 6, wherein the auxiliary conductor (210) is braid of electrical

conductors surrounding the second electrode (208).

11. The structure of claim 1, wherein the first electrode (204) comprises a material selected

from the group consisting of silver, gold, copper, and aluminum.

12. The structure of claim 1, wherein the second electrode (208) comprises a material

selected from the group consisting of PEDOT and PSS.

13. The structure of claim 1, wherein the organic pheteaetive layer (206) further comprises a

nonphotoactive region.

14. The structure of claim 13, wherein the non-photoactive region is an exciton blocking

layer.

15. The structure of claim 1, wherein the photoactive region comprises a heterojunction
between a pair of organic photoactive materials, the pair of organic photoactive materials
being selected from the group consisting of PCBM / MDMO-PPV, CuPc/C60, and CuPc /
PTCBI.

16. The structure of claim 1, further comprising an outer layer (212) surrounding the second
electrode (208).

17. The structure of claim 1, wherein the first electrode (204), the organic photoactive layer
(206), and the second electrode (208) comprise an organic light emitting device, and the

organic photeactive layer (206) further comprises a light emitting layer.

18. The structure of claim 17, wherein the organic layer (206) further comprises a non-

emissive layer.

19. The structure of claim 18, wherein the organic pheteactive layer (206) further comprises
first and second blocking layers disposed adjacent to and in physical contact with the

emissive layer.

20. The photosensitive device structure of claim 1 woven into a cloth.
