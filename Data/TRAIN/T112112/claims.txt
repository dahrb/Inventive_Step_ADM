Main Request — 9th June, 2015
CLAIMS

1. A stent having a coating applied thereto, said coating formed from a mixture of (i) a

polymer and (ii) rapamycin or a macrocyclic lactone analog of rapamycin.

2. The stent of claim 1, wherein the stent is dip-coated.
3. The stent of claim 1, wherein the stent is sprayed with said coating.
4. The stent of any one of the preceding claims, wherein said rapamycin or said

analog is contained in the coating at a weight percentage of 0.001% to 30%.

5. The stent of any one of claims 1 to 4, wherein the polymer is biocompatible and
degradable.
6. The stent of claim 5, wherein the polymer is chosen from: lactone-based polyesters

or copolyesters; polyorthoesters, polyanhydrides; poly-aminoacids; polysaccharides;

polyphosphazenes; poly(ether-ester) copolymers; and blends of such polymers.

7. The stent of any one of claims 1 to 4, wherein the polymer is nonabsorbable and
biocompatible.
8. The stent of claim 7, wherein the polymer is chosen from: polydimethylsiloxane;

poly(ethylene-vinylacetate); acrylate based polymers or copolymers, polyvinyl

pytrolidone; fluorinated polymers; and cellulose esters.
